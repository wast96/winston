### The Trade on the Move

In every hotel there are women who sell themselves, moving unremarked among the guests. Among these sellers are streetwalkers and pheasants alike. They keep up a fair understanding with the hotel boys, and toward nightfall come without being sent for; if a guest takes a fancy to one on the spot and the price is agreed, he may melt his soul with her then and there, and there are cases where the boy plays go-between. When they can find no client, they pace up and down the corridors or fall into idle talk with the boys, and only when the night is far gone and no custom has come do they go disappointed home.

Such women may be called "the trade on the move"; alongside massage-on-the-move and singing-on-the-move, this is the third of the itinerant trades.
