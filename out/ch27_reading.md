## Appendix I. Charter of the Heng Society

### Chapter One. General Principles

Article 1. The purpose of this Society is to foster fellow-feeling and mutual aid.

Article 2. This Society shall be named the Heng Society.

Article 3. This Society shall establish its head office in Shanghai. Where a locality at home or abroad has thirty or more members, a branch office may be established upon petition to and with the approval of the head office.

### Chapter Two. Members

Article 4. A member of this Society must be introduced by two or more existing members, complete an application form, and, upon approval by the Council of Directors, be issued a membership certificate.

Article 5. A member of this Society is under the obligation to pay the membership dues and to observe this Charter.

Article 6. A member of this Society may be declared expelled by the Council of Directors in any of the following cases: (a) failure to pay dues for more than two weeks; (b) damage to the good name of the Society as a body.

### Chapter Three. Organization

Article 7. This Society shall have a Council of Directors, composed of nineteen directors and eleven alternate directors, as its highest executive organ; its members shall be chosen by the general meeting of members, for a term of two years, and may be re-elected, but for no more than three terms.

Article 8. Below the Council of Directors there shall be a Standing Council of Directors, composed of nine standing directors, which shall exercise the powers of the Council during the intervals when the Council is not in session; its members shall be chosen by the Council of Directors.

Article 9. The regulations governing the organization of the Council of Directors shall be laid down separately.

### Chapter Four. Sessions

Article 10. The general meeting of the members of this Society shall be held once every half-year, in the spring and autumn seasons; provided that, should any special circumstance arise, or upon the request of thirty or more members, the Council of Directors may convene an extraordinary general meeting of members.

### Chapter Five. Finances

Article 11. The funds of this Society are of two kinds, special and regular. (a) Special funds. Special funds are raised in the following two ways: (i) as resolved by the general meeting of members; (ii) by voluntary donation from members and non-members. (b) Regular funds. Regular dues are of two kinds, five yuan and two yuan a month; they are collected once every half-year, or paid monthly, as each member elects, but all members enjoy equal rights alike.

### Chapter Six. Supplementary Provisions

Article 12. Where anything in this Charter is found wanting, it may, on the proposal of five or more directors or of thirty or more members, be amended with the approval of the general meeting of members.

Article 13. This Charter shall take effect from the day it is passed by the general meeting of members.
