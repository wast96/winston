### The Big License

The permit carried on a rickshaw is what people call a "license." Some rickshaws have three of them nailed up (that is, for the Chinese city, the British Settlement, and the French Concession—three plates), some have two, and some but one, and no two alike. If a man in the Chinese quarter wants to ride into the concessions and hails a rickshaw, he always asks: "Have you the big"—sounded du—"license?"

The concession permit being called the "big license," a man's own Chinese-quarter permit is of course the "small license"—and this too, I daresay, is one of the ways the Chinese have of honoring the foreigner and abasing themselves!
