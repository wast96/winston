### The Hoodlum's Missus

There is a kind of woman in this society whom people, in an undertone behind her back, all address as a "hoodlum's missus." And what rank of person, precisely, is such a woman? She is one who can get around and get away in society, and who can talk besides, and curse, and brawl; a woman must possess all these several talents and qualifications before the title "hoodlum's missus" will ring out for her with any weight.

There are, too, a few woman-tycoons who, aping the male notables, take in female disciples wholesale, to extend their power and swell the ranks of their claws and teeth.
