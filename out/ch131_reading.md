### Kidnappers

When the snatching of hostages first showed itself a few years back, there was only the one gang, out of Shengxian; now there are close on a dozen, distinguished as the Shandong gang, the Huaiyang gang, the Pudong gang, the Taihu gang and the rest. And within a big gang there is a middle gang, and the middle gang is split again into little gangs. The factions being so many and their numbers so great, the rich men of the propertied class feel all the more a trembling dread, and can no longer sleep easy in their beds.

The big gangs, it is said, are so thoroughly and tightly organized that their cases are very hard to crack; the kidnappings the papers report as solved all belong to the middle-gang or little-gang sort.
