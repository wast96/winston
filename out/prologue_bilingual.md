# Prologue bilingual audit (QC only)

> 南京。夕阳下，黄埔路蒋介石官邸。

Nanjing. Sunset over Chiang Kai-shek's official residence on Huangpu Road.

> 一辆军用小吉普，从一条绿阴覆盖的小路上悄悄驶过来，它在官邸大门前轻捷地绕了个弯，躲过前门那些荷枪的警卫，从左侧偏门驶进了这座戒备森严的大院。小吉普驶过绿阴葱茏的后园，沿着一条无人的青砖甬路缓缓驶进幽深的前院。不久，这辆神秘的小吉普就刹在一幢灰色的小楼下。

A small army jeep came quietly up a lane roofed in green shade, swung nimbly round the bend before the main gate, slipped past the sentries with their slung rifles, and entered the heavily guarded compound by the side door on the left. It rolled through the lush rear garden, crept along a deserted gray-brick path into the hush of the front courtyard, and before long braked beneath a small gray building.

> 从吉普车上走下来的人头戴一顶礼帽，鼻梁上的墨镜遮住了他那双幽幽的眼睛。他就是大特务戴笠。此时戴笠是刚刚接到侍从室的电话，才急匆匆地赶来，走进这座幽深宁静的官邸，他心里顿时感到几分紧张。

The man who stepped down wore a fedora, and the dark glasses on his nose hid a pair of deep, shadowed eyes. This was the master agent Dai Li. The telephone call from the aides' office had only just come; he had rushed straight over, and as he walked into the deep quiet of the residence he felt his nerves pull tight.

> 戴笠悄悄走进小楼的走廊。里面静悄悄的，几乎没有人迹。他屏住呼吸，沿着铺有猩红地毯的螺旋形楼梯，爬上二楼拐弯处，这才发现有位秘书在那里等候。秘书迎上来，小声地对他说："戴先生，委座就在里间等着你，有非常紧要的事情要你去办！"

Dai Li went softly into the corridor of the little building. It was still inside, hardly a sign of life. Holding his breath, he climbed the spiral staircase with its scarlet carpet, and only at the second-floor landing did he find a secretary waiting. The man came forward and said in a low voice: "Mr. Dai, the Generalissimo is in the inner room waiting for you. There is a matter of the greatest urgency for you to handle."

> 戴笠心里怦怦乱跳，他是平生头一次单独来见蒋介石。当他出现在一间偌大的办公室里时，蓦然发现对面青天白日旗下，端坐着一位穿灰布长衫的人，正在那里定定地打量着他，冷冷的，一言不发。

His heart hammered. In all his life he had never been received by Chiang Kai-shek alone. When he stepped into the enormous office he was startled to find, across the room beneath the Blue Sky and White Sun flag, a man in a plain gray cotton gown sitting perfectly upright, taking his measure — steadily, coldly, without a word.

> 戴笠脸上顿时现出紧张的神色："校长，您叫我……？"

The strain showed at once on Dai Li's face. "Commandant — you sent for me…?"

> 蒋介石仍不说话，只是抬起手来，向垂手侍立、紧张得不敢近前的戴笠招了一下，戴笠马上走上几步，再次向端坐不动的蒋躬身致礼："校长，有什么任务，学生我一定不惜一切代价去完成它！……"

Still Chiang said nothing. He only raised a hand and crooked it at Dai Li, who stood stiffly at attention, not daring to come nearer. Dai Li stepped forward quickly and bowed again to the motionless figure. "Commandant, whatever the task, your student will carry it out at any cost!…"

> "好，好好，戴雨农，你过来。"戴笠在偷偷斜视的时候发现，蒋介石与他从前在广州黄埔军校时见过的校长已大不相同。现在的蒋介石已成了国民党的最高首脑，神色更加威严凝重。"雨农，"蒋介石盯他的眼神变得柔和起来。戴笠悄悄望去，发现脸色阴沉的蒋介石忽然从抽屉中取出个纸袋来，随手一抖，从里面露出一叠黑白照片来，蒋介石随手拣起一张，推到他面前："你认识这个人吗？"

"Good. Good, good. Dai Yunong — come here." Stealing a sideways glance, Dai Li saw that this was no longer the Commandant he had known at the Whampoa Academy in Guangzhou. Chiang Kai-shek was now the supreme head of the Nationalist Party, and the gravity sat heavier on him. "Yunong." The eyes fixed on Dai Li softened. Watching covertly, he saw the dark-faced Chiang draw a paper envelope from a drawer and give it a shake; out slid a sheaf of black-and-white photographs. Chiang picked one off the top and pushed it across. "Do you know this man?"

> 戴笠大气不敢出，小心接过蒋递来的那张黑白照片。上面是一个男人模糊的背影！他无言地摇了摇头。

Dai Li scarcely dared breathe. He took the photograph with care. It showed the blurred back of a man — nothing more. He shook his head without a word.

> "呃，你再看看这些。"蒋又将那叠照片都推给了他。戴笠心跳得更厉害了，他急忙接过厚厚一叠照片，一一细看起来。戴笠发现这些照片，都是什么人偷偷在暗处拍下来的。几张照片拍下的几乎都是同一个人，有的是正面，有些是侧面，但是大多数都是背影。

"Mm. Then look at these." Chiang pushed the whole sheaf over. Dai Li's heart beat harder still; he took the thick stack and went through it one photograph at a time. All of them had been taken in secret, from the shadows, and nearly every frame showed the same man: full face here, profile there, but most often only his back.

> 戴笠开始看时，尚未认出这些效果并不理想的照片上，被偷拍的究竟是何人。忽然，他眼里露出惊愕的光，因为他看清其中一张照片是那个人的正面像。脸形轮廓十分熟稔。照片上的人好像正在一个会场上振振有词地讲演，他身后的背景是大会的主席台。几位国民党高官正襟危坐，都是些熟悉的脸孔。其中就有蒋介石以及其他国民党要人李宗仁、冯玉祥、阎锡山等人。

At first he could not tell who the subject of these poor stolen shots might be. Then astonishment lit his eyes: one photograph caught the man's face straight on, and the shape of that face was deeply familiar. He seemed to have been photographed in mid-speech, declaiming from a platform, and behind him rose the presidium of some great assembly — a row of senior Nationalist officials sitting stiff and correct, familiar faces every one. Among them was Chiang Kai-shek himself, with other grandees of the party: Li Zongren, Feng Yuxiang, Yan Xishan.

> "他是谁？"戴笠越看越觉得照片上那个头戴礼帽，身穿黑色长袍的人有些眼熟。特别是那人唇上的小胡子和鼻梁上那架黑框眼镜，立刻让戴笠想起一个人来，他不禁失声叫道："校长，莫非他就是当年在上海名噪一时的斧头帮主……王亚樵吗？"

"Who is he?" The longer Dai Li looked at the man in the fedora and black gown, the more he knew him. It was the small mustache and the black-rimmed spectacles that did it; a name rose, and broke from him before he could stop it: "Commandant — can this be the man whose name rang through Shanghai in those years — the master of the Hatchet Gang… Wang Yaqiao?"

> "娘希匹，就是此人！"蒋介石气咻咻望了戴笠一眼，"从前你在上海落魄的时候，就认识他吗？"

"God damn his mother — the very man!" Chiang shot him a furious glance. "You knew him, did you, in your down-and-out Shanghai days?"

> "我……听说过王亚樵的名字。"戴笠在蒋介石威严的目光下，禁不住哆嗦一下。他不但认识王亚樵，而且当年两人还有非同一般的交情。然而，多年跟随蒋介石南征北伐的戴笠，深知蒋介石的凶险。他知道为这个照片上的王亚樵，就把他召进黄埔路官邸，必有非同一般的缘由。戴笠急忙把想说的话咽回去，迟疑着说："那时我在上海是混日子，生活贫困潦倒，没有和王亚樵接触的机会……""斧头帮厉害吗？"蒋又问。

"I… had heard Wang Yaqiao's name." Under that imperial stare Dai Li could not keep from trembling. He had not merely known Wang Yaqiao; the two had once been closer than most men ever are. But years of campaigning at Chiang's side had taught Dai Li exactly how dangerous his master was. If Chiang had called him into the Huangpu Road residence over the man in these photographs, the reason would be no ordinary one. He swallowed what he had been about to say and answered instead, hesitating: "In those days I was drifting through Shanghai, poor as dirt. I never had occasion to meet him…" "Is the Hatchet Gang formidable?" Chiang pressed.

> "厉害！斧头帮那是上海最厉害的帮派之一，仅次于青红帮。特别是王亚樵，本事更大，听说连上海滩的黄金荣、杜月笙和张啸林三位大亨，也惹不起他！不知校长为何忽然提到此人？"

"Formidable! The Hatchet Gang is one of the deadliest brotherhoods in Shanghai — second only to the Green and Red Gangs. And Wang Yaqiao himself is more formidable than his gang. They say even the three great bosses of the Shanghai waterfront — Huang Jinrong, Du Yuesheng, Zhang Xiaolin — take care not to cross him! But why does the Commandant suddenly speak of this man?"

> "这个叫王亚樵的家伙，现在是非除掉他不可了。"蒋介石这才咬牙切齿地说出他的打算，"早在北伐的时候，就有人对我说起王亚樵，说他乃一乱世枭雄，势力浩大，劝我尽早除掉他。可是，那时我没把此人当回事，以为斧头帮不过是些安徽的地痞无赖，何足挂齿？可是，自南京国民政府组成以来，这个人越来越不像话了。娘希匹，他竟然胆敢当着我的面，大骂我蒋某人不是孙中山的真正信徒！这还了得？如果继续容忍他胡闹下去，那么，我蒋某人恐怕有一天就要让位给他王亚樵了！"

"Because this Wang Yaqiao has become a man who cannot be left alive." Now at last Chiang ground out his purpose through set teeth. "As far back as the Northern Expedition there were people warning me about Wang Yaqiao. A hero-villain of the chaos, they said, with a vast following; root him out early. I didn't take the man seriously — a hatchet gang, some Anhui dirt-toughs and loafers, why waste a thought on them? But since the National Government was formed at Nanjing, this person has passed all bounds. God damn him — he dared stand before my face and roar to the world that I, Chiang, am no true disciple of Sun Yat-sen! Is that to be borne? Indulge his circus much longer and the day may come when I, Chiang, must hand my seat to Wang Yaqiao!"

> "什么，竟然会有这种的事，莫非他王亚樵吃了熊心虎胆，胆敢当面攻击校长？"戴笠听到这里，不由倒吸一口凉气。他忽然从那张抢拍的照片上，发现王亚樵正在国民政府成立大会上讲演，王亚樵身后的蒋介石，正愤怒地盯着他。戴笠这才恍悟蒋介石为何将他紧急召来，忙说，"校长的意思是……"

"What — such a thing actually happened? Has this Wang Yaqiao eaten a bear's heart and a tiger's gall, that he dares attack the Commandant to his face?" Dai Li drew in a breath of cold air. And then he saw it, there in the hastily snatched photograph: Wang Yaqiao speaking at the founding assembly of the National Government, and behind him Chiang Kai-shek, glaring in fury. Only now did he understand why he had been summoned in such haste. "Then the Commandant's meaning is…"

> "雨农，你的十人团，可都是我从武汉带到南京的。"蒋介石道，"以前你的任务只是搜集情报。可是现在不同了，我们在南京成立了政府。你领导的十人团，就应该为保护国民政府而存在。你可懂我的意思？"

"Yunong, your Ten-Man Team — every one of them I brought from Wuhan to Nanjing," Chiang said. "Until now your work has been the gathering of intelligence. But things have changed: we have made a government at Nanjing. From this day the team you lead exists to protect the National Government. Do you follow me?"

> "我懂我懂。"戴笠虽然从蒋介石的话中，明白王亚樵已被纳入蒋介石的黑名单，可是，不知为什么他却明知故问说："莫非校长的意思，是派我们搜集王亚樵最近的活动情况？"

"I follow, I follow." From Chiang's words Dai Li understood perfectly well that Wang Yaqiao had been entered on the blacklist — and yet, for reasons he himself could not have named, he asked what he already knew: "Does the Commandant mean us to gather intelligence on Wang Yaqiao's recent activities?"

> "不，不是搜集情况，我是要你们马上搞清他住在南京什么地方，"蒋介石恶狠狠举起手来，做了个杀头的手势，"懂吗？"

"No. Not gather intelligence. I want you to find out — at once — where he is living in Nanjing." Chiang raised his hand, vicious, and drew it across in the gesture of a beheading. "Understood?"

> 戴笠明白了蒋介石的意图，他是想急于暗杀王亚樵。他知道王亚樵在出席国民政府成立大会以后，还没有返回上海。得到这一密杀命令，戴笠心里忽泛踌躇。但他不敢在手握重权的蒋介石面前有丝毫迟疑，忙将王亚樵的照片收好，双腿一碰，腰杆一挺，大声说道："校长放心，只要他王亚樵现在还在南京，我们马上就提他的人头见您！"

Dai Li understood. Chiang wanted Wang Yaqiao dead, and quickly. He knew that Wang had not yet returned to Shanghai after the founding assembly. At this secret kill order something in him wavered — but before Chiang Kai-shek and all the power gathered in those hands, he dared not show one flicker of hesitation. He put the photographs carefully away, snapped his heels together, straightened his back and said loudly: "Set your mind at rest, Commandant. So long as Wang Yaqiao is still in Nanjing, we will bring you his head!"

> 蒋介石听了，脸上这才露出一丝笑纹。

At that, at last, a thread of a smile crossed Chiang's face.

> 戴笠向他又鞠了一躬，然后向门旁走去。

Dai Li bowed once more and started for the door.

> "雨农，"蒋介石又招手将戴笠叫住，"记住，行动一定缜密，事情要做得麻利一些，千万别给那些到南京参加大会的代表，留下任何暗杀的口实。你可懂我的意思？"

"Yunong." Chiang beckoned him back. "Remember: the action must be watertight, and quick about it. And whatever happens, the delegates who came to Nanjing for the assembly must be given no pretext — none — to cry assassination. Do you follow me?"

> "校长，我懂了！"戴笠再次向坐在青天白日满地红旗帜下的蒋介石，毕恭毕敬地应诺一声，然后悄悄退了出去。

"Commandant, I understand!" Dai Li gave his most reverent assent to the man seated beneath the Blue Sky, White Sun, and Wholly Red Earth, and withdrew as quietly as he had come.

> 夜色如墨。金陵古城灯火辉煌。

Night like ink. The old city of Jinling blazed with lamps.

> 黑幽幽的长江上倒映着点点簇簇的灯火，戴笠独自守候在鸡鹅巷54号幽深的小院里。他将台灯开亮，依次审视蒋介石交给他的那些照片。显而易见，那都是特务们暗中偷拍的王亚樵在南京活动的照片。他眼前现出一个非常隆重的场面，那是南京国民政府成立大会的会场。就在蒋介石和主席台上国民党大员们热烈鼓掌的时候，忽然，从一条红地毯上走来一位身穿黑色长袍的中年人，他就是以工人代表身份出席这次大会的安徽人王亚樵。

Scattered clusters of light lay reflected on the black sweep of the Yangtze. Dai Li kept watch alone in the deep little courtyard at No. 54 Ji'e Alley. He switched on the desk lamp and went through Chiang's photographs one by one. Plainly they were agents' work: Wang Yaqiao in Nanjing, shot from the shadows. And before his eyes rose a scene of high ceremony — the hall of the founding assembly of the National Government. Just as Chiang Kai-shek and the grandees on the presidium were warmly applauding, up the red carpet came a middle-aged man in a black gown: the Anhui man attending the assembly as delegate of the workers, Wang Yaqiao.

> "各位代表先生，我王亚樵今天要说几句心里话！"王亚樵登上主席台开始讲演的时候，全场立刻静了下来。蒋介石望着前面这身材不高，讲起话来土里土气的安徽人，心里感到不悦。后来，蒋的脸色变得越加难看了。因为王亚樵公然讲出影射和攻击蒋介石的话来。

"Delegates! I, Wang Yaqiao, have a few words to speak today from the heart!" As he mounted the rostrum the hall fell silent. Chiang Kai-shek studied the man before him — short of stature, his speech rough with the countryside — and felt a stir of dislike. Soon his face was uglier still. For Wang Yaqiao had begun to speak words aimed, thinly veiled, at Chiang Kai-shek himself.

> 王亚樵大声地讲道："诸位代表都知道，国父孙中山先生留下了遗言，叫做联俄、联共、扶助农工，这就是我们国民党人必须遵守的三大政策。可是，让人大为痛心的是，就在孙先生尸骨未寒的时候，那些从前在孙先生面前自称为学生的人，现在居然举起了杀人的屠刀，在上海向我们工人大开杀戒了！如此背叛孙先生遗嘱的行径，又怎能不让我们这些中山先生的信徒为之寒心呢？……"

Wang Yaqiao's voice rang out: "Every delegate here knows that the Father of the Nation, Mr. Sun Yat-sen, left us his dying charge — alliance with Russia, alliance with the Communists, succor for the workers and peasants: the Three Great Policies that every Nationalist is bound to keep. And what cuts to the heart is this: with Mr. Sun's bones barely cold, the very men who once stood before him calling themselves his students have raised the butcher's knife, and in Shanghai they have opened slaughter upon us — upon the workers! Conduct that so betrays Mr. Sun's testament — how should it not chill the hearts of all of us who keep his faith?…"

> 会场上有人鼓起掌来，看得出王亚樵的讲话在一些人中引起了共鸣。主席台上的蒋介石如坐针毡，他双眼牢牢盯着王亚樵，脸色铁青。

Applause broke out here and there; the speech was plainly striking sparks in some quarters of the hall. On the presidium Chiang Kai-shek sat as if on a cushion of needles, eyes nailed to Wang Yaqiao, his face livid.

> 王亚樵的演讲越来越激昂："孙先生虽然已经故去了，可是，我们这些真正的信徒还在。国民党内还有大量真正革命者，他们是绝不会同意有人践踏孙中山先生三大政策的！所以，我作为工人的代表，今天要在这里向与会的各界代表郑重地发出呼吁：一定要永远牢记孙先生的三大政策，誓死捍卫三民主义！打倒反动的军阀主义，革命万岁！……"

The speech climbed higher and higher: "Mr. Sun is gone — but we, his true disciples, remain. In the Nationalist Party there are still revolutionaries in their numbers, and they will never consent to see the Three Great Policies trampled underfoot! So today, as the delegate of the workers, I call solemnly upon the delegates of every circle here assembled: hold Mr. Sun's Three Great Policies in your memory forever, and defend the Three Principles of the People to the death! Down with reactionary warlordism! Long live the revolution!…"

> 蒋介石不知什么时候已经悄然退席了。

At some point, unnoticed, Chiang Kai-shek had quietly left the hall.

> 戴笠想到这里，心里不禁一惊，他的脸色也顿时变了。从衣袋里小心掏出一支勃朗宁手枪，在灯下他拉开了枪膛，里面露出闪亮的子弹。

Remembering it now, Dai Li started, and his own face changed. From his coat pocket he carefully drew a Browning pistol and worked the action under the lamp; the light found the shine of the rounds inside.

> 就在他咬紧牙关，下决心行刺的时候，耳边忽然传来一个熟悉的声音："戴春风，你可以去广东，也可以另寻出路飞黄腾达。但是，你千万不要忘记，到任何时候，也不能忘记自己曾是我斧头帮的门徒啊！"

And just as he clenched his teeth and fixed his resolve on the killing, a familiar voice sounded at his ear: "Dai Chunfeng — go to Guangdong if you like, or find another road and rise as high as you please. Only never forget, whatever comes, that you were once a disciple of my Hatchet Gang!"

> "放心吧，大哥，我戴春风即便有一天做了官，也不会忘了你对我的救命之恩。"

"Rest easy, elder brother. Even if the day comes when I, Dai Chunfeng, hold office, I will never forget that I owe you my life."

> "我王某人施恩不图报。我只要你不要忘本。你可是受苦人出身呀！"

"I do my kindnesses and look for no return. All I ask is that you not forget your roots. You come of poor suffering stock, boy!"

> "放心吧，我会记着大哥的话。"

"Rest easy. I will keep elder brother's words."

> 戴笠想到这里，拿着勃朗宁手枪的手忽然情不自禁地哆嗦一下。他的前额上沁出了冷汗……

At the memory, the hand holding the Browning shuddered of its own accord. Cold sweat stood out on his forehead…

> 子夜，南京万籁俱寂。

Midnight, and Nanjing lay in utter silence.

> 突然，几辆坐满便衣特务的警车，冲出鸡鹅巷45号那神秘的院落，在漆黑的夜里驰向无人的大街，直向洪武街方向疾驰而去。顿时，雪亮的车灯划破了子夜的黑暗，警车发出的刺耳啸叫，震醒了那些早已入睡的市民。在第一辆警车里，坐着以戴笠为首的"十人团"，他们个个手握短枪，满脸杀气。

Then, all at once, several police cars packed with plainclothes agents burst from the mysterious courtyard at No. 45 Ji'e Alley, tore through the black night down empty avenues, and made straight for Hongwu Street. Blinding headlamps slit the midnight dark; the scream of the cars startled sleeping citizens from their beds. In the lead car sat the Ten-Man Team, Dai Li at their head, every man with a pistol in his fist and murder on his face.

> 戴笠却神不守舍，他脑际里显现出另一幅画面：那是1916年夏天，在上海的十里洋场，有一个到处乞讨的小瘪三，他的名字叫戴春风。那天下着小雨，正在沿街讨饭的戴春风，忽然被迎面飞驰而来的小轿车当头一撞，立刻扑倒在马路上。当时，戴春风已经几天没吃一顿饱饭了，面色枯黄的他被撞倒在地，立刻满面鲜血，昏了过去。

But Dai Li's mind was elsewhere. Another picture had risen before him: the summer of 1916, in the glitter of Shanghai's ten-li foreign strip, a ragged little beggar who went by the name of Dai Chunfeng. It was drizzling that day, and as he begged his way along the street a sedan flying toward him struck him head-on and laid him out on the roadway. Dai Chunfeng had not eaten a full meal in days; sallow and starved, knocked to the ground, his face all at once a sheet of blood, he lost his senses where he lay.

> 就在这时，从马路另一侧过来一辆人力洋车，车上坐着位穿马褂长袍的绅士。他就是当时在上海滩已小有名气的安徽人王亚樵。当他发现一个乞丐被车撞昏，许多行人围在那里唏嘘不禁的时候，马上跳下车来，向另一辆洋车招了招手，说："来呀，帮帮忙，大家都是贫苦人出身，怎么能看着一个后生这样丧命呢？"

At just that moment a rickshaw came up the other side of the road, and in it sat a gentleman in a long gown and riding jacket: the Anhui man Wang Yaqiao, already a name of some weight in Shanghai. Seeing a beggar knocked senseless and a crowd standing round him clucking its pity and doing nothing, he jumped down at once and waved up a second rickshaw. "Come on — bear a hand! Every one of us was born poor. Are we going to stand and watch a young man throw his life away like this?"

> 那车夫得了王亚樵的钞票，马上将受了重伤的戴春风抱上洋车，火速送进了一家教会医院。戴春风在那家医院里整整昏睡两天两夜才被救醒。这个叫戴春风的青年，就是如今已成为蒋介石"十人团"首领的戴笠！

The rickshaw man took Wang Yaqiao's banknotes and rushed the badly injured Dai Chunfeng to a mission hospital, where he lay unconscious two days and two nights before he came back to the world. And that young man, Dai Chunfeng, is the same Dai Li who now commanded Chiang Kai-shek's Ten-Man Team.

> 戴笠在事后多年始终没有淡忘那场可怕的车祸。他记得清醒过来以后，曾问过身边的医生："是何人救了我的性命？"医生告诉戴春风说："你的命大，如果当时不是遇上安徽的王九爷，也许你这小命早就没了。是王九爷大发善心，为你雇来的车，送到医院后又是九爷出了药费，你小子可要记着，王九爷可是你的救命恩人啊！"

In all the years since, Dai Li had never let go the memory of that terrible crash. He remembered asking a doctor at his bedside, once his head had cleared: "Who saved my life?" And the doctor told him: "There's a big life in you, boy. If Ninth Master Wang of Anhui hadn't happened along, that little life of yours would likely be gone. It was Ninth Master Wang whose heart was moved — he hired the car himself, and once you were here it was the Ninth Master again who paid for your medicine. So remember it: Ninth Master Wang is the benefactor who saved your life!"

> "王九爷？"那时的戴春风尚不知道恩人是谁。

"Ninth Master Wang?" Dai Chunfeng did not yet know who his benefactor was.

> 医生说："怎么，你在上海滩上混，居然连王九爷也不知道？他就是斧头帮的首领王亚樵啊！"

The doctor said: "What — you knock about the Shanghai waterfront and you don't know the Ninth Master? He is the chief of the Hatchet Gang: Wang Yaqiao!"

> "王亚樵？！"戴春风大吃一惊，他虽然只是上海滩上一个无名之辈，以沿街乞讨为生，但是，他却早就对王亚樵的大名多有耳闻。如今听说临危相救的人，居然就是那人见人怕的斧头帮帮主，戴春风那双绝望的眼睛忽然亮了。他心中狂喜，口中喃喃地说道："天助我也！我要找王九爷去！……"

"Wang Yaqiao?!" Dai Chunfeng started violently. He was nobody in Shanghai, a nameless beggar living on the streets' leavings — and even he had long known the great name of Wang Yaqiao. Now, told that the man who had pulled him back from the brink was the master of the Hatchet Gang himself, feared by everyone who so much as saw him, the despair went out of Dai Chunfeng's eyes and they came alight. Joy ran wild in him, and he murmured aloud: "Heaven helps me after all! I will go to Ninth Master Wang!…"

> 从那天起，戴春风就投靠到王亚樵的门下，先是千恩万谢王亚樵的救命之恩，后来，他又心甘情愿在王亚樵的麾下当个小门徒。然而，世事无常，事隔几年以后，戴笠做梦也没有想到他竟会受命于蒋介石，前去洪武街逮捕敢在大会上攻击蒋氏的安徽代表王亚樵。

From that day Dai Chunfeng attached himself to Wang Yaqiao's gate — first with a thousand thanks for the saving of his life, then, of his own glad will, as a small disciple under Wang Yaqiao's wing. But the world turns without warning. A few years on, Dai Li could never have dreamed that he would one day ride under Chiang Kai-shek's orders to Hongwu Street, to arrest the Anhui delegate who had dared attack Chiang before the assembled government: Wang Yaqiao.

> "到了，前面就是洪武街51号，王亚樵和他的保镖就住在这里！"当戴笠坐在警车里回想往事的时候，身边"十人团"的成员贺衷寒，忽然拍拍他的肩膀，提醒地向漆黑小街深处一指，示意马上停车。

"Here — up ahead, No. 51 Hongwu Street. Wang Yaqiao and his bodyguards lodge right there!" While Dai Li sat in the police car lost in the past, He Zhongyan of the Ten-Man Team clapped him on the shoulder, pointing in warning into the depths of the pitch-black little street: stop the cars, now.

> 可是，戴笠却不加理睬。那警车仍然鸣叫着冲进洪武街。

Dai Li paid no attention. The car ran on, siren crying, straight into Hongwu Street.

> 贺衷寒再次提醒他说："雨农，如果继续向前冲去，必会要惊动里面的人。到那时王亚樵的保镖可不好惹！"

He Zhongyan warned him again: "Yunong, drive on like this and you'll rouse the whole house. And once they're roused, Wang Yaqiao's bodyguards are nothing to trifle with!"

> 戴笠无奈，只好下令停车。就在这时候，戴笠忽然发现洪武街51号门前有一个守门的保镖。正愁无法摆脱困境的戴笠，灵机一动，他不等身边"十人团"成员有所行动，就抢先跳下车来。戴笠向前冲了几步，忽然拔出腰间的勃朗宁手枪，对准那个守门的保镖，猝不及防地开了一枪。枪声砰然，划破了子夜的岑寂。那守门的保镖被戴笠打倒在地后，洪武街51号院内响起一片慌乱的人声。不久，爆豆般的枪声骤然而起。那些被冷枪突然惊醒的保镖们，一齐冲上大街，向包抄而来的"十人团"疯狂射击。戴笠等人也拼命还击。霎时洪武街上刮起一阵枪林弹雨。

There was nothing for it; Dai Li ordered the cars stopped. And just then he caught sight of a lone bodyguard keeping the gate of No. 51. Cornered, casting about for a way out of the fix, Dai Li had his flash of cunning. Before any man of the Ten-Man Team could move, he was first out of the car. He ran a few steps forward, whipped the Browning from his waist, leveled it at the watching bodyguard and fired without warning. The report cracked the midnight quiet. The gatekeeper went down — and from inside the courtyard of No. 51 rose a clamor of panicked voices, then a sudden rattle of gunfire like beans bursting in a hot pan. The bodyguards, startled from sleep by the shot, poured into the street together and opened furious fire on the encircling Ten-Man Team. Dai Li's men returned it for their lives. In an instant Hongwu Street was a storm of lead.

> 王亚樵就在这枪声的掩护下从后墙翻越而逃了。

And under the cover of that storm, Wang Yaqiao went over the back wall and was gone.

> 次日，南京各报纷纷刊载《国民政府通缉令》，在江南各地搜捕在逃的斧头帮首领王亚樵。正由于蒋介石下令通缉，从前并不引人注目的安徽人王亚樵，一夜之间竟成了民国时期最有影响的焦点人物。可是，王亚樵究竟如何起家？又为什么会引起蒋介石的恐慌与震怒？这一切都要从他暗杀安徽大员陈调元说起。

Next day the Nanjing papers carried the National Government's wanted order, and the hunt for the fugitive chief of the Hatchet Gang ran through every town in Jiangnan. It was Chiang Kai-shek's own warrant that did it: overnight, an Anhui man few had troubled to notice became one of the most talked-of figures of the Republic. But how did Wang Yaqiao rise in the first place? And what had he done, that Chiang Kai-shek should panic and rage at the thought of him? All of it begins with the plot to kill a grandee of Anhui: the assassination of Chen Diaoyuan.
