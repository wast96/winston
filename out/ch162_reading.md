### Counterfeit Banknotes

Among the banknotes the various banks put out, forged notes have again and again come to light. Let some woman or child or ordinary body be for a moment off guard and take one of these counterfeits, and not only is a sum lost out of nowhere; at times an innocent person is dragged into trouble besides—which may truly be called a wrong that cries to heaven!

The criminals who manufacture forged notes think only of getting rich by a windfall, and care nothing that they injure the multitude and throw the money markets into disorder—a crime beyond the reach of death to expiate. Yet of the counterfeiting rings cracked in case after case, nine in ten have had a Japanese ronin playing a leading part within—and is this, forsooth, the way of good-neighbourliness and benevolence?

A friend told me yesterday that last year in Beiping too forged notes came to light; only there they did not copy them by machine, but reproduced them by a chemical process. They would take a genuine note, coat its face with a solution, then lay over it a sheet of plain paper of the very same size and thickness as the real note and press it down hard, whereupon the pattern and colouring of the genuine note came out complete, without a hair's difference. Later, when too many had been passed, several forged notes bearing the same serial number turned up, and only then was the whole dark business exposed, the case cracked and the culprits punished—but by then the harm to the public was no small matter.

And the subsidiary-currency notes put out by the central banks (that is, the jiao notes) are counterfeited too; of these forgers it may be said that there is no crack they will not creep into, and nothing they will not fake.
