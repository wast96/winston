### Selling Ice

Whenever the fierce heat of high summer comes on, one often sees in the streets and lanes the ice-selling boys, a rush-bag in hand packed with blocks of ice, crying as they go, "Ice, who'll buy? Buy your ice...!" The two words "buy ice" are cried so fast and so urgently that they run together into a sound like bu-fei-qie. And your self-appointed moralist, hearing the shout, if he does not stop his ears and hurry off, will at the very least gnash his teeth in disgust, protesting that a thing like this has no business being hawked up and down the road.
