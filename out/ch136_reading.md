### Hat-Snatching

A man will put on a felt hat or a straw one and sit in a tram-car with the window open, and whenever the car slows in its running the hat on his head is very often snatched away; by the time he notices, the car has already carried him a stage further on, and there is nothing for it but to sigh. Those in the know call this "tossing the top-palace." And so an old hand, once he is inside the car, will first take his hat off and hold it in his hand, and then they have no way to work their thieving trick, and you may count yourself safe and sound.
