## Note on the Reissue

The author of *The Autobiography of Huang Mulan* was one of our Party's early workers on the underground front. Her path crossed many of the important events and figures in the history of the Chinese Communist Party; and though she passed through great adversity, she bore it without complaint or regret, making an outstanding contribution to safeguarding and advancing the Party's cause.

Before this publishing house first brought out the autobiography in 2004, we did a great deal of work to verify the historical record. On a number of the more important events in particular, we arrived at conclusive findings with the help of the Central Archives and of institutions for the study of Party history.

After the book appeared, its small print run made it hard for readers around the country to obtain. In recent years, as the meritorious figures of the Party's hidden front have become more and more widely known, more and more readers have asked when *The Autobiography of Huang Mulan* would be reissued. To meet this broad demand, our house has decided to issue a new edition, and by it to commemorate the ninetieth anniversary of the founding of the Chinese Communist Party.

In preparing this new edition we have fully respected the principle of revision the author herself proposed—that it should present "a true life"—and have done only standardizing editorial work, in no case altering her original meaning. After checking the sources and consulting the author and her family, we have corrected a number of errors and omissions in the original manuscript in personal and place names and even in events. The layout of the new edition has also been adjusted: besides adding some historical photographs and a recent portrait of the author, we have set part of the illustrations from the old edition into the body of the text, so that readers may read text and pictures together.
