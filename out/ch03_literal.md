## 第三章 Chapter Three: The Execution of Zhao Tieqiao

### 8. The traitor shows his loathsome face

"What did you say? The traitor is Zhao Tieqiao?" When Wang Yaqiao, at his secret lodging in Hefei, received the telephone call Wu Hongtai made from Shanghai, he could not help eating a start.

"Yes, yes — precisely Zhao Tieqiao's doing!" Wu Hongtai's tone was quite certain.

"You could not have gotten it wrong? How could he sell Wang Leping?" Wang Yaqiao, though he had no deep acquaintance with Zhao Tieqiao, had indeed met this person once in Shanghai not long before. That time, it was Wang Leping in person who introduced this great scholar in western suit and leather shoes, newly returned from study in America, into his residence. Speaking from a level heart, the refined-mannered, belly-full-of-learning Zhao Tieqiao left Wang Yaqiao an impression not at all bad. Wang Yaqiao, whose whole life ran to hacking and killing, out of who knows what psychology genuinely liked from the heart those knowledge-bearing intellectuals; especially toward a high-talent graduate like Zhao Tieqiao, out of the finance and economics department of the University of California in America, he was from the very start full of respect. He even felt some envy that Wang Leping had found a man like Zhao Tieqiao for a secretary. But now the Wu Hongtai in Shanghai had locked his gaze on the Doctor Zhao Tieqiao whom Wang Leping in life had deeply trusted — and this, whichever way turned, he found hard to accept.

"Ninth Master, it was this boy who did it — absolutely no mistake! If there is a mistake, I will stand guarantee with my head!" Wu Hongtai on the telephone bit down with one mouth [一口咬定], and this left the half-believing half-doubting Wang Yaqiao holding his chess piece undecided [举棋不定]. He could neither, on Wu Hongtai's one-sided word alone, lightly believe Zhao Tieqiao an inner traitor; nor could he deny Wu's judgment. Wang Yaqiao, for caution's sake, ordered Xuan Jimin, who had gone to Shanghai with Wu Hongtai, to hurry back to Hefei that same night. He wanted to verify the truth or falsity of this thing from Xuan Jimin face to face. For in Wang Yaqiao's eyes, the man most worth trusting was still the Xuan Jimin who had killed Zhang Qiubai for him at Nanjing.

"Ninth Master, you should not hold extra doubt toward Wu Hongtai; his report has roots and grounds. Even I cannot but admire that Wu Hongtai truly owns a set of fire-eyes and golden pupils [火眼金睛] for picking out traitors. He is, after all, the scholar-killer of our Hatchet Gang!" Xuan Jimin, returned to Wang Yaqiao's secret lodging in Hefei, reported the course of his and Wu Hongtai's investigation in Shanghai. It had been thus: not long after they reached Shanghai, they heard that Dai Li too had come from Nanjing to Shanghai, and in Shanghai's Great World was holding a cocktail party of an open-new-face kind [别开生面]. At the time, Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai were just seeking, through relevant channels, to make clear the inside curtain of Wang Leping's assassination; hearing of this affair, they went together to attend this cocktail party.

When they arrived at the Great World and looked, they learned that this grand banquet of Dai Li's was actually held specially for a person called Zhao Tieqiao. Those hurrying to join the bustle were not only the head-face figures of Shanghai's military and government circles but many great magnates of commerce besides. There were also some flower-branch-waving film stars, Peking opera performers and young misses of tender years. This hot banquet scene rather made Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin feel surprise. They discovered that entering at the same time as Dai Li was a young man tall of figure and elegant of bearing. Around them clustered some gorgeous young women.

"What — you two gentlemen do not know even Zhao Tieqiao? He is the one figure in today's Shanghai riding the highest luck!" Seated beside Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin was a gorgeous society flower [交际花]. She had long been casting envious glances from her seat at the Zhao Tieqiao entering arm in arm with Dai Li; seeing Wu Hongtai quietly ask who the newcomer was, she at once introduced him as one counting family treasures [如数家珍]: "Him? He is a real-power figure now — took the financial power out of that Li Guojie's hands without so much as a courtesy. Look — how much ill-luck sits on Li Guojie's face now!"

Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai were both drawn over to Zhao Tieqiao. They discovered this young man was indeed very full of spirit; between the lifting of a hand and the setting of a foot showed an arrogance different from the crowd. He and Dai Li from time to time quietly said some things, then walked together into the chief-guest seats heaped with fresh flowers; the four seats at once rang with applause and shouted approval: "Boss Dai, today we must not stop short of one full drunkenness!" "Mr. Dai, they say tonight's banquet was laid by your own hand — this scene is too much to be envied!" "And is there a dance after the banquet?"

Dai Li rose proudly to his feet, saluted the four seats with courtesy and polish, and toward those crowds of women like national-color-and-heavenly-fragrance [国色天香] cast a smile in particular, saying: "My thanks to you all. Only — tonight's banquet and dance, I am not the host of them. The true host of the banquet is here: he is the newly appointed supervisor of the Shanghai China Merchants company, Mr. Zhao Tieqiao!"

"Aiya — so it is Supervisor Zhao standing the feast!" "Look at Supervisor Zhao — what free elegance. One look tells you he is a figure who does great things." "Just so. They say he once worked under Wang Leping; now he has toppled Wang Leping, gained fortune out of calamity, and gotten the fattest post on the Shanghai waterfront into his hand." "True — they say it was by Chairman Chiang's gracious approval that Zhao Tieqiao mounted the first chair of the China Merchants company." From the start, Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai had felt that Dai Li's personal attendance at a banquet held by a commercial figure was in itself a thing to draw the eye. Now, hearing besides these womenfolk — who held the inside curtain of Zhao Tieqiao's rise like counted treasure — puffing him seven mouths and eight tongues [七嘴八舌], they felt the more that Zhao Tieqiao's origins were not of the ordinary kind.

"This Zhao once worked under Wang Leping?" Wu Hongtai, playing the out-of-town guest, purposely asked the women guests beside him about Zhao Tieqiao's circumstances.

A young married woman fond of showing off said: "Naturally. Zhao Tieqiao was a student newly back from abroad, but he discovered that working at Wang Leping's side he would hardly rise in a whole lifetime — so he decided to change the door of his allegiance [改换门庭]."

Another woman also said: "Now, they say, once Zhao Tieqiao mounted the stage he gathered all power alone, and turned the China Merchants board chairman Li Guojie into a lone-family solitary man [孤家寡人]. Look — the Li one has started walking the road to Maicheng!"

Xuan Jimin, following the direction the woman pointed, looked forward through the stirring heads, and indeed saw, beside Dai Li and the nose-high-spirit-flying Zhao Tieqiao, a merchant of pale face sitting dull and pent. He lowered his brows and sighed, only smoked there in hundred-fold boredom, not daring to lift his head and hold gaze against the pressing-spirited Zhao Tieqiao. Just as Li Guojie sat in ten-thousand-fold awkwardness, as if on a needle-felt, Zhao Tieqiao rose to toast, lifting his cup and declaiming with rising and falling cadence: "Mr. Dai was just now too modest. In truth, tonight's banquet and dance were both prepared for Mr. Dai. Why such lavishness? Because Mr. Dai is my benefactor of recognition. Had he not led the road for me, I could forever only pace back and forth on a pitch-dark small path. Only now do I deeply feel: if a man wishes to rise and make a name, the thing most needful is a road-pointing man — and Mr. Dai is the best good-teacher-and-helpful-friend of my life. I am proud to know Mr. Dai! Come, friends all, ladies, gentlemen — let us lift cups together and drink deep, and not stop short of drunkenness! Let every seat, with me, drink three full cups for Mr. Dai's coming in person to Shanghai to attend my accession banquet!"

The four seats responded one after another. Just as Xuan Jimin was quietly listening to Zhao Tieqiao's toast, he found Wu Hongtai beside him quietly pulling his lapel. They quietly left the banquet hall and came to the terrace outside, where the whole city of Shanghai's splendid lamps burned brilliant, and the distant Huangpu River under the pitch-black night curtain flickered with shadowy patches of light.

"Brother Jimin — truly, iron shoes worn through in searching, and the thing comes without the spending of effort." Wu Hongtai said in excitement: "The man the Ninth Master set us to seek — is he not before our eyes?"

Xuan Jimin froze a beat: "You mean the traitor who informed to Chiang Kai-shek is the host of tonight's banquet?"

Wu Hongtai nodded, deeply holding it so: "Not one point off! As I see it, Mr. Wang Leping's assassination was certainly this Zhao's mischief in the dark. Perhaps you do not know: when Zhao Tieqiao came back from abroad, it was exactly when he could nowhere find work to do, and Wang Leping gave him a secretary's position. But now Wang Leping has died by assassination, and this Zhao Tieqiao not only shows not half a point of grief — he has suddenly risen to name and fortune, and mounted to be some supervisor of the China Merchants company! Have you thought: this China Merchants supervisorship is a fat vacancy big as heaven. In those years the men who competed with Li Guojie for this fat post were many indeed — how could it fall to a small small Zhao Tieqiao with no root in government circles?"

Xuan Jimin, with much the same feeling, said: "True. If Zhao Tieqiao had not laid down some great merit, Chiang Kai-shek would absolutely never give him the key post of supervisor of the Shanghai China Merchants company. But he, a young man newly back from study abroad, without political root or base — what merit could he lay down before Chiang Kai-shek? This is simply not to be imagined!"

"Zhao Tieqiao's rise plainly has to do with Dai Li." Wu Hongtai had already, in the banquet hall, seen Dai Li's and Zhao Tieqiao's intimate contact one-clear-two-plain. He analyzed: "The thing before our eyes cannot but be linked with Dai Li's earlier going to Hefei to call on the Ninth Master. In former days when Dai Li was in our Hatchet Gang, was he ever so full of spirit as now? Is it not all from his going over to Chiang Kai-shek? So I think: could Zhao Tieqiao's promotion and rise have to do with Wang Leping's murder and General Fang Zhenwu's detention at Nanjing?"

Xuan Jimin greatly ate a start: "You are saying — the man who informed Chiang Kai-shek of the Ninth Master's and Bo Wenwei's secret topple-Chiang plan is Zhao Tieqiao?"

Wu Hongtai mused: "As I see it, only he could fill the traitor's role." But Xuan Jimin did not hold it so: "Yet the Ninth Master's and Bo Wenwei's and Fang Zhenwu's secret turning against Chiang — Zhao Tieqiao could not possibly know of it. He has never so much as touched the Ninth Master's edge; how could he inform to Chiang?"

Wu Hongtai, bamboo formed in the chest, said: "Brother Jimin, how have you forgotten — Wang Leping was the elder brother with whom the Ninth Master had the eight-bows friendship; moreover, before his death Wang Leping bore the liaison duty toward Shi Yousan and Fang Zhenwu. Since Zhao Tieqiao was the secretary at Wang Leping's side, that he learned the secrets against Chiang is not impossible."

"Right — right!" Xuan Jimin, hearing Wu Hongtai's head-to-head orderly analysis, sighed as one suddenly clear: "Your analysis is entirely correct. It is precisely this Zhao who in the dark sold friend to buy glory, and caused Wang Leping to die cruelly under the enemy's dark gun. Now he has killed his master, obtained besides the fine errand Chiang Kai-shek bestowed on him, and therefore feasts his benefactor Dai Yunong. It appears that before Dai Li came to Hefei to see the Ninth Master, he had already obtained from Zhao Tieqiao the entire plan of our Hatchet Gang's action! Seen now — Zhao Tieqiao, what fine venom!"

Wang Yaqiao, hearing Xuan Jimin's report, was still half-believing half-doubting. He said: "Whether Zhao Tieqiao truly is the spy who murdered Wang Leping — you and Wu Hongtai have no more than judgment; at present there is no exact evidence whatever. To avenge Wang Leping, these wind-watching shadow-catching inferences alone will not serve. What I want now is firm proof that Zhao Tieqiao and Dai Li hooked together and sold our side's intelligence. If he truly is the inner traitor who killed Wang Leping — hm — then he, the Zhao one, has been happy nearly to the end!"

Xuan Jimin next day went again to Shanghai, to continue with Wu Hongtai and the rest seeking exact evidence of Zhao Tieqiao's going over to Chiang. By this time Wang Yaqiao in Hefei had also nursed his illness whole. By mid-June, exactly the hottest season on the Shanghai waterfront, Wang Yaqiao, accompanied by his lady Yaying and Wang Ganting, Niu Anru and others, on a pitch-black rainy night, riding a small boat, spirits-unknowing ghosts-unaware, along the wave-rolling Yangtze made straight for Shanghai.

After Wang Yaqiao reached Shanghai he lived deep and went out simply, darkly watching the wind and clouds. He lodged as of old in the old courtyard of the Anhui Guildhall where the Hatchet Gang had first made its family. He was resolved to finish Zhao Tieqiao's life here, to repay in vengeance and wash the hate for the cherished friend Wang Leping who died cruelly under Dai Li's gun-mouth.

"Ninth Master — now we have seized the true proof and real evidence of Zhao Tieqiao's traffic with the enemy!" On a night dripping with fine drizzle, Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin came quietly to Wang Yaqiao's bedchamber. Wang Yaying, knowing the two must come for a great matter, hastily dismissed the attendants; then she let Wang Yaqiao and the two, Wu and Xuan, speak secretly in the inner room, while she herself stood guard outside the door. In the inner room, one lamp like a bean, Wu Hongtai reported to Wang Yaqiao: "Li Guojie told me with his own mouth: Zhao Tieqiao was pulled into the water by Dai Li. It was because he sold his benefactor Wang Leping that he obtained the present scorch-hand-hot China Merchants supervisorship."

"Hongtai, no hurry — tell in detail the origins of Zhao Tieqiao's selling his master to buy glory." Wang Yaqiao, seeing the vengeance-and-hate-washing plan hoped for through many days at last show brow and eye [有了眉目], felt the hot blood of revenge come surging. Now, the ten thousand things stood ready — only the east wind lacking [万事俱备，只欠东风]; he wanted Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin to report to him according to fact the results of two months' investigation of Zhao Tieqiao.

It had been thus: after Xuan Jimin went again to Shanghai, they daily sent out several Hatchet Gang members got up as shoe-menders and rickshaw men, secretly trailing Zhao Tieqiao, so as to seize true proof and real evidence. To get evidence of Zhao Tieqiao's hooking with Dai Li to sell Wang Leping, Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin contrived to draw near the China Merchants staff. But ordinary clerks could hardly grasp Zhao Tieqiao's true circumstances. Just then they discovered that Zhao Tieqiao, since taking office, having Dai Li and Chiang Kai-shek as rear-stage mountain to lean on [后台靠山], set the board chairman Li Guojie at nothing in his eyes. Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin felt the opportunity had come, for Li Guojie too was an Anhui man; so Wu Hongtai, under the name of standing host to wine, approached Li once and once again. At first Li Guojie would not bare his heart's traces to Wu Hongtai, only from time to time let off grievance-air while drinking. One day, Wu Hongtai invited Li to the Disiya restaurant to drink, and poured cups into him with all his strength; and Li Guojie, fire banked in his heart, sure enough after wine spat true words, cursing Zhao Tieqiao greatly: "His mother's — a dog borrowing man's power. If I did not look at his being the red man in Chiang Kai-shek's eye, I would buy a strongman and chop that dog head of his down. Otherwise, this China Merchants company will one day be his private household property!"

Wu Hongtai took the opening: "One small small Zhao Tieqiao — how could he hang on a hook to Chiang Kai-shek?"

Li Guojie said: "Originally Chiang Kai-shek did not know at all who Zhao Tieqiao was. Later, by who knows what channel, he came to know Dai Li. It was he who got into his hand the news heard from Wang Leping's side — the secret of the Hatchet Gang uniting with Fang Zhenwu, Peng Jianguo and Shi Yousan for a mutiny. At that time Dai Li happened to be in Shanghai collecting intelligence about Wang Yaqiao, so Zhao Tieqiao poked to Dai Li all the secrets he had listened out from Wang Leping's side. After Wang Yaqiao's mutiny plan was wrecked, Chiang Kai-shek, secretly congratulating himself on slipping past a death, therefore ordered Zhao Tieqiao commended. Naturally Zhao Tieqiao's intent was never merely to remove one Wang Yaqiao and one Fang Zhenwu; he wanted to scoop real power in Shanghai. So he further reported in the dark to Dai Li the time and place of Wang Leping's joining the rising and coming to Shanghai, and so caused his grace-master Wang Leping to meet assassination. His mother's — this sell-master-buy-glory creature now comes to seize my rice bowl too. Tell me: if in future his relation with Chiang Kai-shek deepens further, will he stop at seizing my money and my position? Who knows when — he may use Dai Li and Chiang Kai-shek to chop down my head as well!"

"Is there such a thing?" Wu Hongtai played the astonished man, sighing over his wine.

"Thousand-true ten-thousand-certain!" Li Guojie struck his chest once. "My old eyes cannot be rubbed with sand either. The day he truly dares put hands on me, I will not stand on courtesy!"

Wang Yaqiao, having heard Wu Hongtai's and Xuan Jimin's reports, now believed without doubt that Zhao Tieqiao filled the spy's and traitor's part. So that same night, in the Shanghai Anhui Guildhall, he convened a secret meeting, to discuss with tight weave how to remove Zhao Tieqiao in Shanghai. But their meeting had only just begun when someone burst in from outside the door with urgent report: "Ninth Master — Li Guojie has telephoned; he asks whether Wu Hongtai is here."

Wang Yaqiao, feeling it rather unexpected, looked at Wu Hongtai. The other chief Hatchet Gang members at the meeting — Xuan Jimin, Wang Ganting, Niu Anru, Liu Decai and the rest — all threw doubting glances at Wu Hongtai. Wu Hongtai too did not know why Li Guojie should suddenly ring the telephone into the Anhui Guildhall.

Wang Yaqiao nodded to Wu Hongtai: "Take his call; see what new intelligence this Li Guojie has."

### 9. A steamship exchanged for a human head

Wu Hongtai went downstairs, finished the call, and hurried back with a tense face, saying urgently to Wang Yaqiao: "Ninth Master — truly I do not know what ghost-trick Li Guojie is playing. He telephoned me and actually said he wishes to invite you, Ninth Master Wang, to a banquet."

"Li Guojie invites me to dinner?" Wang Yaqiao at the words was darkly taken aback. Though he had known this direct grandson of Li Hongzhang as early as his Hefei days, for many years the two had had very little coming and going. For a man of Li Guojie's wealth and power to stand host was, to Wang Yaqiao, truly unexpected. Thinking of the assassinate-Zhao plan they were even now discussing, Wang Yaqiao felt the more doubt: "What does this Li one mean? Can it be that the kill-Zhao plan we are setting in motion has leaked wind again?"

Wu Hongtai, seeing Wang Yaqiao's suspicion rise again, laughed heh-heh: "The Ninth Master over-worries. You mean Li Guojie would plead for the Zhao Tieqiao we hate? That he would absolutely never do. As I know it, though Li Guojie and Zhao Tieqiao both now hold posts in the China Merchants company, the two are already enemy-foes head to head. Li Guojie simply hates Zhao Tieqiao to death; how could he come plead for him?"

"Right, right." Xuan Jimin, seeing Wang Yaqiao sit there drawing zhi-zhi on the water-pipe, half a stretch silent and wordless, knew he had thought again of the lesson of the last failed toppling — had even grown fox-doubt toward the few most-sincere brothers at his side — and explained in Wu Hongtai's stead: "What Hongtai says has much reason. In former days the great power of the China Merchants company was always gathered in Li Guojie's one hand. But since Zhao Tieqiao took office, his situation is greatly otherwise. Zhao Tieqiao sets Li Guojie at nothing in his eyes, grabs power without restraint, and has long since angered Li Guojie. So I dare guarantee: he absolutely does not come to plead for Zhao Tieqiao."

"Then why does he ask me to drink?" Wang Yaqiao found it beyond imagining.

Wang Ganting said: "Ninth Master, the two elder brothers Xuan and Wu have scouted many days in Shanghai; their words cannot go unbelieved." Liu Decai and Niu Anru also felt that Li Guojie's inviting Wang Yaqiao to dinner at this time, though somewhat against the ordinary, still could absolutely not be a pleading for Zhao Tieqiao. As for why Li Guojie set the banquet — that riddle's bottom none could undo.

"Ninth Master — since there is wine to drink, I say we go drink it?" The Wu Hongtai who had sat thinking without words at last opened his mouth. "I think contact with Li Guojie may even help our strike-Zhao action. For in the matter of opposing Zhao Tieqiao, Li Guojie and we are at the least unplanned-yet-agreed."

Wang Yaqiao said: "You mean Li Guojie wants to borrow our hand to kill his enemy?"

Wu Hongtai looked at Xuan Jimin; both nodded, deeply holding it so: "Very possible." All were upstairs discussing why Li Guojie should suddenly feast Wang Yaqiao when the gate-keeping brother came up again, bearing on his hands a great red card — it was the invitation Li Guojie had sent a man to deliver. Wang Yaqiao took it and looked; on it, in small regular brush characters, was written:

To the respected: It is set for tomorrow at high noon, in the private room of the Great Fortune restaurant, a meager cup made ready; at the hour we reverently invite the honored elder brother Jiuguang to grace us with his presence. — Li Guojie, Anhui countryman

Wang Yaqiao read Li Guojie's card over and again, then dropped his gaze onto Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin, saying: "His hatred with Zhao Tieqiao — can it truly have walked to the step of buying killers for a man's life?"

Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai both said: "His hate is no smaller than ours — for Li Guojie's money now all flows into Zhao Tieqiao's pocket!"

"Good then." Wang Yaqiao had taken his decision; suddenly he clapped his hand on the table. "If Li Guojie truly wishes to use the hand of me, Wang Jiuguang, then it strikes squarely into the hollow of my wish [正中下怀]. Since he wants to use our Hatchet Gang, let him bleed a little for it!"

The company, seeing Wang Yaqiao spit this sentence, all gave out knowing laughter.

The next day at the noon hour, Wang Yaqiao, wearing white silk-crepe trousers and jacket, under the cluster-escort of Wu Hongtai, Xuan Jimin, Liu Decai and several other Hatchet Gang brothers, came from the Anhui Guildhall straight to the Great Fortune restaurant on Nanjing Road. Before the second-floor private room there already waited in welcome a smiling gentleman, in gray silk long gown, swaying a paper fan in his hand, showing modest courtesy; seeing Wang Yaqiao come with several killers, he hurried smiling to receive them with full rite: "Please — Ninth Master, quickly, please. The Anhui men making their way on Shanghai ground nowadays all borrow the Ninth Master's light!"

Wang Yaqiao did not return the courtesy, only led Wu Hongtai, Xuan Jimin, Niu Anru, Liu Decai and the rest into the private room, wide and bright within. Before long the serving girls entered fish-file, bearing small plate on plate of fine Suzhou dishes. Wang Yaqiao saw they were all top-grade delicacies: chestnut steamed smothered chicken, vinegar-flavor smoked fish, shrimp-meat lanhu mash and duck in its own broth, and such. One pot of nü'erhong old wine. Once opened, the private room at once suffused with a fragrance that entered the very organs.

"Come, come, Ninth Master — drink this cup full, to comfort a whole life!" Li Guojie dismissed the serving girls and gravely offered a cup. "Guojie has long harbored the intent of setting wine in your entertainment, only never had the luck of a meeting. Today, hard-won, the Ninth Master grants his light — then let me lift the cup in respect?"

But against expectation Wang Yaqiao blocked the cup with his hand: "Worthy brother Guojie — at the wine table I am a man of three refusals. First: the wine of an enemy I do not drink. Second: the wine of a benefactor I do not drink. Third: wine whose bottom I have not seen clear, that too I cannot drink. Today, since the worthy brother lays wine, it is either a debt repaid or a thing sought. I must first understand what matter you seek of me; then I may drain this cup."

Li Guojie pressed wine three times over; Wang Yaqiao only firmly declined and would not drink. Li Guojie, seeing it, could only give one grieving sigh and show a sorrow-laden face: "The Ninth Master too knows I am of an official-gentry family, and that my coming to Shanghai for commerce was itself of necessity. For in Chiang Kai-shek's officialdom there has never been a place for our Li family line. Commerce, then — let it be; but Chiang Kai-shek still comes seeking my trouble. Now, in the China Merchants company, I simply cannot carry on!"

Wang Yaqiao exchanged a knowing eye-light with Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin: all felt it came as reckoned. But he said, playing perplexity: "Worthy brother Guojie, whence such words? You are the name-right board chairman of the China Merchants company, upright in the first chair. In the company your one word should stand for two; why suddenly these words — how can you not carry on?"

Li Guojie, face hung with mourning, said: "In former days I did indeed carry it with free elegance. But since Chiang Kai-shek and Dai Li sent Zhao Tieqiao here and made him company supervisor, my days have turned hard! His mother's — what thing does Zhao Tieqiao count for? On the surface this fellow is loyal-thick and honest, playing the scholar's manner. But keep company with him long and you will know the man's treachery and tyranny. Now, in the company, every road by which wealth might be grown he has walled dead against me. Ninth Master, you say — how am I to survive in the China Merchants company?"

Wang Yaqiao held Li Guojie's banquet-purpose clear in the chest already, and laughed darkly within. He had not expected that he and Li Guojie would prove unplanned-yet-agreed in the matter of assassinating Zhao Tieqiao; but he still looked left and right and spoke of other things, not moving onto the main thread, only waiting for Li Guojie to declare himself. Wang Yaqiao said: "What manner of man Zhao Tieqiao is, we are not acquainted. I simply do not believe that he, an outsider, dares set himself against us Anhui men? Besides — you, brother Guojie, holding money in your hand, why fear him, one pen-man scholar?"

Li Guojie at once pressed close: "Ninth Master, you say rightly. Without venom, no true man [无毒不丈夫]. Now, since he, Zhao Tieqiao, is without humanity, I, Li, will be without righteousness. So, at the end of ten thousand helplessnesses, thinking and thinking again, I suddenly thought of the one man who can save me from the sea of bitterness — and he is you, Ninth Master Wang!"

"I?! And how could I save you?" Wang Yaqiao purposely played riddles with Li Guojie. "In my hand is neither power nor force. One bare pauper — how could I shake a supervisor of the China Merchants company?"

Li Guojie, seeing Wang Yaqiao play the mystery, said: "Ninth Master, sell the pass no longer! Of all who make their way on the Shanghai waterfront, which one does not know the Ninth Master's honor in conduct? The Hatchet Gang under your hand are cold-faced killers every one, who stretch straight the upright air for those men of purpose bullied by tyrant-lords. Now I am bullied in Shanghai by Zhao Tieqiao — can you truly fold your hands in your sleeves and watch?"

"What — you would have me remove Zhao Tieqiao?" Wang Yaqiao, playing shock, widened his eyes, gazing at that small white face of Li Guojie's now seeping sweat.

Li Guojie said gravely: "Exactly that intent, Ninth Master. Now only you can save me. And set your heart at rest: the thing once done, I, Li, will absolutely not be without expression." So saying, he opened his leather case at hand, quietly drew from it a thick paper packet, and pressed it into Wang Yaqiao's pocket: "This is one small meaning. Eight thousand — first, for the luck of it. When the thing is done…"

"Very well. Since worthy brother Guojie suffers bullying in Shanghai, can I, Wang Jiuguang, sit watching and not save?" Wang Yaqiao counted the notes over, then handed them back to Li Guojie, saying: "But — the worthy brother sets a matter this great, and grudges all but this small change, enough for tea?"

"No, no, Ninth Master — this small money is indeed no respect at all. I meant it only for tea for you and the brothers." Li Guojie suddenly set his heart hard: "Then thus. The Ninth Master is a man who has seen the world's faces; how much money to give, I do not know. But — first, do the thing. Only let the Zhao one be finished, and at that hour I will give the Ninth Master one great glad surprise!"

Wang Yaqiao had Niu Anru take in the money, drank a mouthful of wine, and said: "Say on — what glad surprise, exactly?"

"Ninth Master, it is thus: the price-tag of such great business, of course I would not fail to know. But nowadays the banknotes of the China Merchants company have all entered his, Zhao Tieqiao's, hand; how could I at one stroke bring out so much money to honor the Ninth Master?" Li Guojie too was a shrewd figure of the commercial sea; he had seen that Wang Yaqiao had accepted the assassination of Zhao Tieqiao and only found the deposit too small, and so held back and would not strike the board. Li Guojie, to bring the thing to term, also to set Wang Yaqiao moving the sooner, therefore said: "Then thus: to repay the Ninth Master's grace, I, Li Guojie, am willing to part with a great ship. That freighter of our company's — the 'Jiang'an.' That, surely, should stand as a great gift?"

"The freighter Jiang'an?" Wang Yaqiao at first was darkly startled: he had not imagined Li Guojie, to kill one enemy Zhao Tieqiao, would willingly cast off a great sea-going ship as his repayment. In his heart he was glad: a ship, plainly, was worth more to him than several thousand more of Li Guojie's banknotes; if their Hatchet Gang one day owned a great ship, then the run from the Huangpu up to Hefei would gain a tool of transport fitted to the hand's desire. Only Wang Yaqiao grew one more fold of care, and said: "Worthy brother Guojie — in truth, trading a company ship for Zhao Tieqiao's head is worth it to you, for hereafter you may loose your hand and rake money at will. But — know that this 'Jiang'an' is, after all, public property. You give it to me — will it hereafter breed right-and-wrong trouble?"

"It will not. When has Li Guojie ever spoken without faith?"

"Good then — one word settles it." Wang Yaqiao was after all an old hand long-galloped on the rivers and lakes; on the spot he nodded assent and struck the board: "The official trusts the document, the private man trusts the seal. Since worthy brother Guojie is willing to send so great a gift, an empty mouth without proof will hardly do, surely?"

Li Guojie struck his chest once: "Ninth Master, rest easy — I will at once send a man to deliver you a deed of contract. How is that?"

Wang Yaqiao consented on the spot: "Good. A gentleman's one word — four horses cannot chase it back. Your contract once reaches my hand, we will at once let Zhao Tieqiao's head fall to the ground — no more!"

Straightway, the several Hatchet Gang stalwarts and Li Guojie, in the Great Fortune restaurant, settled their counsel; then cup after cup was raised and struck ringing together. With this, the great plan of executing Zhao Tieqiao was fixed amid the pushing of cups and the changing of vessels.

### 10. A daylight assassination where none was looked for

On the pitch-black road, far off, a pair of car lamps came alight.

This was deep night, June 30, 1930. The French Concession, deep in night and still, showed uncommonly secluded and tranquil in the midsummer midnight. Small foreign houses, lamp-lit one by one, all hid silent in stretch on stretch of gloomy-black French parasol trees. Yet at the roadside night-walking lovers still appeared from time to time; some people, sleepless in the summer night's parching heat, were out taking the cool. No one would think that in this quiet summer night, at this crossing where French Concession police drifted on patrol at ordinary hours, there would ring out a burst of ear-piercing gunfire.

The several killers laid beforehand in the shadow of a stand of parasol trees at the Gordon Road crossing of the French Concession each held a short gun, heads hooded in black masks: these were Wang Yaqiao's care-chosen Niu Anru, Wang Ganting and Xia Shao'en, three men. The three killers with Niu Anru at the head had all taken part in the Nanjing attempt on Chen Diaoyuan, and so all had experience of assassination. As early as a month before, when Wang Yaqiao and Li Guojie settled the remove-Zhao plan at the Great Fortune restaurant, Wang Yaqiao had sent out crack killers, formed into two action groups. One was the scouting group formed of Wu Hongtai and Liu Deshan [so printed here; Liu Decai elsewhere] and others, specially watching Zhao Tieqiao's movements from the dark. The other was the assassination group, which had drafted several schemes of attack.

Wang Yaqiao, to remove Zhao Tieqiao the sooner, specially assigned Wu Hongtai a "Naona"-make sedan [脑拿牌; marque unidentified — LOW-CONF on identification, glyphs certain]. Thus Wu Hongtai and his men could ride the motorcar back and forth without cease on the roads between Gordon Road and the Shanghai China Merchants company, keeping secret watch on Zhao Tieqiao — when Zhao went to office, when he returned home, when he went out to banquets and dances; even each day's one-going-one-stopping of Zhao's they held like a thing in the palm. Through Wu Hongtai's men's day-and-night watching it was found at last that Zhao Tieqiao often had social engagements at night, feast after feast almost past answering. Once this every-night-out pattern was grasped, Wang Yaqiao decided to send out the second action group — the killers Wang Ganting and Niu Anru and the rest — to begin the assassination in the hottest deep nights of late June.

That day at dusk, Niu Anru received the news Wu Hongtai reported: Zhao Tieqiao was again at the Great World, attending an evening of Peking-opera pure-singing; he decided therefore to ambush Zhao Tieqiao's motorcar at the Gordon Road crossing deep in the night, and on asking obtained Wang Yaqiao's assent. Since Wu Hongtai and the others had beforehand felt clear Zhao Tieqiao's night-going pattern, and knew too that his home lay only one li from the Gordon Road crossing, they concluded that Zhao Tieqiao's car would surely slacken speed at the Gordon Road turn. At that instant, bursting on him from the front and both flanks at once, firing to shatter his front window — the driver would surely die on the spot or flee for his life, and then Zhao Tieqiao would be a turtle in the jar. One burst of wild fire, and this traitor would surely render up his life on the instant.

Now the clock hands had reached twelve; and sure enough, as Niu Anru and Wang Ganting had reckoned, a Buick-make small sedan came driving through the pitch-dark. The car lamps flashed; the speed did indeed slacken sharply at the crossing turn. At that moment Wang Ganting waved a hand to the Niu Anru and Xia Shao'en hidden in the tree shadows; the three burst out at a stroke, and from three directions flung themselves at the already-slowing motorcar. Wang Ganting, foremost in the rush — say it slow, it happened fast — raised hand, gun spoke, one crack, at the car's front window glass. But — greatly outside his reckoning — his nerves being drawn tight in the act, the bullet flew wide, and did not shatter the front glass; it only shocked awake the Zhao Tieqiao half-asleep in the rear seat. He started awake, looked out of the car, and saw several masked man-shadows rushing out from the roadside at once — knew in his feelings he had met terrible killers. He screamed one scream: "Do not stop the car!" The driver, stunned, seeing several black shadows hurl themselves madly at the car, stamped the accelerator sharp and hard and flung on speed. The motorcar at once, as if gone mad, tore away for dear life into the deep lanes of the French Concession.

"Crack — crack — crack." Almost in the same instant, Niu Anru and Xia Shao'en also raised guns and fired on the flying car; the shots at once rang into one sheet. It was exactly this sudden burst of fire that woke the night-patrol French police: they blew up their whistles at once, and the French police and Chinese constables on night duty came running from all four sides and eight directions toward the Gordon Road where the thing had happened. Niu Anru, Wang Ganting and Xia Shao'en had meant to keep up the chase; hearing now the whistle-sound joined into one sheet, they had no choice but to take the withdrawal plan, quitting the French Concession and fleeing toward the British Concession. There a motorcar already waited: Liu Decai, charged with the meeting-and-receiving. The three leapt into the car; the driver raced the motor; the car flew off into a black small lane. By the time the French police pursued there, the assassins' traces were long gone.

"No panic. Once failing, we come a second time. In all — this time the Zhao one's life must be had, and nothing less!" After the small trial of the blade's point on June 30, rumors of every kind rose on the Shanghai waterfront. Some said this was Huang Jinrong's action against Zhao Tieqiao, for this was the three great magnates' habitual method — the newly risen new-nobility rarely escape such a doom. But Huang Jinrong actually denied in the press, publicly, any connection of the affair with himself — the reason being that Zhao Tieqiao, after taking office as head of the China Merchants company, had already gone to his house and paid respects at the wharf [拜码头]. At this, opinion named it instead a joint action of Du Yuesheng and Zhang Xiaolin against Zhao Tieqiao. Du Yuesheng did not deign to answer the rumor, while Zhang Xiaolin said openly to the press reporters: "Boss Du is Zhao Tieqiao's friend. Since we are all friends, how could there be such an absurd act? Besides — if we truly meant some action against Director Zhao, we would not be so muddled as to go raise the matter inside the French Concession!"

While the rumors rose on four sides, the French police added sentry posts near Gordon Road: past eight at night, all Chinese of suspicious trace and appearance were forbidden entry to the Concession. Zhao Tieqiao, after this brush with peril, dared no longer go out to engagements at night. Almost before the sky darkened he went home from the company, and then dared not step half a pace out of doors. Around him private bodyguards were added; inside and outside his gate, come night, it was nearly a post each ten steps, a sentry each five. Not only dared Wang Yaqiao send killers no more into the streets near Gordon Road — had assassins been sent, there was no way to enter on the procedure of killing.

Thus the assassination was for the time suspended.

Wang Yaqiao from time to time took secret counsel on action plans with Xuan Jimin, Wu Hongtai and the rest. Li Guojie, having paid his deposit, pressed Wang Yaqiao by telephone almost daily: "Ninth Master — did you not say ten-thousand-fold sure, no loss? How is it that at first hand-raising it turned to chickens flying and eggs smashed? You should know my 'Jiang'an' has long been moored at the Wusong wharf."

Wang Yaqiao's heart, naturally, scorched like fire. In all his doings on the Shanghai waterfront he had never met a thing so thorny. Day and night he thought how to hoe down Zhao Tieqiao the sooner. Wang Yaqiao had himself ridden a rickshaw round and round the several crossings near the French Concession that could lead to the Zhao house. When Wang Yaqiao found the French Concession now a forest of sentry posts, police thick as hemp, he knew that to keep working assassination there held no hope at all. He moved his gaze instead to Shanghai's flourishing downtown. Yet in those pleasure places — the bars, the dance halls — where Zhao Tieqiao ordinarily showed his face, his shadow appeared no more. The Zhao Tieqiao who had been startled by guns on a midsummer night — once bitten by the snake, three years afraid of the well-rope. To seek to strike him in those quarters offered almost no chance at all. Just as Wang Yaqiao stood blank without a plan, one day, riding a car, he passed the great gate of the Shanghai China Merchants company. Suddenly he ordered the car to slacken, then to brake far off under the tree-shade not distant from that gate. Wang Yaqiao looked out through the car window, quietly observing the stir before the gate. And in that moment there suddenly sprouted in him one bold conception: the China Merchants company itself was the ideal killing-ground!

"The reason I fix on the inside and outside of the China Merchants gate is precisely that there Zhao Tieqiao's guard is thinnest. And our strike can also no longer be set at night. Zhao Tieqiao, come nightfall, is gall-shaken heart-alarmed — dares not even leave his door; at that hour, wherever we prepare to put hands on him, it is wasted labor." Wang Yaqiao convened yet another meeting under the lamp. Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai at the start expressed worry at, and opposition to, Wang Yaqiao's daylight assassination plan; but when Wang Yaqiao had laid out the reasoning of the daylight strike, all were convinced heart and mouth. Wang Yaqiao said: "All Zhao Tieqiao's measures of guard are gathered on the night. He will not yet think that anyone would go murder him by day. The company, besides, is a place guests enter and leave freely — even the gate-keeper takes little notice of guests coming in. This offers us the best opportunity to shoot him down. This is called: strike where none is looked for, attack where none is guarded [出其不意，攻其不备]. Therefore we do best to raise the attack on Zhao Tieqiao in the morning when he comes to office, or at noon when he leaves."

The new plan of attack was thus fixed.

July 24: Shanghai again a day of burning, matchless high heat.

In the early morning, Wang Ganting, Niu Anru, Xia Shao'en and one small killer added for the occasion, all got up as old customers come to the company to talk business, swaggered through the great gate one after another; the old gateman scarcely questioned the callers. Niu Anru and the others, entering the building, found the corridors cold and clear — hardly an office worker to be seen. This was exactly what the several killers could have begged and not gotten. But from half past seven, the office hour, when they entered the gate, they never once saw Zhao Tieqiao's shadow. By the pattern Wu Hongtai's scouting had established, Zhao Tieqiao should long since have come to the company; yet this morning, for cause unknown, no shadow of him. Could Zhao Tieqiao have sensed something? Or had this good play Wang Yaqiao pre-arranged sprung some flaw again? If Zhao Tieqiao did not come to office this morning, then their further pursuit of this daylight plan would fare greatly ill — for entering and leaving the company many times could hardly fail to draw men's notice.

Just as Niu Anru kept his post in the company reception hall with a scorching breast, suddenly he saw a man-shadow flick past the door: it was Xia Shao'en, charged with watching the great gate, sending him the signal — Zhao has appeared!

Niu Anru leapt up at a stroke and rushed flying out of the ground-floor hall. And there he saw the familiar Buick small motorcar, already driven through the great gate, braking smooth and steady below the terrace before the building! The very car that one month before had brushed shoulders past them — today he could no longer let the Zhao Tieqiao inside it escape from under their eyelids. Niu Anru had barely stepped out of the glass door when from the car there proudly stepped down a man: tall of figure, above a gray-white short shirt, below black western-suit trousers, in his hand the civilization-stick cane that declared his station. Exactly the Zhao Tieqiao they had waited for through so many days! Niu Anru and the rest went tense on the instant; they saw that following behind Zhao Tieqiao was a spectacled secretary — the bodyguards, it seemed, did not attend him by day. The secretary carried Zhao Tieqiao's leather case; the two walked one before one behind toward the glass door. Zhao Tieqiao had just mounted the steps before the building when Niu Anru heard "crack" — one gunshot: Xia Shao'en had fired first. That shot rang uncommonly crisp in the company's great court! Yet the court raised no stir at the sound. For Zhao Tieqiao, having drunk one bullet, did not cry out, nor did he fall; he only pointed at his bleeding chest, looking round to left and right. In pain he opened and opened his mouth, but no sound would come.

"Crack! Crack!" Niu Anru, leveled on Zhao Tieqiao, fired two shots running. Xia Shao'en and the other killer fired at the same time; Zhao Tieqiao in an instant took several bullets, his chest-front one sheet of blood-filth. He could hold no longer; with one falling thud he pitched onto the steps before the door.

"It is bad — someone has assassinated the Supervisor!" The secretary with the leather case rushed for dear life into the empty-echoing great hall, shouting up into the building at the top of his voice. Only this startled those clerks who sat in their offices drinking tea and chatting; and when the people crowded out of the building with panic on their faces, they found Zhao Tieqiao already lying stiff and straight, unmoving, in a pool of blood!

The Shanghai waterfront broke into great clamor. Every paper, in banner-column eye-striking black type, swiftly reported the world-startling news of this new noble of commerce dying cruelly under nameless killers' guns. At that hour, Wang Yaqiao was just then planning how to take over that freighter "Jiang'an" moored on the river at Wusong mouth.

"Jimin, Hongtai — now it is your two turns to take the stage!" One day in early August, when the strike-Zhao affair had settled to quiet, Wang Yaqiao called Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai into the Anhui Guildhall. "Now we have killed Zhao Tieqiao — but Li Guojie no longer mentions the 'Jiang'an.' It appears that if we do not go take delivery ourselves, the Li one will not of himself lift his hands and give it over. Go now, and first seize the 'Jiang'an' into my hand!" Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai at this both cried aloud: "In our hands is the contract Li Guojie wrote at the start — do we still fear his welshing?" So saying, the two, in a rush of anger, took several strong-built fellows and went wind-and-fire straight for the Wusong wharf.

Wang Yaqiao kept to the guildhall drawing on his water-pipe, lying on the smoking couch trading idle talk with his lady Yaying, waiting only for Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin to come report good news. But just as they drifted in their pleasure, outside the door suddenly rang a burst of hurried footsteps; a small chief ran in flurried and shouting: "Ninth Master — it is bad! At the riverside — fighting has broken out!"

Wang Yaqiao and Wang Yaying scrambled up from the couch, neither knowing what on earth had happened at the river.
