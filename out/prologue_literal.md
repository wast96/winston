## 小引 Prologue: Chiang Kai-shek · Dai Li · Wang Yaqiao

Nanjing. Under the setting sun, the Huangpu Road official residence of Chiang Kai-shek.

One military small jeep, from a small road covered by green shade quietly drove over; it at the official residence's great gate lightly-nimbly turned a bend, dodged past those rifle-carrying guards of the front gate, from the left-side side door drove into this heavily-guarded great compound. The small jeep drove past the green-shaded luxuriant rear garden, along a person-less gray-brick paved path slowly drove into the deep front courtyard. Not long, this mysterious small jeep braked beneath a gray small building.

The person who walked down from the jeep wore on his head a formal hat [fedora]; the dark glasses on the bridge of his nose covered that pair of shadowy [幽幽] eyes of his. He was the great special agent Dai Li. At this time Dai Li had just received the telephone call of the Attendants' Office [侍从室], and only then hurried over; walking into this deep and tranquil official residence, his heart at once felt several parts nervous.

Dai Li quietly walked into the small building's corridor. Inside it was still and quiet, almost without human trace. He held his breath, along the spiral staircase laid with scarlet carpet, climbed to the second-floor turning, and only then discovered there was a secretary there waiting. The secretary came up to meet him, in a small voice said to him: "Mr. Dai, the Wei-seat [委座, the Chairman] is in the inner room waiting for you; there is an extremely urgent matter for you to go handle!"

Dai Li's heart wildly thumped peng-peng; this was the first time in his whole life he came alone to see Chiang Kai-shek. When he appeared in an enormous office, he suddenly discovered that opposite, beneath the Blue Sky White Sun flag, sat upright a person wearing a gray cloth long gown, who was there fixedly sizing him up, coldly, not emitting one word.

On Dai Li's face at once appeared a nervous expression: "Principal [校长, Whampoa address to Chiang; rendered Commandant in the polished layer], you called me…?"

Chiang Kai-shek still did not speak, only lifted his hand, toward the hands-hanging-attending, so-nervous-he-dared-not-approach Dai Li beckoned once. Dai Li at once walked up several steps, again toward the upright-sitting unmoving Chiang bowed his body in salute: "Principal, whatever task there is, I your student will certainly not begrudge any price to go complete it!…"

"Good, good good, Dai Yunong, you come over." Dai Li, while secretly looking sideways, discovered that Chiang Kai-shek and the Principal he had seen before at the Guangzhou Whampoa Military Academy were greatly different. The present Chiang Kai-shek had already become the Nationalist Party's highest chief; his expression was more awe-inspiring and heavy. "Yunong," Chiang Kai-shek's gaze fixed on him became soft. Dai Li quietly looked over, discovered that the gloomy-faced Chiang Kai-shek suddenly from a drawer took out a paper bag, with a casual shake, from inside was revealed a stack of black-and-white photographs; Chiang Kai-shek casually picked up one, pushed it before his face: "Do you recognize this person?"

Dai Li did not dare let out a big breath, carefully received that black-and-white photograph Chiang passed over. On it was one man's blurred back view! He wordlessly shook his head.

"Uh, you look again at these." Chiang again pushed that stack of photographs all to him. Dai Li's heart beat still more fiercely; he hurriedly received the thick stack of photographs, one by one carefully looked. Dai Li discovered these photographs were all secretly taken by some person hiding in the dark. What several photographs captured was almost all the same one person: some are front face, some are side face, but the great majority are back views.

When Dai Li began looking, he had not yet recognized on these not-ideal-in-effect photographs, who exactly the secretly-photographed person was. Suddenly, in his eyes appeared a light of astonishment, because he saw clearly that among them one photograph was that person's front-face image. The face shape and outline were extremely familiar. The person on the photograph seemed to be at a meeting place declaiming with vigor [振振有词] in a speech; the background behind him was the great assembly's presidium. Several Nationalist high officials sat straight and proper — all familiar faces. Among them was Chiang Kai-shek together with other Nationalist important men Li Zongren, Feng Yuxiang, Yan Xishan and such people.

"Who is he?" The more Dai Li looked, the more he felt that person on the photograph wearing a formal hat, wearing a black long robe, was somewhat familiar to the eye. Especially that person's small mustache on the lip and that black-framed pair of spectacles on the nose-bridge immediately made Dai Li think of a person; he could not help crying out: "Principal, can it be that he is the one whose name shook Shanghai for a time in those years, the Hatchet Gang master… Wang Yaqiao?"

"Niang xipi [娘希匹, Chiang's Ningbo curse], precisely this person!" Chiang Kai-shek, breath huffing with anger, looked at Dai Li, "Before, when you were down-and-out in Shanghai, did you know him?"

"I… have heard of Wang Yaqiao's name." Dai Li, under Chiang Kai-shek's awe-inspiring gaze, could not keep from shivering once. He not only knew Wang Yaqiao; moreover in those years the two men had a friendship not of the ordinary kind. However, Dai Li, who for many years had followed Chiang Kai-shek campaigning north and south, deeply knew Chiang Kai-shek's ruthlessness [凶险, lit. menace/peril]. He knew that to summon him into the Huangpu Road residence for this Wang Yaqiao of the photographs, there must be a reason not of the ordinary kind. Dai Li hastily swallowed back the words he had meant to say, and hesitating said: "At that time I in Shanghai was drifting through the days, life poor and destitute; I had no opportunity to come into contact with Wang Yaqiao…" "Is the Hatchet Gang fierce?" Chiang asked again.

"Fierce! The Hatchet Gang, that is one of Shanghai's fiercest gangs, second only to the Green-Red Gang. Especially Wang Yaqiao — his ability is greater still. It is said that even the Shanghai waterfront's Huang Jinrong, Du Yuesheng and Zhang Xiaolin, the three great magnates, cannot afford to provoke him! I do not know why the Principal suddenly raised this person?"

"This fellow called Wang Yaqiao — now it has come to where he must be removed." Chiang Kai-shek only now, gnashing his teeth, spoke out his plan. "As early as the Northern Expedition, there were people who spoke to me of Wang Yaqiao, saying he is a hero-villain [枭雄] of the chaotic age, his power vast, urging me to remove him early. But at that time I did not treat this person as a matter; I took the Hatchet Gang for nothing but some Anhui local toughs and loafers — worth how much hanging on the teeth? But since the Nanjing National Government was formed, this person has grown more and more outrageous. Niang xipi — he actually dared, before my face, to curse loudly that I, Chiang, am not a true disciple of Sun Yat-sen! Can this be endured? If I continue to tolerate his wild fooling, then I, Chiang, fear that one day I will have to yield my seat to him, Wang Yaqiao!"

"What — there could actually be such a thing? Can it be that Wang Yaqiao has eaten a bear's heart and tiger's gall, daring to attack the Principal to his face?" Dai Li, hearing to here, could not help sucking in a mouthful of cold air. He suddenly, from that hastily-snatched photograph, discovered Wang Yaqiao was at the National Government founding assembly giving a speech, and behind Wang Yaqiao, Chiang Kai-shek was angrily fixing his eyes on him. Only now did Dai Li suddenly grasp why Chiang Kai-shek had urgently summoned him; he hurried to say, "The Principal's meaning is…"

"Yunong, your Ten-Man Team — all of them I brought from Wuhan to Nanjing." Chiang Kai-shek said. "Before, your task was only collecting intelligence. But now it is not the same; we have established the government at Nanjing. The Ten-Man Team you lead should exist to protect the National Government. Do you understand my meaning?"

"I understand, I understand." Dai Li, though from Chiang Kai-shek's words he understood Wang Yaqiao had been entered into Chiang Kai-shek's blacklist, yet — who knows why — knowingly asked: "Can it be the Principal's meaning is to send us to collect the situation of Wang Yaqiao's recent activities?"

"No — not collecting the situation. I want you to at once find out clearly where he lives in Nanjing," Chiang Kai-shek viciously raised his hand and made the gesture of a beheading. "Understood?"

Dai Li understood Chiang Kai-shek's intent: he wanted urgently to assassinate Wang Yaqiao. He knew that Wang Yaqiao, after attending the National Government founding assembly, had not yet returned to Shanghai. Receiving this secret killing order, Dai Li's heart suddenly floated hesitation. But he dared not, before the heavy-power-grasping Chiang Kai-shek, show a hair's breadth of delay; he hurriedly put away Wang Yaqiao's photographs, his two legs snapped together, his waist straightened, and he said in a loud voice: "Principal, put your heart at rest — so long as he, Wang Yaqiao, is now still in Nanjing, we will at once carry his head to see you!"

Chiang Kai-shek, hearing this, only then revealed on his face a thread of smile-lines.

Dai Li bowed to him again, then walked toward the side of the door.

"Yunong," Chiang Kai-shek again beckoned and called Dai Li to a stop. "Remember — the action must certainly be tight-woven [缜密], the matter must be done deft and quick; ten-thousand-fold do not give those delegates who came to Nanjing to attend the assembly any pretext of assassination to hold. Do you understand my meaning?"

"Principal, I understand!" Dai Li once more, toward Chiang Kai-shek seated beneath the Blue Sky, White Sun, Wholly Red Earth banner, most-respectfully-most-reverently gave a sound of assent, then quietly withdrew out.

The night's color like ink. The old city of Jinling — lamplight brilliant.

On the black-gloomy Yangtze were reflected dots and clusters of lamplight. Dai Li alone kept watch in the deep small courtyard of Ji'e Alley No. 54. He turned the desk lamp bright, in order examined those photographs Chiang Kai-shek had handed him. Obviously, those were all photographs, secretly snatched by special agents in the dark, of Wang Yaqiao's activities in Nanjing. Before his eyes appeared an extremely solemn scene: that was the meeting-hall of the Nanjing National Government founding assembly. Just as Chiang Kai-shek and the Nationalist grandees on the presidium were warmly applauding, suddenly, up a red carpet walked a middle-aged man wearing a black long gown — he was the Anhui man attending this assembly in the capacity of workers' delegate, Wang Yaqiao.

"Delegate gentlemen, I, Wang Yaqiao, today will speak a few sentences of heart-words!" When Wang Yaqiao mounted the presidium and began to speak, the whole hall at once went quiet. Chiang Kai-shek, watching this Anhui man before him — stature not tall, speech carrying earth-in-earth rustic air — felt displeasure in his heart. Later, Chiang's face-color became more and more ugly to see. Because Wang Yaqiao openly spoke words that insinuated against and attacked Chiang Kai-shek.

Wang Yaqiao loudly declaimed: "Every delegate knows, the Father of the Nation Mr. Sun Yat-sen left behind last words, called: unite with Russia, unite with the Communists, assist the farmers and workers — this is the Three Great Policies that we Nationalist Party people must obey. But, what makes people greatly heart-pained is, precisely while Mr. Sun's corpse-bones are not yet cold, those people who before, in front of Mr. Sun, called themselves students, now actually have raised the man-killing butcher knife, and in Shanghai have opened the killing-ring upon us workers! Such conduct betraying Mr. Sun's testament — how can it not make us, these believers of Mr. Zhongshan, chill at heart?…"

In the hall some people struck up applause; one could see Wang Yaqiao's speech had aroused resonance among a portion of people. On the presidium Chiang Kai-shek sat as if on a needle-felt [如坐针毡], his two eyes firmly fixed on Wang Yaqiao, his face-color iron-blue.

Wang Yaqiao's speech grew more and more impassioned: "Though Mr. Sun has passed away, yet we, these true believers, remain. Within the Nationalist Party there is still a great number of true revolutionaries; they will absolutely not consent that anyone trample Mr. Sun Yat-sen's Three Great Policies! Therefore I, in the capacity of the workers' delegate, today will here, toward the delegates of every circle at this assembly, solemnly issue a call: certainly must forever firmly remember Mr. Sun's Three Great Policies, swear-death to defend the Three Principles of the People! Strike down reactionary warlordism — the revolution ten thousand years!…"

Chiang Kai-shek, at what moment none knew, had already quietly left his seat.

Dai Li, thinking to here, could not help a start in his heart; his face-color also at once changed. From his clothes-pocket he carefully drew out one Browning hand-gun; under the lamp he pulled open the chamber [枪膛], and inside was revealed the shine of bullets.

Just as he bit tight his teeth and set his decision to do the killing, at his ear suddenly came a familiar voice: "Dai Chunfeng, you may go to Guangdong, or you may seek another road and fly-yellow-rise-to-glory [飞黄腾达]. But you ten-thousand-fold must not forget: at any time whatever, you also cannot forget that you were once a disciple of my Hatchet Gang!"

"Set your heart at rest, elder brother. Even if I, Dai Chunfeng, one day become an official, I will not forget your life-saving kindness to me."

"I, Wang, bestow kindness and do not scheme for repayment. I only want you not to forget your roots. You are of suffering-people origin, ah!"

"Set your heart at rest. I will remember elder brother's words."

Dai Li, thinking to here, the hand holding the Browning suddenly, unable to restrain itself, shuddered once. On his forehead seeped out cold sweat……

Midnight; Nanjing's ten-thousand sounds all still.

Suddenly, several police cars sitting-full of plainclothes special agents charged out from that mysterious courtyard of Ji'e Alley No. 45 [the number as printed; page 5 printed 54 — see note], in the pitch-black night rushed toward the person-less great street, straight toward the Hongwu Street direction galloping away. At once, snow-bright car lamps slit open the midnight's blackness; the ear-piercing scream the police cars emitted shook awake those city people long since asleep. In the first police car sat the "Ten-Man Team" with Dai Li at the head; every one of them gripped a short gun in hand, faces full of killing air.

But Dai Li's spirit was not keeping its house. In his brain appeared another picture: that was the summer of 1916; in Shanghai's ten-li foreign-strip [十里洋场] there was a small bie-san [瘪三, Shanghai slang: ragged destitute drifter] begging everywhere; his name was called Dai Chunfeng. That day a small rain was falling. Dai Chunfeng, begging meal-scraps along the street, was suddenly by a small sedan flying head-on struck a head-on blow, and instantly sprawled flat on the roadway. At that time Dai Chunfeng had already gone several days without eating one full meal; sallow-faced, struck down to the ground, he was instantly a whole face of fresh blood, and fainted past.

Just at this moment, from the road's other side came a rickshaw [人力洋车]; in the car sat a gentleman wearing a riding-jacket and long gown. He was the Anhui man Wang Yaqiao, at that time already of small fame on the Shanghai waterfront. When he discovered a beggar struck senseless by a car, and many passers-by circled there sighing-and-clucking without end, he at once jumped down from the car, waved his hand at another rickshaw, and said: "Come! Help out! We are all of poor-suffering-people origin — how can we watch a young man lose his life like this?"

That rickshaw man received Wang Yaqiao's banknotes and at once carried the heavily injured Dai Chunfeng onto the rickshaw and at fire-speed delivered him into a mission hospital. Dai Chunfeng in that hospital slept dark-unconscious two whole days and two whole nights before he was rescued awake. This youth called Dai Chunfeng is precisely the Dai Li who today has become the head of Chiang Kai-shek's "Ten-Man Team"!

Dai Li in the many years after the event never once let fade that terrible car calamity. He remembered that after coming clear-headed, he had asked the doctor beside him: "What person saved my life?" The doctor told Dai Chunfeng: "Your life is big. If at that time you had not met Anhui's Ninth Master Wang, perhaps this small life of yours would already be gone. It was Ninth Master Wang who sent forth great mercy, hired the car for you; after you were delivered to the hospital it was again the Ninth Master who put out the medicine-money. You, boy, must remember: Ninth Master Wang is the benefactor who saved your life, ah!"

"Ninth Master Wang?" The Dai Chunfeng of that time did not yet know who the benefactor was.

The doctor said: "What — you knock about on the Shanghai waterfront and actually do not know even Ninth Master Wang? He is the Hatchet Gang's chief — Wang Yaqiao, ah!"

"Wang Yaqiao?!" Dai Chunfeng greatly startled. Though he was only one nameless nobody on the Shanghai waterfront, living by begging along the streets, yet he had long since heard much of Wang Yaqiao's great name. Now, hearing that the person who saved him at the brink of danger was actually that Hatchet Gang master feared by everyone who saw him, Dai Chunfeng's pair of despairing eyes suddenly brightened. In his heart wild joy; in his mouth he murmured, saying: "Heaven helps me! I will go seek Ninth Master Wang!…"

From that day, Dai Chunfeng went over and attached himself under Wang Yaqiao's gate: first a thousand-kindnesses-ten-thousand-thanks for Wang Yaqiao's life-saving grace; afterward he also, heart-glad-and-willing, under Wang Yaqiao's command became a small disciple. However, world-affairs are without constancy. A few years' interval later, Dai Li dreaming could not have thought that he would actually receive orders from Chiang Kai-shek to go to Hongwu Street to arrest the Anhui delegate who dared attack Mr. Chiang at the assembly — Wang Yaqiao.

"Arrived — ahead is precisely Hongwu Street No. 51; Wang Yaqiao and his bodyguards live right here!" While Dai Li sat in the police car recalling past events, beside him the "Ten-Man Team" member He Zhongyan suddenly patted his shoulder, remindingly pointed toward the deep of the pitch-black small street, signaling to stop the car at once.

But Dai Li paid no heed. That police car still crying-out charged into Hongwu Street.

He Zhongyan again reminded him, saying: "Yunong, if we continue charging forward, it will certainly startle the people inside. At that moment Wang Yaqiao's bodyguards will not be easy to provoke!"

Dai Li had no way out; he could only order the car stopped. Just at this moment, Dai Li suddenly discovered that before the gate of Hongwu Street No. 51 there was one gate-keeping bodyguard. Dai Li, just then vexed at having no way to shake off the difficulty, had a spark of quick wit [灵机一动]: not waiting for the "Ten-Man Team" members at his side to take any action, he seized the lead in jumping down from the car. Dai Li rushed forward several steps, suddenly pulled from his waist the Browning hand-gun, aimed at that gate-keeping bodyguard, and — catch-him-unprepared — fired one shot. The gun-sound cracked, slitting open the midnight's lonely-still [岑寂]. After that gate-keeping bodyguard was struck down to the ground by Dai Li, inside the courtyard of Hongwu Street No. 51 rose a stretch of flustered human voices. Not long, gun-sounds like beans bursting suddenly arose. Those bodyguards suddenly startled awake by the cold shot together charged onto the great street, toward the enveloping-encircling "Ten-Man Team" madly firing. Dai Li and the rest also with all their lives returned fire. In an instant, on Hongwu Street blew up a burst of gun-forest bullet-rain [枪林弹雨].

Wang Yaqiao, precisely under the cover of this gun-sound, from the rear wall vaulted over and escaped.

The next day, Nanjing's various papers one after another printed the "National Government Wanted Order," and through the various places of Jiangnan searched to seize the in-flight Hatchet Gang chief Wang Yaqiao. Precisely because Chiang Kai-shek ordered the wanted-listing, the formerly not-eye-catching Anhui man Wang Yaqiao, in the space of one night, actually became the most influential focus figure of the Republican period. But — how after all did Wang Yaqiao rise from the start? And why would he arouse Chiang Kai-shek's panic and thunder-rage? All of this must be spoken starting from his assassination of the Anhui grandee Chen Diaoyuan.
