### The Talent at the Tianduo Bao

In the last years of the fallen Qing, when people spoke of the Shanghai papers that preached revolution, they all praised the Minli Bao, and few knew that there was then another, the Tianduo Bao, which strove no less hard to preach revolution. Its editors and writers were the pick of the day — Dai Jitao, Zhou Hao, Chen Bulei, Li Huaishuang and the like were all old Tianduo hands. By the first year of the Republic Zhou Hao had gone off to found the Minquan Bao, and Dai Jitao then left the Tianduo and moved over to the Minquan.

Later Chen Bulei and Li Huaishuang left in their turn, and the paper's manager, having no wish to carry on, at last announced that it would cease; the general run of readers were sorry to see it go.
