### Neon Lights

The fitting-up of "year-red lamps"—otherwise called nihongdeng, neon lamps—has now reached a great and singular vogue. Along Nanjing Road nearly every large shop has them fitted, and after them the cinemas and the grand hotels have all put them up too. Come nightfall, these dazzling neon lamps meet the eye at every turn. The scientific name of the thing is "neon light"; its glare is intensely bright, and a shop fits it so as to catch the passer-by's notice the more easily. Besides red they now come in green and blue and other distinctions, and some are made to rotate and flow, or to open and close. The companies that install year-red lamps were founded at first by Westerners, but there are now several run by Chinese hands as well. When people hold a happy occasion or a birthday feast, they will even fit up in the middle of the hall a great character for "joy" or a great character for "long life" in neon, for the look of the thing; so that the year-red lamp may be said to be all the rage just now.
