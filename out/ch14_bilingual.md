## H2 Chapter 14. When She Left, Blood Ran the Peach-Blossom Road

> 阿初没有想到，在四太太温文尔雅的外表下、涵意幽怨的字里行间投射出的竟是无限怨毒的杀气。
A-Chu had never imagined that beneath the Fourth Madam's gentle, cultivated exterior, and in the veiled bitterness that ran between her every line, there lurked a killing intent of boundless venom.

> 她在等阿初作出回应。强烈的也好、懦弱的也罢、甚至恐惧的也行，他必须表明自己的态度。
She was waiting for him to respond. Fierce, faint-hearted, even frightened — any of these would do; but he had to declare where he stood.

> 四太太在等答案。
The Fourth Madam was waiting for an answer.

> “做不到！”阿初站了起来。“我做不到。”
"I can't." A-Chu got to his feet. "I can't do it."

> “为什么？”
"Why not?"

> 因为，这将是一场杀戮，血肉横飞的杀戮。阿初知道，自己一旦深陷“复仇”的泥潭，加入所谓的帮派社团，自己将永远无法上岸。
Because it would be a killing — a killing of flesh torn apart and blood flung wide. A-Chu knew that once he sank into the mire of "revenge," once he joined this so-called society, he would never climb back onto dry land.

> “我从小就被残酷的生活所左右，我是一个被您、被荣家四太太收养的弃儿，是荣家大少爷身边的一个卑微的奴才。没有依靠，没有能力养活自己。是主子的恩养和怜悯，把我塑造成大海里流浪的一叶浮萍。这一叶可怜的、没有根基的浮萍，远跨重洋，吸收西学，努力做人，又被命运塑造成一朵完美的、出泥不染的荷花。这朵花虽然身体仍被禁锢在水渊湖泥，可是，他的思想和灵魂是完全自由的、干净的、美好的。我从来就不肯认命，不向命运低头，我自信可以排除万难，去争取自己自由的人生和家庭的幸福。我全心全意地、真诚地去爱，爱社会，爱民众，爱人生，我的生命中充溢着阳光和温馨。现在，您要无情地打破我所拥有的世界，您要夺走我善良的本性。为什么？为什么？为了一个我根本不认识的'父亲'？您要我去讨还血债，您想过没有，我会不会答应您？”
"From my earliest years I was ruled by a cruel life. I am a foundling, taken in by you, by the Fourth Madam of the Rong house — a lowly servant at the side of the family's eldest young master. With nothing to lean on, no means to keep myself alive. It was my masters' bounty and pity that shaped me into a single leaf of duckweed adrift on the great sea. And that pitiful, rootless leaf of duckweed crossed the oceans, drank in Western learning, strove to make itself into a man — and fate shaped it, in the end, into a perfect lotus that rises from the mud unstained. The flower's body is still imprisoned in the deep water and the lake mud, yet its mind and its soul are wholly free, clean, and good. I have never once been willing to bow to my lot, never bowed my head to fate; I believed I could clear away every obstacle and win a free life and the happiness of a family of my own. With my whole heart, in all sincerity, I set out to love — to love society, to love the people, to love life itself; my days brimmed with sunlight and warmth. And now you would shatter, without mercy, the world I have made my own; you would strip me of my very goodness. Why? Why? For the sake of a 'father' I never knew at all? You ask me to go and collect a debt of blood — but have you asked yourself whether I will agree to it?"

> “会的，你会答应。不错，是我，是我把你带到了荣家，是我，是我强加给你一个非主非仆的难堪身份。可是，你知道吗？无论你在何处、无论你置身何地，你都处在强势。你像极了我们的父亲！阿初，世上有太多的事情，无法从正常渠道解决。如果，二十年前我们就能将有罪的人绳之以法，那么，我也何必寻此迂道？牺牲自尊？”
"You will. You will agree. Yes — it was I who brought you into the Rong house; it was I who forced upon you that mortifying station, neither master nor servant. But do you know this? Wherever you are, in whatever place you stand, you stand in the position of strength. You are the very image of our father! A-Chu, there are too many things in this world that cannot be settled through the ordinary channels. If, twenty years ago, we could have brought the guilty to justice under the law, why would I have sought out this crooked road? Why sacrifice my own dignity?"

> “我不想萎缩在一个阴暗的角落里，去布置谋杀的陷阱。我会因此而堕落，堕落成罪人。您懂吗？”
"I have no wish to shrink into some dark corner and lay the snares of murder. I would be dragged down by it — dragged down into a criminal. Do you understand?"

> “那么，你将我弃子养弟的恩情，放在哪里？”
"Then where do you set the kindness I showed you — a woman who gave up her own child to raise her younger brother?"

> “我可以回报恩情，但是，我不会臣服于恩情。”
"I can repay a kindness. But I will not be enslaved to one."

> “有什么不同？”
"And what is the difference?"

> “含义完全不同。您在诱导我杀人，您知道吗？”阿初显然很激动，他的情绪已经无法自控了。“我可以忍受歧视、疾病、痛苦、甚至死亡。但是，我不会、永远不会去杀人。这是我所固守的道德底线。我不可能去杀人，绝不可能。我是医生，医生是治病救人的。您忘了我的职业吗？您叫我把这二十几年来所学到的知识、文化、道德、良知全部抛荒，您叫我放下柳叶刀，拿起屠刀，去杀戮。而二十年前家业凋零、父亲遇害的一场灾难就是逼我去杀戮的唯一动因！我不能接受，接受这种恶性循环！”
"The meaning is entirely different. You are luring me into killing — do you understand that?" A-Chu was plainly agitated now; his feelings had slipped beyond his control. "I can bear discrimination, sickness, suffering, even death. But I will not — I will never — take a life. That is the moral line I hold to. I cannot kill; it is impossible. I am a doctor, and a doctor heals the sick and saves lives. Have you forgotten my calling? You would have me leave to waste all the knowledge, the culture, the morality, the conscience I have gathered these twenty-odd years; you would have me lay down the lancet and take up the butcher's cleaver, and go out to slaughter. And the one and only cause driving me to that slaughter is a catastrophe of twenty years ago — the ruin of a house, the murder of a father! I cannot accept it — I cannot accept such a vicious circle!"

> “那么，你想怎样？你要怎样？你把我这二十来含辛茹苦、忍辱偷生的亲姐姐放在何处？我们的父亲，他的遗骨被草草掩埋在阴暗的泥土里，他的魂魄在废墟中、在烟尘里飘荡，他做了二十年的孤魂野鬼，不得馨享子孙后代的香火。你作为父亲的儿子，你不汗颜吗？这二十年来我什么都想到了，唯独没有想到你是如此的自私和懦弱。我以为杨氏男儿的血性一直隐藏在你内心深处，维系着你的尊严和生命，我没有料到随着你身世秘密的揭开，湮灭已久的'真相'反倒成了隔绝'复仇'火焰的屏障。所谓道德瓦解了仇恨，不如说是你还不了解仇恨，你没有切身体会，没有切肤之痛，你只关心你的切身利益，你要保持信仰、维护名誉，父仇母恨在你的眼里不过是雾霭烟尘？您说我的话对不对？荣先生？您骨子里已经浸泡了太久的'救世渡人'，是我自不量力，是我枉费心机。”四太太尖锐地说。她显然已经清醒地意识到，她所面对的阿初，并不是她想像中的关键“棋子”，阿初原本就是一个超然的“棋手”，而自己才是一颗即将被遗弃的“残子”。
"Then what would you have? What is it you want? Where do you set me — your own elder sister, who for twenty years has swallowed bitterness and endured humiliation to steal a living? Our father — his bones were hastily buried in the dark earth; his soul drifts through the ruins, through the smoke and dust. Twenty years he has been a lonely, wandering ghost, denied the sweet incense of his own descendants. As your father's son, do you not burn with shame? These twenty years I have thought of everything — everything but this: that you could be so selfish and so weak. I believed the hot blood of the Yang men was hidden all along in the depths of your heart, holding up your dignity and your life. I never foresaw that when the secret of your birth was laid bare, a 'truth' long buried would become instead the very screen that walls off the fire of 'revenge.' You say morality has dissolved your hatred; I would say rather that you do not yet know hatred — you have not felt it in your own flesh, have known no pain that cuts to the bone. You care only for your own interests; you would keep your faith, guard your good name, and the wrong done your father, the grief owed your mother, are in your eyes no more than mist and smoke. Tell me, am I right, Mr. Rong? To the very marrow you have soaked too long in your 'saving the world and ferrying men across' — it was I who overreached, I who wasted my scheming." The Fourth Madam spoke sharply. She had plainly come, with a cold clarity, to see that the A-Chu before her was not the crucial 'pawn' she had imagined; A-Chu had been a detached 'player' all along, and it was she who was the 'dead stone' about to be cast aside.

> “姐姐，我需要时间考虑。”阿初神色暗淡地说。
"Sister, I need time to think," A-Chu said, his face gone dim.

> “我不逼你！”四太太眼睛里流露出恨意。
"I will not force you!" Yet hatred showed through in the Fourth Madam's eyes.

> 阿初不知道自己是如何回到荣家的，他的脑海里一片空白，内心深处陷入无限的恐慌，他的精神状态也因突如其来得“真相”，而变得异常颓废。
A-Chu did not know how he made his way back to the Rong house. His mind was a blank; deep within he had fallen into a boundless panic, and the abrupt "truth" had left his spirit strangely wasted and low.

> 杨家的真正主人，社团的新领袖。在阿初眼里不过是杨氏长门的遗孤们借尸还魂的把戏。冤冤相报、颠覆财富的行为，无疑更接近于一场骨肉相残的悲剧。杨羽桦的确该死！他杀死了自己的亲哥哥，霸占了自己哥哥的妻子，侵吞了他的财产，还要杀死哥哥的孩子。他的确丧尽天良！有罪的人应该得到法律的制裁！但是，自己不是法律，自己如果去杀人，就是挑战法律。
The true master of the Yang house; the new leader of the society. In A-Chu's eyes it was all no more than a conjuring trick by which the orphans of the Yang elder branch called a dead man's soul back into a borrowed corpse. To answer wrong with wrong, to overturn a fortune — such acts came, beyond doubt, nearer to a tragedy of kin slaughtering kin. Yang Yuhua did indeed deserve to die! He had killed his own elder brother, seized his brother's wife, swallowed up his brother's property, and now would kill his brother's child besides. He had truly exhausted every last scruple of conscience! The guilty ought to be brought to judgment under the law! But he himself was not the law; if he went out to kill, he would be defying the law.

> 二十年前的旧账如何来算？二十年前为什么不报官？为什么？为什么呢？二十年来，他们和仇人生活在一个城市里，相隔不远，比邻而居。是什么原因让仇恨的火焰“偃旗息鼓”了整整二十年呢？
How was one to settle an account twenty years old? Why, twenty years ago, had they not gone to the authorities? Why? Why indeed? For twenty years they had lived in the same city as their enemy, no great distance apart, neighbor beside neighbor. What was it that had made the fire of their hatred "furl its banners and still its drums" for twenty whole years?

> 阿初反反复复回味着过去四太太种种古怪的言行，重新咀嚼四太太那一段充满仇恨的话，“我要报复！我要你亲手杀死他们！亲手杀死他们！！我要和你，看着他们这对狗男女在眼前化为泡沫，挫成灰烬。”这才是四太太隐忍了多年仇恨的原因。她要自己亲手除去这一对狗男女，以泻切齿之恨。
Over and over A-Chu turned back to all the strange words and acts of the Fourth Madam, chewing anew on that speech of hers so thick with hatred: "I want revenge! I want you to kill them with your own hands! Kill them with your own hands!! I want to stand with you and watch that pair of dogs, that man and that woman, dissolve into foam before our eyes, ground down to ash." This, then, was the reason the Fourth Madam had swallowed her hatred all these years. She wanted that pair of curs wiped out by his very hand, and so drain off a hatred that made her grind her teeth.

> 姐姐“以恩挟报”，逼弟弟“以暴制暴”。
The elder sister "held a kindness for ransom against its repayment," forcing the younger brother to "meet violence with violence."

> 阿初心里很难过，他不想违背自己多年做人的原则。他知道自己无法兼善天下，唯求独善其身。现在，连独善其身也即将成为空花泡影。
A-Chu's heart was heavy. He had no wish to betray the principles by which he had lived all these years. He knew he could not make the whole world good; he asked only to keep his own self good. And now even keeping his own self good was about to become a flower in the void, a bubble on the water.

> 他为自己的处境感到深度的压抑。
He felt, in his situation, a deep and pressing gloom.

> “什么时候回来得？”荣升不知何时走到了阿初身边。
"When did you get back?" Rong Sheng had come to A-Chu's side, he did not know when.

> “哦。”阿初惊醒过来。才发现自己站在院子里发呆。“少爷，您的烟，我忘了。”
"Oh." A-Chu came to with a start, and only then found himself standing blankly in the courtyard. "Young master — your cigarettes. I forgot them."

> “想什么呢？魂不守舍的？”荣升感到奇怪地问。
"What are you thinking about? You look as though your soul has left your body," Rong Sheng asked, puzzled.

> “少爷，您说，有罪的人会反省？会自责吗？”
"Young master — tell me, does a guilty man ever reflect on himself? Does he ever reproach himself?"

> “你在说我吗？”荣升的嘴角挂起了淡淡的笑容。
"Are you speaking of me?" A faint smile hung at the corner of Rong Sheng's mouth.

> “不，不是。”
"No — no."

> “如果每一个有罪的人都会反省、会自责，那么，这个世界一定很美好。”
"If every guilty man reflected on himself and reproached himself, then this world would surely be a lovely place."

> “如果有一个人有目的、有预谋地去杀一个有罪的人，他是否有罪？”阿初问。
"If a man sets out, with purpose and forethought, to kill a guilty man — is he himself guilty?" A-Chu asked.

> “你如何确定被杀的人一定有罪？”荣升反问。“有罪的人和无罪的人都在同一个平面上，'罪孽'是可以转让、嫁祸的。谋杀是邪恶的！无论你是否假借'正义'之名。”
"How can you be certain the man killed is guilty?" Rong Sheng countered. "The guilty and the guiltless stand on one and the same plane; 'sin' can be handed off, its blame shifted onto another. Murder is evil! — no matter whether or not you borrow the name of 'justice' for it."

> “如果为了'报恩'去杀人呢？”
"And if a man kills to 'repay a kindness'?"

> “愚蠢的行为。”
"A stupid act."

> “那么，为了父仇母恨去杀人呢？”
"Then to kill for a father's wrong and a mother's grief?"

> “荒唐的行为。”
"An absurd one."

> “中国人有句话：'杀父之仇不共戴天'。”
"The Chinese have a saying: 'One cannot live under the same sky as the killer of one's father.'"

> “你很想杀人吗？”
"Do you want very much to kill?"

> “不想。”
"No."

> “有人逼你杀人吗？”
"Is anyone forcing you to kill?"

> “没有。”
"No."

> “你有没有坚守如一的信仰？”
"Have you a faith you have held to, unwavering?"

> “有。”
"I have."

> “是什么？”
"And what is it?"

> “救世渡人。”
"To save the world and ferry men across."

> “杀人和渡人是两条截然相反的道路。”
"Killing men and ferrying men across are two roads that run flatly opposite."

> “对。我现在就站在这两条路的分界口，迷失了做人的方向。少爷，我很痛苦。我需要您的帮助。”
"Just so. And I stand now at the fork where those two roads divide, having lost the bearing of how to be a man. Young master, I am in torment. I need your help."

> “路，是自己走的；方向，是自己选择的。自己的一生应该掌握在自己的手里。人应该活在光明里；而不是仇恨中。如果，你一旦选择仇恨，你的心底会永远丧失光明。你在荣家，是唯一一个光明烛照的人，希望你光明的盈余可以多分我一杯羹。”荣升言即此处，居然眼含泪光。“保持善良的本性，做一个真诚的人。永远保持住，不要像我一样堕落，成为黑暗的玩偶，你不了解，只有在黑夜里行走过的人，才知道光明的可贵。”
"A road is walked by oneself; a bearing is chosen by oneself. A man's life should be held in his own hands. A man should live in the light — not in hatred. Once you choose hatred, the depths of your heart will lose the light forever. In the Rong house you are the one person lit by that light; I only hope you will spare me a fuller share of your light's surplus." Coming to these words, Rong Sheng, of all things, had tears glinting in his eyes. "Keep your good nature; be a man of sincerity. Keep it forever — do not fall as I have fallen, into a plaything of the dark. You do not understand: only a man who has walked in the black of night knows how precious the light is."

> “可是我无法逃避。”阿初十分矛盾。
"But I cannot escape it," said A-Chu, deeply torn.

> “我跟你在一起，生活了二十年，从未看见过你如此惶恐惊骇。我不知道在你身上发生了什么可怕得事情，但我确定，你很痛苦。如果现实残酷到让你不能逃避，那就设法远遁吧。”荣升说。
"Twenty years I have lived at your side, and never once have I seen you so terrified, so aghast. I do not know what dreadful thing has befallen you, but of this I am sure: you are in great pain. If reality has grown too cruel for you to escape, then find some way to flee it, far off," said Rong Sheng.

> “少爷？您赶我走？”
"Young master? You are driving me away?"

> “对。你应该走，走得越远越好。不要顾忌，不要犹豫，不要回头。”荣升说完后，昂头背手而去。
"Yes. You should go — the farther the better. Have no scruples, no hesitation; do not look back." And having said it, Rong Sheng lifted his head, clasped his hands behind his back, and walked away.

> 阿初此时此刻忽然冷静了许多，他强迫自己在理性的屏障下，展开感性的思考。
In that moment A-Chu grew suddenly far calmer, and forced himself, behind the barrier of reason, to open out a reasoning of the heart.

> 自己可以远走高飞，丛惠在法国等着自己。
He could take wing and fly far away; Cong Hui was waiting for him in France.

> 四太太呢？她的复仇计划将毁于一旦。
And the Fourth Madam? Her plan of revenge would be undone in a single day.

> “恩情”和“爱情”这两种情感在阿初的脑海里、内心深处进行了一场厮杀、一场殊死搏斗。
"Gratitude" and "love" — these two feelings waged, in A-Chu's mind and in the depths of his heart, a grappling, a fight to the death.

> 他要肃清体内潜在的血腥欲望，从“爱”的精神出发，考虑到人性的尊严。不可以去“杀人”，杀人的行径无疑是卑鄙和无耻的，无论出于何种借口。
He meant to purge the latent lust for blood within him, to set out from the spirit of "love," to keep in view the dignity of human nature. He must not "kill"; the act of killing was, beyond doubt, base and shameless, whatever pretext might be offered for it.

> 四太太用自己忧伤的一生、凄艳的一生来酝酿对“仇恨”的反击。她用亲情和眼泪要求自己回馈，回馈的代价是牺牲自己的宁静祥和的一生，去选择“死亡”和“动乱”，自己一旦背负起“报仇雪恨”、“光复家业”的重任，自己的人生就不可避免的发生一场混乱的“裂变”，一步一步走向泥沼，不能自拔。
The Fourth Madam had brewed her counterstroke against "hatred" out of her whole sorrowful, mournfully beautiful life. With the bond of kin and with her tears she demanded that he give something back — and the price of giving back was to sacrifice his own life of quiet and peace, to choose "death" and "turmoil." Once he shouldered the heavy charge of "avenging the wrong and wiping out the shame," of "restoring the family's fortunes," his life would inevitably undergo a chaotic "fission," step by step sinking into the mire, past all self-extrication.

> 少爷说得对，走吧，走得越远越好。不要顾忌，不要犹豫，不要回头。
The young master was right. Go — the farther the better. No scruples, no hesitation; do not look back.

> 自己有权利选择自己要走的路。
He had the right to choose the road he would walk.

> 情势危急，势如山倒。
The situation was dire, bearing down like a toppling mountain.

> 在阿初回国以前，阿初对四太太来讲是杨氏家族新生的希望，是复仇的火种。但是，现在她不得不承认，她失败了。逐渐浓烈的仇恨情绪，愈益增强了她对阿初的失望和怨气，命运对自己太过苛酷无情。她快要崩溃了。
Before A-Chu came home, he had been, for the Fourth Madam, the newborn hope of the Yang family, the tinder of revenge. But now she could not but admit it: she had failed. Her steadily thickening hatred only deepened her disappointment and rancor toward A-Chu; fate had been too harsh, too pitiless with her. She was on the verge of breaking apart.

> 四太太两眼无助地看着案上的琵琶，猛地将乐器扫荡至尘埃。
The Fourth Madam looked helplessly at the pipa on the table, then with a sudden sweep sent the instrument crashing to the dust.

> “小姐。”嬷嬷惊呼。
"Miss," the old nurse cried out in alarm.

> “我失败了。”四太太喃喃自语。“他急于想摆脱我，是吧？他太有头脑，这一点他像极了我们的父亲。他又太过阴柔有度，这一点，像极了他的母亲。也许是我们，我们编造的故事粗糙了一点，破绽太多，使他无法相信。”
"I have failed," the Fourth Madam murmured to herself. "He is anxious to be rid of me, is he not? He has too good a head on him — in that he is the very image of our father. And he is too soft, too measured — in that he is the very image of his mother. Perhaps it is we — perhaps the story we made up was a little too rough, with too many seams showing, so that he could not believe it."

> “不，小姐。据老奴看来，他对您深信不疑。”嬷嬷说。
"No, Miss. As this old servant sees it, he believes you without the least doubt," the nurse said.

> “我想用二十年的'恩情'来束缚住他的灵魂，利用他的智慧，去掐断那恶魔的咽喉。我刻意对前尘往事滥加篡改，希望他能亲手杀死那个贱人，以消我心头之恨！可是，可是我盲目的封闭了他仇恨的心窗，没有在他心灵深处种下邪恶的种子。这是我失败的关键原因。”
"I meant to bind up his soul with twenty years of 'kindness,' to use his cleverness to throttle that fiend's very throat. I deliberately doctored and twisted the events of the past, hoping he would kill that base creature with his own hands and so dissolve the hatred in my breast! But — but I blindly sealed shut the window of his hatred, and planted no evil seed in the depths of his soul. That is the crux of why I failed."

> “小姐，那是因为您太善良了。”
"Miss, that is because you are too kind."

> “我没有想到培植'恩情'是如此的有害！”
"I never thought that cultivating 'kindness' could do such harm!"

> “小姐，大少爷要是真的不肯做，我们去找二少爷。”
"Miss, if the eldest young master truly will not do it, we can go to the second young master."

> “一个自己亲手扶持了二十年的人，都不肯为我所用。我还能指望另一个在仇人家里养了二十年的孩子吗？”
"A man I raised up with my own hands for twenty years will not be used by me. Can I still count on the other child, the one reared twenty years in an enemy's house?"

> “母亲。”内室的门被推开了，荣初走了进来。他虽然对生母没有什么深厚的情感，但是，他知道，这个历尽沧桑的女人，受尽了人世的折磨。他是她的儿子，为什么，她不肯让自己来完成家族复仇的大业呢？
"Mother." The door of the inner room was pushed open, and Rong Chu came in. Though he felt no deep affection for the mother who bore him, he knew that this woman, who had passed through every vicissitude, had borne the full torment of the world. He was her son — why, then, would she not let him be the one to accomplish the great work of the family's revenge?

> “母亲，我们为什么不能自己做？而偏要假手于人呢？”
"Mother, why can we not do it ourselves? Why must we, of all things, borrow another man's hand?"

> “我要肯自己做，二十年前就做了。”
"Had I been willing to do it myself, I would have done it twenty years ago."

> “为什么？”
"Why?"

> “杨家的事情，一定要杨家的血脉来完成。他不能拒绝我，他没有资格拒绝我。如果我不能驾驭他，不能用亲情来羁绊他，那我就用自己的血去挽留他……”
"The affairs of the Yang house must be accomplished by Yang blood. He cannot refuse me; he has no standing to refuse me. If I cannot master him, cannot bind him with the ties of kin, then I will use my own blood to hold him back…"

> 阿初夜来做了一个很恐怖的梦，他梦见自己跌入了一个喷毒噬血的蜘蛛巢穴。蜘蛛的脸不断变换着方向和诡异的笑容，那张脸的模样：有来诊室看过病的“杨羽柏”；有站在佛堂里的黑衣女人；有抱着琵琶的四太太；甚至还有自己。脸模不断的伸缩，仿佛黏性十足的泥浆，白白的、浓浓的，流化开去，又变成血。
That night A-Chu had a terrifying dream. He dreamed he had fallen into a spider's nest that spat venom and gorged on blood. The spider's face kept shifting its bearing and its uncanny smile; the shapes that face took were these: the "Yang Yubo" who had come to his consulting room as a patient; the woman in black who had stood in the Buddha hall; the Fourth Madam with her pipa in her arms; and, of all faces, his own. The mask stretched and shrank without cease, like a sticky slurry of mud, white and thick, melting and flowing outward — and then turning to blood.

> 死亡的阴影在心头纠缠，始终萦绕不去。
The shadow of death tangled about his heart and would not, for all his trying, be shaken loose.

> 不，不行。
No. It would not do.

> 阿初决定迅速离开这里，不能在此泥足深陷。
A-Chu resolved to leave this place at once; he could not let his feet sink deeper into the mire.

> 他很快联络到了夏跃春，并决定出国前，先搬到夏家去住一段时间。他几近匆忙得到政府的外务部办理出国手续，同时，又给阿丛惠寄去了一封情意绵绵的书信。阿初已经想好了，无论阿丛惠对自己的态度如何，自己也要当面去给她解释清楚。
He quickly got in touch with Xia Yuechun, and resolved that before going abroad he would first move to the Xia house and stay a while. In something close to haste he saw to the exit formalities at the government's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and at the same time sent off to Cong Hui a letter brimming with tenderness. A-Chu had made up his mind: whatever Cong Hui's attitude toward him might be, he would go to her in person and explain himself plainly.

> 荣升知道阿初决定出国，他没有询问确实的原因，他只是给予阿初支持和鼓励，他没有改变自己的生活规律，依然是闲散、悠然，朝看落花，晚对流星。
Rong Sheng knew A-Chu had resolved to go abroad. He did not ask the true reason; he only gave A-Chu his support and encouragement. He altered nothing of his own way of living, idle and unhurried as ever — watching the fallen blossoms in the morning, facing the shooting stars at night.

> 事情办的异常顺利，四太太自始至终没再找过阿初谈话，意外的宁静，让阿初深深地感到不安。
Everything went uncommonly smoothly. From first to last the Fourth Madam never again sought A-Chu out to speak with him; the unexpected quiet left A-Chu deeply uneasy.

> 大约过了两个多星期，夏日的清风开始偷袭晚春的燥热，阿初的出国签证已经下发了，他住在夏家也有将近半个月。半个月来，阿初很嗜睡，很少讲话，很忧郁。令他没有想到的是，在一个晴朗的下午，他在夏家与仇人的女儿、自己的堂妹杨思桐不期而遇了。
Somewhat more than two weeks passed. The cool breeze of summer had begun to steal in upon the parched heat of late spring; A-Chu's exit visa had come through, and he had been living at the Xia house for nearly half a month. For that half-month A-Chu had slept a great deal, said very little, and been very low in spirits. What he had not foreseen was that, on one clear afternoon, at the Xia house he should come face to face, all unlooked-for, with the enemy's daughter — his own cousin, Yang Sitong.

> 杨思桐和夏跃春是通过汤家兄妹认识的。
Yang Sitong and Xia Yuechun had come to know each other through the Tang brother and sister.

> 汤家和夏家是世交，汤少礼和夏跃春是少年同窗，两家关系密切，常有往来。夏跃春年轻有为，有形有款，又是一个留过洋、镀过金的钻石王老五，回国后，很受贵族小姐们的青睐。
The Tang and Xia families were friends of long standing, generation upon generation; Tang Shaoli and Xia Yuechun had been schoolmates in their youth, and the two households were close, forever coming and going between them. Xia Yuechun was young and able, well-favored and well-turned-out, a diamond of a bachelor gilded by his years overseas; since his return he had been much sought after by the daughters of noble families.

> 杨思桐是在汤家举办的舞会上认识夏跃春的，夏跃春对她颇有好感，大家言语投机，一来二去，杨思桐也成了夏家的常客。
Yang Sitong had met Xia Yuechun at a ball given by the Tang family. Xia Yuechun rather took to her; their talk ran easily together, and with one thing leading to another Yang Sitong too became a frequent guest at the Xia house.

> 当阿初在夏家花园里与他们邂逅时，彼此的眼神里都充满了惊讶。
When A-Chu met them by chance in the Xia family garden, the eyes of each were filled with astonishment.

> “看啊，这是谁？”汤少笑着说：“我们英勇无敌的现代骑士！啊！无可挑剔的英俊剑客！刷！刷！”汤少模仿着古代骑士舞剑的姿势。“你心爱的女人呢？哦，小可怜，你是不是被荣家的小妞给甩了？”
"Look — who have we here?" said Young Tang with a laugh. "Our dauntless, unconquerable modern knight! Ah! The flawless, handsome swordsman! Swish! Swish!" Young Tang mimed the pose of an old-time knight flourishing his blade. "And where is your beloved? Oh, you poor little thing — have you been thrown over by that Rong girl?"

> “您还活着？您还没有在女人们的唾骂声中淹死吗？真是奇迹。”阿初彬彬有礼地回应。
"You are still alive? You have not yet drowned in the sound of women cursing you? A true miracle," A-Chu returned, with impeccable courtesy.

> “初先生，您说错了。不是女人们的唾骂，而是女人们的唾液。”汤少油滑的言语中透着春色。
"Mr. Chu, you have it wrong. Not the women's curses — the women's saliva," said Young Tang, a lewd tint showing through his oily words.

> “我为爱过你的女人们感到悲哀。”阿初说。“您家里一定积攒了很多'爱'的墓碑。”
"I grieve for the women who have loved you," said A-Chu. "Your house must have quite a hoard of tombstones to 'love.'"

> “恰恰相反。我家里积攒了无数'爱'的回忆。”
"Quite the contrary. My house has a hoard of countless memories of 'love.'"

> “残缺的？”
"Broken ones?"

> “对！美妙的。得不到的往往是最好的。”
"Just so! Exquisite ones. What one cannot have is often the best."

> “你不怕作孽太多，有一天因为您的'滥爱'要了自己的性命？”
"Are you not afraid that, sinning so much, one day your 'promiscuous loving' will cost you your very life?"

> “你这句话说的实在。性命，性命，有性才有命呢。”汤少放肆地大笑起来。
"Now there you speak truly. Life, life — there is only a 'life' where first there is a 'lust' for it." Young Tang burst into unbridled laughter.

> “您这样点化评析中国文字，我真是无话可说。”
"When you gloss and parse the Chinese language after that fashion, I am truly left with nothing to say."

> “我就喜欢你这种人。你知道吗？你尺竞寸进般的垂死挣扎，令我十分开心。”汤少笑嘻嘻地说。“听说，荣家大少爷把和家小妖精当成一双破袜子给扔了？真是解恨啊。改天我和他见了面，一准谢谢他。”
"I do like your sort. Do you know, the way you struggle for every inch as you go down dying gives me no end of delight," said Young Tang, grinning from ear to ear. "I hear the Rong family's eldest young master threw over that little He-family vixen like a pair of worn-out socks? What a satisfying thing. Next time I meet him I shall be sure to thank him."

> “您真是无耻到了极点，别人的痛苦也可以当作自己开心的作料。”
"You are shameless to the very last degree — you can take another's suffering as a seasoning for your own delight."

> 毫无预见的相会，使大家都有了即兴突发的攻击性语言和充满杀伤力的反攻击。夏跃春对此十分意外。“原来你们认识？”
The wholly unforeseen encounter set them all to improvising sudden, aggressive sallies and cuttingly lethal ripostes. Xia Yuechun was quite taken aback by it. "So you already know one another?"

> “这一位应该是熟人了。”杨思桐语气骄横地说：“我们上次见过面，在我的家里。”
"This gentleman ought to count as an old acquaintance," said Yang Sitong, her tone haughty. "We met once before — at my home."

> 不知为什么，阿初感到杨思桐的话特别刺耳，他故意重复了一句：“对，在家里！您父亲的身体怎么样？他曾经去我的诊室看过病，但是，他并没有依约复诊。”
For some reason A-Chu found her words peculiarly grating, and he deliberately echoed them: "Yes — at your home! And how is your father's health? He once came to my consulting room as a patient, but he never kept his appointment to return."

> “是吗？”杨思桐认为阿初在跟自己套近乎。“我可从来没有听他提起过您。我父亲有私人医生，是德国大夫。”杨思桐骄傲地微笑。她对阿初视而不见，反而充满了热情对夏跃春说：“你不知道，我的父亲因袭了太多的传统观念，他生怕一不留神就丢掉了传统，总是活在死气沉沉的空间里，封闭自己的思想，完全不理解我们年轻人的世界，他认为我们太过肤浅和张狂。”
"Is that so?" Yang Sitong took it that A-Chu was trying to worm his way into her good graces. "I have certainly never heard him mention you. My father has his own private physician — a German doctor." She smiled proudly. Looking straight through A-Chu, she turned instead, all warmth, to Xia Yuechun: "You have no idea — my father clings to far too many of the old notions. He lives in dread of losing the traditions the moment he grows careless, forever shut up in some airless, lifeless space, sealing off his own mind; he does not understand our young people's world at all, and thinks us far too shallow and unbridled."

> “那是因为令尊的自我保护意识太强！一个思想意识曾经洋化过的人，要想化装成一个学识渊博又古板的商人，的确很难。他生怕被人一眼识破，他是一个黄皮白心的'冒牌货'。”阿初冷冰冰地插言。
"That is because your honored father's instinct for self-protection is too strong! For a man whose thinking was once Westernized, to disguise himself as a learned yet hidebound merchant is indeed no easy thing. He lives in terror of being seen through at a glance — a yellow-skinned, white-hearted counterfeit," A-Chu put in icily.

> “你这个人真无耻，你怎么可以出言侮辱一个高尚的人，而且，还是当着他女儿的面。我真的不知道你的企图何在？”杨思桐的脸色由于过度气愤，而显得血液喷张。
"You are a truly shameless creature — how dare you utter insults against a man of nobility, and before his own daughter's face at that? I really cannot tell what you are after." Yang Sitong's face, in her excess of fury, looked flushed with a rush of blood.

> “您说企图，当然是想剥去这世界上一切伪善的包装，以正义的名义，施行暴力的反抗。”
"Since you ask what I am after — why, to strip away every hypocritical wrapping in this world, and in the name of justice to carry out a violent resistance."

> 汤少哈哈大笑起来。
Young Tang roared with laughter.

> “精辟，精辟。”汤少礼说。“初先生完全是一个另类，因为他敢于公开向道德和法律挑战。”
"Incisive, incisive," said Tang Shaoli. "Mr. Chu is an oddity through and through — for he dares to challenge both morality and the law out in the open."

> 杨思桐在汤少的狂笑声中，冷却了激动的情绪，她轻蔑地说：“原来初先生根本不懂法律，我跟一个还没有开化过的野蛮人较什么真？”
Amid Young Tang's wild laughter Yang Sitong cooled her agitation, and said with contempt: "So Mr. Chu understands nothing whatever of the law. Why should I take in earnest a barbarian who has not yet been civilized?"

> “法律意味着维持公平和秩序，不过，公平、秩序有时候显得苍白无力，特别是面对强权的时候。杨小姐，你为什么不反思一下，你自己所享受的、所积累的巨大财富，是否来自你自己的合法劳动呢？”阿初说。
"The law means the keeping of fairness and order — but fairness and order sometimes prove pale and powerless things, above all in the face of naked might. Miss Yang, why not reflect a moment on whether the vast wealth you enjoy and have piled up came from your own lawful labor?" said A-Chu.

> “这个论调很危险，初先生，您像一个共产主义者。”夏跃春微笑着说。
"That is a dangerous line of argument, Mr. Chu; you sound like a Communist," said Xia Yuechun with a smile.

> 汤少棋似乎抓住了阿初的一个把柄，开始帮杨思桐进行反攻。“现在有些人把共产主义挂在嘴边上，以为很时髦。但是，实际行动起来，又很盲目，总是自以为是。胸中也没有什么改善社会的宏图，只是逞一时口舌之快罢了。”
Tang Shaoqi seemed to have caught A-Chu out by a handle, and set about aiding Yang Sitong's counterattack. "There are people nowadays who keep communism forever on the tip of the tongue, thinking it very much the fashion. But when it comes to acting on it, they are blind as bats and forever sure of themselves. They carry no grand design for bettering society in their breasts; they merely indulge a moment's pleasure of the tongue."

> “法国巴黎的大革命也是逞一时口舌之快吗？苏联的革命难道不值得借鉴吗？”
"Was the great Revolution of Paris, in France, too a mere indulgence of the tongue's momentary pleasure? Is the revolution of the Soviet Union really not worth taking a lesson from?"

> “如果路易十六不迷恋他的宫廷舞蹈，法国大革命是完全可以避免的。”
"If Louis the Sixteenth had not been so besotted with his court dances, the French Revolution could have been avoided altogether."

> “如何避免？您幼稚的言谈，使我们的谈话无法继续。”
"Avoided how? Your childish talk makes it impossible for our conversation to go on."

> “您指责我一无可取？”汤少棋怪叫起来。
"You charge that there is nothing whatever to be said for me?" Tang Shaoqi let out a strange yelp.

> “我不否认。”
"I will not deny it."

> “您真虚伪！听说你离开荣家了，初先生。您真是一个忘恩负义的人。”
"What a hypocrite you are! I hear you have left the Rong house, Mr. Chu. You really are a man who forgets a kindness and betrays his benefactor."

> “一个欲求苟活的人。”阿初替她补充了一句。
"A man who wishes only to live on however he can," A-Chu supplied for her.

> 汤少大惊小怪地惊呼：“了不得！初先生和舍妹的论战表情，简直就是一幅绝妙的油画啊，题目就叫：妥协？还是对抗？”
Young Tang exclaimed with great to-do: "Astonishing! The very look of this debate between Mr. Chu and my sister here is nothing less than a superb oil painting — and its title would be: Compromise? Or Confrontation?"

> 夏跃春笑着说：“你就不要再煽阴风，点鬼火了。再争执下去，不是相映成趣，倒成了两败俱伤。”
Xia Yuechun said with a smile: "Do stop fanning cold winds and lighting goblin-fires. If the quarrel goes on any further, it will not be a charming interplay but a case of both sides coming off the worse."

> 阿初和汤少他们在夏跃春善意的调解下，暂息硝烟。但是，杨思桐对阿初的反感却深植于心。
Under Xia Yuechun's well-meant mediation, A-Chu and Young Tang and the rest let the gunsmoke settle for the moment. But Yang Sitong's aversion to A-Chu had struck deep root in her heart.

> 晚上，汤少他们留在夏家吃晚饭，阿初借口要回荣家去辞行，有礼貌地离开了夏家。
That evening Young Tang and the others stayed to dine at the Xia house, while A-Chu, on the pretext of returning to the Rong house to take his leave, politely withdrew.

> 阿初在回荣家的路上，心里一直在盘算，如何找一个适当的时机和四太太再谈一次话，他希望能够找到一条“光明”的途径来伸张正义，而不是利用“阴谋”来制造另一个“悲剧”。不过，阿初知道，愿望始终是愿望，现在他和四太太所面对的是“分离”。离别是最令人伤心和忧郁的，他无法用语言和行动去抹平四太太心灵上的创伤，他只有祈求她的原谅。
On the road back to the Rong house A-Chu turned it over and over in his mind — how to find a fitting moment to speak with the Fourth Madam once more. He hoped he might find a "bright" road by which to see justice done, rather than use a "conspiracy" to make yet another "tragedy." Yet A-Chu knew that a wish remained only a wish; what he and the Fourth Madam faced now was "parting." A leave-taking is the most grievous and melancholy of things; he could not, by any word or deed, smooth away the wound in the Fourth Madam's heart, and could only pray for her forgiveness.

> 荣府“梨云阁”的小客厅里，笑语喧哗。大太太、三太太、四太太和荣升正在“砌长城”，丽水和蝉儿陪着大太太看牌，红儿打起帘子，让阿初进来得瞬间，本来热气腾腾的牌局，顿时变得鸦雀无声。
In the little parlor of the Rong mansion's Liyun Pavilion, laughter and talk rang loud. The First Madam, the Third Madam, the Fourth Madam, and Rong Sheng were "building the Great Wall"; Lishui and Chan'er kept the First Madam company at her tiles, and the moment Hong'er lifted the curtain to let A-Chu in, the game, till then all warmth and bustle, fell at once dead silent.

> “哟，我还以为是谁呢？原来是初先生回来了？”三太太挖苦地笑着说。“听说初先生要出国了？翅膀硬了，可以远走高飞了。”
"Oh! And here I wondered who it might be — so it is Mr. Chu come back?" said the Third Madam, smiling barbed. "I hear Mr. Chu is going abroad? His wings have hardened, then; he can take flight and go far away."

> 阿初并不在意三太太的话，他只是关切地看着四太太，四太太的脸明显衰老了。大太太不说话，一门心思地和丽水研究牌局。
A-Chu paid the Third Madam's words no mind; he only looked with concern at the Fourth Madam, whose face had plainly aged. The First Madam said nothing, her whole attention bent with Lishui on the state of the game.

> “打算到哪里去呢？”四太太问。
"And where do you mean to go?" the Fourth Madam asked.

> “去巴黎。”阿初小心翼翼地回着话。
"To Paris," A-Chu answered, picking his way with care.

> “以后还回来吗？”
"Will you come back, after?"

> “当然。”阿初回答地很勉强，连自己也觉得对不起四太太，又补了一句。“我会回来看您的。”
"Of course." The answer came out of A-Chu with difficulty; feeling, even to himself, that he had wronged the Fourth Madam, he added: "I will come back to see you."

> “不用了。”四太太阴阴地笑笑。“我是一个失魂落魄的病人，你却不是一个有割股之心的医生。你既然看不好我的病，就不用再回头了。”四太太优雅地抬起头，对三太太说：“看起来，养儿养女是不如积攒真金白银的，将来，我也只能靠漫长的回忆来排遣忧虑和释放我一生的悲哀了。”
"There is no need." The Fourth Madam gave a shadowed little smile. "I am a sick woman with her soul scattered and her wits astray, and you are no doctor with the heart to cut the flesh from his own thigh. Since you cannot cure my illness, there is no need for you to turn back at all." She raised her head with grace and said to the Third Madam: "It seems that raising sons and daughters is worth less than hoarding good gold and hard silver. In the days to come, I shall have nothing but long memories to dispel my cares and let out the sorrow of a whole life."

> 三太太得意忘形地笑。
The Third Madam laughed, beside herself with triumph.

> 阿初低着头，
A-Chu bowed his head —

> 特殊环境下孕育出的真挚“亲情”是让人很难割舍的。
for the true and sincere "kinship" nurtured under such extraordinary circumstances is a thing very hard to cast away.

> “我知道，与其粗暴地干涉你的生活，不如放你远行。如果，你能快乐，你就走吧。到时候，我去送你。送你振翼高飞！”四太太幽掩美色，凄凉动人。
"I know that rather than roughly meddle in your life, it is better to let you journey far. If it will make you happy, then go. When the time comes, I will see you off. See you off — beating your wings and soaring high!" The Fourth Madam veiled her beauty in shadow, forlorn and moving.

> 大太太的心里有些替四太太酸痛，冷着脸对阿初说：“你要走，我们也不拦着你。可是你不声不响从家里搬出去住，到底是什么意思呢？难道我们荣家薄待了初先生？四太太现在病得不轻，你倒好，说走就走。做人呢，第一要讲良心，第二要有孝心。人心不可太狠，人情不能做绝。”
Something in the First Madam's heart ached on the Fourth Madam's behalf, and she said to A-Chu with a cold face: "If you wish to go, we will not hold you back. But to move out of the house without a word to anyone — what, in the end, is that supposed to mean? Has our Rong family treated Mr. Chu shabbily? The Fourth Madam is gravely ill just now, and you — you say you will go and off you go. In being a person, the first thing is to have a conscience, and the second is to have a heart of filial care. One's heart must not be too hard; one must not press human feeling to the last extreme."

> 阿初未敢答话，他知道，自己现在荣家人的眼里，无疑是一个忘恩负义的人。丽水斜着眼睛看他，心里骂他是小人。
A-Chu did not dare answer. He knew that in the eyes of the Rong household he was now, beyond doubt, a man who forgot kindness and betrayed his benefactors. Lishui watched him out of the corner of her eye, cursing him inwardly for a petty wretch.

> 荣升不想让阿初出国的事在家里掀起轩然大波，于是，淡淡地说：“没什么事，你就去吧，改天我叫阿福给你送些东西过去。”
Rong Sheng had no wish for A-Chu's going abroad to raise a great storm in the house, and so said lightly: "There's nothing the matter — off you go. One of these days I'll have A-Fu bring some things over to you."

> “不用了。”阿初说。“我什么也不缺。”
"There is no need," said A-Chu. "I want for nothing."

> “什么也不缺？”丽水插话了。“缺点责任感。姑妈你是不知道。”丽水凑近大太太说：“初先生在英国的时候，想怎么样就怎么样。表弟躺在床上发高烧，烧得快死了，他不仅不管不问，居然还要跟一个女人私奔！”
"Want for nothing?" Lishui broke in. "He wants for a sense of responsibility. Auntie, you don't know the half of it." Lishui leaned close to the First Madam and said: "When Mr. Chu was in England, he did exactly as he pleased. My cousin lay in bed burning with fever, near to death with it, and not only did he pay no heed, no care — he was actually about to run off with some woman!"

> “丽水！”荣升大声断喝。
"Lishui!" Rong Sheng barked out loud, cutting her off.

> 晚了，已经晚了。丽水张着的大嘴收不回来了。大太太眼光锐利地逼视过来，她板着脸，一字一顿地问：“她说的是不是真的？”
Too late — it was already too late. Lishui's wide-open mouth could not be drawn back. The First Madam's gaze bore down on him, keen as a blade; her face set hard, she asked, weighing each word: "Is what she says true?"

> 没有人回答。
No one answered.

> 大太太厉声地问：“是不是真的？”
The First Madam asked, her voice stern: "Is it true?"

> 依旧没有人回答，无法作答。大太太肚中雪亮了，每一个人的表情都已经作了最好的回答。大太太脸色铁青，她一步一步走近阿初。冷笑了一声：“初先生贵人多忘事吧？您忘了八年前，您出国的时候，跟我这个老婆子签过一张'为荣家服役十年'的文书吧？”
Still no one answered — there was no answering it. Within, the First Madam saw all as bright as snow; every face had already given the best answer of all. Her own face went iron-grey, and step by step she drew near to A-Chu. She gave a cold laugh: "A great personage has a short memory, does he not, Mr. Chu? You've forgotten, I suppose, that eight years ago, when you went abroad, you signed with this old woman a bond — 'to serve the Rong family for ten years'?"

> 阿初脸色苍白。他真的“忘记”了。
A-Chu's face went pale. He had truly "forgotten."

> 四太太的心底泛起了波澜，阿初走不成了。
In the depths of the Fourth Madam's heart a wave rose up: A-Chu would not be able to leave.

> 荣升大为震惊，问：“这是什么时候的事？我怎么不知道？”
Rong Sheng was greatly shocked, and asked: "When did this happen? How is it I never knew?"

> “你知道什么？我的傻儿子。”大太太说。“你的母亲如果没有些手段，怎么能支撑这么大的家业，怎么能应对上上下下这些'白眼狼'。我算是看透了，什么是'感恩戴德'，什么是'上楼抽梯'。初先生，您的运气很不好，遇见我这个做事精细的女人。我不防君子，但是防着小人。”
"What do you know, my foolish son?" said the First Madam. "If your mother had no arts of her own, how could she have held up so great an estate, how could she have managed all these 'white-eyed wolves,' high and low? I have seen clean through it — what 'gratitude and thanksgiving' comes to, and what 'pulling up the ladder once one is upstairs' comes to. Mr. Chu, your luck is very poor — you have met with a woman who does her business finely. I take no precautions against a gentleman; but against a small-minded knave I do."

> “大太太。”阿初恭敬地说。“我在国外已经服侍大少爷八年了。我并没有爽约，我会兑现承诺，但不是现在。请您理解。”
"First Madam," said A-Chu respectfully, "I have already served the eldest young master abroad for eight years. I have not broken faith; I will make good on my promise — only not now. I beg you to understand."

> “理解？你要我理解一个把我儿子的死活完全不放在心上的奴才？你要我理解一个把养育恩情弃之如粪土的不孝之子？对不起，我不可能理解。因为你犯了不可原谅的错！你要付出代价！”
"Understand? You would have me understand a servant who set my son's very life and death entirely at naught? You would have me understand an unfilial son who casts off the kindness that reared him like so much dung? I am sorry — I cannot possibly understand. For you have committed an unforgivable wrong! And you shall pay the price!"

> “大太太……”阿初要分辩。
"First Madam…" A-Chu made to defend himself.

> “阿初！”荣升厉声喝止了阿初。荣升知道母亲说一不二的脾气，这个时候需要时间来缓冲彼此的情绪，而不是继续争执。“出去！”荣升对阿初说。
"A-Chu!" Rong Sheng cut him off sharply. Rong Sheng knew his mother's temper — that what she said was law, brooking no second word — and that what was needed now was time to let both tempers cool, not more quarreling. "Get out!" Rong Sheng said to A-Chu.

> “阿初！”四太太拖住了他，满脸是泪。“对不起。我完全不知情。”
"A-Chu!" The Fourth Madam caught and held him back, her whole face wet with tears. "I am sorry. I knew nothing of this at all."

> “不关你事。”阿初安慰她，转身出去了。
"It is not your doing," A-Chu said, comforting her, and turned and went out.

> 大太太的怨气未平，四太太却从绝望中生出希望来……
The First Madam's rancor was not yet spent; but out of her despair the Fourth Madam found a hope springing up…

> 也许大太太会阻止阿初出国，也许阿初会留下来，只要留下来，复仇就有指望，死灰就会复燃。
Perhaps the First Madam would stop A-Chu from going abroad; perhaps A-Chu would stay. Only let him stay, and revenge would have its chance yet — the dead ashes would kindle and blaze again.

> 不知道从什么时候起，四太太对生活开始厌倦，她幻想死亡能给自己带来解脱的快感，她一度沉迷于死亡后的超升。
From some point she could not name, the Fourth Madam had begun to weary of life. She fancied that death might bring her the sweet ease of release, and for a time she was rapt in the thought of an ascent beyond death.

> 她对死亡的迷恋超越了对死亡的恐惧。
Her infatuation with death had outgrown her fear of it.

> 如果自己的生命陨落，可以换来阿初的“复仇”行动，她就会义无反顾地去死。她甚至祈求苍天可怜自己，给自己一次痛快的了断。
If the falling of her own life could buy A-Chu's act of "revenge," she would go to her death without a backward glance. She even prayed that Heaven might pity her and grant her one clean, decisive end.

> “畸形的复仇”心理，让四太太夜来难眠，她披上外套，沿着蜿蜒地幽径，向“墨菊斋”走去。
This "deformed revenge" that possessed her mind kept the Fourth Madam from sleep that night. She threw a coat over her shoulders and, along the winding, secluded path, made her way toward the Moju Studio.

> “墨菊斋”的灯依然亮着，四太太呆呆地伫立在黑夜底，遥望着一线光明。
The lamp of the Moju Studio still burned. The Fourth Madam stood blankly at the bottom of the black night, gazing from afar at that single thread of light.

> 她在祈求，这一线光明，一定要延续下去，永不可灭。
She was praying: that this one thread of light must go on and on, and never be put out.

> “墨菊斋”中，阿初正给荣升续茶，清新的茶香，翠绿的嫩叶，飘浮在精致的茶杯里，溢出清新的气息，透着满室的静谧。
Within the Moju Studio, A-Chu was refilling Rong Sheng's tea; the fresh scent of it, the tender kingfisher-green leaves floating in the exquisite cup, gave off a clean fragrance that let a stillness through the whole room.

> 宁静的夜晚，安静的书斋，朝夕相处了二十年的宾主，此刻都平静地享受着清茶所赐予的洗心养气。很难想像，今夜的话题就是“分别”二字，不过，他们二人的脸上都没有一丝即将分别的难舍情绪，相反，他们仿佛期待着彼此的人生帆船都能早日起航。
A tranquil night, a quiet study; host and man, who had lived side by side these twenty years, at this hour calmly enjoying the washing of the heart and the nourishing of the spirit that clear tea bestows. It was hard to imagine that tonight's subject was those two words, "parting"; and yet on the faces of the two of them there was not a trace of the reluctance of an imminent separation. On the contrary, it was as if each awaited the day when the sailing-boat of the other's life might, and soon, at last put out to sea.

> “我母亲心理负重太多。”荣升微微地咳嗽了一下。“她活得很痛苦。在我的记忆里，她很坚强、精明、能干。在这腐败的'妻妾成群'的大宅院里，她始终坚守住了她的阵营。为了维护自己在家庭里的地位和荣誉，她曾经亲手酿成了无可挽回的悲剧。她对你这样做，在我看来，也许并不过分。”
"My mother carries too heavy a burden in her mind." Rong Sheng gave a slight cough. "She lives in great pain. In my memory she was strong, shrewd, capable. In this corrupt great house of 'wives and concubines by the flock,' she has held her ground from first to last. To guard her place and her honor within the family, she once, with her own hands, brewed a tragedy past all mending. That she should act so toward you is, as I see it, perhaps not excessive."

> “我能理解。”阿初说。“我从来没有埋怨过大太太。直到现在，我还是很感激她。是我，无止尽的欲望，渴求苟活于乱世的心理，导致了今天大太太和四太太的不谅解。”
"I can understand it," said A-Chu. "I have never once borne the First Madam any grudge. To this very day I am still deeply grateful to her. It was I — my endless craving, my hankering to live on somehow in an age of chaos — that has brought about the want of understanding between the First Madam and the Fourth Madam today."

> “你什么时候走？”
"When do you leave?"

> “明天。明天早上我先去趟医院，处理一下私人文件。中午，夏先生替我饯行，下午四点钟我就得启程了。”
"Tomorrow. In the morning I will first go to the hospital and see to some personal papers. At noon Mr. Xia gives me a farewell dinner, and by four o'clock in the afternoon I must set out."

> “走得了无牵挂？”荣升问。
"You leave with nothing to tie you down?" Rong Sheng asked.

> “没办法。我想这一次，无论任何事情都无法阻挠我前进的步伐。”
"There is no help for it. This time, I think, nothing whatever can bar the forward step I take."

> “为了你的理想和自由？”
"For the sake of your ideals and your freedom?"

> “也为了四太太。”
"And for the Fourth Madam's sake as well."

> “我听不懂。”
"I don't follow you."

> “我想，我走了之后，她会想通一个道理，人应该为自己活，活得轻松一点，愉快一点。”
"I think that once I am gone, she will come to see one truth: that a person should live for herself — live a little more lightly, a little more gladly."

> “你有没有想过，这样的结局，对她很残忍？”
"Have you considered that such an ending is very cruel to her?"

> “希望我的'背叛'带给她'妥协'，我想，她会原谅我的。因为她善良。”
"I hope my 'betrayal' will bring her round to 'compromise.' She will forgive me, I think. Because she is kind."

> “你认为，我的母亲会轻易放过你吗？”荣升含蓄地笑着。
"Do you suppose my mother will let you off so easily?" Rong Sheng smiled, holding something back.

> “我和大太太签的文书，并没有第三人在场，没有公证人，也就缺乏了法律的依据。”
"The bond I signed with the First Madam had no third party present, no notary — and so it wants any basis in law."

> “白眼狼！”荣升笑骂了一句。“看来我母亲没有看错你，你太狡猾了。不，不仅仅是狡猾，是狡诈。是狡赖。”
"White-eyed wolf!" Rong Sheng cursed with a laugh. "It seems my mother did not misjudge you — you are too crafty by half. No, not merely crafty: cunning. A slippery welsher of debts."

> “少爷，一个人处在劣势，孤军奋战，他必须得有头脑。”
"Young master, a man at a disadvantage, fighting on alone, must have a head on his shoulders."

> “你知道吗？我以为自己在关键时刻，一定会帮上你的忙。所以，我叫蝉儿偷偷地把你的这张'卖身文书'给拿来了。”荣升从口袋里取出了那薄如蝉翼的纸。“其实，我很傻。原来这只不过是一张毫无法律效力的废纸。看来我做了件蠢事，而不是什么义举。”他把阿初的“卖身文书”伸到阿初面前，示意他点燃。
"Do you know, I thought that at the crucial moment I was sure to be of some help to you. So I had Chan'er secretly fetch me this 'bond of sale' of yours." Rong Sheng drew from his pocket the paper, thin as a cicada's wing. "The truth is, I was a great fool. It turns out this is nothing but a scrap of waste paper with no force in law at all. It seems I did a stupid thing, and no chivalrous deed." He held A-Chu's "bond of sale" out before him, motioning for him to set it alight.

> “我今天很感动。”阿初并没有去掏打火机。“我想留下来，做个纪念。”他把那张文书折起来。
"I am much moved today," said A-Chu, making no move to reach for a lighter. "I should like to keep it, as a memento." And he folded the bond up.

> 荣升莞尔一笑。“纪念什么？二十年来得勤苦？八年来得忍耐？”
Rong Sheng smiled a small smile. "A memento of what? Of twenty years' toil? Of eight years' forbearance?"

> “二十年来得友谊，八年来得心灵成长！”阿初说。“无论何时何地，荣家需要我回来，我一定回来。”
"Of twenty years' friendship, and eight years' growth of the soul!" said A-Chu. "Whenever, wherever, if the Rong family needs me back, I will surely come."

> “四太太那里呢？她最需要你的关怀。”
"And the Fourth Madam? She needs your care most of all."

> “如果，四太太平平安安，我会来接她离开这里，我希望她有一个全新的生活，生活在全新的世界。如果，她有什么不测，也许我会履行自己的使命！用一生去偿还她所付出的一切。”这一段耐人寻味地话，荣升并不理解。可是，在窗外伫立的四太太，眼眶湿润了，四太太想，原来错在自己，自己不能自私的毁掉他的前程，自己不能做出那种血腥的事来。因为，阿初是善良的。
"If the Fourth Madam is safe and well, I will come and take her away from here; I hope she may have a wholly new life, living in a wholly new world. And if some ill should befall her — then perhaps I will carry out my mission, and spend a whole life repaying all that she has given." This turn of speech, so full of hidden savor, Rong Sheng did not understand. But the Fourth Madam, standing outside the window, felt her eyes grow wet. It was she who had been wrong, she thought; she could not selfishly ruin his future, could not do a thing so steeped in blood. For A-Chu was good.

> 二十年前的决定也许错了。
The decision of twenty years ago had, perhaps, been wrong.

> 但是，自己已经不能回头了。
But she could no longer turn back.

> 她在微风里哭着，在花荫下哽咽着，在黑夜里行走着，无人知晓她的隐衷，她是一个脖子上套着绞索的舞蹈者。她跳不远了，舞不久了，她累了，她想睡了……
She wept in the light wind, choked back her sobs in the shade of the flowers, walked on in the black night; no one knew her secret heart. She was a dancer with a hangman's noose about her neck. She could not leap far now, could not dance much longer; she was weary, and she longed to sleep…

> 回到“红梨阁”的四太太，情绪渐渐有所好转，她幻想自己还是二十年前的小姐杨慕莲。而不是什么荣家四太太。
Back in the Hongli Pavilion, the Fourth Madam's spirits little by little took a turn for the better; she fancied herself still the young lady of twenty years ago, Yang Mulian — and no Fourth Madam of the Rong house at all.

> 失败的苦果，自己早就应该有所准备。沉重的代价，也许就来源于自己二十年前那一刹那错误的决定。自己玩了命的要将所谓血腥的复仇计划付诸于实践，造成今天自己无可挽回的人生悲剧。
For the bitter fruit of failure she ought long ago to have made herself ready. The heavy price, it may be, sprang from that one instant's wrong decision twenty years back. She had staked her very life on putting into practice this so-called bloody plan of revenge, and so had wrought the irretrievable tragedy of her own life today.

> 违心的“狠毒”在真诚的“善良”面前，丧失了强悍和勇气，不得不“丢盔卸甲”。
Her "ruthlessness," worn against the grain of her heart, lost all its ferocity and courage before his sincere "goodness," and could not but "throw down helmet and cast off armor."

> 唯一使自己欣慰的是，阿初的善良，没有辱没杨氏家族的门风。
The one thing that gave her comfort was that A-Chu's goodness had not disgraced the good name of the house of Yang.

> 他是自己的弟弟。
He was her younger brother.

> 他是父亲的儿子。
He was their father's son.

> 他不是自己复仇的工具。
He was not the instrument of her revenge.

> 自己没有权利毁掉他的幸福，前程，乃至生命。
She had no right to ruin his happiness, his future, or even his life.

> 她想着阿初明天下午即将扬帆远航了，明天上午，自己一定要到医院去送送他。她打开灯，她想连夜给阿初赶制一个香袋给阿初，让弟弟对姐姐留下一个永远美好回忆。
She thought how tomorrow afternoon A-Chu would spread his sails and voyage far, and how tomorrow morning she must go to the hospital to see him off. She turned on the lamp; she wanted to work the night through and make A-Chu a scented sachet in haste, so that her brother might keep of his sister a memory forever sweet.

> 当精神的羁绊一旦释去，四太太反而一身轻松，二十年来第一次感到身心的轻松和亲情的美好。
Once the fetters of her spirit were loosed, the Fourth Madam found herself, on the contrary, wholly at ease — feeling, for the first time in twenty years, the lightness of body and mind and the sweetness of kinship.

> 离罪恶远一点，靠幸福多一分。
A little farther from sin, a little nearer to happiness.

> 她把这句话绣在了香袋上，又把二十年前父亲从德国带回来得纯钢制的“护身符”放进了香袋，这是父亲的遗物，应该会保佑阿初，希望这个“护身符”能带给阿初永远幸福的人生。
These words she embroidered upon the sachet; and into it she placed as well the pure-steel "talisman" her father had brought back from Germany twenty years before. It was her father's keepsake, and it ought to watch over A-Chu; she hoped this "talisman" might bring A-Chu a life forever happy.

> 清晨，同济医院的走廊上，护士和病人都寥寥无几，阿初特意早来处理一些私人物件，譬如他的医学论文、病例检查报告、临床药理等书籍，小护士一直在给他帮忙，打捆文件，还有一些医生不停地过来询问一些由阿初曾经处置过的病例。阿初就这样忙忙碌碌的工作到早上十点钟左右，四太太和荣荣来了，她们是专程来送别阿初的。
Early morning, and in the corridors of the Tongji Hospital there were but a scattered few nurses and patients. A-Chu had come early on purpose to see to some personal things — his medical papers, case reports, books on clinical pharmacology and the like — and a young nurse had been helping him all the while, tying up the bundles of documents, while now and again a doctor would come by to ask about some case A-Chu had once handled. So he worked, busy and preoccupied, until about ten in the morning, when the Fourth Madam and Rongrong arrived; they had made the trip especially to see A-Chu off.

> 阿初看见她们挽着手进来，颇有些意外，四太太脸上荡漾出的女儿情态，让阿初摸不透她此时此刻的心态，她的脸上已经杜绝了悲哀，她的眼睛清纯，已经没有丝毫的沉渣泛滥了。一夜之间的改变，却使阿初有了不祥的预感。
A-Chu was rather taken aback to see them come in arm in arm. The girlish air that rippled across the Fourth Madam's face left him unable to fathom her state of mind at that moment; sorrow had been shut clean out of her face, and her eyes were clear and pure, with not the least dreg stirred up from the bottom. Yet this change wrought in a single night gave A-Chu a foreboding of ill.

> “您来了？”阿初谦恭地礼让着四太太和大小姐。
"You've come?" A-Chu deferred courteously to the Fourth Madam and the eldest young lady.

> “你还说？你不知道四太太最近病得很厉害吗？”荣荣跨进阿初的诊室，就教训阿初。“也只有这里，这里才是她滋心润肺的好去处。”
"And still you talk? Don't you know the Fourth Madam has been terribly ill of late?" Rongrong strode into A-Chu's consulting room and set about lecturing him. "This is the one place — this is the only place that nourishes her heart and soothes her breast."

> “喝茶吗？我去打瓶开水。”阿初说。
"Will you take tea? I'll go and fetch a flask of hot water," said A-Chu.

> “不用了，我们来就是看看你。”四太太脸上挂着笑容。“我有东西送给你，你好好收着，这是长辈的遗物。”四太太语带双关。“长辈的遗物”想必就是“父亲”的遗物。阿初规规矩矩地伸双手接了过来。香袋浸出了玫瑰花的香气，细细密密的针脚绣成一句话：离罪恶远一点，靠幸福多一分。阿初的眼眶湿润了，四太太用行动原谅了他的背叛。他从香袋里取出那十公分厚的钢制“护身符”，感到遥远的父爱向自己展开了宽容的怀抱。阿初止不住涕泪飘零……此刻，任何语言都不足以表达阿初对四太太的感恩之情，他第一次向四太太伸开双臂，紧紧地拥抱他的亲人。
"There is no need — we came only to look in on you." A smile hung on the Fourth Madam's face. "I have something to give you; keep it well. It is a keepsake of your elders." Her words carried a double meaning; the "keepsake of your elders" must surely be a keepsake of the "father." A-Chu received it properly in both hands. The sachet gave off the scent of roses; its fine, close-set stitches had embroidered a single line: A little farther from sin, a little nearer to happiness. A-Chu's eyes grew wet — by this act the Fourth Madam had forgiven his betrayal. From the sachet he drew out the ten-centimeter-thick steel "talisman," and felt a far-off father's love open wide its forgiving arms to him. A-Chu could not stop the tears from scattering down… In that moment no words could suffice to speak A-Chu's gratitude to the Fourth Madam, and for the first time he opened his arms to her and held his own kin close and tight.

> 荣荣也忍不住鼻酸，对四太太说：“好好地来看看他，干吗做出生离死别的样子来？好像这一辈子都见不着面了似的。”
Rongrong too could not keep her nose from stinging, and said to the Fourth Madam: "We came for a nice visit with him — why put on such an air of parting in life and death? As though you'd never set eyes on each other again as long as you lived."

> “你这张嘴真是晦气。”四太太说：“把一屋子的人都咒了，多不吉利。”
"What an ill-omened mouth you have," said the Fourth Madam. "Cursing a whole roomful of people — how unlucky."

> “您啊，您就不该带我来。”荣荣说：“我要是今天不送他，我就专程赶到巴黎去送他，到时候，我叫大太太和您给我报车马费，直接出国旅游。偏偏您今天把我拽来，这倒好，出国的借口也没了。”
"You — you never should have brought me along," said Rongrong. "If I weren't seeing him off today, I'd have made a special trip all the way to Paris to see him off, and then I'd have had the First Madam and you cover my travelling costs and gone straight abroad for a holiday. But you would drag me here today, and now — well, now there goes my excuse to go abroad."

> 阿初破涕为笑。说：“下次吧，下次我请你们一块去巴黎。”
A-Chu laughed through his tears. "Next time, then — next time I'll treat you both to a trip to Paris together."

> 这时，走廊上突然人声杂乱，有人在高喊：“初医生，有急症病人。初医生。”阿初对荣荣说：“替我照顾一下四太太，你们先坐一坐。”紧接着，阿初来到走廊上。“怎么回事？”
Just then a confusion of voices broke out in the corridor; someone was shouting: "Doctor Chu, there's an emergency case! Doctor Chu." A-Chu said to Rongrong: "Look after the Fourth Madam for me; you two sit a while." And then he came out into the corridor. "What's the matter?"

> 一幅担架上躺着一个女子，头发散乱，脸色苍白。一个纨绔少爷打扮的人正跟医护人员解释。“我完全懵了，这，这是一个不要命的。原本好说好商量的事，她就这样了……”阿初认出来人是汤少，他到担架前细看那女子，这女子不是别人，正是和雅淑小姐。
On a stretcher lay a young woman, her hair in disarray, her face pale. A man got up in the style of a wastrel young dandy was explaining to the medical staff. "I'm completely at a loss — this, this is someone who doesn't care whether she lives or dies. It was a thing we could perfectly well have talked over, and then she went and did this…" A-Chu recognized the man as Young Tang. Coming up to the stretcher, he looked closely at the young woman: she was none other than Miss He Yashu.

> “脉搏怎么样？”阿初询问护士。
"How is her pulse?" A-Chu asked the nurse.

> “脉搏很弱。”
"Her pulse is very weak."

> “血压？”
"Blood pressure?"

> “很低。”
"Very low."

> “有意识吗？”
"Is she conscious?"

> “有。”护士扶着担架往“急救处”去了。
"She is." The nurse steadied the stretcher and set off toward the "emergency room."

> “你对她到底做了什么？”阿初在质问汤少。
"What, exactly, did you do to her?" A-Chu demanded of Young Tang.

> 汤少心慌意乱地说：“我什么也没做。她是疯子，你知道吗？玩自杀。我只是……”
Young Tang said, flustered and shaken: "I did nothing at all. She's a madwoman, do you know that? Playing at suicide. I only—"

> “只是嘲笑？讥讽？挖苦？”
"Only mocked her? Sneered? Jeered?"

> “对。”汤少的跋扈气焰已经荡然无存了。“对不起。我简直，简直不知所措。我不想看见她这样。”
"Yes." Every trace of Young Tang's overbearing swagger was gone now. "I'm sorry. I simply — I simply don't know what to do. I didn't want to see her like this."

> “她已经这样了。”
"She is already like this."

> “对。可是，我以为她不会太认真。你知道吗？朝秦暮楚的女人应该不在乎男人们嘲讽的目光。”汤少一边走，一边说。
"Yes. But I thought she wouldn't take it so much to heart. Don't you see? A woman who's Qin by morning and Chu by nightfall oughtn't to care about men's mocking looks." Young Tang spoke as he walked.

> 阿初停下脚步，目光凶恶地瞪着汤少。汤少大为不满。“我没有，没有对她有任何侮辱性质的语言。我向耶稣起誓。你当时不在场，要知道受害者其实是我。”汤少挽起衣袖，露出受伤的胳膊。“这是我拼命阻止她干蠢事的代价！她像一只愤怒的海燕，我才是一个过路的天使，是我救了她。”
A-Chu stopped in his tracks and glared at Young Tang with a fierce eye. Young Tang was much put out. "I did not — I used no words of any insulting nature against her. I swear it by Jesus. You weren't there; you must understand that the real victim is me." Young Tang rolled up his sleeve to show an injured arm. "This is the price of my fighting for all I was worth to stop her doing something foolish! She was like a raging petrel, and I no more than a passing angel — it was I who saved her."

> “令我遗憾的事，她为什么不变成一只愤怒的海豹，那样就可以轻而易举的掐断你的脖子。”阿处继续往前走。
"What I regret is that she did not turn into a raging seal instead — then she might have wrung your neck with the greatest of ease." A-Chu walked on.

> 汤少耸了耸肩，他没有继续跟着阿初走，而是摊开双手，大声地说：“再次抱歉。”
Young Tang shrugged. He did not go on following A-Chu, but spread his hands and said loudly: "My apologies once again."

> 和雅淑平躺在担架上，她恨，自己为什么没有死掉。她还在继续吐清水，她恨这些无情的水为什么独独对她有情，沉下去，居然不死。居然被汤少像捞鱼一样捞上来，自己蠢啊，蠢到在阿初面前来丢人现眼！
He Yashu lay flat on the stretcher, hating herself — why had she not died? She was still bringing up clear water, and she hated that this heartless water should be, of all things, tender with her alone: she had sunk, and yet had not died. She had actually been fished out by Young Tang like a fish scooped from a pond. What a fool she was — fool enough to make a shameful spectacle of herself before A-Chu's very eyes!

> 阿初赶上几步，握住她的手，虽然阿初没有说话，雅淑心里却百感交集，万千只情虫从她的肚腹里爬出来，停留在心房搅动。
A-Chu quickened his step to catch up and took her hand; and though he said nothing, a hundred feelings crowded together in Yashu's heart — a thousand, ten thousand little worms of love crawled up out of her belly and settled, stirring, in the chambers of her heart.

> 突然，“轰”的一声巨响，浓烟滚滚笼罩在医院上空，只听得一片惨烈的叫声……恐惧的声音撕裂了晴空，天幕仿佛被人狠狠揭开，乌云塌下了来。满地是血……
Suddenly — a great "boom," and thick smoke came rolling up to shroud the sky above the hospital; there rose only a wave of harrowing screams… Sounds of terror ripped the clear heavens open, the very canopy of the sky seemed torn savagely apart, and black clouds came crashing down. Blood everywhere across the ground…

> 阿初和抬担架的人一起被震飞。
A-Chu and the stretcher-bearers were all thrown into the air by the blast.

> 一只带血的胳膊炸飞在雅淑的担架上，雅淑没有气力去阻挡，任由这只手臂安静地靠在自己身上。
A blood-smeared arm was blown onto Yashu's stretcher; Yashu had no strength to fend it off, and let the arm rest quietly against her body.

> 医院里一片鬼哭狼嚎。
Throughout the hospital rose a wailing of ghosts and a howling of wolves.

> 阿初不敢回头。
A-Chu dared not turn his head.

> 惨剧，是从他的诊室里传出来得，他怕，怕四太太和荣荣有什么不测。可是，他看见了荣荣的手臂。
The horror had come from his own consulting room; he was afraid — afraid that some ill had befallen the Fourth Madam and Rongrong. And then he saw Rongrong's arm.

> 阿初从残肢的衣袖上就看见了他最怕看见的悲剧。“荣荣？”阿初的嘴角在战栗。
From the sleeve upon the severed limb A-Chu saw the very tragedy he most dreaded to see. "Rongrong?" The corner of A-Chu's mouth trembled.

> “阿初！是我救了你！”汤少满面黑灰的站在阿初身后，阿初回过头去……满目凄惨！自己的诊室霎时间灰飞烟灭，断壁残垣间他仿佛看见四太太和荣荣满身是血地向自己走过来。
"A-Chu! It was I who saved you!" Young Tang stood behind him, his whole face black with ash. A-Chu turned to look… wretchedness filled his eyes! His consulting room had in an instant gone up in smoke and ash, and amid the broken walls and ruined rafters he seemed to see the Fourth Madam and Rongrong walking toward him, drenched in blood.

> “是我救了你。”汤少说：“你看，你的诊室，里面的人全完了，不是我，你也完了。你得罪谁了？青红帮？”
"It was I who saved you," said Young Tang. "Look — your consulting room, everyone inside is finished. If not for me, you'd be finished too. Whom have you offended? The Green and Red Gangs?"

> “是雅淑救了我，不是你。”阿初冷冷地说，他机械地向前走，雅淑想说话，却又说不出声。她突然伸手抓住了阿初的腿。
"It was Yashu who saved me, not you," A-Chu said coldly, and walked mechanically forward. Yashu wanted to speak but could not get the sound out. Suddenly she reached out and seized A-Chu by the leg.

> 阿初松开雅淑的手，向自己的诊室走去。
A-Chu loosed Yashu's hand and walked on toward his consulting room.

> 他的耳边响起此起彼伏的警笛声、他的眼前到处都是医院里医生、护士和病人忧伤的神情，他知道这是谁干的，一个二十年前就想要自己命的人，现在，终于来索命了。
In his ears the police sirens rose and fell one after another; before his eyes on every side were the grieving faces of the hospital's doctors, nurses, and patients. He knew whose work this was — a man who had wanted his life twenty years ago had come, at last, to claim it.

> 这不是什么偶发的“意外凶杀”。四太太在替自己“驱凶避祸”的同时，有人把她送到死亡的深渊。
This was no chance "accidental killing." At the very moment the Fourth Madam was "warding off evil and turning aside disaster" for him, someone had sent her down into the abyss of death.

> 恐怖的祸事还株连了荣荣。一个青春少女美丽的躯体此刻就残缺不全的躺在冰凉黑暗的泥土上……
The terrible calamity had dragged Rongrong down with it too. The lovely body of a girl in the bloom of youth now lay, broken and unwhole, upon the cold, dark earth…

> “你不能进去。”有人拦腰抱住阿初。“里面危险。”
"You can't go in." Someone caught A-Chu about the waist. "It's dangerous in there."

> “房梁会断裂的。”医院的医生和护工拼命地拦住阿初。
"The beams will give way." The hospital's doctors and orderlies held A-Chu back with all their might.

> 阿初不说话，往前冲。
A-Chu said nothing, and charged forward.

> “不行啊！初医生。”
"You mustn't, Doctor Chu!"

> “你疯了吗？那里是火场中心地带。小心。”
"Have you gone mad? That's the very heart of the fire. Careful!"

> “冷静点，冷静点。”汤少也死拽住阿初不放。
"Calm down, calm down." Young Tang, too, clung to A-Chu and would not let go.

> “啊！！！”被困住的阿初近野兽般的咆哮回荡在医院上空。阿初抱着汤少号啕大哭，汤少的酒色身子根本撑不住，两个人一块倒下去，滚在黑泥里。
"Aaah!!!" Held fast, A-Chu let out a near-bestial roar that echoed over the hospital. He clutched Young Tang and wept aloud in great, tearing sobs; Young Tang's body, wasted by wine and women, could not bear the weight at all, and the two of them toppled together and rolled in the black mud.

> 阿初暂时失去了知觉。
A-Chu lost consciousness for a time.

> 茫茫血色中，四太太、荣荣、小护士等人面色从容地向自己走过来，她们优雅、雍容、飘逸，她们从自己身边走过去，不跟自己说话，阿初看见她们白皙的毛孔里滴着伤心的泪和冤屈的血……小护士的脸上带着天真的稚气；荣荣像一朵刚刚绽放就在眼前凋谢的昙花；四太太在笑，也许她枯萎得太久了，死亡反而让她解脱了血色的阴霾。阿初的腿像灌满了铅，一动也不能动，就这样痴痴地站着，眼睁睁看着她们渐行渐远。
Amid a vast blur of blood, the Fourth Madam, Rongrong, the young nurse, and the others walked toward him with composed faces — graceful, stately, ethereal. They passed by his side without speaking to him, and A-Chu saw grieving tears and wronged blood dripping from their fair pores… On the young nurse's face was an innocent, childish simplicity; Rongrong was like an epiphyllum that had only just bloomed and, before his very eyes, was already withering; the Fourth Madam was smiling — perhaps she had withered too long, and death had at last freed her from the blood-red gloom. A-Chu's legs were as though filled with lead, unable to stir; and so he stood, dazed, watching helplessly as they drew farther and farther away.

> 倒在地上的阿初慢慢睁开了朦胧的泪眼，他在心底发下了血誓。“苍天在上，父母亡灵在上！姐姐幽冥路上！荣荣！我阿初对天发誓！不杀杨羽桦我杨慕初誓不为人！！”
Fallen to the ground, A-Chu slowly opened his blurred and tear-dimmed eyes, and swore in his heart a blood oath. "By Heaven above, by the departed souls of my father and mother! By my sister on the road to the shades! By Rongrong! I, A-Chu, swear before Heaven — if I do not kill Yang Yuhua, then I, Yang Muchu, vow that I am no man!!"

> 人生价值观在终决对垒的最后一瞬间，发生了质变。血腥占领了正义的舞台，眼泪淹没了宽容和善良，他要换一种活法了，他被逼到了悬崖深渊。没有路可逃，没有路可以选择，没有人可以救自己。他要自救，他要复仇，他要脱胎换骨的蜕变。杨家的新主人、金龙会的新帮主在血火中诞生了。
In the last instant of that final, decisive confrontation, his whole scale of values underwent a change of substance. Blood seized the stage from justice; tears drowned tolerance and goodness. He would take up another way of living now; he had been driven to the very brink of the abyss. There was no road by which to flee, no road left to choose, no one who could save him. He must save himself; he must have his revenge; he must be utterly remade, sloughing off the old flesh for the new. The new master of the house of Yang, the new chief of the Golden Dragon Society, was born amid blood and fire.

> 四太太没有预计到今日之死。也不知黄泉路上她是否如愿以偿？
The Fourth Madam had not reckoned on her death this day. Nor is it known whether, on the road to the Yellow Springs, she got at last her heart's desire.

> 杨慕初的认祖归宗，预告了一个“死亡”的秋天。
Yang Muchu's return to his ancestors and his name foretold an autumn of "death."
