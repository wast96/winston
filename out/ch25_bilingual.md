## H2 Chapter 25. To Step Backward Was, All Along, to Go Forward

> 春和医院，重症看护室的过道里沉沉寂寂的，再柔和的灯光投射到这又深又窄的走廊上，都回荡着阴森的气息。
The corridor of the intensive-care ward at the Chunhe Hospital lay deep in silence; however soft the light that fell across that long, narrow passage, it only set an eerie, deathly air ringing along the walls.

> 侦缉队的队员们，踩着幽暗的水泥地，裹挟着杀气，跟着李沁红大跨步地走来。他们藏在衣袂下的手枪，在阴冷的风底肆意招摇。
The men of the Detective Division came striding after Li Qinhong, their tread ringing on the dim concrete, a killing air wrapped about them. The pistols hidden beneath the folds of their coats swaggered brazenly in the cold, cheerless draught.

> 很快，他们来到了“杨慕次”的病床前，不过，“杨慕次”的病势好像并没有很大的改善，相反，夏跃春等医生、护士正借助医疗仪器，准备替他吸痰。
Before long they reached the sickbed of "Yang Muci." His condition seemed to have improved very little; on the contrary, Xia Yuechun and the other doctors and nurses were just then, with the aid of the medical apparatus, preparing to suction the phlegm from his throat.

> 李沁红和高磊凝神敛气地站在了医生的背后，夏跃春和护士们一律戴着白色口罩、穿着白色大褂、套着白色医用指套，全神贯注地工作，没提防，夏跃春退步时踩了李沁红的脚。
Li Qinhong and Gao Lei held their breath and stood behind the doctors. Xia Yuechun and the nurses were all masked in white, gowned in white, their fingers sheathed in white surgical cots, wholly absorbed in their work; and Xia Yuechun, stepping back without warning, trod on Li Qinhong's foot.

> “对不起。”夏跃春偶然一回头，看见了李沁红等人，他皱了皱眉头，很不欢迎的神态，埋怨说：“你们怎么进来得？这里是重症看护室，病人身体很虚弱，容易感染病菌……”
"I beg your pardon." Happening to glance round, Xia Yuechun caught sight of Li Qinhong and her party. He knitted his brows, his whole manner most unwelcoming, and complained, "How did you get in here? This is the intensive-care ward. The patient is very weak; he catches infection easily—"

> “我想，我来得时候，已经跟您打过招呼了。而且，我也很尊重您这位医学博士的意见，耐心地又等了二天。我已经让步了。”
"I believe I announced myself when I came in. And I have shown every respect for the opinion of a doctor of medicine such as yourself; I have waited patiently these two extra days. I have already given ground."

> “您搞错了。您不是对我让步，您的让步，跟我一样，同样是出于对病人的关爱。如果我没有弄错的话，杨先生，应该是您的下属，而不是您的犯人。”
"You have it wrong. You have not given ground to me. Your yielding, like mine, springs from the same care for the patient. And unless I am much mistaken, Mr. Yang is your subordinate, not your prisoner."

> “夏医生。”李沁红很难得地对夏跃春露出一丝微笑。“我们不会耽误您很多时间，我们只需要跟杨先生讲几句话……”
"Doctor Xia." Li Qinhong, most unusually, let a thread of a smile show at him. "We shall not take up much of your time. We need only exchange a few words with Mr. Yang—"

> “几句？”夏跃春咬住她的话。
"A few?" Xia Yuechun snapped at her words.

> “三句。”李沁红肯定地说。“就三句。”
"Three." Li Qinhong said firmly. "Just three."

> “然后呢？”
"And then?"

> “然后啊？”李沁红目不转睛地盯着夏跃春的眼睛。“看他的表现了。如果他的回答，令我满意，我立即就走。如果，他的回答，不能令我满意……”李沁红的目光恶毒地回荡在“杨慕次”的脸上，她在寻找谜底。尽管“杨慕次”呼吸很急促，脸色很难看，但是，李沁红仍然敏锐地感觉到，病人在有意无意之间，刻意回避她那咄咄逼人的凶光。她笑了，她认为，“杨慕次”自认“末日将临”，“困兽犹斗”。“那么，也许，我会替杨副官重新找一个适合他住的医院。”
"And then?" Li Qinhong fixed her eyes, unblinking, on Xia Yuechun's. "That depends on how he answers. If his answers satisfy me, I leave at once. If his answers do not satisfy me—" Her gaze prowled venomously over the face of "Yang Muci," searching it for the answer to her riddle. Though "Yang Muci" was breathing hard and his color was ghastly, Li Qinhong nonetheless sensed, with all her sharpness, that the patient—whether by design or not—was deliberately avoiding the aggressive, murderous glint in her eye. She smiled. She took it that "Yang Muci," reckoning his last day had come, was a cornered beast still fighting. "In that case, perhaps I shall find Adjutant Yang another hospital better suited to him."

> 此刻，重症看护室的电话铃声响了，整个看护室里的人都为之一震。
At that moment the telephone in the intensive-care ward began to ring, and everyone in the ward gave a start.

> “高队长，让他听电话。”这是命令。
"Captain Gao, let him take the call." It was an order.

> 高磊接起电话，简要说了几句：“我是。对。你听仔细了，他马上和你通话，”紧接着，高磊很不忍心地走近“慕次”，说：“兄弟，对不起啊。处座的意思……你也懂得，出了这么大的事，每个人都得过筛子。”
Gao Lei picked up the receiver and said a few brief words: "Speaking. Yes. Listen carefully—he'll speak with you in a moment." Then, most unwillingly, he went up close to "Muci" and said, "Brother, I'm sorry. It's the chief's idea… you understand. With so great a thing having happened, every man has to be put through the sieve."

> “杨慕次”的眼睛没有光泽，他迟疑了片刻，艰难地点头。伸出右手来……
There was no light in the eyes of "Yang Muci." He hesitated a moment, then, with difficulty, nodded, and put out his right hand—

> 他的手背红腥腥的一片，夏跃春急步上前，用早已准备好的棉纱布裹住他的手背和手心。
The back of his hand was a raw, bloody smear; Xia Yuechun stepped quickly forward and wrapped it, palm and back, in gauze he had ready.

> “喂。”“杨慕次”的喉咙干涩，但吐字依旧清晰。
"Hello." The throat of "Yang Muci" was dry, but he spoke as clearly as ever.

> “我找李沁红组长。”电话那边的声音很闷，显然，对方的声音进行了伪装。
"I want Section-head Li Qinhong." The voice on the other end was muffled; plainly it had been disguised.

> “她在。您要她听电话吗？”“慕次”回答的时候，刻意看了李沁红一眼，这一眼，镇定得反而令李沁红有些不自在了。
"She's here. Do you want her to take it?" As "Muci" answered, he deliberately shot a glance at Li Qinhong—a glance so composed that it left her, of all people, a little ill at ease.

> “熊处长呢？他也在吗？”电话那边继续问。
"And Chief Xiong? Is he there too?" the voice went on.

> “他不在。”“慕次”说话的同时，及时有效地收回了自己的目光。
"He isn't." As he spoke, "Muci" drew his own gaze back, in good time and to good effect.

> “请您务必转告他们一句话。戈登路恒吉里1141号。”
"Be sure to pass one thing on to them. Gordon Road, Hengji Li, No. 1141."

> “好的。戈登路恒吉里1141号。”“慕次”神色自若地重复了一句，然后，将电话递给高磊。高磊正要接电话，被李沁红抢先拿去，李沁红的手在接触“慕次”手的瞬间，她感觉到“阿次”指尖冰凉，凉得令她犹如过电般闪惊了一下。
"Very well. Gordon Road, Hengji Li, No. 1141." "Muci" repeated it, his manner perfectly easy, and then handed the receiver to Gao Lei. Gao Lei was about to take it when Li Qinhong snatched it first; and in the instant her hand touched "Muci's," she felt "A-Ci's" fingertips ice-cold—so cold it startled her like a jolt of current.

> “怎么样？”李沁红问。
"Well?" Li Qinhong asked.

> “报告组长，可以确定不是他。”
"Reporting, Section-head: it can be confirmed—it is not him."

> “哦？”李沁红的眼神开始游移不定地扫荡其他手下了。“你这么肯定？”
"Oh?" Li Qinhong's eyes began to rove uncertainly over the rest of her men. "You're that sure?"

> “是。绝对是两个人。我对声音非常敏感，不会错的。不是他。”
"Yes. They are absolutely two different people. I have a very keen ear for voices; I cannot be wrong. It is not him."

> 就在李沁红说话间，“慕次”的喉咙里发出很难受的声音。夏跃春趁机把高磊、李沁红等人隔开。“到隔壁诊疗室去。”
Even as Li Qinhong spoke, a dreadful sound came from "Muci's" throat. Xia Yuechun seized the chance to screen Gao Lei and Li Qinhong off from him. "To the treatment room next door."

> “等一下。”李沁红制止。“为什么急着走？”她质问夏医生。
"Wait." Li Qinhong stopped him. "Why the hurry to leave?" she demanded of the doctor.

> “因为，这里是看护室，而不是什么诊疗室。我的治疗仪器全在隔壁。为了他能接你们这个该死的电话，我把病人像皮球一样踢来踢去，现在电话已经接了，我希望他立即回到诊室继续治疗。你是否愿意看到他因肺部感染，或是剧烈咳嗽造成他的胸骨再断一次？”夏跃春虽然戴着口罩，仍然可以使房间里的人感觉到医生的愤怒。
"Because this is a nursing ward, not a treatment room. All my therapeutic apparatus is next door. To let him take this cursed call of yours, I have kicked my patient about like a football; the call is done now, and I want him back in the treatment room continuing his treatment at once. Would you like to see his lungs turn septic, or a fit of violent coughing snap his breastbone a second time?" Though Xia Yuechun was masked, he still contrived to make the whole room feel the doctor's fury.

> “好吧，医生。我尊重您的建议。”李沁红表面妥协地说，她看着病床从她眼前推过，“杨慕次”似乎因痰而堵，完全丧失了讲话能力。紧接着，她听见隔壁房间重重的关门声。
"Very well, Doctor. I respect your advice." Li Qinhong said, yielding on the surface. She watched the bed wheeled past her; "Yang Muci" seemed choked with phlegm, and had wholly lost the power of speech. A moment later she heard the door of the room next door slam shut, hard.

> “怎么办？”高磊问。
"What now?" Gao Lei asked.

> “把电话接到诊疗室，我要再试一次。”李沁红面无表情地说。
"Put the call through to the treatment room. I want to try once more." Li Qinhong said without expression.

> “再听一次？”高磊愕然。
"Listen again?" Gao Lei was dumbfounded.

> “执行命令。”李沁红说。
"Carry out the order." Li Qinhong said.

> 诊疗室里，灯光明亮。
In the treatment room the light was bright.

> 杨慕次双眼朦胧地望着天花板上令人眩目的挂灯。今天的“春和医院”仿佛是一个不设幕的舞台，暗景的转换，灯光的布控，全在杨慕初的掌控之中，而井然有序的进行。
Yang Muci gazed with clouded eyes at the dazzling lamp on the ceiling. Today the Chunhe Hospital was like a stage without a curtain: the shifting of the dark scenery, the marshalling of the lights, all lay in the hands of Yang Muchu, and all proceeded in perfect order.

> 他看见了李沁红的脸，阴晴不定。
He saw Li Qinhong's face, its weather uncertain.

> 他看见高磊的脸，堆着歉意地笑。
He saw Gao Lei's face, wearing a smile of apology.

> 医用器械不断地碰撞声，令李沁红听来很不舒服。不到一刻钟，高磊的手下已经把电话接到了诊疗室。
The ceaseless clatter of medical instruments grated on Li Qinhong's ear. In less than a quarter of an hour Gao Lei's men had put the call through to the treatment room.

> “怎么样？杨副官？我们再听一次。”
"Well, Adjutant Yang? We'll listen once more."

> “如果，您不信任我……”阿次费力地说：“您叫他来，当面对质。”
"If you don't trust me—" A-Ci said with an effort, "call him here, and let us face each other."

> “你知道他不能来。”李沁红说。“再听一次，就可以完全排除你的……嫌疑。我想，这也是你所希望的。”
"You know he can't come," Li Qinhong said. "One more listen, and your… suspicion can be entirely cleared. I imagine that's what you want too."

> 慕次无语。
Muci said nothing.

> 李沁红拨通了电话。说：“再听一次。”她主动地把电话递到慕次冰凉的手上。
Li Qinhong dialed the number and said, "Listen again." She herself pressed the receiver into Muci's frozen hand.

> 杨慕次接过电话：“喂。”
Yang Muci took it. "Hello."

> “我找李沁红组长。”电话那边的声音很脆。
"I want Section-head Li Qinhong." The voice on the other end was crisp.

> “你不是刚才跟我通话的人。”慕次平静地说。
"You are not the man I spoke with just now," Muci said calmly.

> “你的声音好像也在变。”
"Your voice seems to be changing too."

> “人的声线很难改变，不过，人的记忆多多少少会有误区。”
"A man's voice is very hard to change; a man's memory, though, has its blind spots, more or less."

> “你不觉得，你不应该跟我说这么多的话。”
"Don't you think you oughtn't to be saying so much to me?"

> “我心怀坦荡。”
"My heart is clear."

> “知道为什么要你接这个电话？”
"Do you know why you were made to take this call?"

> “不知道。”慕次喘起来。
"No." Muci began to gasp.

> “好了，够了。”夏跃春发怒了。“够了。”
"All right, that's enough." Xia Yuechun flew into a rage. "That's enough."

> 李沁红从阿次手中接过电话，问：“怎么样？”
Li Qinhong took the receiver back from A-Ci and asked, "Well?"

> “……无法确定。”
"…I cannot be certain."

> “什么？”李沁红大声嚷嚷起来。
"What?" Li Qinhong began to shout.

> “可能……我自己的听觉记忆有些混乱。或许是我太紧张……有点像。”
"It may be… my own auditory memory is a little confused. Or perhaps I'm too much on edge… it sounds somewhat alike."

> “刚才你斩钉截铁的告诉我，不是。现在你小心翼翼地说，有点像？我问的是答案！到底是？还是，不是？”
"A moment ago you told me flatly it was not him. Now you say, ever so cautiously, that it sounds somewhat alike? I asked you for an answer! Which is it—is it, or is it not?"

> “我……不能确定。”
"I… cannot be certain."

> “混蛋！”李沁红狠狠地掼下话筒。
"Idiot!" Li Qinhong slammed the receiver down hard.

> “我来告诉你答案。”夏跃春摘下口罩，说：“虽然，我不明白，你们这样做的目的是什么？但是，我可以告诉你，所谓分辨人的声音，是靠人的听觉记忆来完成的。听觉记忆虽然没有情绪记忆那样深刻，但是第一次所刻意记住的声音，应该是很强烈的。但是，这种记忆属于听觉线索，而不是视觉线索。也就是说，记忆中的主观因素往往会破坏整个认知的过程。就像你们一进门，就认定了病床上躺的是杨慕次先生。你们不会去苛求他的声音，因为视觉线索，给了你们一个明确的答案。中国人有句古话说得好：耳听为虚，眼见为实。如果，你们对杨先生有什么疑问，可以请那位朋友亲自过来，彼此见见，也许很快就会得出最正确的结论。”
"Let me give you the answer." Xia Yuechun took off his mask and said: "I do not understand what you people are trying to accomplish by all this; but I can tell you that the so-called telling apart of a man's voice is done by way of auditory memory. Auditory memory is not so deep as emotional memory, yet a voice deliberately fixed in the mind the first time round ought to leave a strong impression. But this kind of memory belongs to the auditory cues, not the visual ones. Which is to say, the subjective element in memory will very often wreck the whole process of recognition. Just as, the moment you walked in, you took it for granted that the man lying in that bed was Mr. Yang Muci. You did not trouble to scrutinize his voice, because the visual cue had already given you a definite answer. There is a fine old Chinese saying: what the ear hears is empty; what the eye sees is real. If you have any doubt about Mr. Yang, you may ask that friend of yours to come in person, so that the two can meet face to face; perhaps you will very soon arrive at the most correct conclusion."

> 李沁红是属于多疑的，苛刻的人。不过，对夏医生地说法，她还是比较认可的，毕竟夏跃春是英国留学生、医学博士。
Li Qinhong was a suspicious, exacting sort of person. Still, she rather accepted the doctor's account of it; Xia Yuechun was, after all, a returned student from England, a doctor of medicine.

> 可是，她偏偏有些信不过眼前这个躺在病床上，有气无力的人。
And yet, of all things, she could not quite trust the feeble man lying there before her in the bed.

> “还能讲话吗？”她有些虚伪地低声安抚着杨慕次。
"Can he still speak?" she asked, soothing Yang Muci in a low, somewhat hypocritical voice.

> “能。”慕次很配合。
"He can." Muci was most cooperative.

> “不能！”夏跃春倔强地高举左手。“我反对！”
"He cannot!" Xia Yuechun stubbornly raised his left hand high. "I object!"

> 杨慕次示意医生情绪不宜过激。
Yang Muci signaled to the doctor that he should not let his feelings run too high.

> “她是我长官。”慕次说。“您说……”
"She is my superior," Muci said. "Go on—"

> “现在的情形显然比刚才好多了。”李沁红说。“我想问你几个小问题。我们在拉网行动前，你是否单独返回过熊处长的办公室？”
"The situation is plainly much better now than it was a moment ago," Li Qinhong said. "I want to ask you a few small questions. Before the dragnet operation, did you return alone to Chief Xiong's office?"

> “是。”慕次回答地异常干脆。
"Yes." Muci answered with unusual readiness.

> “那么，你出入熊处长办公室的时候，是否听到电话铃声响？”
"Then, going in and out of Chief Xiong's office, did you hear a telephone ringing?"

> “是。”慕次面不改色地说。
"Yes." Muci said, without a change of countenance.

> 所有特务的目光都因这一个“是”字，锁定在阿次身上。空气霎时凝固般安静。“你有没有接听电话？”
At that single word "yes," the eyes of every agent fastened on A-Ci. The air froze all at once into stillness. "Did you answer the telephone?"

> “没有。”
"No."

> “为什么不接？”
"Why not?"

> “因为我……当时拿了熊处的公文包后，……刘副官一直在底下……按喇叭催我，所以，我跑得很快，我跑到走廊的尽头时，才听见处座办公室的电话铃声响了。我第一反应，就是……是……谁的电话也不接了。可是，当我继续往下跑的时候，……我听见电话铃声依然响……个不停，我怕有事，又折回，刚走进走廊……电话铃声就断了。”
"Because I… had just taken the chief's document case, and… Adjutant Liu was down below the whole time… honking the horn, hurrying me, so I ran fast; and it was only when I reached the far end of the corridor that I heard the telephone in the chief's office ring. My first reaction was—was—I wasn't going to answer anyone's call. But as I kept running on down, I heard the telephone still ringing—on and on without stopping; and I was afraid something might be wrong, so I doubled back, and just as I stepped into the corridor… the ringing stopped."

> “然后呢？”
"And then?"

> “我就下楼了。”
"Then I went downstairs."

> “还记得，那个开车撞你的女共党吗？”
"Do you still remember the Communist woman who drove her car into you?"

> “不记得了。”
"I don't remember."

> “为什么？”
"Why not?"

> “很恐怖。”
"It was too terrible."

> “她漂亮吗？”
"Was she beautiful?"

> “不知道。”慕次很痛苦。
"I don't know." Muci was in great pain.

> “你当时怕不怕？”
"Were you afraid at the time?"

> “来不及害怕。不过，现在很害怕。”
"There was no time to be afraid. But now I am very afraid."

> “夜里做噩梦吗？”
"Do you have nightmares at night?"

> “是。”
"Yes."

> “她对你说什么？”
"What did she say to you?"

> 杨慕次摇头。
Yang Muci shook his head.

> “她一定对你哭过？你们认识？很久以前就认识！你们彼此信任！”
"She must have wept at you? You knew each other? You had known each other a long time! You trusted each other!"

> “不！”慕次发自内心的痛楚发泄出来。他的手用力抬起，拉扯到输液的针管，血浸出来。“不！”他激动，而且愤怒！“她在笑！她冲我笑！她笑我们的愚蠢！愚蠢！”一口血痰喷射出来，几乎溅到李沁红略有扭曲的脸颊。
"No!" The pain welled up from the depths of Muci and burst out of him. His hand jerked violently upward, dragging at the drip tube, and blood seeped out. "No!" He was beside himself, and furious besides. "She was laughing! She laughed at me! She was laughing at how stupid we were! Stupid!" The whiff of gunpowder vanished into thin air.

Yang Muchu's appearance told everyone present, plainly and beyond dispute:

Xia Yuechun let a pent-up breath out through his nose with a "Hmph."

In his heart Gao Lei sneered. Women.

"Captain Gao, is it?" Yang Muchu nodded toward Gao Lei.

"That day, I'm sorry—my brother's life hung on it, and I was rash. From now on my brother must trouble you for your kind care."

"You put it too strongly, far too strongly." Gao Lei courteously cupped his hands.

"And this is—?" Li Qinhong's attention shifted to He Yashu.

"How do you do." He Yashu greeted her politely.

"Miss He is very beautiful," Li Qinhong said.

"Thank you." He Yashu answered with easy composure.

"Come from the ballroom?"

"No." Yashu said. "We went to the Lanxin Theatre to see a Shaoxing opera, and thinking it was still early, came by to look in on A-Ci."

A thorough answer.

Gao Lei drew close to Li Qinhong, leaned against her, and asked in a low voice, "Any more questions?"

A-Chu asked He Yashu to wait for him in the superintendent's office.

Only three people were left in the room.

"Yes."

Hearing this, Xia Yuechun felt an uneasiness in his heart, and glared at A-Chu with his eyes.

What sort of man, after all, was Yang Muchu? A-Ci fell into deep thought.

Someone knocked; A-Chu opened the door, and Liu A-Si came in. He whispered a few words in A-Chu's ear, and A-Chu nodded.

"What is it?" The strings in Xia Yuechun's mind were drawn taut.

> “没事，没事。”阿初说：“杨羽桦来了。”

Yang Yuhua? A-Ci turned the name over in puzzlement.

I hope you can cherish the kinship and love I give you—and not turn a kinship regained after loss into a weapon to fend me off! Once a man looks anxiously before and behind, he loses the power of decisive judgment.

Set out in words, this principle is plain to anyone, dull and unremarkable. But without the ground-work of profound scientific knowledge, it is very hard to carry off in practice.

On this principle Li Qinhong lost.

A-Chu bade Liu A-Si lead Yashu down from the superintendent's office, and himself waited for her at the hospital gate.

> 和雅淑是前天夜里接到阿初的正式邀请的。当时，她很困惑。因为，她不知道阿初有什么意图。可是，强烈的好奇欲望驱使她如期赴约。
He Yashu had received A-Chu's formal invitation on the night before last. At the time she had been much perplexed, for she did not know what A-Chu intended by it. But a fierce curiosity drove her to keep the appointment.

> 那天晚上，他们在国际大饭店的豪华雅间，共进晚餐。
That evening they had dined together in a luxurious private room at the Park Hotel.

> 雅淑是天生的享受型女人，无论她的地位处于优势，或是劣势，她都会把握住机会，充分利用自己所擅长的温柔和妩媚，去征服对手的心灵。她把每一个曾经向她示好的男子都当做自己的“对手”，认真交往，区别对待，就是自己所“爱”，也不能免。
Yashu was by nature a woman made for enjoyment: whether her station stood high or low, she would seize her chances, and turn to the fullest account the tenderness and charm in which she excelled, to conquer the hearts of her adversaries. Every man who had ever shown her favor she treated as an "adversary," courting him in earnest and treating each by his own measure—and not even the man she "loved" was exempt.

> 她对阿初的探奇还不深入，但是，她的芳心总是背叛她大脑的指令，而对阿初情有独钟了。
Her exploration of the enigma of A-Chu was not yet deep, and yet her heart forever betrayed the orders of her head, and had come to dote on A-Chu alone.

> 雅淑夜来也想过几回，虽然阿初曾经是一个“家奴”，自己不也做过“舞女”吗？
Yashu too had turned it over a few times in the night: A-Chu had once been a "house-slave"—but then, had she herself not once been a "dance-hall girl"?

> 而阿初在非正常的“工作”状态中，身心疲惫，他非常渴求有一个对自己没有负担的女人，来满足自己的正常欲望和感性的需求。他希望，自己在残酷的现实中，能够寻找到一点点生活乐趣，也可以弥补自己对爱地向往。
And A-Chu, in his abnormal state of "work," worn out in body and mind, craved intensely a woman who would be no burden to him, to satisfy his ordinary desires and the needs of his senses. He hoped that in this cruel reality he might find a little of life's pleasure, and might make up too for his own longing for love.

> 他心灵里纯真美丽的“爱情”世界，已经无情地被冰封了。他的生命里，再也不需要如水般的纯情了。
The pure and lovely world of "love" within his soul had already been mercilessly frozen over. In his life there was no more need for a tenderness limpid as water.

> 他们的会谈很欢畅，当然，彼此都触及到了一些有关婚姻的敏感话题。雅淑几次试图告知阿初，她曾经“诬陷”过他，都被阿初善意地化解了。
Their conversation had been most agreeable; and of course each had touched on certain delicate matters that bore on marriage. Several times Yashu had tried to tell A-Chu that she had once "falsely accused" him, and each time A-Chu had smoothed it away with good grace.

> 阿初告诉她，每一个人都有“自择其途”的权利。
A-Chu told her that every person has the right to "choose his own road."

> 至于，今天的戏剧性表演，都是阿初一手安排的。雅淑无条件地接受了他的指挥，还他一个实实在在的人情。她一个人待在停尸房等他，为他准备了两个捂手的热水袋，她惊奇地发现自己，一点也不畏惧死人的阴气，她就像在等待自己的丈夫一样，内心充满幸福和温馨。
As for today's dramatic performance, it had all been of A-Chu's contriving. Yashu had submitted to his direction without condition, repaying him a solid, tangible favor. She had waited for him alone in the mortuary, and had made ready for him two hot-water bottles to warm his hands; to her own surprise she had found that she was not in the least afraid of the dead's cold gloom—she was like a wife waiting for her husband, her heart full of happiness and warmth.

> 雅淑想留住阿初的身体，她知道，阿初的心灵地带，她也许永生难以进入。但是，留住他的身体，其实，就是自己爱情的胜利。
Yashu wished to keep A-Chu's body. She knew that the realm of A-Chu's soul she might, perhaps, never in her life be able to enter. But to keep his body was, in truth, the very victory of her love.

> 一念所至，欣欣动情。
At the single thought, joy stirred in her, and her feeling quickened.

> 汽车顺着大路，开往“梅花巷”七号。
The motorcar ran along the main road, bound for No. 7, Plum Blossom Lane.

> 雅淑留阿初吃晚饭，阿初同意了。晚饭吃得很简单，青菜豆腐，梅干菜，小份鸡丁，大碗鸡汤。鸡汤是昨夜炖的，汤面有金色浮油，很腻，很香。
Yashu pressed A-Chu to stay for supper, and A-Chu agreed. It was a simple supper: greens and bean curd, dried preserved vegetables, a small dish of diced chicken, a large bowl of chicken soup. The soup had been stewed the night before, with golden fat floating on its surface, rich and fragrant.

> 晚饭后，雅淑提议，带阿初参观她的小院，阿初欣然做陪。
After supper Yashu proposed showing A-Chu round her little courtyard, and A-Chu gladly went along.

> 小院内外三层，靠里屋最后面是池塘，中间是书房兼卧室，有雕花窗子，晚上能看月亮。最外面是花径，有盆栽。花是阿初精心挑选的，雅淑并不知情，她一味地夸赞原主人的雅致和浪漫。
The little courtyard ran three tiers deep, front to back: at the very back, against the inner room, was a pond; in the middle a study that served also as bedchamber, with carved lattice windows, from which one could watch the moon at night; and outermost a flower path, with potted plants. The flowers A-Chu had chosen with care, though Yashu did not know it; she praised, over and over, the taste and romance of the former owner.

> 阿初一边踱步，一边微笑聆听她的细语柔声，很惬意。
A-Chu strolled about, smiling as he listened to her soft, murmuring voice, thoroughly at ease.

> 他们从花的种类，谈到花的类型，花的气质和性格。
From the kinds of flowers they passed to the types of flowers, and to the temperament and character of flowers.

> “梅花巷前面的梅花树，还没到冬天，到有了新气像。听街坊说，到了冬天，整个巷子里都透着暗香。”
"The plum trees in front of Plum Blossom Lane—winter isn't here yet, and already they have a new air to them. The neighbors say that when winter comes, the whole lane is threaded through with a hidden fragrance."

> “喜欢梅花吗？”
"Do you like the plum blossom?"

> “喜欢。”雅淑说。“梅花有傲骨。”
"I do." Yashu said. "The plum blossom has a proud spine."

> “是呀，梅花有傲骨，比人强。”
"Yes, the plum blossom has a proud spine—stronger than most people."

> 雅淑止步，说：“你的心底是否曾经嘲笑过我，莠穗妄称良稻？”
Yashu stopped and said, "Have you ever, in your heart, laughed at me—a stalk of weed that falsely claims to be good grain?"

> 阿初止步，认真地说：“阿初不敢，自与雅淑小姐相识以来，从未生此心此意。”
A-Chu stopped, and said in earnest, "A-Chu would not dare. Since I first knew Miss Yashu, I have never harbored such a thought or intent."

> “腹诽心谤也没有？”
"Not even a slander in your belly, a reproach in your heart?"

> “没有。”
"None."

> 雅淑继续往前走，她的眼底有些迷离。“喜欢散步吗？”她问。
Yashu walked on, a certain mistiness in her eyes. "Do you like to walk?" she asked.

> “喜欢。”
"I do."

> “喜欢一个人散步？还是两个人？”
"Do you like to walk alone? Or in a pair?"

> “看对方是谁。”
"That depends on who the other is."

> 雅淑面色潮红，心潮浮动。
Yashu's face flushed, and her heart-tide rose and fell.

> 他们走到池塘边，无路可走了。
They came to the edge of the pond, and there was no further path.

> “此路不通。”雅淑笑着说。
"The road ends here," Yashu said, smiling.

> 阿初其实比雅淑更加熟悉道路，他牵着雅淑的手，一起穿过树荫深处，来到房檐下。
A-Chu in fact knew the way better than Yashu did; he took her by the hand, and together they passed through the deep of the tree-shade and came to the shelter of the eaves.

> “你好像比我还熟悉我家的路径。”雅淑说。
"You seem to know the paths of my home even better than I do," Yashu said.

> “男人有随机应变的能力。”
"A man has the knack of adapting as the moment demands."

> “你的意思是，女人缺乏自信心？”雅淑不服气。
"You mean that a woman lacks self-confidence?" Yashu would not have it.

> “你为什么总是在我的面前要强呢？”阿初意味深长地说。
"Why is it that you must always strive to have the upper hand before me?" A-Chu said, with weight in the words.

> 雅淑的表情极为惊讶。
Yashu's face showed the utmost astonishment.

> 阿初的这句话，几乎是赤裸裸的表达，他要自己在他的面前顺从、服从，只有一个男人想拥有一个女人的时候，他才会提出这种类似的要求，这句平平淡淡的话，浸透着情意，暗示着结合。
This remark of A-Chu's was an all but naked declaration: he wished her to be pliant, submissive, before him; only when a man wishes to possess a woman does he make a demand of such a kind. Plain and unadorned as the words were, they were steeped in feeling, and hinted at a union.

> 雅淑的心底急流跌宕，起伏不平。
Yashu's heart surged and plunged, rising and falling without rest.

> “前唐布袋和尚有一首咏农夫插秧的诗：手把秧禾插稻田，低头便见水中天，顺其自然方是道，退步原来是向前。”
"There is a poem by the Cloth-Bag Monk of the former Tang, on a farmer planting rice: Rice-shoots in hand, he sets them in the paddy field; bowing his head, he sees the sky within the water. To go along with nature—that is the Way; to step backward was, all along, to go forward."

> 雅淑明白了，在情郎面前的退步，就是确定彼此关系的第一步，这一步，至尊至贵，推动感情的前进。
Yashu understood: to step back before one's lover was the first step in fixing the bond between them, and that step, most precious and most exalted, drove their feeling forward.

> “在我曾经住过的地方中，我最难忘的就是在英国卡迪芙那一段平凡的日子，仿佛我人生中最美好的时光，宁静、幸福、祥和、温暖，我深切地怀念那些简单而又充满生活乐趣的岁月。我希望这个梅花巷能够成为我人生中第二个避风港，而你，就是这个避风港唯一的女主人。”
"Of all the places I have ever lived, the one I can never forget is that stretch of ordinary days in Cardiff, in England—as if they were the finest hours of my life: quiet, happy, serene, warm. I miss deeply those simple years that were yet so full of life's pleasure. I hope this Plum Blossom Lane can become the second harbor of my life—and you, the one and only mistress of that harbor."

> “我不是天使。”雅淑因为激动，而脸泛红晕。
"I am no angel." Yashu, stirred, flushed.

> “我知道。”阿初平静地说。
"I know," A-Chu said calmly.

> “为什么选我？”
"Why choose me?"

> “一定要回答吗？”
"Must I answer?"

> “是。”
"Yes."

> “我需要一个女人。”阿初凝视着雅淑的眼睛说。“我的生命里需要一个女人，一个可以给我身心带来愉悦的女人，而且，她对我始终能够宽宏大量。最重要的是，我跟她在一起，没有负罪感。不知道，这个答案有没有伤害到您？”
"I need a woman." A-Chu said, gazing into Yashu's eyes. "My life needs a woman—a woman who can bring joy to my body and mind, and who can always be magnanimous toward me. Most important of all, when I am with her, I feel no guilt. I wonder—has that answer hurt you?"

> “继续。”雅淑鼓励他。
"Go on," Yashu encouraged him.

> “如果您同意，您将成为我形影不离的伴侣，我在您面前将永远温顺如羔羊。”
"If you consent, you will become the companion who is never parted from me, and before you I shall be forever gentle as a lamb."

> “如果我辞而不受呢？”
"And if I decline the offer?"

> “我自信您不会拒绝。因为，我不会在同一个女人面前，讲两次相同的话。”
"I am confident you will not refuse. Because I never say the same words twice to the same woman."

> “做你的女人，有没有任期？”
"Being your woman—is there a fixed term to it?"

> 阿初不提防，雅淑有此一问。
A-Chu had not been on his guard against such a question from Yashu.

> 雅淑“扑哧”一笑。“如果有任期，我想连任。”
Yashu gave a little snort of laughter. "If there's a term, I should like to serve a second one."

> 阿初会心地笑了。“如果你要连任，就没有报酬了。”
A-Chu smiled in understanding. "If you want a second term, there'll be no remuneration."

> “倒贴也干。”
"I'd do it and pay you besides."

> “这句话可不像出自名门闺秀之口。”
"That's hardly talk to come from the lips of a young lady of good family."

> “那你是答应？还是不答应呢？”
"So—is it yes? Or is it no?"

> “我想这个问题，应该用行动来回答。”阿初的胸口贴紧了雅淑那丰盈饱满的胸脯，他的手轻轻捧起雅淑的头，雅淑的双唇在月光的照射下泛起一层透明而柔软的光泽。他的唇主动去温暖她的唇，她的手情不自禁地搂紧他的腰，爱欲的流水潺潺不懈地渐次灌注在彼此心田。
"I think this question is one that should be answered by deeds." A-Chu pressed his chest close against Yashu's full, ample bosom; his hand gently cupped her head, and Yashu's lips, in the moonlight, took on a soft, translucent sheen. His lips of their own accord went to warm hers, and her arms, unbidden, clasped tight about his waist, while the flowing waters of desire poured, murmuring and unceasing, little by little into the fields of each other's hearts.

> 雅淑希望用这种最简单、最原始的方式来确定自己在阿初心目中的地位。那亲切而又持久的香吻，自然而然地将阿初导入至尊无上的人性佳境，欲海情舟。
Yashu wished, by this simplest, most primal of ways, to fix her place in A-Chu's heart. That warm, lingering, fragrant kiss led A-Chu, as naturally as could be, into the supreme fair country of human feeling—a boat of passion on a sea of desire.

> “我们是前缘？还是孽缘？”雅淑喃喃地问。
"Are we a bond from a former life? Or a bond of ill fate?" Yashu murmured.

> “是因缘。”阿初不让她讲话，继续缠绵。
"It is a karmic bond." A-Chu would not let her speak, and went on with his caresses.

> “今天晚上，留……”
"Tonight, stay—"

> 阿初轻轻用手指堵住她的唇。“这句话，留给男人说。”他把她打横抱起来，走向房帷深处，飘浮的步履，靡丽的月色，两个清丽的人影，被勾画得越来越清晰。
A-Chu gently stopped her lips with a finger. "Those words—leave them for the man to say." He lifted her crosswise into his arms and carried her toward the depths of the bed-curtains; the drifting tread, the voluptuous moonlight, the two fair figures, were drawn ever more clearly into view.

> 月光下，阿初第一次露出了男人粗犷的美，雅淑一味地守而不卫，须臾一瞬，共赴巫山。
In the moonlight A-Chu for the first time showed the rough beauty of a man; Yashu held fast, but kept no guard at all, and in the space of an instant they went together to the clouds and rain.
