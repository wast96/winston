# Chapter 5 bilingual audit (QC only)

> 王亚樵利用"江安号事件"智挫上海三大亨后，情知三大亨对他并没有真正解除戒意，而且也知道杜月笙无时不在寻找机会报复他，所以决定在上海隐居当寓公，不再抛头露面。他在上海法租界浦柏路24号买了一幢小洋楼，日日深居简出，时时小心遭到三大亨的暗算。这无疑是王亚樵的精明之处。

Having outwitted the three great bosses of Shanghai over the Jiang'an affair, Wang Yaqiao knew perfectly well that the three had not truly put away their guard against him, and knew too that Du Yuesheng was never not looking for a chance to pay him back. So he resolved to live retired in Shanghai as a private gentleman and show his face in public no longer. He bought a small foreign-style house at No. 24 Rue Pottier in the French Concession, kept indoors day after day, and watched constantly for a stroke out of the dark from the three bosses. It was, beyond question, the shrewd course.

> 然而，树欲静而风不止。1931年春节过后，他那浦柏路的幽静小院里，忽然走进一位少见的客人，只因此人的到来，让王亚樵本已平静下来的生活又掀起了波澜。而且就从那天起，他又一次卷进一场更加凶险的暗杀中去。

But the tree may wish for stillness; the wind does not stop. After the Spring Festival of 1931, into that quiet courtyard on Rue Pottier there walked a rare visitor — and merely by coming, this man raised a wave in a life that had settled into calm. From that day, Wang Yaqiao was drawn once more into an assassination more dangerous than any before it.

> 来访的客人名叫李少川。此人原是国民党上层人物，早年王亚樵从安徽初来上海闯码头的时候，处境困难，就多得李少川的照顾和提携。有一年过年，王亚樵连手下弟兄的年饷也发不下，多亏仗义疏财的李少川加以援手，方才得以渡过年关。后来在王亚樵为"江安号"和上海三大亨斗法时，也是这位李少川从中疏通，最后才使杜月笙、黄金荣等不得不向王亚樵服输。

The caller's name was Li Shaochuan. He had been a man of the Nationalist Party's upper ranks, and in the early years, when Wang Yaqiao first came from Anhui to try the Shanghai wharves and found the going hard, it was Li Shaochuan's protection and patronage that carried him. One New Year, when Wang Yaqiao could not even pay his brothers' holiday money, it was the open-handed Li Shaochuan who put out a hand and got him over the turn of the year. And later, when Wang Yaqiao was matching wits with the three great bosses over the Jiang'an, it was this same Li Shaochuan who worked the channels until Du Yuesheng and Huang Jinrong had no choice but to concede.

> 如今正是三月天，上海阴云笼罩。虽然已是春天，但却寒气逼人，似乎随时都有大雨将至。就在这时候李少川忽然不请自来，确让隐居在法租界的王亚樵暗吃一惊，因为李少川是不轻易登他门槛的。王亚樵不敢怠慢，急忙吩咐女侍上茶，随后屏退从人，对李少川说："仁兄无事不登三宝殿，今日莫非有什么事情要我去办吗？"

It was March now, and Shanghai lay under heavy cloud. Spring by the calendar, but the cold pressed in on a man, and it seemed a downpour might come at any hour. That Li Shaochuan should arrive at such a moment, unasked, gave the retired Wang Yaqiao a private start — for Li Shaochuan did not cross his threshold lightly. Wang Yaqiao dared not be slow: he called for tea, sent the servants out, and said to him: "An honored elder brother does not climb to the Three Treasures Hall without business. Is there something today you would have me do?"

> 李少川和王亚樵交情深厚，说话从不绕圈子。他环顾左右，忽然压低声音说："亚樵兄，你可知道胡汉民这个人？"

Li Shaochuan and Wang Yaqiao were close, and he never talked in circles. He glanced around the room and dropped his voice. "Brother Yaqiao — do you know the man Hu Hanmin?"

> "胡汉民？当然知道，从前我在广州孙中山先生身边走动的时候，就和胡先生相熟。那时孙先生是大总统，胡先生就是总统府的秘书长，岂有不熟之理？"王亚樵心里暗暗称奇，一时猜不透李少川为何开口竟谈起与他们关联不大的胡汉民来。

"Hu Hanmin? Of course. In the days when I moved about at Mr. Sun Yat-sen's side in Guangzhou I knew Mr. Hu well. Mr. Sun was Grand President then, and Mr. Hu was secretary-general of the presidential office — how should I not know him?" Wang Yaqiao marveled privately, unable for the moment to guess why Li Shaochuan should open by speaking of a Hu Hanmin who had so little to do with either of them.

> 李少川叹息一声："九光兄，你可知胡先生现在遭难了吗？"

Li Shaochuan sighed. "Brother Jiuguang — do you know that Mr. Hu is now in trouble?"

> "这个谁不知道？报上不久前刊载了蒋介石软禁胡先生的消息，天下人谁不为蒋的倒行逆施愤慨呢？"王亚樵见李少川话题不离胡汉民，心里不由紧张起来。王亚樵虽是上海的帮派首领，可他始终和国民党官场人物保持着联系。他自然知道就在一月前的某天晚上，蒋介石以邀请胡汉民去南京汤山晚宴为借口，将时任南京政府立法院长的胡汉民骗至汤山，然后出其不意地下令幽禁了胡汉民。一时全国哗然，各派政治势力纷纷谴责蒋介石的流氓行径，惹得朝野共愤。王亚樵作为多年受蒋介石压制的帮派首脑，对胡汉民的境遇自然十分同情，然而他并没有想到有一天会为失去自由的胡汉民做些什么。因为那时他担心自己树大招风，已经决意偃旗息鼓了。

"Who does not know it? The papers carried the news not long ago that Chiang Kai-shek has him under house arrest. Who in this country is not outraged at Chiang's shameless conduct?" Seeing that Li Shaochuan would not leave the subject of Hu Hanmin, Wang Yaqiao felt the tension rise in him. Gang chief of Shanghai he might be, but he had never let go his connections in the Nationalist official world. He knew well enough what had happened one evening a month before: Chiang Kai-shek, on the pretext of inviting Hu Hanmin to a dinner at Tangshan outside Nanjing, had lured the president of the Legislative Yuan there and then, without warning, ordered him confined. The country had erupted. Every political faction condemned the gangster's trick, and the anger ran through court and countryside alike. As a gang leader long suppressed by Chiang Kai-shek, Wang Yaqiao naturally felt for Hu Hanmin's plight — but it had never occurred to him that he might one day do something about it. In those months he feared that a tall tree draws the wind, and had resolved to furl his banners and still his drums.

> 李少川见王亚樵仍猜不透他的来意，就进一步晓知内幕："九光兄，你可知自蒋某人把胡汉民囚禁以后，孙科就鼓动那些反蒋的粤派官员愤然地辞去南京政府的官职，一怒之下都去了广州吗？"

Seeing that Wang Yaqiao still could not read his purpose, Li Shaochuan opened the inside of the matter further. "Brother Jiuguang — do you know that since Chiang imprisoned Hu Hanmin, Sun Ke has stirred the anti-Chiang Cantonese officials into resigning their posts in the Nanjing government in a body, and that they have all gone off to Guangzhou in their fury?"

> "这个……报上好像也登出来了！"王亚樵仍不知李少川来意何在。他知道现在蒋介石远不比两年前在南京当选国民政府主席时那样红火，蒋如今已由众人拥戴而转为百官憎恨。随着蒋介石名声的日渐狼藉，孙中山的子嗣孙科见蒋介石幽禁了他父亲在世时的重臣胡汉民，一怒之下煽动大批政府要人飞往广州，形成了西南反对派势力。在这些国民党军政大员中，不仅有民国著名将领李烈钧，还有唐生智和唐绍仪等人。他们的分道扬镳，使蒋的处境大为不妙。王亚樵疑惑地望望至友李少川说："我不明白，这些事与我们有什么关系？我王九光毕竟一介平民，官场里的两派斗争，咱只能坐山观虎斗啊！"

"That… I believe the papers printed that too." Still Wang Yaqiao could not see where this was going. He knew Chiang Kai-shek's fortunes stood nothing like as high as two years before, when he was elected chairman of the National Government at Nanjing; where the man had once been carried on everyone's shoulders, he was now hated by every official in the hall. As Chiang Kai-shek's name grew steadily fouler, Sun Yat-sen's son Sun Ke — seeing Chiang confine Hu Hanmin, who had been his father's chief minister in life — had in his anger stirred a great number of government dignitaries into flying to Guangzhou, and so formed the Southwest opposition. Among these Nationalist military and political grandees were not only the famous Republican general Li Liejun but Tang Shengzhi and Tang Shaoyi besides. Their parting of the ways had put Chiang in a very poor position. Wang Yaqiao looked at his old friend in perplexity. "I don't follow. What have any of these things to do with us? I am a commoner when all is said. When two factions fight in the official world, men like me can only sit on the mountain and watch the tigers."

> "莫非仁兄就不恨蒋介石吗？"李少川见王亚樵仍在那里顾左右而言他，索性直来直去，道出他这次来访的真意，"我记得蒋介石早想派戴笠暗杀你，这说明蒋某人不仅仅会伤害一个敢反对他独裁政治的胡汉民，将来也一定容不下你等帮派首领的。蒋某人是天生的杀人魔王，仁兄不是早就对我说起过吗？既然如此，现在为什么不站出来为民除害呢？"

"Does my honored brother truly not hate Chiang Kai-shek?" Seeing Wang Yaqiao still looking left and right and talking of other things, Li Shaochuan went at it straight and spoke the real purpose of his visit. "I remember that Chiang Kai-shek long ago wanted to send Dai Li to murder you. Which tells you that Chiang will not merely injure one Hu Hanmin for daring to oppose his dictatorship — in time he will have no room for gang chiefs like you either. Chiang is a born king of killers; did you not say so to me yourself, long ago? And if that is so, why not stand up now and rid the people of him?"

> "让我站出来去救胡汉民？"王亚樵听了李少川的话，心里暗吃一惊。从小富有正义感的王亚樵，何尝不恨利用手中权力大肆镇压革命势力的蒋介石。可是，如果真让他单枪匹马站出来去行刺蒋介石，王亚樵确实感到突然。他连连摇头说："不妥不妥，少川兄，你的好意我当然领会，我对蒋某人的仇恨也绝非一日。可是，如果对蒋某人马上下手，可绝不比赵铁桥啊。谁不知道蒋的身边不仅有'十人团'在刺探情报，又新设了一个侍从室。那里有数十个精明的侍卫，他们大多是百步穿杨的射手。我们斧头帮不过是些打打拼拼的莽汉，哪里敢去南京造次？再说，胡汉民的事毕竟和你我两人关系不大，少川兄又何必要老弟去冒此风险呢？"

"You want me to stand up and rescue Hu Hanmin?" At Li Shaochuan's words Wang Yaqiao felt a private jolt. Rich in a sense of justice since boyhood, how should he not hate the Chiang Kai-shek who used the power in his hands to crush the revolutionary forces? And yet, asked to ride out alone and kill Chiang Kai-shek, Wang Yaqiao found it sudden indeed. He shook his head again and again. "It won't do, it won't do. Brother Shaochuan, I take your meaning kindly, and my own hatred of Chiang is no matter of a single day. But to move against Chiang now — that is nothing like Zhao Tieqiao. Everyone knows that at Chiang's side there is not only the Ten-Man Team gathering intelligence but a newly established Attendants' Office as well. There are dozens of sharp men in it, and most of them can put a shot through a willow leaf at a hundred paces. We of the Hatchet Gang are only rough men who hack and brawl. How should we dare go making trouble in Nanjing? Besides, Hu Hanmin's affair has little enough to do with either of us. Why should my brother ask me to run such a risk?"

> "不，这个风险你是非冒不可了。因我也是受人之托，前来这里求你鼎力相助的！"李少川见王亚樵不肯应允，情知不说出实情，王亚樵绝不上阵。于是他把胡汉民被囚汤山后国民党内部两派势力尖锐对立的情况，详细说与他听。李少川苦苦求情说，"现在由于孙科等人远在广东，大有另立国民政府，架空蒋介石之势。而胡汉民则是孙科和西南反对派的精神领袖。两股力量如此激烈斗争，很可能引起蒋介石的破釜沉舟。我这次来，是因为胡汉民的亲家林焕庭，已经得到了确切的消息，蒋介石很可能最近就秘密处死胡汉民。你也许知道，林焕庭是我多年至友，他亲自找上门来求我相助，我岂有见死不救之理？"

"No. This risk you must run. For I too am here at another man's asking, come to beg your full strength!" Seeing Wang Yaqiao unwilling, Li Shaochuan knew that without the truth the man would never take the field. So he laid out in detail the sharp division between the two Nationalist camps since Hu Hanmin's imprisonment at Tangshan, and pleaded hard. "With Sun Ke and the rest far off in Guangdong, they are near to setting up a rival National Government and hollowing Chiang Kai-shek out. And Hu Hanmin is the spiritual leader of Sun Ke and the Southwest opposition. With two forces fighting this bitterly, Chiang Kai-shek may very well break the cauldrons and sink the boats. I have come because Hu Hanmin's kinsman by marriage, Lin Huanting, has had certain word that Chiang Kai-shek may put Hu Hanmin secretly to death very soon. Lin Huanting, as perhaps you know, has been my close friend for years. He came to my door himself to beg my help. How could I see a man die and not save him?"

> 王亚樵听到这里，方知好友李少川为什么突然来访，并求他向蒋介石下手的真意。王亚樵想起蒋介石从前对他的种种恶行，再想起李少川对他的多年情谊，心里蓦然一动，说："蒋介石确是万夫所指的千古罪人，论理我王九光杀他也是情理中的事情。可是，仁兄可要知道，杀蒋绝非一件小事。如果弄得不好，可要打虎不成，反被老虎吞掉啊！再说，蒋介石如今已不再是从前上海滩上混日子的小瘪三了，他现在自知仇人太多，身边才配备了那么多善于用枪的侍卫。据说，只要他一出门就有百余人随行，简直就成了一个当代的皇帝了。我们斧头帮的人即使真想行事，怕也不是他蒋某人的对手。"

Only now did Wang Yaqiao understand why his friend had come so suddenly, and what he truly wanted. Thinking of all Chiang Kai-shek had done to him over the years, and then of Li Shaochuan's long friendship, something turned over in him. "Chiang Kai-shek is indeed a criminal of the ages, pointed at by ten thousand fingers. By any reasoning, for me to kill him would be no more than right. But my brother must understand: killing Chiang is no small business. Handled badly, a man goes out to hunt the tiger and is swallowed by it! And besides, Chiang Kai-shek is no longer the little biesan scraping a living on the Shanghai waterfront. He knows now how many enemies he has, which is why he keeps so many guards about him who can use a gun. They say he never goes out with fewer than a hundred men behind him — he has simply become an emperor of our own day. Even if my Hatchet Gang men truly wished to act, I doubt we are a match for him."

> 李少川从王亚樵话里听出他虽对蒋介石仇恨多年，但认为此时还不到向蒋下手的时机。情知作为朋友不好强勉，就说："九光兄，我也是受人之托，才来找你疏通此事的。因胡汉民的亲家认为，天下第一好汉当属你王亚樵了，唯有你才能够救胡汉民。但是，现在仁兄有所顾虑，这也在情理之中。我也不好强勉你一定行动。但我仍然相信你王亚樵会为国除害，因为你毕竟是有血性的中国人啊！"

Li Shaochuan heard in this that Wang Yaqiao's hatred of Chiang Kai-shek was of long standing but that he judged the hour not yet come. Knowing that a friend should not press too hard, he said: "Brother Jiuguang, I come at another's asking, and only to open the matter with you. Hu Hanmin's kinsman believes that the first good man under heaven is Wang Yaqiao, and that you alone can save Hu Hanmin. But you have your reservations, and that too stands to reason. I will not force you to act. And still I believe that you, Wang Yaqiao, will rid the country of this evil — because you are, when everything else is said, a Chinese with blood in him!"

> "好吧，少川兄。"王亚樵见李少川把话说到这份上，情知不能再做推托，想了许久，终于说道："此事关系重大，再容兄弟认真思考几日，再做决定，如何？"李少川见他终于应允，心中大喜，急忙告辞说："如此最好，这才是一个好汉说的话。不过，此事无论做与不做，都望仁兄千万保密才好。"

"Very well, brother Shaochuan." With the thing put to him in those terms, Wang Yaqiao knew he could not go on refusing. He thought a long while and at last said: "This is a matter of the gravest consequence. Allow me a few days to think it through seriously before I decide. Would that do?" Delighted to have his assent at last, Li Shaochuan rose at once to take his leave. "Best so — that is a good man's answer. Only, whether the thing is done or not done, I beg my brother keep it absolutely secret."

> 李少川走后，王亚樵独自饮酒沉思。望着对面墙上的一幅斧头帮静安寺大聚会的照片，王亚樵不禁又想起自创立斧头帮以来他所经历的重重险恶。特别是1928年秋天在南京出席国民政府成立大会时，蒋介石密派戴笠对他刺杀的往事更让他心惊肉跳。他知道那次行刺，如不是戴笠率"十人团"向他洪武街住地扑来时及时开枪报警，那么，那天晚上他也许当真惨死在"十人团"的手里。憎恨蒋介石他就不免想起戴笠，而戴笠又让王亚樵心中怆然。

After Li Shaochuan had gone, Wang Yaqiao sat drinking alone with his thoughts. Looking at the photograph on the opposite wall — the great Hatchet Gang assembly at Jing'an Temple — he found himself running back over all the dangers he had come through since founding the gang. Above all, the memory of the autumn of 1928, when he attended the founding assembly of the National Government at Nanjing and Chiang Kai-shek secretly sent Dai Li to kill him, still turned his heart cold. He knew that had Dai Li not fired his warning shot as the Ten-Man Team closed on his lodging in Hongwu Street, he might well have died that night at their hands. And hating Chiang Kai-shek, he could not help thinking of Dai Li — and thinking of Dai Li left a desolation in him.

> "大哥，你是我的救命恩人。今生今世，我们不能同时同日生，但愿同时同日死。"王亚樵眼前出现的是1924年在上海安徽会馆和戴笠歃血为盟的情景。那是他从车下救出戴春风不久，这个从浙江来沪乞讨为生的汉子，有一天竟忽然找上门来。那时的戴春风已脱去了叫花子的破衣裤，换上了他赠送的一套新西装，显得英武逼人。王亚樵抬头一看，误以为认错了人。原来洗去脸上血污秽迹的戴春风竟也相貌堂堂，眉宇间有一股豪气。王亚樵将戴上下打量了一番，当即说："戴春风，看你的相貌，现在虽是个寄人篱下之人，可久后你必有出头之日。如你不嫌弃的话，索性就在我的会馆里住下吧。"

"Elder brother, you are the man who saved my life. In this life we cannot be born on the same day, but I pray we may die on the same day." What rose before Wang Yaqiao's eyes was the scene in the Anhui Guildhall in 1924, when he and Dai Li smeared blood in covenant. It was not long after he had pulled Dai Chunfeng out from under the car, and the man who had come from Zhejiang to beg his living in Shanghai turned up one day at his gate. By then Dai Chunfeng had shed the beggar's rags for a new Western suit that Wang Yaqiao had given him, and he cut a strikingly martial figure. Wang Yaqiao looked up and thought for a moment he had the wrong man. Washed clean of the blood and filth, Dai Chunfeng proved to be a handsome fellow with a certain boldness between the brows. Wang Yaqiao looked him up and down and said at once: "Dai Chunfeng — by your face, you may be living under another man's roof today, but your day will come. If you don't think it beneath you, stay here in my guildhall."

> "大哥，谢谢你的救命之恩。将来有一天我借兄吉言，真能发迹显身时，绝不会忘记今日之事。"戴春风见王亚樵如此厚待自己，立即跪倒在地，磕了一个响头。

"Elder brother, thank you for saving my life. If one day your good words come true and I really do rise in the world, I will never forget what has been done for me today." Seeing himself treated so generously, Dai Chunfeng dropped to his knees on the spot and knocked his head on the floor.

> 从那时起，戴春风就视王亚樵为恩人兄长。他处处尊王亚樵的旨意行事，在十里洋场倒也很快闯出了名堂。王亚樵发现戴春风虽出身低微，但不失为有勇有谋的机灵人物，便处处重用他。有时还带着他出席各种有头面人物出席的酒会，所以从那时起戴春风就借助王亚樵的关系，结识了戴季陶、胡宗南等后来成为国民党要人的朋友。也就在上海期间，经戴春风多次请求，喜欢交结朋友的王亚樵同意和他结为八拜之交。但是，大出王亚樵意外的是，戴春风1925年忽然接到已去广州的戴季陶来信，信中说："现在蒋介石正在这里组建自己的力量，在孙中山先生指示下成立黄埔军校，你何不来此深造，以求将来有个发达之日……"

From that time Dai Chunfeng regarded Wang Yaqiao as benefactor and elder brother. He acted in everything by Wang Yaqiao's wishes, and made a name for himself quickly enough in the ten-li foreign strip. Wang Yaqiao saw that Dai Chunfeng, though of low birth, was a quick-witted man with both courage and cunning, and used him everywhere. Sometimes he brought him along to banquets where men of standing gathered — and it was thus, through Wang Yaqiao's connections, that Dai Chunfeng came to know Dai Jitao and Hu Zongnan and others who would later be men of weight in the Nationalist Party. It was in this Shanghai period too that, at Dai Chunfeng's repeated urging, the friendship-loving Wang Yaqiao agreed to bind himself to him in the eight-bow brotherhood. But then, wholly against Wang Yaqiao's expectation, in 1925 Dai Chunfeng received a letter from Dai Jitao, who had gone to Guangzhou. It said: "Chiang Kai-shek is building his own strength here, and under Mr. Sun Yat-sen's direction the Whampoa Academy has been founded. Why not come and study, and look for a day of advancement…"

> "大哥，既然帮派不如从军有前程，那我还是到广州去投戴季陶吧？"大约就是那年的秋天，戴春风终于向王亚樵道出了他心中秘密：想去广东投奔黄埔军校。王亚樵知道戴春风投奔戴季陶只是借口，实际是去投奔蒋介石。尽管王亚樵从心里看不起蒋介石，但他不想因为戴春风是他的磕头弟兄，就以兄长的威严逼迫戴春风改变投奔军界的意愿，他毅然对戴春风说："你我虽是弟兄，但我从不勉强别人。人各有志，你只管前去广东好了！但愿你将来飞黄腾达的时候，别忘记你我结拜的情谊。如你能以友情为重，我王九光也就没枉和你相交一场！"

"Elder brother — since the gangs offer less of a future than the army, hadn't I better go to Guangzhou and join Dai Jitao?" It was about the autumn of that year that Dai Chunfeng finally told Wang Yaqiao his secret: he wanted to go to Guangdong and enter the Whampoa Academy. Wang Yaqiao knew that Dai Jitao was only the pretext and Chiang Kai-shek the destination. Yet though he despised Chiang Kai-shek in his heart, he would not use an elder brother's authority to force his sworn junior to give up the army, and told him firmly: "We are brothers, but I have never compelled any man. Every man has his own ambition; go to Guangdong if you will! Only, when you have risen high, don't forget the bond we swore. If you can weigh friendship heavily, then I, Wang Jiuguang, will not have known you in vain!"

> 戴春风听了这话，扑通一声跪倒在地，当场给王亚樵磕了三个响头，声泪俱下地发誓："大哥，请相信我戴春风绝不是忘恩负义的小人，如我真有出头之日，绝不忘记大哥当初的相救之恩。"

At this Dai Chunfeng dropped to his knees with a thud, knocked his head three times before Wang Yaqiao, and swore with tears running down his face: "Elder brother, believe me — Dai Chunfeng is no ingrate. If my day truly comes, I will never forget that you saved me."

> 分手以后，王亚樵不时探听戴春风去南粤投军的情况。但是王亚樵后来得到的信息，却每每让他失望。戴春风果真在戴季陶介绍下进了黄埔军校炮兵科，没有想到后来他却在北伐过程中，投靠到蒋介石身边去了。当北伐军打到武汉时，戴春风竟然还改了一个名字，叫做戴笠。据说此名还是蒋介石亲自为他改的。从那时起，戴笠就成了专为蒋介石刺探情报和暗杀党内反对派的特务"十人团"领班了。

After they parted, Wang Yaqiao kept an ear out for news of Dai Chunfeng's soldiering in the south. What came back disappointed him again and again. Dai Chunfeng did indeed enter the Whampoa Academy's artillery section on Dai Jitao's introduction — and then, in the course of the Northern Expedition, went over to Chiang Kai-shek's side. By the time the expeditionary army reached Wuhan, Dai Chunfeng had even changed his name, to Dai Li; and the name, they said, was given him by Chiang Kai-shek himself. From that time Dai Li was foreman of the Ten-Man Team, the special-service outfit that gathered intelligence and murdered the party's opponents for Chiang Kai-shek alone.

> "大哥，您还记得我吗？我就是当年在上海险些成了车下之鬼的戴春风啊！"大约在1927年1月，戴笠追随已升任国民革命军总司令的蒋介石从湖北来南昌以后，他曾奉蒋之命前往上海联络各路人马。这时，他又来到从前曾和王亚樵换帖拜把子的安徽会馆。那时出现在王亚樵面前的戴笠，已是军装笔挺、枪刺披挂的北伐军英武的军官了。那时的戴笠果然没敢淡忘王亚樵从前对他的知遇之恩，见面后依然执弟子之礼。对酒恳谈，俨如久别弟兄，戴笠振振有词地向这位斧头帮首领讲了他当年离开上海后的情况，特别说到他在北伐路上如何和蒋介石的结识。当然，戴笠不忘这次奉蒋之命，来沪联络各派政治势力的初衷，他竟向王亚樵夸奖起蒋介石来，说蒋如何如何喜爱人才，如何如何器重各派政治人物。最后，戴笠终于露出了他求见王亚樵的本意，郑重地说："大哥，依您的才华能力，虽在上海滩上可呼风唤雨，但您现在终究只是个帮派首领，孤掌难鸣，纵有鸿鹄之志，却终难成大气候。依愚弟之见，大哥倒不如和我一样，去南昌投奔蒋先生。我敢保证，您只要投到蒋先生麾下，肯定会前程无量。如果大哥弄得好，甚至可以得个将军部长当当。到那时候，还不比在上海经营个小小斧头帮强上百倍吗？所以，我劝大哥你……"

"Elder brother — do you still know me? I am the Dai Chunfeng who nearly became a ghost under a car in Shanghai!" It was about January 1927, after Dai Li had followed Chiang Kai-shek — by then commander-in-chief of the National Revolutionary Army — from Hubei to Nanchang, that he was sent on Chiang's orders to Shanghai to make contact with the various forces there. And so he came again to the Anhui Guildhall where he and Wang Yaqiao had exchanged cards and sworn brotherhood. The Dai Li who stood before Wang Yaqiao now was a smart officer of the Northern Expedition army, uniform pressed, bayonet at his belt. He had not, it seemed, forgotten the debt he owed, and greeted Wang Yaqiao with a disciple's courtesy. Over wine they talked warmly, like brothers long parted, and Dai Li gave the Hatchet Gang's chief a fluent account of his life since leaving Shanghai, dwelling in particular on how he had come to know Chiang Kai-shek on the road north. Nor, of course, did he forget the errand that had brought him: he began actually praising Chiang Kai-shek to Wang Yaqiao — how Chiang loved talent, how he valued political men of every stripe. And at last Dai Li came out with his real purpose, and said gravely: "Elder brother, with your gifts you may summon wind and rain on the Shanghai waterfront — but in the end you are only a gang chief. One palm cannot clap alone, and though you have the ambitions of a swan you will never make real weather this way. In your younger brother's view, elder brother would do better to do as I have done and go to Nanchang and join Mr. Chiang. I guarantee that once you are under his command your prospects will be without limit. Play it well, and you might even come out a general or a minister. Would that not be a hundred times better than running a little hatchet gang in Shanghai? So I urge you, elder brother…"

> 王亚樵听了这话，心里虽不以为然，但他并没当面责怪戴笠的有奶便是娘。他只将头轻轻一摇："春风，你以为在当今世界，能到国民党里弄个一官半职，就是最大的荣幸吗？其实不然，我仍然坚信当年你去广东时对你说的话：人各有志，不可强勉。你戴春风既然有做官的瘾，就到老蒋那里求官好了。至于我王九光，从生下的那一天起，就注定不是做官的料。特别是那个姓蒋的，我和他从不是一条道上跑的车，你还是不要再替他游说的好！"

Wang Yaqiao thought little of it, but he did not reproach Dai Li to his face for going wherever the milk was. He only shook his head slightly. "Chunfeng, do you imagine that in this world of ours the greatest honor a man can have is some petty office in the Nationalist Party? Not so. I still hold to what I said when you went to Guangdong: every man has his own ambition, and none should be compelled. If you have the itch for office, then go and ask Chiang for one. As for me — from the day I was born I was never the stuff officials are made of. And as for that Chiang: he and I were never carts on the same road. You had better not go on canvassing for him."

> 戴笠知道王亚樵的品性人格，绝非以高官厚禄就可说动的。谈了两天，没有结果，他只好灰溜溜而退，临别时戴笠又再三向王亚樵拱手拍胸，信誓旦旦说："大哥，虽然你我今后走的是两股道，可我戴春风是个不忘旧情的人。只要哪一天大哥有事用我，我戴春风定是肝脑涂地，在所不辞！"

Dai Li knew Wang Yaqiao's character well enough to know that high rank and rich pay would never move him. Two days of talk brought nothing, and he withdrew crestfallen. At parting he cupped his hands again and struck his chest, swearing oath on oath: "Elder brother, though our roads run apart from now on, Dai Chunfeng does not forget an old kindness. The day you have need of me, I will spill my brains on the ground and not decline it!"

> 后来，蒋介石从南昌移师南京，并利用权术弄到了国民政府主席兼国民党军事委员会委员长要职，只因王亚樵在庆祝大会的讲台上，讲了几句拥护孙中山三民主义的话，就惹来蒋介石对他的杀身之祸，若不是戴笠暗中相助，那天晚上他也许真惨死在"十人团"乱枪之下了。

Later Chiang Kai-shek moved his army from Nanchang to Nanjing and, by political maneuver, secured the chairmanship of the National Government together with the chairmanship of the Nationalist Party's Military Affairs Commission — and merely because Wang Yaqiao spoke a few words from the platform of the celebratory assembly in support of Sun Yat-sen's Three Principles of the People, he brought Chiang Kai-shek's murder down on himself. Had Dai Li not helped him from the shadows, he might truly have died that night under the Ten-Man Team's guns.

> 现在，当王亚樵想起自孙中山病殁，蒋介石上台以来的种种倒行逆施，心里感慨万千。刚才好友李少川又代胡汉民亲家林焕庭到他家里苦苦求助，点燃了他心中的新仇旧恨。"杀了这独夫民贼，为国人除害！"但当他想到蒋介石身边那些佩带德国枪械的侍从人员，王亚樵又犹豫起来。正在他拿不定主意的时候，一个女人悄悄走进来，他抬头一看，原来是他那秀气聪慧的妻子王亚英！

Now, thinking back over everything Chiang Kai-shek had done since Sun Yat-sen's death and his own rise, Wang Yaqiao's feelings ran a thousand ways. And his friend Li Shaochuan had just come to his house to plead, on behalf of Hu Hanmin's kinsman Lin Huanting — and lit the old hatred and the new together. Kill this autocrat, this thief of the people, and rid the nation of him! But when he thought of the attendants around Chiang Kai-shek with their German weapons, Wang Yaqiao wavered again. And as he sat there unable to decide, a woman came quietly in. He looked up: his elegant, clear-minded wife, Wang Yaying.

> "亚英，你来得正好！"王亚樵的眼睛顿时一亮。

"Yaying — you come at exactly the right moment!" Wang Yaqiao's eyes lit at once.

> 王亚英见多日不曾饮酒的丈夫，今日竟一人喝得脸膛潮红，心里不由暗暗吃惊，便问道："九光，今日为何事如此酗酒？"

Seeing her husband, who had not touched wine for days, drinking alone until his face was flushed, Wang Yaying felt a private alarm. "Jiuguang — what has happened, that you drink like this today?"

> 王亚樵借助酒力，索性将李少川前来替胡汉民说项，请求他和斧头帮弟兄们为国除害，行刺蒋介石，以及他对刺蒋的种种忧虑都细说一番。王亚樵最后说："亚英，若依我多年夙愿，早就想把姓蒋的碎尸万段，方解心头之恨了。因他蒋某人对我也是恨之入骨啊。只是我想，行刺蒋介石干系太大，如果我们稍有疏忽，就会反遭侍卫人员乱枪射杀，所以我一时拿不定主意。"

With the wine to help him, Wang Yaqiao simply told her the whole of it: Li Shaochuan's coming to plead for Hu Hanmin, the request that he and his Hatchet Gang brothers rid the nation of its evil by killing Chiang Kai-shek, and every misgiving he had about it. He finished: "Yaying, if I followed the wish I have carried for years, I would long since have torn that Chiang to a thousand pieces and eased my heart. He hates me to the marrow as it is. Only — killing Chiang Kai-shek touches too much. The smallest carelessness and we are shot down by his guards. So for the moment I cannot decide."

> 王亚英理解丈夫的苦衷，知他既想为国锄奸暗杀蒋介石，却又担心种种行刺后果。如是一般女子，必然出面劝阻，可是王亚英从少年时起就是一位志向远大的女子。从前凡是王亚樵想做重大案子的时候，都必问计于王亚英，然后再做最后的决定。如今面临着是否行刺党国要人蒋介石的大事，就连王亚英听了也感心绪紧张。她来到桌前，自斟一杯水酒饮下，本来白皙丰满的面庞变得状如桃花。她坐下沉吟片刻，忽问丈夫："九光，我想问你一事。你究竟想从此庸庸碌碌一生，还是希望自己青史留名呢？"

Wang Yaying understood her husband's difficulty: he wanted to purge the nation's traitor and feared every consequence of the attempt. An ordinary woman would have come forward to dissuade him. But Wang Yaying had been a woman of far-reaching ambition since her girlhood, and in the past, whenever Wang Yaqiao contemplated a great case, he had always asked her counsel before making his decision. Now, facing the question of whether to assassinate the ruling figure of the party and the state, even Wang Yaying felt her nerves tighten. She came to the table, poured herself a cup and drank it down, and her fair, full face took on the color of peach blossom. She sat, thought a moment, and then asked him suddenly: "Jiuguang, I want to ask you one thing. Do you mean to live out your days in mediocrity — or do you hope your name will stand in history?"

> 王亚樵听了一愣，半晌没有作答。妻子那双深邃的目光正直视着他，似已洞悉他的内心。王亚樵思考良久，毅然点头："大丈夫顶天立地，我当然不甘做浑浑噩噩的酒囊饭袋！"

Wang Yaqiao was startled and for a long moment made no answer. His wife's deep gaze was fixed on him, as though she had already seen into him. He thought a long while, and then nodded, hard. "A great man stands between heaven and earth. Of course I have no wish to be a muddled wine-sack and rice-bag!"

> "那么，你是想成就一番大事了？"她追问。

"Then you mean to accomplish something great?" she pressed.

> 王亚樵郑重地说："青史留名自不敢当。不过，我王九光绝不是个没志气的孬种软蛋。不知此话和刺蒋有何关系？"

"To stand in history I would not presume," Wang Yaqiao said gravely. "But Wang Jiuguang is certainly no spiritless coward, no soft egg. What has this to do with killing Chiang?"

> 王亚英借助酒力吐出肺腑之言："如你真要做顶天立地男儿汉，那么李少川所求之事，就是你最好的机会了。"

The wine drew out what was in her heart. "If you truly mean to be a man who stands between heaven and earth, then what Li Shaochuan asks of you is your best opportunity."

> "此话怎讲？"

"How so?"

> "非常简单。你想，蒋介石既然是个逆历史潮流而动的国民革命的叛徒，那么，你如果把他一枪打死了，天下人谁会不赞成你王亚樵呢？"

"It is very simple. Consider: since Chiang Kai-shek is a traitor to the national revolution who moves against the current of history, then if you put a bullet in him, who under heaven would not approve of you?"

> 王亚樵没有想到妻子居然会出语惊人，半晌他问："亚英，如此看来，我想向姓蒋开枪你不反对？"

Wang Yaqiao had not expected words like these from his wife, and after a moment he asked: "Yaying — then you do not oppose my firing on Chiang?"

> "当然，如夫君果然成此大事，必定成为千古英雄，这是毫无疑问的。谁都知道蒋介石现在是利用了孙中山先生忠实信徒的身份，才成为国民党首脑的。正是因他上台后就大肆破坏孙先生的三民主义，屠杀革命党人，才激起了万民共愤。所以，我们刺杀蒋介石，不仅在为营救胡汉民一人，重要的是为国民铲锄大奸呀！"王亚樵知道妻子一旦说话，必然头头是道，计谋也高人一筹。现在她已倾心冒险行刺蒋介石，这就越加打动了王亚樵的心。

"Of course not. If my husband accomplishes it, he will beyond all question be a hero of the ages. Everyone knows Chiang Kai-shek became the head of the Nationalist Party by trading on his standing as Mr. Sun Yat-sen's faithful disciple. It is precisely because he set about destroying Mr. Sun's Three Principles and butchering revolutionaries the moment he took power that the anger of ten thousand people rose against him. So in killing Chiang Kai-shek we do not act to save one Hu Hanmin — what matters is that we hoe out a great traitor for the nation!" Wang Yaqiao knew that once his wife spoke, the argument would march in order and the strategy stand a head above others. That she had now set her heart on the risk moved him further still.

> "亚英，你说得好！"王亚樵以敬佩的眼神凝视着出语惊人的妻子，刚才的一番话说得他不再犹豫，他冲动地说："夫人一介女流，也敢面对枪林弹雨。我王亚樵一个汉子，为什么面对强敌要心软手虚呢？纵然为刺蒋壮烈一死，也死得其所呀！不过，杀蒋绝非儿戏，我们必须认真筹划。不到行刺方案天衣无缝之时，我们是绝不能动手的。"王亚英沉着而又机敏。她既支持丈夫在举国仇恨蒋介石的大好时机，挺身而出毅然刺蒋，同时她又冷静心细，料事如神。她见王亚樵杀蒋决心已定，反而又劝他说："每临大事，务要冷静。不冷静非但杀不得蒋，反倒让自己陷身绝境。所以，此事不可心急，等我们先派出几路人马去南京，把蒋介石的行动规律都摸清以后才能下手。"

"Yaying, well said!" Wang Yaqiao gazed at his startling wife with admiration; her speech had put the last of his hesitation to flight. "My wife, a mere woman, dares face a storm of bullets. Why should I, Wang Yaqiao, a grown man, go soft in the heart and weak in the hand before a strong enemy? Even to die gloriously for the killing of Chiang would be to die in the right place! Only — killing Chiang is no child's game. We must plan it in earnest. Until the plan is seamless as heaven's own robe, we do not move." Wang Yaying was composed and quick at once. She supported her husband in rising to kill Chiang at this best of hours, when the whole country hated him; and she stayed cool and exact, and reckoned the future like a diviner. Seeing his mind now made up, she turned round and urged restraint: "In any great matter, be cool. Without coolness you will not only fail to kill Chiang, you will put yourself past all escape. So there must be no haste. Let us first send several parties to Nanjing and learn the pattern of Chiang Kai-shek's movements. Only then do we act."

> 王亚樵心悦诚服地说："好，就依夫人的主意行事！"

Wang Yaqiao said, wholly convinced: "Good. We do it by my wife's plan!"

> 就在王亚樵夫妇暗中密议刺蒋的时候，远在广州的孙科等人也在计议杀蒋之事。那时刚从国外归来的国民党中央常委汪精卫也从南京飞来广州，一时，国民党大员云集南粤，各路反蒋大军在羊城布阵，大有当年孙中山在此组成临时政府之势。就在各派反蒋势力筹划成立广州临时政府的同时，在汪精卫白云山下的别墅里，也在暗中筹划一个非常重大的事情——就是尽快刺杀蒋介石。

And while Wang Yaqiao and his wife were laying their secret plans against Chiang, Sun Ke and the others, far off in Guangzhou, were debating the same killing. Wang Jingwei, member of the Nationalist Central Standing Committee, newly returned from abroad, had flown down from Nanjing to Guangzhou; and for a season the party's grandees gathered thick in the south, with the anti-Chiang forces of every road drawn up in the city — much as Sun Yat-sen had once formed a provisional government there. And while these factions were planning a provisional government at Guangzhou, in Wang Jingwei's villa at the foot of Baiyun Mountain another matter of the greatest weight was being laid in secret: the killing of Chiang Kai-shek, and quickly.

> "如果在这时候把蒋介石杀掉，不但能保住胡汉民先生的性命，又会让蒋介石的南京政府处于群龙无首的境地。到那时候，我们广州就可成为中国革命的前哨阵地了。"这是汪精卫在刺蒋秘密会议上的发言。

"If Chiang Kai-shek is killed at this moment, we not only preserve Mr. Hu Hanmin's life; we leave his Nanjing government a body of dragons without a head. And then Guangzhou becomes the forward post of the Chinese revolution." So Wang Jingwei spoke at the secret meeting on the killing of Chiang.

> 孙科自然对杀蒋最为积极，他说："对于蒋介石这个三民主义的叛徒，即便家父在世，也会赞成我们对他采取强硬手段的。现在的困难是，蒋介石在南京作威作福，他早把自己当成了袁世凯。身边有那么多侍从警卫，每天24小时值班坚守，一般的刺客恐怕难以近身。所以，只有求助那个在上海曾杀过赵铁桥的安徽人王亚樵了。"

Sun Ke was naturally the most eager of them. "As for Chiang Kai-shek, that traitor to the Three Principles — were my father alive, he too would approve strong measures. The difficulty is that Chiang Kai-shek lords it in Nanjing, and has long since made himself into a Yuan Shikai. He has so many attendants and guards about him, on watch twenty-four hours a day, that an ordinary assassin could hardly get near him. So there is nothing for it but to seek out that Anhui man in Shanghai, Wang Yaqiao, who killed Zhao Tieqiao."

> 唐生智叹息："据我从上海得到的情报说，李少川虽已和王亚樵谈过多次，可是，此人却始终没下最后的决心。看起来王亚樵正在等我们广州的态度，然后才能决定是否行事。"

Tang Shengzhi sighed. "By the intelligence I have from Shanghai, Li Shaochuan has spoken with Wang Yaqiao a number of times, and the man still has not made his final decision. It looks as though Wang Yaqiao is waiting to see what attitude we in Guangzhou take before he decides whether to act."

> 汪精卫当年也曾有去北京行刺摄政王的经历，颇为在行地说："王亚樵迟迟不肯行动，也是可以理解的。我当年去北京行刺时也是曲折多多。更何况现在蒋介石有重兵守卫，难以近身呢？我想王亚樵既已默许了替我们除害，那么，他就必有下手杀蒋的能力。现在的问题是，我们不能只这样以口头上对他许诺，而应该来一些实际上的支持才行。到那时候，他收到了咱们的订金，就不会继续这样迟迟不动手了。"

Wang Jingwei, who had himself once gone to Beijing to assassinate the Prince Regent, spoke as one who knew the trade. "That Wang Yaqiao is slow to move is understandable. My own attempt in Beijing had twists enough. And how much more so now, when Chiang Kai-shek is guarded by whole companies and hard to approach? I think that since Wang Yaqiao has tacitly agreed to rid us of this evil, he must have the means to do it. The problem is that we cannot go on promising him things by word of mouth; we must give him some support in fact. Once he has our deposit in hand, he will not go on holding back."

> "对对，如若让王亚樵替我们除去国贼大奸，势必要有一笔行动经费才行。"孙科这才忽然醒悟，"我们既然求他替我们除掉蒋某人，为什么不马上给他备足行动经费呢？"

"Just so — if Wang Yaqiao is to remove this traitor for us, there must be a fund for the operation." Sun Ke woke to it suddenly. "Since we are asking him to kill Chiang for us, why do we not equip him properly at once?"

> 唐生智说："现在我们的经费也捉襟见肘，又如何支付一笔昂贵的行刺经费呢。"汪精卫将拳头在桌上重重一捣道："如果为了砍掉那颗专与革命为敌的头颅，我们就是少开几个月军饷又有何妨？如果我们一时无法筹集到这笔开支，那么，我情愿自己解囊，也要满足王亚樵的所用之需！"

Tang Shengzhi said: "Our own funds are stretched thin as it is. How are we to pay out a costly assassination fund?" Wang Jingwei brought his fist down hard on the table. "If it is to cut off that head which is the sworn enemy of the revolution, what harm in paying the troops a few months less? And if we cannot raise the sum at once, then I will open my own purse rather than leave Wang Yaqiao short of what he needs!"

> 孙科立刻赞成汪精卫的主张，决计慨然捐款，说："好，既然杀蒋是我们的当务之急，索性就由我们这些人来解囊吧，不愁凑不齐那20万元经费！"

Sun Ke seconded him at once and resolved to give generously. "Good. Since killing Chiang is our first business, let us of this company open our purses. We shall not fail to scrape together the two hundred thousand!"

> 不久，由汪精卫、孙科、唐生智等各路大员筹集了行刺经费20余万。孙科和汪精卫商议后，决定派两个最可靠的亲信，秘密携带装有巨款的箱子，悄悄从广东乘火车，再经香港搭船来到了上海。

Before long Wang Jingwei, Sun Ke, Tang Shengzhi and other grandees had raised more than two hundred thousand for the operation. Sun Ke and Wang Jingwei conferred and decided to send two of their most trusted men, carrying a case of the money in secret, quietly out of Guangdong by train, then by ship through Hong Kong to Shanghai.

> "王先生，这可是广东西南派将领们倾全力集来的行动经费，足见汪先生和孙先生对您的重视啊！"当两位使者秘密来到法租界浦柏路王宅，将装满钞票的箱子摆在王亚樵面前的时候，正是王亚樵暗中策划刺蒋最紧张的时期。他见广东方面送来大笔钱款，当即表示拒绝说："汪先生和孙先生也太小瞧我王九光了，他们以为我王九光是为等他们的经费才迟迟不肯下手吗？其实不然，自从李少川委托我办这事以来，眨眼一个月过去了。你们哪里知道，我们在这里已经秘密开了多少次会议了？只要是我王亚樵想干的事情，就是一分钱经费没有，我也会去做的。否则，如果你们让我杀的人是正义君子，你们就是出百万元经费，我王某人也是绝不会干的。"

"Mr. Wang, this is the operating fund the Southwest commanders of Guangdong have raised with all their strength. It shows you what weight Mr. Wang Jingwei and Mr. Sun Ke place on you!" When the two envoys came secretly to the house on Rue Pottier in the French Concession and set the case of banknotes in front of Wang Yaqiao, he was at the tensest stage of his own planning. Seeing the great sum from Guangdong, he refused it on the spot. "Mr. Wang Jingwei and Mr. Sun Ke think too little of Wang Jiuguang. Do they suppose I have held back all this while waiting for their money? Not at all. It is a month to the day since Li Shaochuan first put this business in my hands. How would you know how many secret meetings we have held here already? Whatever I, Wang Yaqiao, mean to do, I would do without a single copper of funding. And conversely — if the man you asked me to kill were a righteous gentleman, you could put up a million and Wang would not touch it."

> 送钱者苦苦相劝："从前都说你王亚樵为人仗义，现在我亲眼见了，才知道果然名不虚传。既然王先生和我们一样仇恨蒋某人，那么，又为何拒绝这笔行动经费呢？因为这笔经费是你们行动中必不可少的。如果王先生拒绝接收，岂不冷了广东那些支持者的心吗？"

The money-bearers pleaded hard. "It was always said that Wang Yaqiao was a man of honor, and now I have seen it with my own eyes and know the name is no empty one. But since Mr. Wang hates Chiang as we do, why refuse the operating fund? It is a thing your action cannot do without. And if Mr. Wang refuses it, does that not chill the hearts of your supporters in Guangdong?"

> "也好。"王亚樵见来者说得恳切，又听说广东方面对他们斧头帮寄予厚望，最后还是收下了这笔款子。

"Very well." Seeing the earnestness of it, and hearing what hopes Guangdong placed in the Hatchet Gang, Wang Yaqiao took the money in the end.

> 5月下旬的一个午后，古城南京下起了霏霏细雨。

One afternoon in late May, a fine drizzle came down over the old city of Nanjing.

> 就在这天傍晚，一艘从上海开来的客轮徐徐驶进了浦口码头。当时小雨下得淅淅沥沥，整个古城笼罩在一片浓黑的雨云之下。从客轮上走下的旅客中间，有位披着黄色雨衣的青年人，他生得文质彬彬，鼻梁上一架黑边眼镜后藏着一双睿智的眼睛。他就是从东北逃到南方后，在上海淞沪抗战期间加入王亚樵"铁血锄奸团"的孙凤鸣。此时他站在细雨蒙蒙的码头上，透过如烟似雾的雨幕，凝望着远方的巍峨钟山，耳畔响起王亚樵的叮嘱："凤鸣，现在我们正做着日后将载入史册的一件大事，希望你到南京以后，尽快把蒋某人的行踪搞清，争取尽快行动。"

Toward evening a passenger steamer up from Shanghai eased in to the Pukou wharf. The rain came steadily, and the whole old city lay under a thick black canopy of cloud. Among the passengers who came down the gangway was a young man in a yellow oilskin, a cultivated-looking man with a pair of shrewd eyes behind black-rimmed spectacles. This was Sun Fengming, who had fled the Northeast for the south and, during the Shanghai fighting, joined Wang Yaqiao's Iron and Blood Traitor-Purging Corps. Standing now on the wharf in the misting rain, gazing through a veil of water at the far bulk of Zhongshan, he heard again Wang Yaqiao's charge to him: "Fengming, we are doing a thing now that will one day stand in the histories. When you reach Nanjing, get Chiang's movements clear as fast as you can, and act as soon as you are able."

> "凤鸣，我们已经等候你多时了。"孙凤鸣急忙回头一看，发现一辆小汽车已从泥泞小路上驶过来了，从车里走出一位身材窈窕的女人，她就是孙凤鸣新婚的妻子崔正瑶。她身后又走出个男人，他认出正是王亚樵派往南京监视蒋介石行迹的军人余立奎。

"Fengming — we have been waiting a good while for you." Sun Fengming turned quickly to see a small car coming up the muddy lane; out of it stepped a slender woman, his newly married wife Cui Zhengyao. And behind her a man: Yu Likui, the soldier Wang Yaqiao had posted to Nanjing to watch Chiang Kai-shek's movements.

> 孙凤鸣和崔正瑶也不多说，就拉着她的手急忙钻进汽车，一路上和余立奎悄悄谈着王亚樵对他的指令。孙凤鸣告诉余立奎，进入5月以来，王亚樵等人一直在上海加紧策划刺蒋行动，现在已到了最后行动关头。孙凤鸣记得早从4月上旬开始，他就随在金陵大学领导学联斗争的华克之，从南京秘密前往上海浦柏路王亚樵的小楼，参与了他领导的"铁血暗杀团"。那是一个民间的抗日团体，孙凤鸣在"暗杀团"里结识了许多志同道合者。这些人大多是王亚樵手下的斧头帮成员，但是，知识分子出身的孙凤鸣发现，王亚樵并不像民间传说的那样浑身匪气，他感到王亚樵非但不是令人谈虎色变的杀人魔王，而且还是颇有知识分子气质的文人。王亚樵虽多次在安徽和南京、上海制造惊天动地的血案，甚至亲自动手行刺杀人；可是，当孙凤鸣与他接触的时候，才发现他原来很富有人情味。有一天，他和王亚樵去"老正兴菜馆"吃饭，席间听他谈了许多与杀人无关的趣事。特别是谈起江南的风土人情来，王亚樵真可谓头头是道，如数家珍，让当过兵的孙凤鸣感到王亚樵是位和蔼的长者。

Without wasting words, Sun Fengming took his wife's hand and got quickly into the car, and all the way in they talked quietly with Yu Likui about Wang Yaqiao's instructions. Sun Fengming told him that through May, Wang Yaqiao and the others had been pressing the planning in Shanghai, and that it had reached the final stage. He remembered how from early April he had gone with Hua Kezhi — who led the student-union struggle at Jinling University — secretly from Nanjing to Wang Yaqiao's little house on Rue Pottier, and joined the Iron and Blood Assassination Corps that Wang led. It was a civilian anti-Japanese body, and in it Sun Fengming had met many of like mind. Most were Hatchet Gang men under Wang Yaqiao; but Sun Fengming, an educated man himself, discovered that Wang Yaqiao was not the creature of bandit swagger the popular tales made him. He found Wang Yaqiao to be not the murder-king that made men blanch, but a man with a good deal of the scholar in him. Wang Yaqiao had made blood-cases that shook the country, in Anhui and Nanjing and Shanghai, and had killed with his own hands. Yet in Sun Fengming's dealings with him, what he found was a man full of human feeling. One day the two of them went to eat at the Lao Zhengxing restaurant, and over the table Wang Yaqiao talked of many things that had nothing to do with killing — above all the customs and country of Jiangnan, on which he was fluent and exact, reciting it like a man counting his family treasures. To Sun Fengming, who had been a soldier, Wang Yaqiao seemed a kindly elder.

> 自从他经华克之引荐结识王亚樵以后，孙凤鸣才知道王亚樵的暗杀团，实则是一些爱国志士组成的团体。王亚樵每次得到可观的经费后，从来不独吞，而是分给锄奸团的弟兄们。逢年遇节王亚樵还给他们这些投身抗日的青年们，特别支付一笔生活费用。就这样，国难当头之际，孙凤鸣找到了一个让他情愿为之献身的进步暗杀组织。最让孙凤鸣兴奋的是今年春天，华克之对他布置的一个重要任务："凤鸣，王先生可能要对蒋介石采取重大行动了。你敢参加吗？"

Only after Hua Kezhi introduced them did Sun Fengming learn that Wang Yaqiao's assassination corps was in fact a body of patriots. Whenever a substantial sum came in, Wang Yaqiao never kept it to himself but shared it among the corps; at festivals he paid these young men who had given themselves to resisting Japan a living allowance besides. And so, with the nation in extremity, Sun Fengming had found a progressive assassination organization he was willing to die for. What thrilled him most was the important task Hua Kezhi set him this spring: "Fengming, Mr. Wang may be about to take major action against Chiang Kai-shek. Do you dare take part?"

> "他真想刺杀独夫民贼吗？敢，我为什么不敢？"

"He truly means to kill the autocrat and thief of the people? Dare? Why should I not dare?"

> "王先生已经下定决心了。"

"Mr. Wang has made up his mind."

> "太好了，如果真能除掉这个卖国贼，我就是为此抛头洒血，也在所不惜呀！"

"Splendid! If we can really rid the country of this traitor, I would spill my head and my blood for it and not begrudge them!"

> 从小就向往革命，对孙中山三民主义充满热情的进步青年孙凤鸣，早对蒋介石的倒行逆施充满深深的憎恨，得知王亚樵正密谋策划刺蒋，他浑身的热血沸腾了。

Sun Fengming, a progressive young man who had longed for revolution since boyhood and was full of passion for Sun Yat-sen's Three Principles, had long hated Chiang Kai-shek's misrule; and learning that Wang Yaqiao was plotting to kill him, the blood came up boiling through him.

> 进入4月，孙凤鸣和华克之几乎每天都去浦柏路王宅。那里集聚着20多位爱国志士，他们当中还有像陈惘这样的共产党人。由于时代的变迁，王亚樵的暗杀组织中吸收了许多斧头帮之外的人。孙凤鸣在王亚樵的家里结识的爱国青年中，有郑抱真、余立奎、龚春浦、贺光坡、郑绍成、刘文成、郑海龙、谢文达等等。这些人都对孙中山的三民主义充满深深感情，而对蒋介石在上海屠杀工人义愤不已。

Through April, Sun Fengming and Hua Kezhi went almost daily to the house on Rue Pottier. Twenty-odd patriots gathered there, among them Communists such as Chen Wang. With the change of the times, Wang Yaqiao's assassination organization had taken in many who were not of the Hatchet Gang. Among the patriotic young men Sun Fengming met at Wang Yaqiao's house were Zheng Baozhen, Yu Likui, Gong Chunpu, He Guangpo, Zheng Shaocheng, Liu Wencheng, Zheng Hailong and Xie Wenda. All of them felt deeply for Sun Yat-sen's Three Principles, and burned with indignation at Chiang Kai-shek's slaughter of the workers in Shanghai.

> "现在我们马上要把行刺计划变成行动了，而且越快越好。"王亚樵每天夜里都在家召开秘密会议。

"Now we must turn the plan into action at once, and the faster the better." Wang Yaqiao held his secret meetings at home every night.

> "刺蒋无非是两条路。一条是在南京将蒋杀死；第二条是在蒋外出南昌或庐山的半路上，将他的专车炸毁。除此之外没有其他办法。据我在南京的据点近一个月的观察，蒋介石始终深居简出，极难找到行刺的机会。他出行的路线只在南京和庐山之间。我们只能把刺蒋的行动定在这一区域里！"说这话的是奉王亚樵之命在南京监视蒋介石行踪的军人余立奎。

"There are only two roads to killing Chiang. One is to kill him in Nanjing. The second is to blow up his special train on the road when he goes out to Nanchang or Lushan. Beyond those there is no other way. From my post in Nanjing, watching this past month, Chiang Kai-shek keeps close indoors and gives almost no opening at all. The only route he travels is between Nanjing and Lushan. We can only set our action inside that stretch." So spoke Yu Likui, the soldier posted to Nanjing on Wang Yaqiao's orders to watch Chiang's movements.

> "可是如果在南京对蒋下手，也绝非一件易事呀。"华克之也是刺蒋行动中一个关键性人物，他多年在南京搞学运，养成了沉着老练的性格。华克之说："最明显的不利因素，是蒋介石把侍从室的警卫人员扩大了一倍，这些侍从几乎每天寸步不离蒋的身边。只要蒋介石走出黄埔路官邸，那个长长的车队就尾随而来。让人不好下手的是，蒋介石的车队有大小轿车12辆，你不可能知道蒋每次出行坐在哪一辆车里。而且只要我们埋伏的杀手刚刚接近他的车队，汽车里的侍从们就会马上以手里的德国短枪和步枪，组成一道道密集的火网。而我们却没有如此强大的火力，可以击败那些训练有素的侍从人员。"

"But moving against Chiang in Nanjing is no easy business either." Hua Kezhi was another key man in the operation; years of student work in Nanjing had given him a settled, seasoned temper. "The plainest difficulty is that Chiang Kai-shek has doubled the guard strength of the Attendants' Office, and these men are almost never a step from him. The moment Chiang leaves the Huangpu Road residence, that long motorcade follows. What makes him so hard to strike is that his motorcade runs to twelve cars of various sizes, and you cannot know which one Chiang is riding in on any given day. And the moment our killers came near the column, the attendants inside the cars would throw up a dense net of fire with their German pistols and rifles. We have no firepower of that kind, nothing that could beat down men so well trained."

> 余立奎也补充说："至于在蒋的官邸里下手，更是不可能的。因为我亲自去那座大院附近观察了多次，别说从大门进去，就是墙边也无法接近。里面的布局更是不可能知道，即便我们能够进得去，也不能顺利找到蒋的住处。因为那样肯定会落在侍从室的监视罗网之中。所以，在南京行动，只有当蒋将要离开南京到外地巡视的时候，在明故宫机场才能找到下手的机会。"

Yu Likui added: "As for striking inside Chiang's residence, that is still more impossible. I have gone to look at the compound many times myself. Never mind entering by the gate — you cannot even get near the wall. There is no way of knowing the layout inside; and even if we got in, we could not readily find where Chiang lodges, because we would certainly fall into the Attendants' Office's surveillance net. So for action in Nanjing, the only opening is at the Ming Palace airfield, when Chiang is about to leave the city on an inspection."

> "好吧，立奎。你现在就去南京，先在明故宫机场附近租一所房子，以备行动时使用。"最后，王亚樵批准了众人一致认同的方案，利用蒋介石离开南京之时，在明故宫机场向随员不多的蒋氏座车开火。

"Very well, Likui. Go to Nanjing now, and first rent a house near the Ming Palace airfield to use when the time comes." In the end Wang Yaqiao approved the plan they had all agreed on: to fire on Chiang's car at the Ming Palace airfield, with few attendants around him, as he left Nanjing.

> 现在，孙凤鸣就是带着王亚樵的这个密令，从上海来到南京的。他和余立奎来到明故宫附近那所租用的民宅，发现这幢日本式小灰楼内，早已埋伏着七八个杀手，都是余立奎在南京组成的特别行动组成员。他们当中有宣济民、王干庭和牛安如等斧头帮早期成员，同时也有王亚樵在上海新发展的力量，如郑绍成和贺光坡等。

And it was carrying this order of Wang Yaqiao's that Sun Fengming had now come from Shanghai to Nanjing. He and Yu Likui went to the rented house near the Ming Palace, and in that little gray Japanese-style building he found seven or eight killers already lying up — members of the special action group Yu Likui had assembled in Nanjing. Among them were early Hatchet Gang men such as Xuan Jimin, Wang Ganting and Niu Anru, and also the newer strength Wang Yaqiao had developed in Shanghai, such as Zheng Shaocheng and He Guangpo.

> "这是行动经费和枪械。"孙凤鸣将沉甸甸的皮箱放在余立奎等人面前，一口气传达了王亚樵的密令，"现在广东方面催促甚紧。王先生要求我们最迟不能晚于6月初对蒋下手。如我们继续拖延下去，很可能失去最佳刺蒋时机。所以他要求我们马上寻找下手的时机。"

"Here are the funds and the weapons." Sun Fengming set the heavy leather case in front of Yu Likui and the others and passed on Wang Yaqiao's orders in a breath. "Guangdong is pressing hard now. Mr. Wang requires that we move against Chiang no later than early June. If we drag on, we may lose the best moment. So he wants us to find our opening at once."

> 就在孙凤鸣来南京传达王亚樵密令的次日，余立奎从侍从室的内线获悉了一个十分重要的情报：蒋介石决定6月初去江西庐山避暑！

The very day after Sun Fengming came to Nanjing with these orders, Yu Likui learned from his source inside the Attendants' Office a piece of intelligence of the first importance: Chiang Kai-shek had decided to go to Lushan in Jiangxi in early June to escape the heat.

> 这一消息对在明故宫附近民房里坐待时机的行动小组来说，不啻是一大喜讯。余立奎等人都十分清楚，只要蒋介石从南京前去庐山，必然在明故宫机场搭乘专机，到了那时候，他们这支特别行动组，就可就近潜入平时没多少飞机起落的明故宫机场。届时，只要蒋介石从汽车里走出来，他们就可以近距离向蒋开枪将其在登机之前击毙！

For the action group waiting out their hours in the house near the Ming Palace, this was nothing short of splendid news. Yu Likui and the rest understood perfectly: if Chiang Kai-shek went from Nanjing to Lushan, he would take his private aircraft from the Ming Palace airfield — and then their special action group could slip in close to a field where few aircraft came or went at the best of times. Once Chiang Kai-shek stepped out of his car, they could fire on him at short range and kill him before he ever boarded.

> "现在是万事俱备，只欠东风了。"孙凤鸣听余立奎等人汇报的突袭方案，感到万分振奋。当夜，孙凤鸣就携带余立奎的密件，悄悄地返回了上海。在那里，王亚樵和华克之等人正等候他从南京带来的行动方案。

"Now everything stands ready; we lack only the east wind." Hearing Yu Likui's plan of ambush, Sun Fengming was greatly stirred. That same night he carried Yu Likui's sealed report quietly back to Shanghai, where Wang Yaqiao and Hua Kezhi were waiting for the plan he brought from Nanjing.

> 地处九江南端、鄱阳湖畔的庐山，在盛夏到来之时更显出它的雄浑伟岸。

Lushan, at the southern end of Jiujiang by the shore of Poyang Lake, shows its full grandeur when high summer comes.

> 当华克之带着一队精悍刺客，化装成上山的游客，从九江登上庐山的时候，这里正是山风徐来的消夏时节。他和手下八位特别行动队员人人西装革履，风度翩翩，乘坐几乘软轿过了三峡洞，又过了观音桥，直奔含鄱口而来。一路上他坐在那软软颤颤的滑竿上，貌似看遍山景陶然自乐，实则心中万分紧张，他是为暗杀蒋介石才到庐山的。

When Hua Kezhi brought his party of crack assassins up from Jiujiang, disguised as tourists, it was the cool season on the mountain, with the wind coming steadily over it. He and his eight men of the special action team were all in Western suits and elegantly turned out; they took mountain chairs through the Three Gorges Cave and past Guanyin Bridge and made straight for Hanpokou. All the way up, seated on that soft, swaying chair, he had the look of a man drinking in the scenery at his ease. In fact his nerves were drawn to breaking. He had come to Lushan to murder Chiang Kai-shek.

> "华先生，现在我决定分两路向蒋下手。你敢到庐山执行这刺蒋的任务吗？"前天那个难忘的子夜，在上海法租界那幢灯火幽暗的小洋楼里，烟雾弥漫。十多个刺蒋核心人物集聚在王亚樵周围。那时孙凤鸣从南京带来了蒋介石于6月6日上庐山的准确情报，王亚樵连夜召开秘密会议，研究即将开始的刺蒋行动。当时，华克之没想到王亚樵会把上庐山的任务交给他。从王亚樵郑重的神情上看出这是对他的信任。华克之早就对执行刺蒋任务充满信心，他见王亚樵和暗杀团成员都把目光投向他，顿感心血沸腾，说："九哥放心，只要我华克之上了庐山，就定要刺死这个卖国贼。如我杀不得蒋介石，情愿让他把我杀死！"

"Mr. Hua, I have decided to move against Chiang along two lines. Do you dare go to Lushan and carry out the killing there?" That unforgettable midnight two days before, in the dim-lit little house in the French Concession, the smoke had hung thick. A dozen of the core men were gathered around Wang Yaqiao. Sun Fengming had brought exact word from Nanjing that Chiang Kai-shek would go up Lushan on June 6, and Wang Yaqiao called a secret meeting that same night to work out the coming action. Hua Kezhi had not expected Wang Yaqiao to give him the mountain; from the gravity of Wang Yaqiao's face he saw that it was a mark of trust. He had long been confident of the work, and seeing Wang Yaqiao and the whole corps turn their eyes on him he felt his blood come up. "Set your mind at rest, Ninth Brother. Once Hua Kezhi is on Lushan, this traitor dies. And if I cannot kill Chiang Kai-shek, I would as soon he killed me!"

> 王亚樵闻言大喜："好，华先生，这才像我王亚樵的人。只是此去风险重重。咱们共分两路举事。一路是余立奎和郑抱真率第一小组在南京行事。他们在明故宫机场守候，如果在这里结果了蒋介石，那么你们闻讯后就马上下庐山；第二步，万一郑抱真和余立奎在南京失手，就全靠你们在庐山寻机下手了，因为那时蒋介石已经安全上了庐山。你带着人必须在蒋上庐山之前，进入阵地。否则等蒋带着宋美龄上了山，你们就休想再进山了。所以，现在你必须马上去九江，尽快登上庐山，听我从上海送去的信息后再决定是否行动。"

Wang Yaqiao was delighted. "Good, Mr. Hua — that is what a man of mine should sound like. Only there are dangers on every side of this. We divide into two lines. The first is Yu Likui and Zheng Baozhen with the first group, acting in Nanjing; they wait at the Ming Palace airfield, and if Chiang Kai-shek is finished there, you come down off the mountain the moment you hear. The second step: if Zheng Baozhen and Yu Likui miss in Nanjing, then everything rests on your finding your chance on Lushan, because by then Chiang Kai-shek will be safely up. You must be in position before Chiang goes up. Once he is on the mountain with Soong Mei-ling, you may as well forget entering it at all. So you must leave for Jiujiang at once, get up Lushan as fast as you can, and decide whether to act on the word I send you from Shanghai."

> 华克之说："既然如此，我就宜早不宜迟，马上去庐山隐藏起来，待蒋上山后即刻行动。只不知有哪几位弟兄与我同行？"

Hua Kezhi said: "In that case the sooner the better. I go up Lushan now and hide, and act the moment Chiang arrives. Only — which of the brothers goes with me?"

> "华先生，我去！""我也去！""九爷，让我也上山吧，我早就盼着这一天了！"坐在灯影里焦盼了多日的暗杀团成员，见王亚樵已点了华克之的将，都摩拳擦掌，纷纷请战。

"Mr. Hua, I'll go!" "I'll go too!" "Ninth Master, let me up the mountain — I've waited for this day!" The corps members who had sat so many days in the lamplight, aching for it, saw Wang Yaqiao name Hua Kezhi as his commander and pressed forward, rubbing their fists.

> "这才像我王九光的弟兄。"王亚樵亲自点了陈成、赵士发、龙林、唐明、张宪庭等八个暗杀队员，连夜随华克之离开上海。

"Now that is what my brothers should be." Wang Yaqiao himself named Chen Cheng, Zhao Shifa, Long Lin, Tang Ming, Zhang Xianting and three others — eight men of the assassination team — to leave Shanghai with Hua Kezhi that night.

> 华克之欣然受命后又不无忧虑地说："为行大事，早在一个月前，我和陈成两人已悄悄去了次庐山。发现宋美龄、蒋介石用来避暑的美庐，四周几乎无处藏身。那些国民党高级官员的别墅附近，没有普通旅客下榻的旅店。这样，我们就只能在太乙村一带藏身，牯岭街附近有一些中下等客栈。"

Having taken the charge gladly, Hua Kezhi added a worry. "For the sake of the work, Chen Cheng and I went quietly up Lushan a month ago. We found that around the Meilu villa, where Soong Mei-ling and Chiang Kai-shek pass the summer, there is almost nowhere to hide. Near those senior Nationalists' villas there are no inns for ordinary travelers. So we can only lie up around Taiyi Village; there are some middling and lower inns near Guling Street."

> 陈成是位机灵而有心计的刺客，前次他随华克之上庐山以后，早将高级别墅区四周都悄悄侦察了一遍。发现美庐原是一处最不宜接近的别墅，附近到处都有国民党的特工人员秘密监视，陌生人几乎难以接近。他向王亚樵报告了美庐的地势后说："我看如在美庐下手，几乎根本不可能。即便我们上山后可以接近美庐，也难以突破侍从室设在前面的三道防线。所以，最好的办法，是在蒋、宋两人走出美庐，去山上游玩的半路上下手，山间小路是最好的行刺地点。"

Chen Cheng was a quick and calculating assassin, and on that earlier trip with Hua Kezhi he had scouted the whole villa quarter. He had found the Meilu the least approachable of all — Nationalist operatives watching it secretly on every side, a stranger scarcely able to get near. Having reported the ground to Wang Yaqiao, he said: "As I see it, a strike at the Meilu itself is very nearly impossible. Even if we could get near it after going up, we could not break the three lines of defense the Attendants' Office has laid in front of it. So the best plan is to take Chiang and Soong on the road, once they have left the Meilu to go touring the mountain. The mountain paths are the best killing-ground."

> 华克之也赞成说："陈成的主意甚好。因为蒋介石既然上了庐山，就不会每天都龟缩在美庐里不出来。只要他出来，我们就会找到向他射击的机会。山上到处都是曲曲折折的山路。他带的侍从人员虽然很多，但是在山路上却很难施展。到了那时，我们在暗中就可射中目标。现在的问题是，我们的武器如何才能带上庐山，那一路上几乎到处都是关卡呀。"

Hua Kezhi agreed. "Chen Cheng's idea is a good one. Since Chiang Kai-shek is going up Lushan, he will not shut himself in the Meilu every day. And the moment he comes out, we shall find our chance to shoot. The mountain is nothing but winding paths. He may bring many attendants, but on a mountain path they have little room to work. That is when we can hit our target from cover. The problem now is how our weapons are to get up Lushan at all — that road is nothing but checkpoints."

> 王亚樵听了华克之和陈成两人关于庐山上的伏击之策，心里十分赞许。当谈到如何向庐山运送枪支弹药时，他也感到束手无策。华克之、陈成等九个人上山，至少要有八九只短枪才行。可是，由于庐山历来是国民党军政要人消夏的禁区，所以得到特别通行证上山的普通游客本来就凤毛麟角，在路上到处是关卡的情况下，暗携枪支几乎是不可能的。尽管王亚樵疏通南京的关系，总算弄到了九张上庐山的通行证，但是，无论如何也不可能携枪上山。自从蒋介石当上国民政府主席并每年盛夏都携宋美龄上庐山避暑以来，从九江到庐山的路上，便设下了一层层严密的军警检查站。特务们专门检查上山游客的行李，即便女客也不放过，更不要说华克之等九个男人的目标更大，他们的行李如何能够躲过十几道哨卡的眼睛呢？

Wang Yaqiao thought highly of the plan of ambush Hua Kezhi and Chen Cheng laid out. But when the talk came to getting guns and ammunition up the mountain, he too found himself without a move. Hua Kezhi, Chen Cheng and the rest — nine men — needed eight or nine pistols at the least. Yet Lushan had always been the summer preserve of Nationalist military and political dignitaries, so ordinary tourists with a special permit to go up were rare as phoenix feathers to begin with; and with checkpoints the whole way, carrying guns concealed was next to impossible. Wang Yaqiao had worked his Nanjing connections and had managed nine permits — but there was no conceivable way to carry weapons up. Ever since Chiang Kai-shek became chairman of the National Government and began taking Soong Mei-ling up Lushan every summer, layer upon layer of military-police checkpoints had been set along the road from Jiujiang. The agents made a specialty of searching tourists' luggage, and not even women were spared; still less would nine men together escape notice. How was their baggage to get past ten and more checkpoints?

> "这确是个难题，不过，你们只管上山好了。"王亚樵见华克之等人因无法携枪上山愁肠百结，情知一时想不出解决的办法，便说："至于枪支如何上山，由我再来想办法。总之，明天华先生就要上庐山安营扎寨。我保证在行动之前，准时派人把枪弹送上山就是了。"

"It is a hard problem — but never mind; you go up." Seeing Hua Kezhi and the others tied in knots over it, and knowing no answer would come at once, Wang Yaqiao said: "As for how the guns go up, leave that to me. In short: Mr. Hua pitches camp on Lushan tomorrow, and I guarantee that before the action, I will have men deliver the weapons on time."

> 现在，当华克之乘坐滑竿来到牯岭街头，就吩咐把滑竿停下来。然后他走进街边一家酒肆里坐定，不一会儿，陈成和赵士发、龙林、唐明、张宪庭等八位弟兄也都进了店门。华克之吩咐店家先上几碟时鲜果馔，九个人围坐在圆桌前吃酒，华克之边吃边警惕地观察着窗外，发现牯岭街上不时闪过一些特务的身影。他悄悄告诉身边的陈成、赵士发和龙林说："吃了酒后，大家要分开上山。刚才我发现，从九江上山的路上，我们一行人已经引起哨兵的注意了！这是来前没有考虑周全，你们想，哪有九个男人一起上山游览的道理？而且，我们又都持有香港护照，这样就太显眼了！"

Now, riding his mountain chair into Guling Street, Hua Kezhi had it set down. He went into a wine shop by the road and settled himself, and before long Chen Cheng, Zhao Shifa, Long Lin, Tang Ming, Zhang Xianting and the rest of the eight came in as well. Hua Kezhi ordered a few plates of fresh fruit and delicacies, and the nine sat round a table drinking. As he ate he watched the street warily, and saw agents' figures passing along Guling Street from time to time. Quietly he told Chen Cheng, Zhao Shifa and Long Lin: "After the wine, we go up separately. I noticed on the road from Jiujiang that our party has already drawn the sentries' attention. We didn't think it through before we came — what nine men ever went up a mountain sightseeing together? And we are all carrying Hong Kong passports, which makes us more conspicuous still."

> 陈成和龙林听了，回想起刚才上山时遇见的许多狐疑目光，也感到九个人目标太显眼了。华克之放下酒杯说："等会儿我和陈成、龙林三人乘滑竿直上太乙峰。你们大家要分头进山，最好都是徒步走。这样目标可以分散一些，不致引起山上人的怀疑。而且，我和陈成前次在太乙峰下订的太白酒店，也不能再用了。最好的办法是，咱们分住在三家小客栈里。不然，树大招风。小心山上的警察查夜的时候惹是生非。"

Chen Cheng and Long Lin, recalling the many suspicious glances on the way up, agreed that nine made too large a mark. Hua Kezhi set his cup down. "Presently Chen Cheng, Long Lin and I will take chairs straight up to Taiyi Peak. The rest of you go into the mountain separately, and on foot if you can. That spreads the target and keeps the people up here from wondering. And the Taibai Hotel that Chen Cheng and I booked below Taiyi Peak last time cannot be used again either. The best course is for us to lodge in three separate small inns. Otherwise a tall tree draws the wind, and the police up here make trouble on their night rounds."

> "对，大家化整为零，分头行动为好。"陈成听了华克之的安排，也深以为然。

"Right — break the whole into parts and move separately." Chen Cheng thought the arrangement thoroughly sound.

> "记住，咱们在太乙峰下几家客栈住下后，白天千万不要公开联络，小心暗哨。"华克之将大家分成三伙，分别由他和陈成、龙林为小组长，然后选好三家山顶客栈投宿。同时也为三个小组的联络确定了暗号和接头地点。大约过了半小时，他们匆匆用过午餐，便分头沿着牯岭街向通往太乙峰的山路走去。

"Remember: once we are in our inns below Taiyi Peak, no open contact by day; watch for plainclothes." Hua Kezhi divided them into three parties under himself, Chen Cheng and Long Lin, chose three hilltop inns, and fixed the recognition signals and meeting places for the three groups. About half an hour later they took a hurried lunch and set off separately along Guling Street toward the mountain road to Taiyi Peak.

> 上海阴雨连绵。

In Shanghai the rain went on and on.

> 自从华克之等九人赶赴庐山以后，王亚樵在法租界公馆里整日苦思如何派人将九支德国手枪秘密送上庐山。就在王亚樵心中无策时，妻子王亚英那窈窕倩丽的身影，又从门外闪了进来。她还像从前那样安恬清丽，没有因为丈夫在日夜策动的杀蒋行动有丝毫改变。王亚英遇事不惊的本能很让王亚樵佩服。如今她见王亚樵愁眉紧锁，不由问道："九光，大家都已上了山，莫非你还有什么难办的事吗？"

After Hua Kezhi and the eight others left for Lushan, Wang Yaqiao spent whole days in the Concession house working at the problem of getting nine German pistols secretly up the mountain. And as he sat there without a plan, his wife's slender figure came in at the door again. She was as calm and clear as ever; her husband's day-and-night plotting of a murder had not altered her by a hair. Wang Yaqiao had always admired that unshakable steadiness in her. Now, seeing his brows knotted, she asked: "Jiuguang, everyone is up the mountain. Is there still something that will not go right?"

> "唉唉，这事太难办了。"王亚樵只好将枪械无法送上庐山的情况细说一遍。王亚英听了，在落地窗前沉思许久，忽然漂亮的眼睛一眨，叫道："有了！亚樵，此事也难不倒我的，你为何不早对我说呢？"

"Ai. This one is too hard." Wang Yaqiao told her the whole difficulty of the weapons. Wang Yaying listened, stood thinking a long while at the French window, and then her fine eyes flashed. "I have it! Yaqiao, this need not defeat you — why did you not tell me sooner?"

> "莫非夫人真有什么办法？"

"Does my wife really have a way?"

> "办法自然有的，索性让我也上庐山就是了。"

"Naturally there is a way. Let me go up Lushan myself and be done with it."

> "夫人亲自去？那山上可有鬼门关啊！使不得，你千万去不得。庐山如今已成是非之地，你一个女子，又如何能冒此风险，进山送武器呢？"

"You go yourself? There are gates of hell on that mountain! It won't do — you must on no account go. Lushan is a place of trouble now. How could a woman run such a risk and carry weapons into the mountain?"

> 王亚英见王亚樵直急得摇头摆手，不禁笑道："我一个女子如何？莫非我王亚英就不敢面对枪林弹雨？九光，我跟随你这么多年了，难道还怀疑我的胆量吗？"

Seeing Wang Yaqiao waving his hands in agitation, Wang Yaying could not help laughing. "And what of a woman? Am I, Wang Yaying, not to be allowed to face a storm of bullets? Jiuguang, I have followed you all these years. Do you still doubt my nerve?"

> 王亚樵说："并不是怀疑夫人胆量，而是带八九支短枪上山，绝非儿戏呀。万一被搜出有枪，那可是要掉脑袋的！须知那些为蒋介石和国民党大员守山的人，现在就连女人的内衣也要搜的，你又不会孙悟空的七十二变，如何能把那些杀人的家伙，带到山上去呢？这可千万使不得呀！"

"It is not your nerve I doubt. But to carry eight or nine pistols up that mountain is no child's play. If they find a gun on you it costs your head! You should know that the men guarding that mountain for Chiang Kai-shek and the Nationalist dignitaries now search even a woman's underclothes. You cannot work Sun Wukong's seventy-two transformations. How would you get those killing tools up there? It absolutely will not do!"

> 王亚英冷笑道："九光，我笑你们这些爷们，平时只会抖浑身的傲气，却全然没有我们女人的机敏。其实就算关卡的军警搜得再严，也总有他们搜不到的地方。"

Wang Yaying laughed coldly. "Jiuguang, I laugh at you men. You go about shaking your arrogance out of every limb, and you have none of a woman's quickness. However hard the checkpoints search, there is always somewhere they will not look."

> 王亚樵见妻子说得煞有介事，也知亚英历来不说空话，便急忙地问道："你有什么好主意，不妨说出来给我听。如果当真可行的话，我自然同意你上山。可是，如你没什么超人之术，就千万别逞能冒险了，那可是拿自己的脑袋开玩笑呀！"

Seeing his wife in such earnest, and knowing Wang Yaying never talked emptily, Wang Yaqiao asked quickly: "What is your plan? Let me hear it. If it will really serve, I shall of course agree to your going. But if you have no extraordinary art, then don't play the hero for nothing — that would be gambling with your own head!"

> 王亚英莫测高深地一笑，然后一拍胸脯："九光，你听我的吩咐去办，不会出事的。先派人买八九只金华火腿来，我自有用处！"

Wang Yaying gave an unfathomable smile and struck her chest once. "Jiuguang, do as I tell you and nothing will go wrong. First send men to buy eight or nine Jinhua hams. I have a use for them."

> "你要金华火腿何用？这不是开玩笑吗？"

"What do you want Jinhua hams for? Is this a joke?"

> "金华火腿确是好东西，你只管给我买来最好的就是了，到时候我自有用处。"王亚樵知妻子聪敏过人，也就不再追问，马上派人前往浙江金华，连夜买回九只色泽诱人、成色甚佳的火腿来。

"Jinhua ham is a fine thing. Just get me the best there is; I shall have my use for it when the time comes." Knowing his wife's cleverness, Wang Yaqiao asked no more, and sent men off to Jinhua in Zhejiang, who bought overnight and brought back nine hams of tempting color and excellent grade.

> 王亚英将九只色泽油亮的火腿拿在手里，一一看过后，屏退从人。又找来一把锋利的快刀，动作麻利地将火腿内的肉丝，一刀刀剔除。然后再将王亚樵送来的九只德国枪悄悄卸开，眨眼间都变成了散落的零部件，再将其小心地用油棉包好，分头装进那些掏空了肉丝的金华火腿内，再用针线将所有切开的火腿，用细密的针线一一缝好，又以油泥封了针线。当她做完这一切后，再将九只火腿摆在桌面上，让王亚樵来看。在灯光的映照下，九只火腿光彩照人，诱人食欲，看不出一点破绽。

Wang Yaying took the nine glossy hams in her hands, looked each one over, and sent the servants out. Then she found a sharp, quick knife and, working deftly, pared the meat out of each ham stroke by stroke. Next she quietly stripped down the nine German pistols Wang Yaqiao had sent in, until in a blink they were so many loose components; these she wrapped carefully in oiled cotton and packed, divided up, into the hollowed hams. Then she sewed every cut ham closed with fine, close stitches, and sealed the stitching over with oiled clay. When it was all done she set the nine hams out on the table and called Wang Yaqiao to look. Under the lamp the nine hams shone splendidly, mouth-watering, with not a flaw to be seen.

> "我的天，亚英，原来你这是在变戏法？"王亚樵立刻明白了妻子的妙计所在，连连抚掌叫绝说，"妙妙！只是你将这些掏空了肉丝的火腿带上庐山，也不是小事，胆量小的人，是断然不敢这样做的。"

"Heaven — Yaying, you have been performing conjuring tricks!" Wang Yaqiao understood the whole of it at once, and clapped his hands and called it marvelous. "Marvelous! Only, carrying these hollowed hams up Lushan is no small thing. A woman with less nerve would simply never do it."

> 王亚英显然成竹在胸："我已说了，这些枪械就统统由我送上庐山。不过，我还要再配个机灵的女助手才行。"

Wang Yaying clearly had it all drawn out already. "I have said it: I take all these weapons up Lushan myself. Only I shall need a quick-witted woman assistant besides."

> "哦哦，你是想以旅行的女学者身份上庐山？那么，你的身边当然要有女佣跟随了。"

"Ah — you mean to go up as a traveling woman scholar? Then of course you must have a maid with you."

> "不，我不是女学者，我是想做国民党大员的太太上山。"

"No. I am not going as a scholar. I am going up as the wife of a Nationalist dignitary."

> 王亚樵到现在已全然明了了妻子的锦囊妙计。他忽然想起一个人来，说："亚英，就让亚凤随你同上庐山，如何？"

By now Wang Yaqiao saw his wife's whole plan, brocade bag and all. A person came into his mind. "Yaying — let Yafeng go up Lushan with you. How would that be?"

> 王亚凤乃是王亚樵的胞妹，此时也从安徽来到上海。王亚英听了，正合心意。王亚凤从前在合肥女子高中就读，既知书达理，又机敏睿慧，深明大义，早就对蒋介石的倒行逆施心怀仇恨。所以王亚英连连点头说："亚凤随我上山最好了。不过，为了让我真正成为那些兵痞们眼里的官太太，最好再给我身边配上两三位马弁侍从。那样的话，我敢保证带着这些火腿上山，一点麻烦也不会有的。"

Wang Yafeng was Wang Yaqiao's own younger sister, and she too had come from Anhui to Shanghai by then. The suggestion suited Wang Yaying exactly. Wang Yafeng had studied at the Hefei girls' high school; she knew books and manners, she was quick and shrewd, she understood what mattered, and she had long hated Chiang Kai-shek's misrule. So Wang Yaying nodded again and again. "Yafeng with me is best of all. Only — to make me a real official's wife in the eyes of those soldier-ruffians, I had better have two or three orderlies at my side as well. With that, I guarantee I can carry these hams up the mountain without a particle of trouble."

> 王亚樵也感到妻子想出的送枪办法确实奇妙稳妥。他马上派出孙凤鸣和另两个暗杀团成员，随王亚英、王亚凤上山。两日后，王亚英带王亚凤和孙凤鸣等一行人，从上海乘船直奔九江，然后从九江码头上了庐山。

Wang Yaqiao too felt his wife's method wonderfully sound. He sent Sun Fengming and two other corps members to go up with Wang Yaying and Wang Yafeng. Two days later the party sailed from Shanghai for Jiujiang, and from the Jiujiang wharf went up Lushan.

> 一路上她和亚凤都坐在雇人抬的滑竿上。王亚英身穿紫红色紧身旗袍，红色高跟皮鞋，头戴一顶巴拿马雪白女式凉帽，加上她清丽端庄、仪态雅典的容貌，足以显现出高官显宦的贵妇身份。与她同行的王亚凤则淡妆素服，俨然是二姨太模样。孙凤鸣扮成了随行的副官，他西装革履，文质彬彬，跑前跑后，忙个不亦乐乎。另外两个杀手，则挑着两箱子行李，累得汗流浃背。她们一路上在那些设卡的警特们面前，都显得十分泰然自若。在这些国民党大兵的眼里，只有像王亚英和王亚凤这样的阔夫人、姨太太，才有资格登上这夏日冠盖云集、女眷如云的庐山。而她们的行李里带有上乘的金华火腿，自然不会引起哨兵军警们的注意，因为她们带礼品上山是合情合理的，谁会想到那火腿里面会暗藏枪支弹药？

All the way up, she and Yafeng rode in hired mountain chairs. Wang Yaying wore a close-fitting purple-red qipao and red high-heeled shoes, with a snow-white Panama sun hat on her head; and with her elegant, well-bred bearing, she looked every inch the lady of a high official. Wang Yafeng, beside her in plain dress and light makeup, was to all appearances a second concubine. Sun Fengming played the attending aide-de-camp, in a Western suit, cultivated and polished, running back and forth and busy as a man could be. The other two killers shouldered two cases of luggage and sweated through their clothes. Past every checkpoint the women held themselves perfectly at ease. In the eyes of those Nationalist soldiers, only great ladies and concubines such as Wang Yaying and Wang Yafeng had the standing to go up a Lushan thronged in summer with the carriages of the great and with women in clouds. And that their luggage held first-rate Jinhua hams would naturally rouse no sentry's interest, since carrying gifts up the mountain was perfectly reasonable. Who would imagine guns and ammunition inside the hams?

> 当那些设卡守山的国民党士兵想检查王亚英行李时，王亚英坐在滑竿上大模大样地说："让他们看好了，反正我的东西里面，不会有大烟土和手枪，怕个什么呢？"

When the soldiers at one checkpoint made to search Wang Yaying's luggage, she said grandly from her chair: "Let them look. There's no opium and no pistols in my things — what have I to fear?"

> 孙凤鸣开始时有些紧张，可是，当那些哨兵开启两只箱笼，才发现里面全是让他们大吃一惊的东西：例如蒋介石、冯玉祥、张学良和宋美龄写给女主人的亲笔信，还有一些女人用的上等化妆品和南洋华侨特有的玉器珠宝，直映得人眼花缭乱。大兵们虽然发现里面还有八九只亮晶晶的金华火腿，有些刺目，但都误认为是女主人带上山来送给某大员的礼物，哪个还敢多翻多看，故而一路是畅通无阻。

Sun Fengming was tense at first. But when the sentries opened the two cases, what they found astonished them: letters in the hands of Chiang Kai-shek, Feng Yuxiang, Zhang Xueliang and Soong Mei-ling to the lady of the house; fine women's cosmetics; jade and jewelry of the kind the overseas Chinese of the South Seas carry — dazzling stuff. The soldiers did notice eight or nine glossy Jinhua hams, which caught the eye somewhat; but they took them for gifts the lady was carrying up to some dignitary, and who would dare rummage further? So the party passed the whole way unhindered.

> 不久，王亚英一行就经牯岭小镇前的大街，沿着曲曲折折的山路，直向太乙峰上爬去。孙凤鸣直到快爬到山顶时，才如释重负吁出一口气。这时他发现王亚英的额上原来也沁出了汗水。至于随他们一起上山的王亚凤，更是吓得面色苍白。不过，她们都明白鬼门关终于闯过来了。

Before long Wang Yaying's party had passed along the main street of the little town of Guling and were climbing the winding mountain road toward Taiyi Peak. It was not until they were nearly at the top that Sun Fengming let out his breath. And then he noticed that Wang Yaying's forehead too was beaded with sweat. As for Wang Yafeng, who had climbed with them, she was white with fright. But they both knew that the gate of hell had been passed at last.

> 南京酷热无比，进入6月以来，俨然成了让人喘不上气来的大火炉。

Nanjing was murderously hot; since the start of June it had become a furnace where a man could hardly draw breath.

> 余立奎和他的行动小组，一直隐藏在距明故宫机场不远的居民区里。6月3日，也就是王亚英、孙凤鸣一行，带着行刺枪械冒险登上庐山的当天，王亚樵密派郑抱真从上海来到南京，向余立奎等人通报了华克之、陈成等九人已上了庐山待命和王亚英、王亚凤、孙凤鸣等人上庐山配合行动的消息。

Yu Likui and his action group had been lying up all this while in a residential quarter not far from the Ming Palace airfield. On June 3 — the very day Wang Yaying and Sun Fengming took the assassination weapons up Lushan — Wang Yaqiao secretly sent Zheng Baozhen from Shanghai to Nanjing to inform Yu Likui that Hua Kezhi, Chen Cheng and the other nine were on the mountain awaiting orders, and that Wang Yaying, Wang Yafeng and Sun Fengming had gone up to support them.

> "当然，九爷仍然希望你们在南京得手。"郑抱真转达王亚樵的命令后说："因为庐山风险更大。南京只要在蒋登机时远射几枪，就可结果他的性命，然后大家分头逃走也很方便；可是在庐山行刺以后，下山也相当危险。"

"Of course, the Ninth Master still hopes you will succeed in Nanjing." Having passed on Wang Yaqiao's orders, Zheng Baozhen added: "Because Lushan carries the greater risk. In Nanjing, a few shots at long range as Chiang boards the plane will finish him, and everyone can scatter easily afterward; but after a killing on Lushan, getting down the mountain is dangerous in itself."

> 余立奎对郑抱真带到南京的新式武器很感兴趣。王亚樵在上海通过外国人购买的达姆枪，具有远程射击的特别效能，这种枪里的达姆弹一旦射中人的身体，可在半小时内让饮弹者全身中毒而亡。

Yu Likui was much taken with the new weapons Zheng Baozhen had brought. The dumdum guns Wang Yaqiao had bought in Shanghai through a foreigner had a special efficacy at long range: once one of their bullets entered a man's body, the poison could kill him within half an hour.

> 6月6日，郑抱真、余立奎和他们的行动小组在晨色初露的时候，已进入他们预先选好的射击阵地。那是紫金山麓朝阳高坡上一片茂盛的枫树林，他们分别潜藏在树林深处，在这里可以居高临下看到明故宫机场的跑道。如果他们从国民政府主席侍从室得到的情报是准确的，那么今天上午9点，蒋介石将偕夫人宋美龄一行人，从这里起飞，前往江西的庐山避暑。

On June 6, at the first light, Zheng Baozhen, Yu Likui and their group moved into the firing position they had chosen: a thick maple wood on the sunrise slope at the foot of Purple Mountain. They hid themselves deep among the trees, from where they could look down on the runway of the Ming Palace airfield. If the intelligence from the chairman's Attendants' Office was accurate, then at nine that morning Chiang Kai-shek and his wife Soong Mei-ling would take off from here for Lushan in Jiangxi.

> 天色渐渐明亮以后，余立奎等发现机场的跑道上确实停着一架客机，认出那正是蒋介石的座机"美龄号"。看到那架飞机，余立奎等人心情顿时冲动起来，因为他们马上可以在这里用达姆枪向老蒋射击了。那杀伤力甚强的达姆弹，一旦从密林中飞向正在登机的蒋介石，完全可以设想，一代枭雄定会顷刻化为弹下之鬼！

As the light came up, Yu Likui and the others saw that an aircraft did indeed stand on the runway, and recognized it as Chiang Kai-shek's own plane, the Meiling. At the sight of it their blood rose, for now they could shoot at old Chiang from here with the dumdum guns. One had only to imagine that fearsome bullet flying out of the thick wood at a Chiang Kai-shek in the act of boarding, and the hero-villain of an age would be a ghost under the gun in a moment.

> 但是，时间一分一秒地过去，那架飞机却丝毫没有起飞的迹象。机场上见不到机械师地勤人员的身影，也没有出现戒备森严的状态。郑抱真不时掏出怀表查看时间，当时针指向9点半时，他预感到余立奎南京行刺小组在此前所进行的大量准备工作，都已变成了一阵毫无意义的空忙。

But the minutes and the seconds went by, and the aircraft showed no sign whatever of departing. No mechanics or ground crew appeared on the field, and there was none of the tight security of a great man's departure. Zheng Baozhen kept taking out his pocket watch; and when the hands stood at half past nine, he foresaw that all the enormous preparation of Yu Likui's Nanjing group had come to a stretch of perfectly meaningless waiting.

> 可是，余立奎等人仍对他们得到的情报深信不疑。就这样，行刺小组一直在紫金山的密林里空候一天一夜，直到次日凌晨才返回住所。这时，一个让大家大失所望的消息传来了：原来蒋介石和宋美龄早已在前一天的夜里，从南京浦口码头乘兵舰秘密驶往了九江。如此一来，余立奎等人在南京明故宫机场设下的必死之阵，就失去了应有的作用。

Yet Yu Likui and the others still believed their intelligence absolutely. And so the party waited in the dense woods of Purple Mountain a whole day and a whole night, and did not return to their lodgings until the small hours of the next morning. And then the news came that dashed them all: Chiang Kai-shek and Soong Mei-ling had left the night before, secretly, by warship from the Pukou wharf at Nanjing, bound for Jiujiang. So the killing-ground Yu Likui had laid at the Ming Palace airfield had come to nothing at all.

> 此时，南京失去刺蒋机会的消息，也传到了庐山太乙峰。

Word that the Nanjing chance had been lost reached Taiyi Peak on Lushan.

> 华克之收到这一信息，心情自然相当复杂。他为王亚樵设计的机场偷袭计划落空感到遗憾，同时也意识到自己的责任更加重大。因为现在王亚樵的刺蒋方案，只有依靠他的行动小组来实施了。不久，蒋介石夫妇已上了庐山的消息也得到了证实。神圣的使命感让华克之和住在太乙峰附近三家客栈里的杀手们，既兴奋又紧张，他们行刺的目标近在咫尺，九支德国造远射程手枪，也准时送到了他们的手边。在这种情况下，华克之决定马上把王亚樵的行刺计划付诸实施。

Hua Kezhi's feelings at the news were naturally mixed. He regretted the failure of the airfield ambush Wang Yaqiao had designed — and understood at the same time that his own responsibility had grown far heavier, since Wang Yaqiao's whole plan now rested on his group alone. Before long, word that Chiang Kai-shek and his wife were on the mountain was confirmed too. The sense of a sacred mission left Hua Kezhi and the killers in the three inns near Taiyi Peak both elated and tense: their target was within arm's reach, and the nine long-range German pistols had been delivered to their hands on time. In these circumstances Hua Kezhi resolved to put Wang Yaqiao's plan into effect at once.

> 华克之首先去牯岭街附近的美庐踏查。结果发现，那里是根本无法接近的禁区。他隐藏在距美庐有几百米的草丛里，远远望见那幢建在长冲河畔一片浓密绿阴中的欧式小楼，果然是地处要害，防守甚严。美庐背靠嵯峨大月山，北有起起伏伏的悬崖峭壁，南有一个幽深的谷口，绝非寻常人可以随便进入。

Hua Kezhi went first to reconnoiter the Meilu near Guling Street — and found it a forbidden zone that could not be approached at all. Hidden in the grass a few hundred meters off, he looked across at the little European house standing in dense green shade by the Changchong River, and saw that it was indeed sited at a commanding point and heavily guarded. The Meilu had the crags of Dayue Mountain at its back, cliffs rising and falling to the north, and a deep ravine mouth to the south; no ordinary person could simply walk in.

> 此前华克之只知这座美庐是一位著名英国医师赫莉太太1922年建在山间的别墅，当时取名为牯岭街13号。1930年这位英国太太返回伦敦前夕，把它作为圣诞节的礼物送给了宋美龄，从此这里就成了蒋、宋两人消夏的所在。由于宋美龄认为13号不吉利，才改名美庐。现在，当华克之发现美庐前后左右都布满荷枪实弹的哨兵时，感到要在美庐对蒋下手，简直比登天还难。

Until then Hua Kezhi had known only that the Meilu was a mountain villa built in 1922 by a well-known Englishwoman, a doctor whose name the Chinese renders as Heli, and named at the time No. 13 Guling Street. In 1930, on the eve of the Englishwoman's return to London, she gave it to Soong Mei-ling as a Christmas present, and from then on it was where Chiang and Soong passed their summers. Because Soong Mei-ling considered the number 13 unlucky, it was renamed the Meilu. Now, finding sentries with loaded rifles posted on every side of it, Hua Kezhi felt that to strike at Chiang inside the Meilu would be harder than climbing to heaven.

> 后来，他和陈成又来到距此不远的"美龄桥"上侦察，发现这里虽然可以埋伏，但附近都是潺潺流水，万一开枪，逃跑却是个大问题。于是，华克之最终放弃了在蒋介石住地行刺的打算。

Afterward he and Chen Cheng went to scout the "Meiling Bridge" not far off, and found that although a man could lie up there, the ground all around was running water, and if a shot were fired, escape became a serious problem. So Hua Kezhi abandoned at last the idea of a killing at Chiang Kai-shek's residence.

> 王亚英虽然完成了运送枪支的任务，可她没马上下山。美丽的庐山景色宜人，风光独具，可她无心观赏，一个心思只为如何尽快把蒋杀死在庐山上。那些天，王亚英在太乙峰下的"山村酒家"里蛰居。她不敢公开露面，到了夜晚才出来和华克之、孙凤鸣联系。王亚英把五老峰下的白鹿洞书院，当成她们的联络点。幽静的月色下，王亚英在存书堂前徘徊。

Wang Yaying had completed her delivery of the guns, but she did not go straight down. Lushan's beauty is a pleasure and its scenery all its own, yet she had no heart to look at it; her whole mind ran on how Chiang might be killed on the mountain, and quickly. In those days Wang Yaying lay low at the Mountain Village Inn below Taiyi Peak. She dared not show herself openly, and came out only at night to make contact with Hua Kezhi and Sun Fengming. She made the White Deer Grotto Academy below Wulao Peak their meeting point. Under the still moonlight, Wang Yaying would pace before the Hall of Preserved Books.

> 华克之报告的情况是：最近几天，蒋介石和宋美龄很少外出，即便出行，也只是去了庐山北麓的东林寺，去看那里的聪明泉和白莲寺等古迹，再就是去了一回龙首岩。即便这两次外出，也是大批侍卫随行，一路上其他游客根本无法接近，更不要说向坐在滑竿上的蒋、宋两人开枪了。

Hua Kezhi's report ran thus: in the last few days Chiang Kai-shek and Soong Mei-ling had gone out very little, and when they did it was only to Donglin Temple on the northern foot of the mountain, to see the Spring of Wisdom and the White Lotus Temple and other old sites, and once to Longshou Rock. Even on those two outings a large body of guards went with them, and no other tourist could get near along the road, still less fire on a Chiang and a Soong seated in mountain chairs.

> "更难办的是，蒋的行踪从不固定。他今天去这里，明天去那里。看起来都是临时决定想去的地方。所以我们采取预先埋伏的办法，几乎是行不通的。再说，他身边的侍从太多，粗略一算，至少有四十多人。而且人人都手持双枪，随时都在四周巡视。只要见到可疑行人，轻则上去训斥，重则当场逮捕问罪。蒋介石现在简直比封建帝王出巡还要戒备森严了。不过，这些天来我们发现了一条规律，蒋外出的时间大多是在上午，下午因天气转热，很少外出。""华先生，千万不要急，这已是很有收获了，能知道蒋每天的作息时间，就可以发现其行动规律了。"王亚英留在庐山，成为了华克之的行动参谋。

"What makes it harder is that Chiang's movements are never fixed. Today he goes here, tomorrow there — always, it seems, decided on the spur of the moment. So the method of lying in wait beforehand is next to unworkable. And he has too many attendants: at a rough count, forty and more. Every one of them carries two guns and patrols the surroundings constantly. Let them see a suspicious passer-by and at best he is barked at, at worst arrested on the spot. Chiang Kai-shek travels under heavier guard now than an emperor of the old dynasties on progress. Still — these last days we have found one regularity: Chiang goes out mostly in the morning, and rarely in the afternoon, when the weather turns hot." "Mr. Hua, don't be impatient; that is already a real gain. Once you know Chiang's daily hours, you can find the pattern of his movements." Wang Yaying, staying on the mountain, had become Hua Kezhi's operational adviser.

> "现在蒋的行动规律已经掌握了，"一天晚上，华克之再次到白鹿洞书院与王亚英见面，激动地说，"前几天，蒋是随意行动。后来他和宋的行动就变得规律起来。我和陈成发现，他们夫妇几乎每天上午都要离开，去庐山的东北坡。他们好像从没去过南坡和西坡。因为北坡有座东林寺。如果我们派人守在东北坡，总有一天会遇上前去那里焚香的蒋某人的。"

"We have his pattern now," said Hua Kezhi excitedly one evening, meeting Wang Yaying again at the White Deer Grotto Academy. "For the first few days Chiang moved as he pleased. Then he and Soong became regular. Chen Cheng and I found that the pair of them leave almost every morning for the northeast slope of Lushan. They seem never to have gone to the south slope or the west. Because Donglin Temple is on the north slope. If we post men on the northeast slope, one day we shall meet Chiang going up to burn incense there."

> "好吧，这就是你们多日的跟踪成绩。"王亚英想了想，又不以为然说："但是，你们也要提防着蒋，他是个凡事多疑的人。北坡去得次数多了，他会担心形成规律，被外人知道以后，那里反而就不安全了。我昨天上午借去北坡游玩时发现，那里的地形极不适于我们埋伏，都是些宽宽的石板路，万一被发现，就没有逃走的退路，所以在北坡最好不抱希望。我想，理想的伏击地点，应该是庐山的西南坡。"

"Very well — that is the fruit of your many days' shadowing." Wang Yaying thought, and then dissented. "But you must also guard against Chiang. He is a man who doubts everything. Go often enough to the north slope and he will begin to fear he is forming a pattern that outsiders may learn — and then the north slope becomes unsafe precisely because of it. I went up the north slope yesterday morning on the pretext of sightseeing, and found the ground there quite unsuited to an ambush: broad flagstone paths, with no line of retreat at all if we were spotted. So I would hope for nothing from the north slope. The ideal ambush ground, I think, is Lushan's southwest slope."

> "西南坡？"华克之听后一怔。

"The southwest slope?" Hua Kezhi was taken aback.

> 王亚英说得头头是道："我发现庐山的西南，净是些羊肠小路，且又大多坡陡难行。而且有小路的地方，两旁多是树阴覆盖，便于隐藏。所以，那里才是最适合我们行动的地方啊。"

Wang Yaying laid it out in order. "I find the southwest of Lushan is nothing but goat-track paths, and most of them steep and hard going. And where the little paths run, the sides are mostly under tree cover, which makes concealment easy. That is why it is the best ground for us."

> 华克之叹息说："可惜，他蒋某人就是不肯去西南方向。也许正是因为道路难行，极不安全，所以才不肯到那里去吧？"

Hua Kezhi sighed. "A pity, then, that Chiang will not go in the southwest direction. Perhaps it is precisely because the road is hard and thoroughly unsafe that he refuses to go there."

> 不料王亚英却摇头说："我想，他总有一天要去西南坡的。因为蒋是个行踪不定的人，而且西南坡上的古迹甚多，蒋某人迟早要去的。所以，我劝你们一定要在那里做些准备，早些踏查地形，做好埋伏。兵书上讲：出其不意，攻其不备。就是这个道理！"

But Wang Yaying shook her head. "I think he must go to the southwest slope one day. Chiang is a man of unfixed movements, and there are many antiquities on the southwest slope; sooner or later he will go. So I urge you to make your preparations there, scout the ground early, and lay your ambush. The war books say: appear where you are not expected, attack where he is not prepared. That is exactly the principle here."

> 华克之见她说得颇有道理，加之在庐山东北坡上发起攻击确实十分危险，便同意道："好吧，夫人，我们在西南坡上派人守候着，只要有一天蒋在那里一露头，我们就一枪结果了他！"

Finding real sense in it, and knowing besides how dangerous an attack on the northeast slope would be, Hua Kezhi agreed. "Very well, madam. We shall keep men waiting on the southwest slope, and the day Chiang shows his head there, one shot finishes him!"

> 华克之和王亚英碰头后，回到太乙大旅社连夜召集特别行动会议，重新布置人力，制定了一个在庐山西南坡山间小路上刺蒋的方案。华克之这次布置人马，十分周密。他分别在蒋介石可能出行的路上，暗布刺客，每两人划为一个小组，形成互相照应的暗杀行刺网。具体的部署是：在西南坡的上下山必经之路，如卢林大桥、含鄱口、乌龙潭、御碑亭、天池寺、太乙村、狮子岩等处，都暗布杀手，刺客们都化装成游人散客，坐待蒋介石在那里经过。一旦蒋出现，无论是遇上哪一个行动小组，都要不失时机地向蒋开枪。

After the meeting with Wang Yaying, Hua Kezhi went back to the Taiyi Hotel and called a special action meeting that same night, redeployed his men, and drew up a plan to kill Chiang on the mountain paths of the southwest slope. This time Hua Kezhi's dispositions were thorough. He set assassins along every road Chiang Kai-shek might travel, in pairs, forming a mutually supporting net of ambush. Specifically: at every point on the necessary up-and-down routes of the southwest slope — Lulin Bridge, Hanpokou, Wulong Pool, the Imperial Stele Pavilion, Tianchi Temple, Taiyi Village, Lion Rock — killers were hidden, disguised as ordinary sightseers, waiting for Chiang Kai-shek to pass. Once Chiang appeared, whichever group he met was to fire without letting the moment slip.

> 华克之和孙凤鸣一个小组，他和小组成员们宣誓说："任何人只要杀了蒋，都要迅速逃走。万一落入蒋的手中，要宁死坚不吐实。杀身成仁，舍生取义！"

Hua Kezhi and Sun Fengming formed one group, and to his men he swore this oath: "Whoever kills Chiang must get away fast. And if a man falls into Chiang's hands, he dies before he tells the truth. Die to accomplish benevolence; give up life to hold to the right!"

> 当天夜里，以华克之为首的行刺人员，在太乙峰下小客栈的套间喝下杯杯血酒，立下一个共同的誓言，不把蒋介石的人头拿下来，誓不罢休！

That night the assassins under Hua Kezhi drank cups of blood-wine in the suite of a small inn below Taiyi Peak and swore a common oath: until Chiang Kai-shek's head was taken, there would be no rest.

> 就在华克之按王亚英的意见，把全部人手都撒向庐山西南坡的第二天，那个自登上庐山以来从不去西南坡游览的蒋介石，终于在那里露头了！

And on the second day after Hua Kezhi, following Wang Yaying's advice, had thrown his whole strength onto the southwest slope, the Chiang Kai-shek who had never once gone sightseeing there since coming up the mountain appeared on it at last.

> 蒋介石为何忽又去了西南坡呢？这应该感谢宋美龄。

Why did Chiang Kai-shek suddenly go to the southwest slope? For that, thanks are owed to Soong Mei-ling.

> 美庐内环境幽雅。特别是那座花园，更是让蒋介石夫妇感到恬静怡人。坐在小楼阳台上，可望见园中那棵高达20米的玉兰树。此外，那些早年由英国医生亲手栽下的凌霄花，已经沿着小楼墙壁爬上了三楼阳台，形成了绿葱葱的一片。

Inside the Meilu the setting was elegant and quiet, and the garden in particular gave Chiang Kai-shek and his wife a feeling of ease. Sitting on the little balcony, one could see the magnolia in the garden, twenty meters high. And the trumpet creeper the English doctor had planted years before had climbed the wall of the house to the third-floor balcony and made one green mass of it.

> "达令，咱们这样每天去东北坡转来转去，怎么行呀？长此以往，岂不会形成规律了吗？那样的话，万一有人在暗中注意咱们的行踪，可就太危险了！"说这番话的是宋美龄。就在王亚英和华克之在白鹿洞书院月下碰头，暗商大计的当天下午，宋美龄忽然提出个让侍卫们大为吃惊的问题。她对坐在那里看电报的蒋介石说："早在我们上山之前，戴雨农不就多次发来告急的电报吗，说上海斧头帮的人，时时在暗中观察着你的行踪。我想，王亚樵会不会和南方那些反对派人士勾结在一起呢？"

"Darling, how can we go on circling round the northeast slope every day like this? Kept up long enough, won't it form a pattern? And then, if anyone is watching our movements from the dark, it becomes far too dangerous!" It was Soong Mei-ling speaking. On the very afternoon of the day when Wang Yaying and Hua Kezhi met under the moon at the White Deer Grotto Academy to lay their plans, Soong Mei-ling suddenly raised a question that startled the guards considerably. She said to Chiang Kai-shek, who was sitting reading a telegram: "Before we ever came up, didn't Dai Yunong send urgent telegrams again and again, saying the Shanghai Hatchet Gang men are watching your movements from cover all the time? I wonder — could Wang Yaqiao be in league with those opposition people in the south?"

> 蒋介石正在看的，恰好是戴笠从上海发来的密电。

What Chiang Kai-shek was reading happened to be exactly a coded telegram from Dai Li in Shanghai.

> 电文上说，最近从相当可靠的渠道获悉，南方政府正千方百计派员秘密赴沪，也有重金收买斧头帮的传闻。现虽发现可疑迹象，但具体内幕不详。盼委座在山上多加戒备，小心刺客上山。

It said that word had lately come through a thoroughly reliable channel that the southern government was doing everything possible to send men secretly to Shanghai, and that there was also a report of the Hatchet Gang being bought with a heavy sum. Suspicious indications had been found, though the details were not clear. It urged the Generalissimo to increase his precautions on the mountain and to beware of assassins coming up.

> 蒋介石将戴笠的电报反复看了看，初时并不以为然，听了宋美龄的提醒以后，才真正重视起来。他知道戴笠的电报绝不是空穴来风。万一汪精卫和孙科等人暗中和王亚樵勾结起来，后果将不堪设想。蒋介石想起1928年敢在他面前公开攻击他的安徽人，心里就不禁一阵紧张。他也不会忘记那个当年投靠他的赵铁桥，在上海招商局大门前惨死的现场照片！那血淋淋的尸体，直到现在还让蒋介石想起来就心有余悸。

Chiang Kai-shek read Dai Li's telegram over several times. He had thought little of it at first; only after his wife's warning did he take it seriously. He knew Dai Li's telegrams were never wind out of an empty cave. If Wang Jingwei and Sun Ke were secretly in league with Wang Yaqiao, the consequences did not bear thinking about. Remembering the Anhui man who had dared attack him openly to his face in 1928, Chiang Kai-shek felt a spasm of tension. Nor could he forget the photograph of the scene at the gate of the China Merchants company in Shanghai, where that Zhao Tieqiao who had come over to him died so wretchedly. That bloody corpse still put a chill in Chiang Kai-shek whenever it came to mind.

> "夫人，你说什么？"蒋介石用帕子拭拭额上的冷汗，回转身一看，发现几位主要侍卫人员，都垂手肃立在后边，恭听宋美龄发话。"我说小心无大错，咱们为什么上了庐山，就每天上午去东北坡转悠呢？长此下去，时间、地点，就都成了别人眼里的规律性东西。"宋美龄不愧是一位心性聪敏，在政治舞台上叱咤风云的女强人。自从蒋介石幽禁了胡汉民，以孙科、汪精卫为首的国民党反对派人士，大批辞去政府要职，纷纷飞往广东以后，宋美龄就不能不暗中对丈夫可能遭遇的不测多加小心了。这次她即便随行游览，也始终暗中观察着周围的一切，凡是她和蒋经过的路上，宋美龄只要发现一点反常迹象，就会喝令身边侍从查个一清二楚。今天她忽然意识到一个非常重大的失误，就是每天到同一地点去游览。

"What was that, my dear?" Chiang Kai-shek wiped the cold sweat from his forehead with a handkerchief and turned to find several of his senior guards standing at attention behind him, listening respectfully to Soong Mei-ling. "I say there's no great harm in caution. Why is it that since coming up Lushan we go strolling on the northeast slope every single morning? Kept up long enough, the time and the place both become a pattern in other people's eyes." Soong Mei-ling was a woman of quick mind who had made her weather on the political stage. Ever since Chiang Kai-shek confined Hu Hanmin, and the Nationalist opposition led by Sun Ke and Wang Jingwei resigned their offices in numbers and flew off to Guangdong, Soong Mei-ling had been obliged to think privately about what might befall her husband. Even on these sightseeing outings she watched everything around her; anywhere she and Chiang passed, the least irregularity and she would order the attendants to run it to the ground. Today it had struck her all at once that there was a very serious error in going to the same place every day.

> "夫人多疑了。"站在蒋、宋两人身后，多时不敢说话的侍从室将领俞济时，这时站出来答话。俞济时是蒋介石浙江奉化的同乡，且与蒋家还有亲戚关系。所以他在蒋、宋身边担任侍从官，可谓是蒋、宋最放心的人物。现在俞济时见宋美龄对他选定的游览路线产生质疑，急忙近前辩白道："是这样，夫人，早在蒋主席上庐山之前，我们就已到山上观看了地形，发现西南方向虽然可供游览的名胜较多，但却地势险要。上山下山时走的都是些细如羊肠的山间小路，无法保护主席和夫人的安全。而庐山的东北坡，倒是地势平坦，道路也宽。在这些地方，即便有人想在暗中隐藏，也是根本不可能的。为主席和夫人安全计，我们只好把游览的方向选在了东北坡上。"

"Madam is over-suspicious." Yu Jishi, the Attendants' Office commander standing behind the pair, who had long kept silent, now came forward to answer. Yu Jishi was Chiang Kai-shek's fellow townsman from Fenghua in Zhejiang and was besides connected to the Chiang family by marriage; serving as their attendant officer, he was the man Chiang and Soong trusted most. Now, seeing Soong Mei-ling question the route he had chosen, he stepped up quickly to defend it. "It is like this, madam. Before Chairman Chiang came up Lushan we had already been over the ground here. We found that although the southwest offers more famous sights, the terrain is precipitous. Going up and coming down, the paths are as thin as goat tracks, and there is no protecting the Chairman and yourself. Whereas the northeast slope has level ground and broad roads. In such places, even if someone wished to hide himself, it would be simply impossible. For the safety of the Chairman and yourself, we had to choose the northeast slope."

> "你们当然是好意。可是，如若老让蒋先生去同一个地点，那么日久生厌倒还次要，万一引起什么人的注意，出了事又如何是好？"宋美龄的话说得有理有据，听得俞济时等侍从人员频频点头，哪个敢当她面去反对。

"You meant well, of course. But if Mr. Chiang is always taken to the same place, then his growing bored of it is the lesser matter — what if it draws somebody's notice and something happens?" Soong Mei-ling's argument stood on its evidence, and Yu Jishi and the other attendants nodded and nodded; who would contradict her to her face?

> "你们都退下去吧，既然夫人说去西南坡，明天我们就到那里游览吧！"蒋介石向俞济时等人挥挥手，示意他们可按宋美龄的主意行事了。但是，宋美龄却把俞济时等叫住了，她显然对蒋在庐山的安全更加认真，所以备加叮嘱说："但是，这绝不意味着去西南坡游览就可放松警戒。我是说，蒋先生出游的规律，千万不能让山上游客察觉出来。那样就非常危险了。至于去西南坡如何游览，俞济时，你们仍要给我拿出个万无一失的路线图来。否则，万一出了什么事情，我就拿你们是问！"

"You may all withdraw. Since madam says the southwest slope, tomorrow we shall go sightseeing there!" Chiang Kai-shek waved Yu Jishi and the rest away, signaling that they were to do as Soong Mei-ling wished. But Soong Mei-ling called Yu Jishi back. She was plainly in earnest about Chiang's safety on Lushan, and charged him particularly: "But this by no means implies that we relax our guard because we go to the southwest slope. I mean that the pattern of Mr. Chiang's outings must on no account be detectable by the tourists on this mountain. That would be extremely dangerous. As for how we are to tour the southwest slope, Yu Jishi, you will still produce me a route map that cannot fail. Otherwise, if anything happens, I shall hold you all to account!"

> 侍卫官们神色紧张地退出后，蒋介石让宋美龄看了戴笠的电报。读着电文宋美龄的心不由得提了起来，她说："从戴雨农的电报上看，南方反对派已经有了行动。特别是他们正和王亚樵进行勾结，更说明事情的严重性。达令，现在我们在山上，就更应该加小心了，万一王亚樵的人也混上山来，那我们的美庐可就不安全了。"

After the guard officers had withdrawn with their nerves showing, Chiang Kai-shek let Soong Mei-ling read Dai Li's telegram. Reading it, her heart rose in her. "By Dai Yunong's telegram, the southern opposition has already begun to move. That they are in league with Wang Yaqiao in particular shows how serious this is. Darling, now that we are on the mountain we ought to be more careful than ever. If Wang Yaqiao's men slip up here too, then even our Meilu is not safe."

> "不会不会，我的夫人。"蒋介石虽然也被电文搅得心慌，可他毕竟不相信孙科和汪精卫敢买通刺客对他下手，就嘿嘿一笑："汪精卫和孙科，他们虽然心里恨我，但我料想他们都无那种胆量。那个姓王的安徽人，我对他早有防备，如果他上了庐山，戴雨农不会不知道的。"

"No, no, my dear." Though the telegram had unsettled him as well, Chiang Kai-shek did not in the end believe Sun Ke and Wang Jingwei would dare buy assassins to move against him, and laughed. "Wang Jingwei and Sun Ke hate me in their hearts, but I judge neither has the nerve for that. As for that Anhui man named Wang, I have long been on guard against him; if he came up Lushan, Dai Yunong would not fail to know it."

> 宋美龄仍提心吊胆说："达令，还是小心无大错。这些天庐山虽然风平浪静，可是，不知为什么，我这心里老是紧张得要命，好像有一个可怕的影子，时时在我眼前闪动。我担心胡汉民的人，会不会收买杀手上山？"

Still Soong Mei-ling was uneasy. "Darling, caution costs nothing. These days Lushan has been calm enough — and yet, for some reason, my heart is tight with fear all the time, as though a dreadful shadow keeps flickering in front of my eyes. I worry that Hu Hanmin's people may buy killers and send them up."

> "不会，夫人，胡汉民他有那么大力量来与我对抗吗？"蒋介石见宋那么紧张，反而不以为然地冷笑起来。那天夜里，蒋介石在美庐做起了噩梦。在梦中他见一个满脸浓须的大汉，手握双枪向他和宋美龄猛然扑来，大喝道："姓蒋的，你杀了那么多无辜百姓，今天，我王亚樵替那些死去的冤鬼，来向你讨还血债！"蒋介石急忙逃走，可是王亚樵竟疾疾追来，没等蒋介石逃出美庐，他身后已响起一片枪声。吓得蒋介石大喊一声，蓦然从梦里惊醒了！

"They will not, my dear. Has Hu Hanmin such strength as that to set against me?" Seeing her so tense, Chiang Kai-shek laughed it off coldly. And that night, in the Meilu, Chiang Kai-shek had a nightmare. In it a big man with a heavy beard came at him and Soong Mei-ling with a pistol in each hand, roaring: "Chiang! You have killed so many innocent people — today Wang Yaqiao comes on behalf of all those wronged ghosts to collect the blood debt!" Chiang Kai-shek fled, but Wang Yaqiao came after him fast; and before Chiang could get out of the Meilu, shots broke out behind him. Chiang Kai-shek gave a great cry and woke with a start.

> 次日天明时分，蒋介石向宋美龄讲起那可怕的噩梦，宋美龄正感心虚，忙说："达令，既然我们都有不祥之感，今天索性就不外出了吧？"

At first light next morning Chiang Kai-shek told Soong Mei-ling of the fearful dream, and she, uneasy already, said quickly: "Darling, since we both have this feeling of ill omen, let us simply not go out today."

> 可是到了下午，蒋介石在美庐里呆得寂寞无聊，又对宋美龄说："夫人，其实夜里的梦，都是白天听你进言太多所致。哪会有人敢上庐山来和我们过不去？不如就出去走走，又有何妨？"

But by afternoon Chiang Kai-shek, bored and restless in the Meilu, said to her: "My dear, the truth is that dreams at night come of hearing too much advice by day. Who would dare come up Lushan to trouble us? Why not go out for a walk — where's the harm?"

> 当天下午，蒋氏夫妇等一行人又出了美庐。

That afternoon the Chiangs and their party went out from the Meilu again.

> 俞济时等大批侍卫由于是第一次向庐山西南方向出行，所以沿途警戒倍加小心。蒋介石和宋美龄来到仙人洞，只见那里巨石嵯峨，山岩间有棵古松，屹立在一方巨大的蟾蜍石后，显得苍翠而挺拔。他牵着宋的手走了进去，发现洞内泉水叮咚，石壁间清溪泪泪，壁上有古人留下的"洞天玉液"四字，实在是神仙休息的好去处。

Since this was the first time Yu Jishi and his large body of guards had gone toward the southwest of Lushan, the precautions along the way were redoubled. Chiang Kai-shek and Soong Mei-ling came to the Immortal's Cave, where great rocks stand jagged and an ancient pine rises among the crags behind a huge toad-shaped stone, dark green and straight. He led Soong by the hand inside and found a spring dripping within, clear water running between the stones, and on the wall four characters left by some ancient hand: JADE LIQUOR OF THE CAVERN HEAVEN. It was truly a fine resting place for immortals.

> 蒋介石和宋美龄在仙人洞里玩得好不快活，可是，他们做梦也没有想到，就在回来的路上，俞济时忽然在路旁的草丛里，发现一堆奇怪的东西，被人掩埋在土里。他急忙唤来侍卫，在草丛里一扒，原来是八九只空空的金华火腿壳！俞济时用树枝挑起一只，那被夏风吹得干瘪的火腿，竟然是空空的皮囊。

Chiang Kai-shek and Soong Mei-ling enjoyed themselves greatly in the Immortal's Cave. But never in their dreams could they have guessed what was coming: on the way back, Yu Jishi suddenly noticed a strange heap of something in the grass by the path, half buried in the earth. He called the guards over quickly and they scratched in the grass — and it proved to be eight or nine empty Jinhua ham casings! Yu Jishi lifted one on a stick: the ham, dried out by the summer wind, was an empty skin and nothing more.

> 俞济时没敢惊动蒋介石和宋美龄，他连夜召集几个亲信，将那些从草丛里发现的火腿皮囊反复看了又看。最后他们确认，这些看来平常的火腿外壳，都是曾经被人用刀子小心扒剥过的，而且里面曾秘藏过利器。他说："我敢断定，这些火腿皮内，曾经暗藏过一些铁器什物。即便不是枪械，也是禁止上山的犯禁之物，如大烟土之类的东西。如果是烟土，倒也无妨。我怕的就是枪支弹药，若真是这样，那我们可就要倒霉了呀！"

Yu Jishi did not dare disturb Chiang Kai-shek and Soong Mei-ling. That night he called several trusted men together and they examined the ham skins from the grass over and over. In the end they concluded that these ordinary-looking ham casings had all been carefully pared open with a knife, and that sharp implements had been hidden inside. He said: "I dare state it: iron objects of some kind were concealed in these ham skins. Even if not weapons, they were contraband forbidden on the mountain — opium and the like. If opium, no great matter. What I fear is guns and ammunition; and if that is what it was, we are in serious trouble!"

> 几个侍从官听了俞济时的分析，都感到事情的严重性："还是劝蒋先生改一下外出线路吧。不然出了大事，咱们这些侍卫可就难脱干系。""是啊是啊，发现火腿皮的事情，也该通报给夫人才是，看到这些东西后，她也许会改变主意的。"那些参与讨论的侍从，都七嘴八舌地向俞济时建议。可是俞济时自有他的主见，他知道在美庐里与其说蒋介石说了算，不如说宋美龄一言九鼎。而宋美龄既已做出要去庐山西南坡游览的决定，他再提出与夫人相左的意见，必然遭到宋的反对。他打断人家的话说："山上如果真有图谋不轨之人，我们就是不走西南坡，他们也会在北坡下手的。既然如此，最好的办法，只有加强警戒，除此之外，别无他法。"

Hearing Yu Jishi's analysis, several of the attendant officers felt the gravity of it. "Better urge Mr. Chiang to change his route. Otherwise, if something serious happens, we guards will hardly escape our share." "Just so, just so — the business of the ham skins ought to be reported to madam as well; seeing these things, she might change her mind." So the attendants in the discussion advised Yu Jishi, seven mouths and eight tongues at once. But Yu Jishi had his own view. He knew that inside the Meilu it was less Chiang Kai-shek's word that settled matters than Soong Mei-ling's, whose single word weighed as nine tripods; and since Soong Mei-ling had already decided on the southwest slope, to propose the contrary would certainly meet her opposition. He cut across them: "If there truly are men with evil designs on this mountain, then even if we avoid the southwest slope they will strike on the north. That being so, the only course is to strengthen the guard. There is no other."

> 就在那天夜里，俞济时确定了新的保护蒋、宋的方案。就是明一队暗一队，两只侍从队伍并行前进，随时防止可疑者接近蒋介石。

That same night Yu Jishi settled a new scheme for protecting Chiang and Soong: one party open and one concealed, two guard details moving in parallel, to keep any suspicious person from approaching Chiang Kai-shek at any time.

> 俞济时说："我们把侍卫团化为两个分队，一队还像以往那样，公开伴随蒋先生和夫人到各处游览。而我带着另一队侍卫，暗中在密林深处悄悄跟随，这样一来，如果真有胆大妄为的刺客，想在蒋先生身上打主意。那么，即使我们明行的侍卫不能发现，暗中隐藏的一队人马，也可以发现目标的。"

Yu Jishi said: "We divide the guard regiment into two detachments. One goes as before, openly accompanying Mr. Chiang and madam wherever they tour. And I will take the other detachment and follow quietly through the deep woods. That way, if there really is some assassin bold enough to plot against Mr. Chiang, then even if our open guards fail to spot him, the concealed party will."

> "这个办法太好了！"与会者听了俞济时的安排，都纷纷赞同。

"An excellent method!" The meeting approved Yu Jishi's arrangement with one voice.

> 6月17日，一个雨云密布的上午，庐山上下起了霏霏细雨，这给闷热的空气平添了一丝凉意。俞济时在蒋介石和夫人由一队荷枪侍从护卫着离开狮子岩以后，就带着十几个侍从悄悄隐进附近一片葱郁的竹林里。这是实施明暗两种保护方案以来，俞济时第一次亲自率人在暗中护卫蒋介石。他们一行人在竹林深处拉开了散兵线，尾随着在山岩上打着雨伞缓缓向美庐方向行进的队伍，亦步亦趋地跟进。

On the morning of June 17, under a heavy overcast, a fine rain began to fall on Lushan, adding a thread of coolness to the sultry air. When Chiang Kai-shek and his wife left Lion Rock escorted by a detachment of armed attendants, Yu Jishi took a dozen and more men and slipped quietly into a dense stand of bamboo nearby. This was the first time since the two-party scheme began that Yu Jishi had personally led the concealed guard. His party spread into a skirmish line deep in the bamboo and followed, step for step, the column moving slowly along the crags under umbrellas toward the Meilu.

> 这几天，俞济时连觉也睡不着，时时担心在庐山上会发生什么意想不到的事情。今天下着牛毛细雨，他心里不知为什么更加紧张和焦虑。大约走了一个时辰，小雨竟越下越大了。这时候，俞济时忽然发现前方不远的竹林里，闪动着一个穿着云湖衫的男人背影。他蓦然一惊，发现那人在前方一闪，就不见了。这种装束的人在庐山上并不鲜见，那些从东南亚地区来到庐山消夏的大亨巨贾，大多喜欢穿香港出产的云湖衫。可是，在俞济时手下的侍从中，却很少有人穿这种惹人注目的短袖汗衫。而且现在正下着雨，在浓密竹林里悄然行走的，只有俞济时率领的侍从室便衣，一般游客早在雨中返回了客栈。会不会是刺客？！当这一想法蓦然在俞济时头脑闪现时，他的心顿时怦怦狂跳起来。他已经看见那个穿云湖衫的陌生人，正从前方不远的竹丛中，向前方一块巨大的馒头石悄悄挨近了。俞济时加快了脚步，当他来到距馒头石还有几丈远的地方，突然发现那人的手正从腰间抽出，而且他断定那人手里正紧紧握着一支枪！俞济时透过密密层层的青翠竹林，已经望见对面高耸的岩间小路上，正有一队手擎雨伞的便衣，沿着山路走来，他们中间有两乘人抬的滑竿，上面坐着披了雨衣的蒋介石和宋美龄！天啊，此人就是刺客！一刹那，俞济时的心快要跳出胸膛来了，他担心由于自己的过于紧张而惊动了伏在馒头石后准备向蒋偷袭的刺客。恰在此时，那穿云湖衫的刺客猛然回转身来，将一张冷峻的面孔暴露在他面前！吓得俞济时急忙招呼左右的侍从一齐向刺客扑去！

For several days Yu Jishi had not been able to sleep, fearing constantly that something unforeseen would happen on the mountain. Today, with the fine rain falling, his tension and anxiety were worse than ever, for reasons he could not name. About an hour on, the rain grew heavier. And then Yu Jishi suddenly saw, in the bamboo not far ahead, the back of a man in a Yunhu shirt. He started violently; the man flickered forward and was gone. Such dress was not rare on Lushan — the magnates and great merchants who came up from Southeast Asia to escape the heat mostly favored these Hong Kong-made shirts. But among Yu Jishi's own attendants very few wore so conspicuous a short-sleeved garment. And it was raining now; the only people walking quietly in that thick bamboo were the plainclothes men of the Attendants' Office under Yu Jishi, ordinary tourists having gone back to their inns out of the rain. Could it be an assassin? The moment the thought flashed into his head, Yu Jishi's heart began to hammer. He could see the stranger in the Yunhu shirt now, easing forward through the bamboo toward a huge bun-shaped boulder ahead. Yu Jishi quickened his pace; and when he came within a few yards of the boulder he suddenly saw the man's hand coming up from his waist — and knew there was a gun gripped in it. Through the layered green of the bamboo Yu Jishi could already see, on the high path across the way, a file of plainclothes men coming along under umbrellas, and among them two mountain chairs bearing Chiang Kai-shek and Soong Mei-ling in their rain capes. Heaven — this man was the assassin! In that instant Yu Jishi's heart nearly came out of his chest; he feared his own tension would startle the killer lying behind the boulder to take Chiang from ambush. And at that very moment the assassin in the Yunhu shirt spun round and thrust a cold, hard face into full view. In terror Yu Jishi shouted to the attendants on either hand and they rushed him together.

> 那人正是特别行动队副队长陈成！

The man was none other than Chen Cheng, deputy commander of the special action team.

> 昨天晚上，陈成又和华克之暗中计议到凌晨三点，最后陈成认为蒋介石次日上午，很可能从美庐前去狮子岩。因为他算计着蒋介石最近几天去庐山西坡游历的景点，只有狮子岩一带没去过了，而狮子岩一带又是景色优雅，名胜古迹众多之地，那里既有百丈梯，又有方印石。他对华克之说："如果蒋当真要到那里去，倒是个杀他的好地方。因我观察过狮子岩一带，大多都是悬崖峭壁，幽洞深谷。即便山间有些小路，也多是只可容一人可单独通过的。一旦他们当真去了那个地方，我一个人就可以置他于死地了。"

The night before, Chen Cheng and Hua Kezhi had sat plotting until three in the morning, and Chen Cheng had concluded that Chiang Kai-shek would very likely go from the Meilu to Lion Rock the following forenoon. For by his reckoning, of the sights on the western slope, Lion Rock was the only one Chiang had not yet visited in these last days; and Lion Rock is a place of fine scenery and many famous relics — the Hundred-Fathom Stair is there, and the Square Seal Stone. He said to Hua Kezhi: "If Chiang really does go there, it is a good place to kill him. I have looked over the ground around Lion Rock: it is mostly cliffs and precipices, deep caves and ravines. Even the paths among the crags are mostly wide enough for one man alone. Once they truly go to that place, I can put him to death by myself."

> "不过，千万不能大意。"华克之听了陈成对狮子岩地形的描述，当即认为那里正是行刺蒋介石的理想区域。"如果蒋当真到那里去，我们就要多派些人去才是。你一个人在那里埋伏，即便得手，也是无法逃出来的。"

"All the same, we cannot be careless." Hearing Chen Cheng's description of the ground at Lion Rock, Hua Kezhi at once agreed it was the ideal district for the killing. "If Chiang really goes there, we must send more men. One man lying in ambush there, even if he succeeds, has no way out."

> 在华克之的极力主张下，陈成最后同意他和华克之、孙凤鸣三人同去狮子岩埋伏。次日上午，他们三人出来不久，天色忽见阴云。不久便飘起了细如牛毛的雨丝，空气顿时变得凉爽起来。陈成按照华克之的安排，隐藏在狮子岩左侧浓密的竹林边上。从这个隐身之处恰好可以望见对面山岩上那条从狮子岩通往美庐的必经之路——一条半山坡间的羊肠小路。如果蒋介石今天从这里经过，那么，他完全可以冲出去，就近向在山岩上艰难行走的蒋介石开枪射击。

On Hua Kezhi's strong insistence Chen Cheng agreed at last that he, Hua Kezhi and Sun Fengming should go together to lie in wait at Lion Rock. The next forenoon, not long after the three of them came out, the sky clouded over; soon a rain fine as ox-hair began drifting down and the air turned cool. Following Hua Kezhi's arrangement, Chen Cheng hid at the edge of the thick bamboo on the left of Lion Rock. From this place of concealment he could see, on the crags opposite, the road that must be taken from Lion Rock back to the Meilu — a goat track along the middle of the slope. If Chiang Kai-shek passed that way today, he could burst out and fire on him at short range as he made his difficult passage across the rock face.

> 陈成来到竹林不多时，小雨就渐渐沥沥下起来了。在沙沙雨声中，竹林里显得格外宁谧。这时，陈成忽听前方传来一阵杂沓的脚步声。循声望去，他的心顿时怦怦狂跳起来。他看见前面半山腰间小路上，一支疲惫的队伍，在如麻的细雨中无精打采地缓缓移动。那些侍卫大多没有雨具，腰间都掖着手枪。只有几人擎着雨伞，紧紧护卫着两乘人抬的滑竿，在湿漉漉的山岩小路上向美庐方向移动着。陈成知道自己等盼多日的机会终于到了，他已看清那前乘滑竿上坐着的人，正是他们日想夜盼的刺杀目标蒋介石！陈成那时也顾不得许多，就一个箭步冲出葱葱郁郁的竹林，来到距山岩较近的馒头石后面隐藏起来。

Chen Cheng had not been in the bamboo long when the rain came down in earnest. In the hiss of it the grove was uncommonly still. And then Chen Cheng heard a confusion of footsteps ahead. He looked toward the sound, and his heart began to pound. On the path along the middle of the slope in front of him a weary column was moving slowly through the raking rain, dispirited. Most of the guards had nothing against the wet, with pistols stuffed into their belts. Only a few held umbrellas, closing tightly around two mountain chairs that moved along the streaming rock path toward the Meilu. Chen Cheng knew that the chance he had waited so many days for had come at last: he could see clearly that the man in the forward chair was the target of all his waking and sleeping thought — Chiang Kai-shek! With no thought for anything else, Chen Cheng broke from the deep green bamboo at a bound and hid himself behind the bun-shaped boulder nearer the crags.

> 他从这里可以看清雨中行进的侍从队伍，但要对准乘坐滑竿的蒋介石射击，距离还是远了些。陈成那时只有一个想法，抓住这千载难逢的良机，将好不容易遇上的蒋介石开枪击毙！

From there he could see the guard column clearly through the rain, but for a shot at the Chiang Kai-shek in the chair the distance was still somewhat long. Chen Cheng had one thought only: to seize this chance that comes once in a thousand years and shoot dead the Chiang Kai-shek he had at last, and with such difficulty, come upon.

> 可是，就在他将手枪从腰里拔出，准备向渐渐走近的蒋介石开枪射击的时候，突然听到身后的竹林里，隐隐传来一阵沙沙的响声。他误以为是埋伏在另一方向的华克之和孙凤鸣也闻讯赶到了。可是，当陈成回头看时，忽然发现一个陌生人，正从竹林里飞蹿而出。同时，陈成又发现附近竹林里也闪出七八个头戴礼帽，手拿着美式驳壳枪的特务。

But just as he drew the pistol from his waist and made ready to fire on the approaching Chiang Kai-shek, he heard behind him, in the bamboo, the faint rustle of movement. He took it for Hua Kezhi and Sun Fengming, hidden in the other direction, coming up at the news. But when Chen Cheng looked back he saw a stranger springing out of the bamboo — and at the same moment saw seven or eight more agents flickering out of the grove nearby, in fedoras, with American Mauser pistols in their hands.

> "坏了！"陈成猛然意识到自己无论如何也难以逃离险境了。他原准备等待蒋介石的大队人马走得近一点再瞄准射击，以求击中目标，但是，持枪的特务已向他扑来，他已经顾不得许多，匆忙之中一跃而起，对准正在山间小路上行走的人群，"砰"的一声扣动了枪机——

"Done for!" Chen Cheng understood at once that there was no escaping now. He had meant to wait until Chiang Kai-shek's column came a little nearer and then aim for a certain hit; but the armed agents were already on him, and with no time left he sprang up and, leveling at the file of people moving along the mountain path, squeezed the trigger — crack.

> 清脆的枪声在岑寂的峡谷中激起了回响！

The clean report woke echoes through the silent gorge.

> 隐藏在狮子岩一侧松林丛中的华克之，猛听对面竹林里响起了枪声，情知必是陈成已发现了目标。这一声冷枪响过后，华克之正想冲上山岩前去支援，蓦然听到同一方向突然响起了爆豆般的枪声。华克之料定此时的陈成已身陷重围，密集的枪声必是蒋介石的侍从们发现刺客后的群起围攻。华克之一口气冲到山顶上，他透过松林的枝丫，看见狮子岩下那片青翠的竹林里，已是人头攒动。他想冲下去救护陈成，可是已经来不及了！

Hua Kezhi, hidden in the pines on the flank of Lion Rock, heard the shot in the bamboo opposite and knew Chen Cheng must have found his target. After that single cold shot, and just as Hua Kezhi was about to rush the crags to support him, gunfire like beans bursting in a pan broke out from the same direction. Hua Kezhi knew then that Chen Cheng was surrounded, and that this dense fire was Chiang Kai-shek's attendants swarming an assassin they had discovered. Hua Kezhi ran to the summit in one breath, and through the pine branches saw heads swarming in the green bamboo below Lion Rock. He wanted to go down and save Chen Cheng, but it was already too late.

> 山风中，细雨里，顿时弥漫起呛人的血腥。陈成已经遇难了！

In the mountain wind, in the fine rain, the choking smell of blood rose all at once. Chen Cheng was dead.
