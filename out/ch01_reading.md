## Chapter 1

Mr. C. L. Stephen and Mrs. C. L. Stephen request the great honour of the company of Mr. and Mrs. ×× at the birthday dinner-dance of Mrs. Stephen, to be held on 18 March 1940 at their residence, No. 41308 Route Lafayette.

R.S.V.P.

Stephen and his wife? I began to wonder. Stephen, with a wife? Was that not an odd thing?

Then it must be some other Stephen.

But the only Stephen I knew was this C. L. Stephen.

And how could C. L. Stephen fail to know that I was a man with no wife!

So there had to be yet another C. L. Stephen.

And him I did not know.

Yet it was he who had sent me this grand invitation.

Or could it be that this very C. L. Stephen was playing a joke on me?
