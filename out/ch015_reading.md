### Secrets of the Grove of Flesh

A friend of mine, who once held the post of inspecting the licenses of the brothels and has now retired from the work, met me yesterday at the Huang Family Garden in Caojing and told me two curious secrets of the Grove of Flesh, which struck me as very comical; I set them down here.

Everyone knows that the men who go to the salt-meat houses go for the venting of lust; so a whorer bent on being rid of his lust — is that not a strange thing? There was one such client, his hair all white, past the age of knowing his destiny, who came to a certain house and at once called for three or four of its girls. When they came he took off his gown and jacket, lay face-down on the couch, and bade the three or four of them make fists and pummel him, the harder the better. The beating done, he seemed to feel a lightness through his whole body, pleased past all bearing; then he paid each her fee, sent them off one by one, drew the quilt over himself and slept alone, and did not stir until daybreak. Was the man born with so base a frame that he could take no pleasure unless a ring of women stood beating him?

Another client, come to a certain house, charged the keeper to find him a girl with three-inch golden lotuses; he cared nothing for beauty or ugliness, set no limit of age, only that the feet be small. The house had none to offer; so the client laid out banknote upon banknote in a heap on the table and said, "Bring it off as I wish, and this shall be your reward." The keeper, greedy for the money, ran about in every quarter, and in the end tracked down, in a pheasant-den on Fourth Avenue, two old Yangzhou pheasants, both getting on for forty, and fetched them to the house. The client, seeing that the pair did indeed have three-inch golden lotuses, slender enough to close a hand around, was wild with joy. He settled himself on the sofa, set a low stool before him for the girls to sit on, bade them unwind their foot-bindings all together, and — drawing on a Manila cigar the while — slowly took in the smell of their feet, puffing and sniffing by turns, like a man savoring some flavor without end. One girl finished, another took her place; and when the sniffing was done he brought out a heavy sum to send them off, tipped the keeper handsomely, and only then straightened his clothes and cap and went bounding away in delight. A client with a fetish so strange must be of the school of Gu Hongming, who was fond of sniffing women's bound feet and called it a flavor all its own.
