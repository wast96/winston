### The Young Master Who Murdered a Courtesan

In the summer of the eleventh year of the Republic (the renxu year), the case in which Yan Ruisheng murdered Wang Lianying, a courtesan of Fuxiang Li, broke upon the public and caused a great sensation; people reckoned it the one great atrocity the flower-world had seen in some decades. Yan Ruisheng afterward fled to Xuzhou, where the local troops and police seized him and sent him back to Shanghai. The highest authority in Shanghai at that time was the Songhu Defence Commissioner, an office then held by He Fenglin (courtesy name Maoru); the case was examined and confirmed by Chief Deng of the military-law bureau, and Yan Ruisheng and Wu Chunfang were both sentenced to death under Article Three, clause two, of the Law for the Suppression of Banditry, while Zhu Zhijia (that is, Zhu Laosi, son of the late Ningbo gentleman Zhu Baosan) was pronounced not guilty. Afterward the New Stage at Jiumudi rushed a play onto the boards, entitled Yan Ruisheng, with Zhao Junyu as Wang Lianying and Wang Youyou as Yan Ruisheng; it ran four months to a full house every night, its success no less than that of Twin Sisters, shown this spring at the Xinguang. Later a ×× film company made it into a moving picture as well, which was screened for a long while; and hawkers of songs worked it up into ditties too, such as Lianying Visits in a Dream and Lianying's Lament through the Five Watches, which the women of the city took to most of all.

The verdict proper and the reasons for judgment issued by the Defence Commissioner's office ran to more than eight thousand characters—a truly grand production. Lianying had been the Premier of the Flower Kingdom, elected at the first Flower-Kingdom election held at the New World in the dingsi year; and after her election, to cut a figure, she sought nothing but luxury in dress and ornament, forever wearing a great diamond ring on her finger, dazzling to the eye—which was why she came at last to her cruel death. Yan Ruisheng, for his part, had been a student who left Aurora University without graduating; being of bad character, he fell in by mistake with criminals and sank into the gutter, and his murder of Lianying was purely a peeling-white gang's robbery for money. In the end he too could hope for no clemency, and was duly put to death by law.
