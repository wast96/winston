### Selling Sheep

A curses B for "selling sheep," and B in turn curses C for "selling sheep"; the cry of "selling sheep" is loudest in the mouths of the hoodlums and of those who eat official rice—the detectives and inspectors and their like. But how are these two words, "selling sheep," to be understood? Say a man is no scholar, yet gets himself up in a scholar's genteel airs; or say another is no honest sort, yet contrives to look the very picture of a law-abiding citizen: people of these several stamps are all of the "selling sheep" order.

There is an old two-part saying, "hang up a sheep's head and sell dog's flesh," which mocks a man whose conduct and dealings carry a taint of fraud and bad faith—such a one belongs to the "hang up a sheep's head, sell dog's flesh" sort. The two words "selling sheep" in the Shanghai vocabulary of abuse must surely derive from that old two-part saying.
