## Appendix I. Afterword

*In tender years I entered school; in the Northern Expedition I took up arms; a whole life's road — a diligent old soldier.*

*Charged with the sweeping of the fine dust within and without; an odd soldier of odd encounters, quick and alert to sound the alarm.*

*Guarding the leaders, by good fortune I escaped the star of calamity; ordered into hiding, I rescued the bulwarks of the state.*

*Justice finds many helpers; gladly I met kindred hearts; facing peril unafraid, meeting danger unshaken.*

*Again and again through strange dangers, where dark willows opened onto bright flowers; keeping safe, keeping the secret, holding to discipline.*

*Friends to the death, heart and liver laid bare, sharing disgrace and glory alike; an old steed at the stall, still hale; and justice cleared at last.*

*Never forgetting how I was nurtured, never failing the fellowship; the sweet-pear tree's kindly grace — a debt of love too deep to repay.*

*Friendship unbroken, loyalty forever felt; rehabilitated and vindicated, great virtue graven on my heart.*

*In venerable years, a flourishing age; the four seas sing in chorus; breaking new ground, ever climbing on.*

*Rule the land by virtue, govern cleanly and love the people; may noble character be borne aloft forever, and the red flag stand forever in glory.*

Winter 2002. Huang Mulan, an old soldier of ninety-six, at Hangzhou.
