### Gentleman Xue Loses His Nerve

Xue Dake was one of the "Six Gentlemen of Hongxian." On Yuan Shikai's secret orders he came to Shanghai with a heavy purse and opened a paper, the Yaxiya Bao, at the corner of Wangping Street and Nanjing Road, and threw himself into trumpeting the birth of the monarchy. When the Hongxian enthronement came, the Yaxiya Bao was the first to print, out in the margin of the sheet, the dateline "such-and-such a day of such a month, first year of Hongxian," by way of flattering Yuan. Hot-blooded citizens, learning what Xue was about, sent him warning letters again and again, but Xue was unmoved. Then one night a bomb came: with a splitting crash the great glass windows at the office door were shattered to bits, and the staff fled to a man. Xue, in the manager's room upstairs, heard the blast and shook in every limb, scuttled back to his lodgings, and for several days did not dare show his face at the office.

After this blow, though the Yaxiya Bao could not wholly change its tune, the cloying stuff it printed in praise of Yuan fell off a great deal. Later, when Yunnan rose in revolt and the provinces answered, the monarchy was cancelled and Yuan brought low, and this organ that had trumpeted the imperial cause thereupon ceased publication, while Gentleman Xue too went stumbling off from Shanghai, no one knows where.
