# Chapter 3 bilingual audit (QC only)

> "你说什么？叛徒是赵铁桥？"当王亚樵在合肥秘密寓所接到吴鸿泰从上海打来的电话时，不禁吃了一惊。

"What did you say? The traitor is Zhao Tieqiao?" When the call from Wu Hongtai in Shanghai reached Wang Yaqiao at his secret lodging in Hefei, he could not keep the start out of his voice.

> "对对，就是赵铁桥干的！"吴鸿泰的语气相当肯定。

"Yes — Zhao Tieqiao. It was his doing!" Wu Hongtai's tone left little room.

> "你们不会弄错吧？他怎么能出卖王乐平呢？"王亚樵虽然和赵铁桥没有深交，可是不久前在上海确也见过此人一面。那时，是王乐平亲自把这位西装革履，刚从美国留学归来的大学者，引荐到他的公馆里。平心而论，举止斯文，满腹经纶的赵铁桥，留给王亚樵的印象并不坏。一生喜欢杀杀砍砍的王亚樵，不知出于什么心理，竟然从心里喜欢那些颇有学识的知识分子，特别对赵铁桥这样在美国加州大学财经系毕业的高才生，从一开始就充满了敬意。甚至对王乐平找到赵铁桥这样的人做秘书，感到有些羡慕。可是，如今在上海的吴鸿泰竟把目光锁定在王乐平生前十分信任的赵铁桥博士身上，无论如何让他难以接受。

"You're sure there's no mistake? How could he have sold Wang Leping?" Wang Yaqiao had no deep acquaintance with Zhao Tieqiao, but he had in fact met the man once in Shanghai not long before, when Wang Leping himself brought this great scholar — western suit, leather shoes, newly home from study in America — to his residence. In fairness, the refined, book-fed Zhao Tieqiao had not left a bad impression. Wang Yaqiao, who had spent his life among hatchets, nursed for reasons he never examined a genuine fondness for men of learning, and toward a graduate in finance from the University of California he had been all respect from the first — he had even caught himself envying Wang Leping such a secretary. That Wu Hongtai should now be locking his sights on Doctor Zhao Tieqiao, the man Wang Leping had trusted most in life, was hard, whichever way he turned it, to accept.

> "九爷，就是这小子干的，绝不会错！如果有错，我愿以人头担保！"吴鸿泰在电话里一口咬定，这使将信将疑的王亚樵举棋不定。他既不能只凭吴鸿泰的一面之词，就轻信赵铁桥是内奸，但也无法否认吴的判断。王亚樵为慎重起见，命令和吴鸿泰同去上海的宣济民，连夜赶回合肥。他要当面向宣济民求证此事的真伪。因为在王亚樵眼里，最值得信任的人，还是在南京为他杀了张秋白的宣济民。

"Ninth Master, it was him. There is no mistake — and if there is, my own head stands surety!" Wu Hongtai bit the words off down the wire, and Wang Yaqiao, half believing, could not set his piece down on the board. He would not condemn Zhao Tieqiao as the inside man on Wu Hongtai's word alone; neither could he wave the judgment away. To be prudent, he ordered Xuan Jimin, who had gone to Shanghai with Wu Hongtai, back to Hefei that same night, to confirm the thing face to face — for in Wang Yaqiao's eyes the man most worth trusting was still the Xuan Jimin who had killed Zhang Qiubai for him in Nanjing.

> "九爷，您对吴鸿泰不该多疑，他的报告是有根有据的，连我也不得不佩服吴鸿泰确有一套识别叛徒的火眼金睛。他可是咱们斧头帮里的秀才杀手呀！"宣济民回到合肥王亚樵的秘密寓所，禀报了和吴鸿泰在上海侦察的经过。原来，他们到上海不久，就听说戴笠也从南京来到了上海，并在上海大世界里，举行一次别开生面的鸡尾酒会。当时，宣济民和吴鸿泰正想通过相关渠道，搞清王乐平遇刺的内幕，听说此事，便同去出席这个鸡尾酒会。

"Ninth Master, you wrong Wu Hongtai by doubting him. His report stands on solid ground — even I have to admire the man. He has a pair of fire-eyes with golden pupils for picking out traitors. He is the scholar among our killers, after all!" Back in the secret lodging at Hefei, Xuan Jimin reported the whole course of the Shanghai investigation. Soon after they arrived, they had heard that Dai Li too had come up from Nanjing — and was throwing a cocktail party, of all new-fangled things, at the Great World. Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai, who were just then working their channels to get at the inside of the Wang Leping assassination, heard of it and went together.

> 他们到了大世界一看，才知戴笠这场盛大酒会，竟是专为一个叫赵铁桥的人举办的，赶来凑热闹的不仅有上海军政两界的头面人物，而且还有许多商界大亨。还有一些花枝招展的电影明星、京戏演员和妙龄小姐们。这热烈的酒会场面颇让吴鸿泰和宣济民感到意外。他们发现与戴笠同时入场的，是一位身材颀长，风度翩翩的青年男子。在他们身旁簇拥着一些艳丽少女。

One look inside the Great World told them that Dai Li's grand affair was being held in honor of a single man — one Zhao Tieqiao. The crowd that had come to bask was not just the front-rank faces of Shanghai's military and official worlds but a host of commercial magnates besides, with a scattering of dressed-to-kill film stars, Peking opera performers, and young misses of tender years. The heat of the occasion took Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin by surprise. And they noticed that the man who entered at Dai Li's side was young, tall, elegant in his bearing, with a cluster of gorgeous girls around the pair of them.

> "怎么，两位先生连赵铁桥也不认识吗？他可是当今上海一位最走鸿运的人物了！"坐在吴鸿泰和宣济民身旁的，是一位艳丽的交际花。她早就在座位里向和戴笠相携而入的赵铁桥投去了羡慕的目光，她见吴鸿泰悄悄询问来者何人，马上如数家珍地向他介绍说："他嘛，如今可是个实权派人物了，把那李国杰手里的财权，也不客气地夺了过去。你们看，那李国杰的脸上如今有多晦气呀！"

"What — you gentlemen don't know even Zhao Tieqiao? Only the luckiest man in Shanghai today!" The speaker, seated beside them, was one of the celebrated flowers of Shanghai society. She had been throwing envious glances at the man on Dai Li's arm since he entered, and when Wu Hongtai quietly asked who the newcomer might be, she recited him like the family treasures: "Him? A man of real power now. Took the purse-strings straight out of Li Guojie's hands, and never a by-your-leave. Look at Li Guojie's face — did you ever see such a picture of ruin?"

> 宣济民和吴鸿泰都被赵铁桥吸引过去了。他们发现这青年人果然神气得很，举手投足间都显出了与众不同的倨傲。他和戴笠不时悄悄说些什么，然后相随走进堆满鲜花的主宾席，四座立刻响起了掌声和叫好声："戴老板，今天可要一醉方休啊！""戴先生，听说今晚的酒宴是你亲自摆下的，这场面可太让人羡慕了！""是不是酒宴过后还有舞会呀？"

Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai let themselves be drawn toward Zhao Tieqiao with the rest. The young man was in glorious feather, an arrogance in every lift of the hand that set him apart from the room. From time to time he and Dai Li exchanged something quietly; then the two moved together to the flower-heaped seats of honor, and the room broke into applause and shouts. "Boss Dai — no one goes home sober tonight!" "Mr. Dai, they say you laid tonight's banquet with your own hand — a man could die of envy!" "And is there dancing after?"

> 戴笠傲然地站起身来，彬彬有礼地向四座致意，特别对那些国色天香般的女人群里投去一笑，说："谢谢诸位，可惜，今晚的酒会和舞会，我并不是主人。真正的酒会主人在这里，他就是新上任的上海市招商局总办赵铁桥先生！"

Dai Li rose in his pride, saluted the room with every polish, and spared a particular smile for the banks of women fair as the national color and the heavenly fragrance. "My thanks to you all. Only I regret to say tonight's banquet and ball are not mine to host. The true host is here beside me — the newly appointed Supervisor of the China Merchants company of Shanghai, Mr. Zhao Tieqiao!"

> "哎呀，原来是赵总办做东啊！""你们看，赵总办真潇洒呀。一看就知他是个干大事的人物。""就是呀，听说他从前在王乐平手下做事，如今他扳倒了王乐平，又因祸得福，把上海滩最肥的差差也弄到手了。""是啊，听说赵铁桥是得到了蒋委员长的恩准，才坐上招商局第一把交椅的。"当初，宣济民和吴鸿泰都感到戴笠亲自出席一个商界人物举行的酒会，这件事的本身就引人注目。如今再听到那些对赵铁桥升迁内幕了若指掌的女眷们七嘴八舌地捧场，越加感到赵铁桥的来历非同一般。

"Well! So it's Supervisor Zhao who stands the feast!" "Look at him — there's ease for you. One glance and you know a man marked for great things." "That's it exactly — they say he served under Wang Leping once. Now he's toppled Wang Leping, come out of the calamity better than he went in, and landed the fattest post on the whole Shanghai waterfront." "True enough — and they say it took Chairman Chiang's own gracious nod to seat Zhao Tieqiao in the company's first chair." Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai had thought from the start that Dai Li's personal attendance at a businessman's banquet was a thing worth staring at in itself. Listening now to these women — who held the inside story of Zhao Tieqiao's rise like beads on a familiar string — chorus his praises seven mouths and eight tongues at a time, they felt more strongly still that Zhao Tieqiao's origins were nothing ordinary.

> "这姓赵的原来在王乐平手下做事？"吴鸿泰装成外地来客，故意向身边的女客打听有关赵铁桥的情况。

"So this Zhao worked for Wang Leping once?" Wu Hongtai, playing the out-of-town guest, angled for more from the ladies beside him.

> 一位喜欢卖弄的少妇说："那是自然，赵铁桥本是刚从国外回来的学生，可他发现在王乐平的身边做事，一辈子也难以发迹，所以才决定改换门庭嘛。"

A young matron who liked an audience obliged. "Naturally. Zhao Tieqiao was a student fresh off the boat, and he saw soon enough that at Wang Leping's side he'd wait a lifetime and never rise. So he changed doors, didn't he."

> 另一位女人也说："现在，听说赵铁桥上台后就大权独揽了，把个招商局的董事长李国杰，给弄成了孤家寡人。你们瞧，姓李的现在开始走麦城了！"

Another woman chimed in: "And now that he's up, they say he's gathered every rein into his own fist and turned the board chairman, Li Guojie, into a king of nothing at all. Look at him — the Li family's man has started down the road to Maicheng!"

> 宣济民顺着那妇人指的方向，透过攒动的人头向前望去，果见戴笠和趾高气扬的赵铁桥身边，呆呆闷坐着一位面色苍白的商人。他低眉叹息，只在那里百无聊赖地吸烟，不敢抬头和盛气凌人的赵铁桥目光对峙。就在李国杰尴尬万状，如坐针毡的时候，赵铁桥起来祝酒了，他手举酒杯抑扬顿挫地讲道："戴先生刚才过谦了，其实，今晚的酒会和舞会，都是为戴先生准备的。为什么如此铺张？就因为戴先生是我的知遇恩人呀。如果没有他给我引路，我永远都只能在漆黑的小路上徘徊。我现在才深深感到，一个人若想发迹成名，最要紧的就是要有指路人啊，戴先生就是我人生最好的良师益友。我为结识戴先生而自豪！来，诸位朋友，女士们，先生们，让我们共同举杯痛饮，一醉方休！请在座各位都和我一样，为戴先生亲自到上海出席我的就职酒会而痛饮三杯！"

Xuan Jimin looked where the woman pointed, through the swaying heads, and there indeed, beside Dai Li and the strutting Zhao Tieqiao, sat a pale-faced merchant, dull and shut in on himself. He kept his brows down and sighed, smoked with a boredom past all curing, and did not dare lift his head to meet the conquering gaze of Zhao Tieqiao. And just as Li Guojie sat there in his ten-thousandfold discomfort, on his cushion of needles, Zhao Tieqiao rose to give the toast, cup high, voice swinging in fine cadence: "Mr. Dai was too modest just now. The truth is that tonight's banquet and ball were laid for Mr. Dai. Why such extravagance? Because Mr. Dai is the benefactor who first saw worth in me. Without him to point the road, I would be pacing a black little lane to this day. Only now do I understand it fully: for a man to rise and make his name, the one needful thing is a guide to point the road — and Mr. Dai has been the best teacher and truest friend of my life. I am proud to know him! So come — friends, ladies, gentlemen — cups together, and drink deep, and no one home sober! Every seat with me now: three full cups, for Mr. Dai, who came to Shanghai in his own person to grace my accession!"

> 四座纷纷响应。就在宣济民在那里静听赵铁桥祝酒时，他发现身边的吴鸿泰在悄悄拉他的衣襟。他们悄悄出了宴会厅，来到厅外的阳台上，可见上海全城华灯灿烂，远处的黄浦江在漆黑夜幕下闪动着幽幽的光斑。

The room answered him round after round. And while Xuan Jimin stood listening to the toast, he felt Wu Hongtai quietly pulling at his lapel. They slipped out of the banquet hall onto the terrace, where the lamps of the whole city burned in splendor and the Huangpu flickered its ghost-lights far off under the black curtain of the night.

> "济民兄，真是踏破铁鞋无觅处，得来全不费工夫。"吴鸿泰兴奋地说道："九爷让我们寻找的人，不就在眼前吗？"

"Brother Jimin — iron shoes worn through in the hunting, and the thing drops in your lap." Wu Hongtai's voice was tight with excitement. "The man the Ninth Master set us to find — isn't he standing right there?"

> 宣济民一怔："你是说向蒋介石告密的叛徒，就是今晚酒会的主持人？"

Xuan Jimin stopped a beat. "You mean the traitor who informed to Chiang Kai-shek — is the host of tonight's party?"

> 吴鸿泰深以为然地点头："一点不差！我看，王乐平先生被刺肯定是这姓赵的暗中捣蛋呀。你也许不知道，赵铁桥从国外回来后，正是到处找不到事做的时候，王乐平给了他一个秘书职位。可是，现在王乐平被刺身亡，他赵铁桥不但没有半点悲伤，反而突然发迹成名，又当上了什么招商局的总办！你想过没有，这招商局总办可是天大的肥缺呀。当年和李国杰一同竞争这一肥差的大有人在，哪里能轮上一个在政界没有根底的小小赵铁桥呢？"

Wu Hongtai nodded slowly. "Not a point off. The murder of Mr. Wang Leping was this Zhao's work in the dark — I'd swear to it. Maybe you don't know: when Zhao Tieqiao first came home from abroad he couldn't find work anywhere, and it was Wang Leping who gave him his secretary's desk. Now Wang Leping dies by the gun — and this Zhao Tieqiao shows not half an ounce of grief; instead he leaps up famous overnight, Supervisor of the China Merchants company, no less! Have you thought what that post is? A fat vacancy the size of heaven. When it was contested in Li Guojie's day, great men stood in line for it. By what right does it fall to a little Zhao Tieqiao with no root in the world of government?"

> 宣济民也颇有同感地说："是啊，赵铁桥如不立下大功，蒋介石是绝然不会把上海招商局总办的要职给他的。可是，他一个刚从国外留学回来的青年人，又无政治根基，他会在蒋介石的面前立下什么功呢？这简直是不可思议啊！"

Xuan Jimin felt the same current. "True. Unless Zhao Tieqiao had laid some great service at Chiang Kai-shek's feet, the supervisorship of the China Merchants company would never come to him. But a young man just back from his studies, without a single political root under him — what service could he possibly have rendered? It defies imagining!"

> "赵铁桥的发迹，显然和戴笠有关。"吴鸿泰早在宴会厅里将戴笠和赵铁桥亲昵的接触，看得一清二楚。他分析说："眼前的事情，就不能不把戴笠先前去合肥拜见九爷的事连在一起了。从前戴笠在咱们斧头帮里的时候，哪像现在这样神气呢？还不是他投靠了蒋介石？所以我想，赵铁桥的提升和发迹，会不会和王乐平被杀、方振武将军在南京被扣有关呢？"

"Zhao Tieqiao's rise runs through Dai Li — that much is plain." Wu Hongtai had watched the two men's intimacy across the banquet hall and read it clean. "And what's before our eyes can't be separated from Dai Li's little visit to Hefei to call on the Ninth Master. When Dai Li was one of us, did he ever strut like this? It all dates from his going over to Chiang Kai-shek. So I ask myself: does Zhao Tieqiao's promotion trace to the murder of Wang Leping — and to General Fang Zhenwu's arrest at Nanjing?"

> 宣济民大吃一惊："你是说，把九爷和柏文蔚等人暗中倒蒋的计划，向蒋介石告密的人，就是赵铁桥吗？"

Xuan Jimin started. "You're saying the man who carried the Ninth Master's and Bo Wenwei's plan to Chiang Kai-shek — was Zhao Tieqiao?"

> 吴鸿泰沉吟说："我看只有他可能充当叛徒的角色。"宣济民却不以为然说："可是，九爷和柏文蔚、方振武暗中倒戈反蒋，赵铁桥根本不可能知道呀。他甚至连九爷的边也不曾沾过，又怎能向蒋告密呢？"

"As I read it, no one else could have played the traitor's part," Wu Hongtai mused. But Xuan Jimin balked: "And yet — the Ninth Master's dealings with Bo Wenwei and Fang Zhenwu — Zhao Tieqiao had no way to know them. The man never so much as brushed the Ninth Master's sleeve. How could he inform on what he never touched?"

> 吴鸿泰胸有成竹地说："济民兄，你怎么忘了，王乐平可是九爷有八拜之交的兄长，再说，王乐平死前就负责对石友三和方振武的联络任务。既然赵铁桥是王乐平身旁的秘书，他了解到蒋的机密，不是不可能的。"

Wu Hongtai had the answer drawn and ready. "Brother Jimin, you're forgetting. Wang Leping was the Ninth Master's sworn elder brother of the eight bows — and before he died, it was Wang Leping who carried the liaison to Shi Yousan and Fang Zhenwu. Zhao Tieqiao was the secretary at Wang Leping's elbow. That he came to know the secrets is no impossibility at all."

> "对，对呀！"宣济民听了吴鸿泰头头是道的分析，恍然地叹息说："你的分析完全正确。就是这个姓赵的暗中卖友求荣，致使王乐平惨死在敌人的暗枪之下。如今他杀了主人，又得到蒋介石赏赐给他的一份美差，所以才宴请恩人戴雨农嘛。看起来，戴笠来合肥面见九爷以前，已经从赵铁桥那里获得了我们斧头帮行动的全部计划呀！现在看来，赵铁桥好狠毒啊！"

"Right — right!" The orderly march of it drew a long breath from Xuan Jimin. "Your analysis holds at every joint. It was this Zhao who sold his friend in the dark to buy his own glory, and set Wang Leping in front of the enemy's guns. He kills his master, collects the fine post Chiang Kai-shek tosses him — and then throws a banquet for his benefactor Dai Yunong. Which means Dai Li had the whole plan of our action from Zhao Tieqiao's hand before he ever came to Hefei to smile at the Ninth Master! What venom there is in this man!"

> 王亚樵听了宣济民的报告，仍是将信将疑。他说："赵铁桥究竟是不是暗杀王乐平的奸细，你和吴鸿泰不过只是判断，现在并没有任何确切的证据。要给王乐平报仇，光凭这些望风捕影的推理是不成的。我现在要的是赵铁桥和戴笠相互勾结，出卖我方情报的确证。如果他确是杀害王乐平的内奸，哼，那么他姓赵的，可就快活到头了！"

Wang Yaqiao heard Xuan Jimin's report out, and still would not close his hand. "Whether Zhao Tieqiao is truly the spy who murdered Wang Leping — what you and Wu Hongtai have is judgment, and nothing more. There is no hard proof anywhere in it. You do not avenge Wang Leping on inference and shadows in the wind. What I require is firm proof that Zhao Tieqiao and Dai Li are hooked together and that he sold our intelligence. And if he is the inside man who killed Wang Leping — hm. Then the Zhao one's happy days have nearly run their course!"

> 宣济民次日再次赴沪，和吴鸿泰等人继续在上海寻找有关赵铁桥投蒋的确切证据。这时候，王亚樵在合肥也养好了病。到了6月中旬，正是上海滩最炎热的时节，王亚樵由夫人亚英和王干庭、牛安如等陪着，在一个漆黑的雨夜，乘一艘小船神不知鬼不觉沿着波涛滚滚的长江，直向上海进发了。

Xuan Jimin went back down to Shanghai the next day to continue the hunt, with Wu Hongtai, for hard evidence of Zhao Tieqiao's dealings with the Chiang camp. By then Wang Yaqiao had nursed himself whole again in Hefei; and in mid-June, at the burning height of the Shanghai summer, he set out — his lady Yaying beside him, Wang Ganting, Niu Anru and others attending — on a pitch-black rainy night, in a small boat down the rolling Yangtze, unseen by spirits and unknown to ghosts, straight for Shanghai.

> 王亚樵到了上海后，深居简出，暗观风云。他仍旧住在从前斧头帮起家时的安徽会馆旧院落里。他决心要在这里结果赵铁桥的性命，来为惨死在戴笠枪口下的挚友王乐平报仇雪恨。

In Shanghai Wang Yaqiao kept deep lodgings and simple movements, watching the wind and clouds from the dark. He put up, as of old, in the old courtyard of the Anhui Guildhall where the Hatchet Gang had first made its name. Here he meant to finish Zhao Tieqiao's life — vengeance, and the washing of hatred, for the cherished friend who had died under Dai Li's guns: Wang Leping.

> "九爷，现在我们已抓到赵铁桥通敌的真凭实据了！"一个下着淅沥细雨的夜里，吴鸿泰和宣济民悄悄来到王亚樵的卧房。王亚英知两人必为大事而来，急忙屏退了从人。然后让王亚樵和吴、宣两人在内室密谈，而她则亲自在门外担任警卫。内室里，一灯如豆，吴鸿泰向王亚樵禀报说："李国杰亲口对我说，赵铁桥就是被戴笠拉下水的。他是因为出卖了恩人王乐平，才得到现在这炙手可热的招商局总办的。"

"Ninth Master — we have it. Hard proof of Zhao Tieqiao's traffic with the enemy!" It was a night of thin drizzle when Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin came quietly to Wang Yaqiao's bedchamber. Wang Yaying knew the two came on great business; she cleared the attendants at once, let the three men close themselves in the inner room, and stood guard at the door herself. Inside, by a lamp-flame small as a bean, Wu Hongtai made his report: "Li Guojie told me with his own mouth — it was Dai Li who pulled Zhao Tieqiao into the water. He sold his benefactor Wang Leping, and the price paid him was the supervisorship he now holds, hot enough to scorch the hand."

> "鸿泰，别急，详细说出赵铁桥卖主求荣的来由。"王亚樵见多日盼望的复仇雪恨之计，终于有了眉目，心里就涌来复仇的热血。现在，万事俱备，只欠东风，他要吴鸿泰和宣济民把两个月来侦察赵铁桥得到的结果，如实地向他报告。

"Hongtai — slowly now. Every detail of how Zhao Tieqiao sold his master for glory." The revenge Wang Yaqiao had waited on for so many days finally had a face, and he felt the hot blood of it come up in him. Everything stood ready now but the east wind. He would have Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin lay out the whole two months of surveillance, fact by fact.

> 原来，宣济民再次赴沪后，他们每天派出几个斧头帮成员扮成修鞋匠和车夫，暗中跟踪赵铁桥，以便抓到真凭实据。为得到赵铁桥和戴笠勾结出卖王乐平的证据，吴鸿泰和宣济民设法和招商局人员接近。但是，一般职员难以掌握赵铁桥的真实情况。就在这时他们发现赵铁桥到任以后因有戴笠和蒋介石做后台靠山，根本不把董事长李国杰放在眼里。吴鸿泰和宣济民感到机会来了，因为李国杰也是安徽人士，于是吴鸿泰就以请客吃酒的名义，一次又一次接近李。初时，李国杰不肯对吴鸿泰表白心迹，只在饮酒之时不时发着怨气。有一天，吴鸿泰把李请到迪斯亚餐厅喝酒，他拼命地灌了几杯，当时心有积火的李国杰果然酒后吐真言，大骂赵铁桥："他妈的，狗仗人势，如果不看他是蒋介石眼里的红人，老子就买通强人，砍下他那狗头来。不然，将来这招商局可就是他的私人家当了！"

And this was the story. After Xuan Jimin returned to Shanghai, they had put men on the street daily — Hatchet Gang members got up as cobblers and rickshaw pullers — trailing Zhao Tieqiao for the hard proof. To reach evidence that Zhao Tieqiao had hooked hands with Dai Li to sell Wang Leping, they worked at befriending the China Merchants staff, but ordinary clerks could not see to the bottom of Zhao Tieqiao's affairs. Then they noticed something better: since taking office, Zhao Tieqiao — with Dai Li and Chiang Kai-shek at his back like a mountain — treated the board chairman Li Guojie as nothing at all. There was the opening. Li Guojie was Anhui-born besides; so Wu Hongtai, under the flag of hospitality, invited him to drink, once, and again, and again. At first Li Guojie kept his heart shut and only let grievance leak out with the wine. Then one day Wu Hongtai got him to the Disiya restaurant and poured with a will, and Li Guojie, with fire banked deep in him, did what wine makes true men do, and cursed Zhao Tieqiao to the rafters: "His mother's — a dog wearing a great man's power! If he weren't the apple of Chiang Kai-shek's eye, I'd have bought a strongman long ago and had that dog's head off him. Otherwise, mark me, this China Merchants company ends up his private estate!"

> 吴鸿泰趁机道："一个小小赵铁桥，他怎能和蒋介石挂上钩呢？"

Wu Hongtai slid in at the opening. "A little Zhao Tieqiao — how did he ever get his hook into Chiang Kai-shek?"

> 李国杰说："本来蒋介石根本就不认识谁是赵铁桥。后来，他不知通过什么途径结识了戴笠。就是他把从王乐平那里听来的消息，如斧头帮联合方振武、彭建国和石友三兵变的秘密掌握到手里了。那时，恰好戴笠就在上海搜集有关王亚樵的情报，所以赵铁桥就把从王乐平那里探听到的秘密，都捅给了戴笠。破坏了王亚樵的兵变计划以后，蒋介石为躲过一死暗自庆幸，于是才下令嘉奖赵铁桥。自然，赵铁桥的用意绝非为除掉一个王亚樵和方振武，他是想在上海捞到个实权。所以，他又把王乐平参与起事和来上海的时间、地点，都暗中通报给戴笠，致使他的恩主王乐平遭到暗杀。他妈的，这个卖主求荣的家伙，如今又来夺我的饭碗了。你说，将来他如果和蒋介石的关系更加深了，岂止夺我的钱财和地位，说不定什么时候，他还会利用戴笠和蒋介石，把我的脑袋也砍下来呀！"

"Chiang Kai-shek didn't know Zhao Tieqiao from any other name in the street — at first," said Li Guojie. "Then, by some channel or other, the man got to know Dai Li. And what he put into Dai Li's hand was the news he'd overheard at Wang Leping's side — the secret of the Hatchet Gang's rising with Fang Zhenwu, Peng Jianguo and Shi Yousan. Dai Li happened to be in Shanghai just then collecting on Wang Yaqiao, so Zhao Tieqiao poked everything he'd gleaned from Wang Leping straight to him. The mutiny was wrecked; Chiang Kai-shek counted himself escaped from under the knife and ordered Zhao Tieqiao rewarded. And of course Zhao Tieqiao's game was never just the removing of a Wang Yaqiao or a Fang Zhenwu — he wanted real power in Shanghai for himself. So he went further: he passed to Dai Li, in the dark, the day and the place of Wang Leping's coming to Shanghai for the rising — and so his own grace-master Wang Leping walked into the assassins' guns. His mother's! And now this same seller-of-masters comes for my rice bowl. You tell me — if his standing with Chiang Kai-shek deepens, will it stop at my money and my chair? One of these days he'll borrow Dai Li and Chiang Kai-shek and have my head off into the bargain!"

> "有这样的事？"吴鸿泰故作惊讶，对酒唏嘘。

"Can such things be?" Wu Hongtai played the astonished guest, sighing into his wine.

> "千真万确！"李国杰将胸口一拍，"老子眼睛里也揉不得沙子，有一天，他真敢对我下手，我就不客气了！"

"A thousand times true, and ten thousand times certain!" Li Guojie struck himself on the chest. "These old eyes take no sand in them either. The day he dares raise a hand against me, courtesy is over!"

> 王亚樵听了吴鸿泰和宣济民的报告，对赵铁桥充当奸细和叛徒已确信无疑。于是，当日夜里，他就在上海安徽会馆召开一个机密会议，周密讨论如何在上海除掉赵铁桥。可是，他们的会议刚刚开始，门外就有人闯进来急报说："九爷，李国杰打来一个电话，他问吴鸿泰在不在这里？"

With Wu Hongtai's and Xuan Jimin's reports laid side by side, Wang Yaqiao's last doubt of Zhao Tieqiao's treason went out. That same night, in the Shanghai Anhui Guildhall, he convened a secret council to work through, stitch by stitch, the removal of Zhao Tieqiao. The meeting had barely opened when a man burst in from the door with an urgent word: "Ninth Master — a telephone call from Li Guojie. He asks whether Wu Hongtai is here!"

> 王亚樵颇感意外地望着吴鸿泰。与会其他斧头帮主要成员宣济民、王干庭、牛安如、刘德才等，都把疑虑目光投向了吴鸿泰。吴鸿泰也不知李国杰为什么忽然把电话打进了安徽会馆。

Wang Yaqiao turned to Wu Hongtai in open surprise. The other principals at the table — Xuan Jimin, Wang Ganting, Niu Anru, Liu Decai — all swung doubting eyes the same way. Wu Hongtai could no more explain than they could why Li Guojie's call should come into the Anhui Guildhall.

> 王亚樵向吴鸿泰点头说："接他的电话，看他李国杰还有什么新的情报？"

Wang Yaqiao nodded him toward the stairs. "Take his call. Let's hear what new intelligence Li Guojie is carrying."

> 吴鸿泰到楼下接完电话，神色紧张地赶回来，急急地对王亚樵说："九爷，真不知李国杰在搞什么鬼把戏，他给我打电话，居然说要请您王九爷赴宴。"

Wu Hongtai took the call downstairs and came hurrying back with his face pulled tight. "Ninth Master — I don't know what ghost-game Li Guojie thinks he's playing. He telephones me — to say he wishes to invite you, Ninth Master Wang, to a banquet."

> "李国杰请我吃饭？"王亚樵闻言暗暗一怔。他虽然早在合肥时就结识了这位李鸿章的嫡孙，可是，多年来两人却极少往来。像李国杰这样有财有势的人请客，对于王亚樵来说，实在感到意外。想起他们正在讨论的暗杀赵铁桥计划，王亚樵越加感到疑惑："这姓李的是什么意思，莫非我们正在策动的杀赵计划，又跑风了吗？"

"Li Guojie asks me to dinner?" Wang Yaqiao was quietly taken aback. He had known this grandson of Li Hongzhang since the Hefei days, but in all the years since, the two had barely crossed paths. An invitation from a man of Li Guojie's wealth and station was, for Wang Yaqiao, genuinely unexpected — and with the Zhao Tieqiao plan on the table between them at that very hour, the doubt came up fast. "What does the Li one mean by it? Has our kill-Zhao plan sprung a leak of wind already?"

> 吴鸿泰见王亚樵又起疑心，就嘿嘿笑道："九爷多虑了，你是说李国杰想为咱们所痛恨的赵铁桥说情？他是绝然不会的。据我所知，李国杰和赵铁桥如今虽然都在招商局供职，可是，他们两人却已是冤家对头了。李国杰简直恨死了赵铁桥，他怎么会为他来讲情呢？"

Wu Hongtai chuckled at the rising suspicion. "The Ninth Master worries too far. You think Li Guojie comes to plead for the Zhao Tieqiao we hate? Never in this life. As I know it, Li Guojie and Zhao Tieqiao may draw pay from the same company, but the two are sworn foes now. Li Guojie hates the man to death. Plead for him!"

> "对对，"宣济民见王亚樵坐在那里吱吱吸水烟，半晌沉默无语，知他又想起了前次倒蒋失败的教训，甚至对身边的几位至诚弟兄也暗生狐疑，就替吴鸿泰解释说："鸿泰所言甚有道理。从前招商局的大权，始终都是李国杰一人独揽。可是，自赵铁桥到任，他的处境就大大不同了。赵铁桥根本不把李国杰放在眼里，他放肆地抓权，早就惹恼了李国杰。所以，我敢保证，他绝不是为赵铁桥来说情的。"

"That's so," Xuan Jimin put in. He had watched Wang Yaqiao sitting there pulling on the water-pipe, silent through a long moment, and knew where the silence went: back to the last defeat, and to the fox-doubt that had crept even over the truest brothers at his side. So he spoke for Wu Hongtai: "What Hongtai says stands to reason. The power of the China Merchants company used to gather in Li Guojie's one hand. Since Zhao Tieqiao took office, that world is gone. Zhao Tieqiao holds him at nothing, grabs at power with both fists, and has been feeding Li Guojie's anger for months. Whatever this banquet is, I will guarantee it is no pleading for Zhao Tieqiao."

> "那么，他为什么要请我喝酒？"王亚樵感到不可思议。

"Then why does he want me at his wine?" Wang Yaqiao turned it over and found no bottom.

> 王干庭说："九爷，宣、吴两位兄长，在上海已侦察多日，他们的话不可不信。"刘德才和牛安如也觉得李国杰这时请王亚樵吃饭虽然有些反常，但是他们也坚信李绝不可能来为赵铁桥说情。至于李国杰为何设宴，大家都难以解开这个谜底。

Wang Ganting said: "Ninth Master, brothers Xuan and Wu have been scouting Shanghai for days on end. Their word is not to be set aside." Liu Decai and Niu Anru agreed: the invitation was strange in its timing, but a man pleading for Zhao Tieqiao it could not be. What Li Guojie did want, none of them could riddle out.

> "九爷，既然有酒可喝，我看还是去喝吧？"沉思不语的吴鸿泰终于开口，"我想，和李国杰接触，也许对咱们的刺赵行动有所帮助。因在反对赵铁桥这件事上，李国杰至少和我们是不谋而合的。"

"Ninth Master — where there's wine, I say we drink it." It was Wu Hongtai, out of his silence, who settled it. "Contact with Li Guojie may even serve the action. On the matter of Zhao Tieqiao, at the least, Li Guojie and we have come — unplanned — to the same place."

> 王亚樵说："你是说李国杰想利用咱们的手，去杀他的仇人？"

Wang Yaqiao said: "You mean Li Guojie wants to borrow our hand for his own enemy?"

> 吴鸿泰望望宣济民，两人都深以为然地点点头："非常可能。"大家正在楼上议论着李国杰为何忽然宴请王亚樵，这时，守门的弟兄又走上来，手上托着张大红帖子，原来是李国杰派人送来的请柬。王亚樵接过一看，上写毛笔小楷：

Wu Hongtai glanced at Xuan Jimin; both nodded slowly. "Very possible." They were still turning it over when the brother from the gate came up again, a great red card on his palms: Li Guojie's formal invitation, delivered by messenger. Wang Yaqiao took it and read the neat small brush-hand:

> 敬启者：定于明日正午12点，在大鸿运酒楼雅座，略备菲酌，届时敬请九光仁兄光临。安徽乡人李国杰

To the respected: Tomorrow at twelve noon, in the private room of the Great Fortune restaurant, a meager cup stands ready. We reverently beg the honored elder brother Jiuguang to grace us with his light. — Li Guojie, countryman of Anhui

> 王亚樵将李国杰的帖子反复看了看，又把目光落到吴鸿泰和宣济民身上，说："他和赵铁桥的仇恨，莫非真到了买凶杀人的地步吗？"

Wang Yaqiao read the card over twice and then looked up at Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin. "This hatred of his for Zhao Tieqiao — has it truly walked as far as hiring the knife?"

> 宣济民和吴鸿泰都说："他的仇不比我们小，因为他李国杰的钱，现在都流进赵铁桥的口袋里去了！"

"His hatred is no smaller than ours," they answered him. "Every dollar of Li Guojie's flows into Zhao Tieqiao's pocket now!"

> "那好，"王亚樵已将主意拿定，忽然将手在桌上一拍，"如果李国杰真想利用我王九光的手，那可就正中下怀了。他既然想用我们斧头帮，那就让他出点血了！"

"Good, then." Wang Yaqiao's palm came down flat on the table; the decision was made. "If Li Guojie means to borrow the hand of Wang Jiuguang, he has played directly into my wishes. And since he wants the use of our Hatchet Gang — let him bleed for it!"

> 众人见王亚樵吐了这话，都发出了会心的笑声。

The room answered him with knowing laughter.

> 翌日正午时分，王亚樵身穿白色纺绸裤褂，在吴鸿泰、宣济民和刘德才等几位斧头帮兄弟们的簇拥之下，从安徽会馆，径直来到南京路上的大鸿运酒店。二楼雅座前早已迎候一位笑吟吟的绅士，他灰绸长袍，手摇一把纸扇，显得谦恭客气，见王亚樵带着几位杀手走来，急忙笑迎如仪："请，九爷快请。现在在上海地面上混的安徽人，可都沾了九爷的光了！"

At noon the next day Wang Yaqiao, in white silk-crepe jacket and trousers, with Wu Hongtai, Xuan Jimin, Liu Decai and several more of the brothers around him, walked from the Anhui Guildhall straight to the Great Fortune restaurant on Nanjing Road. At the door of the second-floor private room a beaming gentleman already stood waiting — gray silk gown, paper fan swaying, courtesy itself — and at the sight of Wang Yaqiao arriving with his killers he came smiling through the full rite of welcome: "Please — Ninth Master, please! Every Anhui man making his way on Shanghai ground these days walks in the Ninth Master's light!"

> 王亚樵也不答礼，只顾带吴鸿泰、宣济民、牛安如、刘德才等走进雅座，里面宽敞明亮。不久，女侍们鱼贯而进，上来一碟碟精致的苏州菜。王亚樵看时都是上乘美味：栗子蒸闷鸡、醋味熏鱼、虾仁烂糊和原汁香鸭等。一壶女儿红老酒。开启以后，雅座里顿时弥漫起沁人的馨香。

Wang Yaqiao returned no courtesies. He simply led Wu Hongtai, Xuan Jimin, Niu Anru and Liu Decai into the wide bright room. Soon the serving girls filed in with plate after small plate of fine Suzhou cooking — all of it, Wang Yaqiao saw, of the first water: chestnut-smothered chicken, smoked fish in vinegar, creamed shrimp, duck in its own broth. And one pot of aged nü'erhong, which, opened, filled the room with a fragrance that went straight to the marrow.

> "来来。九爷，满饮此杯，以慰平生！"李国杰屏退女侍，郑重地敬了一杯，"国杰虽早有置酒款待之意，可惜始终得不到相聚之机。今日好不容易得到九爷赏光，那就让我举杯为敬吧？"

"Come, come — Ninth Master, a full cup, to the comfort of a whole life!" Li Guojie waved out the serving girls and gravely raised the wine. "Guojie has long meant to set a table in your honor and never had the luck of the meeting. Today, at long last, the Ninth Master grants his light. Let me lift the first cup in respect."

> 不料王亚樵竟以手将杯子挡住："国杰贤弟，我这人在酒席上有三不喝。一是仇人的酒不喝；二是恩人的酒不喝；三是不明究竟的酒也不能喝。今天既蒙贤弟摆酒，不是还人情，就是有所求。我必要先明白你有何事求我，方可满饮此杯。"

Against all custom, Wang Yaqiao's hand came over the cup. "Worthy brother Guojie — at the table I am a man of three refusals. The wine of an enemy, I do not drink. The wine of a benefactor, I do not drink. And wine whose bottom I cannot see — that I do not drink either. You have laid this table, brother, either to repay a debt or to ask a thing. I will know first what you ask of me. Then I will drain the cup."

> 李国杰再三劝酒，王亚樵只是坚辞不喝。李国杰见状，只好哀叹一声，现出一副愁容说："九爷也知我是官宦人家出身，来上海经商也是出于无奈。因在蒋介石的官场上，历来不会有我们李氏家族的位置。经商倒也罢了，可是，蒋介石还找我的麻烦。现在我在招商局里简直没法混下去了！"

Li Guojie pressed three times; three times Wang Yaqiao stood firm and would not drink. At last, with a long sigh, Li Guojie let the sorrow show. "The Ninth Master knows the family I come from — officials and their sons. Coming down to trade in Shanghai was never my choice: in Chiang Kai-shek's officialdom there is no seat, and never will be, for the house of Li. Very well — trade, then. But Chiang Kai-shek will not even leave my trade alone. And now, in the China Merchants company, I can no longer live at all!"

> 王亚樵与吴鸿泰、宣济民等互换个会心眼神，都感到不出所料。但他故作困惑地说："国杰贤弟何出此言？你本是招商局名正言顺的董事长，堂堂正正的第一把交椅。在招商局你理当说一不二，何故忽出此言，怎么混不下去？"

Wang Yaqiao traded one knowing glance with Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin: as foreseen, all of it. But he played the puzzled guest. "Worthy brother, where do such words come from? You are chairman of the company's board by right and by name — the first chair, in the light of day. In the China Merchants company your word should fall like law. What talk is this of not living?"

> 李国杰哭丧着脸说："从前我确也混得潇洒。可是，自蒋介石和戴笠把赵铁桥派来，当了招商局总办以后，我的日子就难过了！他妈的，赵铁桥算个什么东西？这家伙表面上忠厚老实，装出学者的模样。可是当你和他相处久了，就会知道此人的奸诈和跋扈。现在他在招商局里，把凡能生财的路，都给我堵死了。九爷你说，让我还在招商局里如何生存呢？"

Li Guojie's face went to mourning. "Once, true enough, I lived in style. But since Chiang Kai-shek and Dai Li sent me Zhao Tieqiao for a supervisor, my days have gone black. His mother's — what is Zhao Tieqiao? On the surface, loyal, honest, every inch the scholar. Keep his company a while and you'll learn the treachery and the tyranny of him. Today, inside that company, every road by which money might grow he has walled shut against me. You tell me, Ninth Master — how does a man survive in the China Merchants company now?"

> 王亚樵对李国杰的宴请已了然于胸，心里暗暗好笑。没想到他和李国杰在暗杀赵铁桥一事上竟然不谋而合，但他仍然顾左右而言他，不往正题上说，只等李国杰主动说明。王亚樵说："赵铁桥何许人，我们并不认识。我就不信他一个外来人，敢和咱们安徽人作对？再说，你国杰弟既然手里有钱，何必怕他一个文人秀才？"

Wang Yaqiao had the whole shape of the banquet clear in his chest by then, and was quietly laughing at the joke of it — that he and Li Guojie should arrive, each by his own road, at the same assassination. But he kept the talk wide of the mark and waited for Li Guojie to carry it there himself. "Zhao Tieqiao — we've never so much as met the man. And I don't believe an outsider dares set himself against Anhui men on this ground. Besides, brother — with money in your hand, what is there to fear in one pen-and-paper scholar?"

> 李国杰马上凑近说："九爷，你说得对。无毒不丈夫。现在他赵铁桥既然不仁，我李某人也就不义了。所以，我在万般无奈之下，想来想去，忽然想起个能救我出苦海的人，他就是你王九爷啊！"

Li Guojie leaned close. "Ninth Master, you say it exactly. No venom, no true man. Since Zhao Tieqiao has cast off humanity, Li Guojie casts off righteousness. At the end of every helpless road I turned it over and turned it over — and then I thought of the one man who could lift me out of this sea of bitterness. And that man is you, Ninth Master Wang!"

> "我？！我又怎能救你？"王亚樵故意与李国杰打哑谜，"我手里既无权也无势。穷光蛋一个，又怎能动摇一个招商局的总办？"

"Me? And how would I save you?" Wang Yaqiao made him spell the riddle out. "I hold no office and no army. A bare pauper — how does a pauper shake the Supervisor of the China Merchants company?"

> 李国杰见王亚樵故弄玄虚，就说："九爷，别卖关子了！凡在上海滩混的人，哪个不知九爷为人的仗义？您手下的斧头帮，个个都是冷面杀手，专为那些受恶霸欺诈的有志之士伸张正气。如今我在上海受赵铁桥欺负，你难道能袖手旁观吗？"

At this show of mystery from Wang Yaqiao, Li Guojie burst out: "Ninth Master — enough selling of the pass! "Show me the man on the Shanghai waterfront who doesn't know the Ninth Master's honor. The Hatchet Gang under your hand are cold-faced killers to a man, and their trade is stretching justice straight for men of purpose whom the tyrants have trodden on. I am being trodden on, here in Shanghai, by Zhao Tieqiao. Will you truly fold your hands into your sleeves and watch?"

> "怎么，你是想让我除掉赵铁桥？"王亚樵故作惊讶地瞪了眼睛，望着李国杰那张沁出汗水的小白脸。

"What's this — you want me to remove Zhao Tieqiao?" Wang Yaqiao gave him wide eyes, studying the small white face with the sweat starting out on it.

> 李国杰郑重地说："正是此意，九爷，现在能救我的只有你了。请您放心，事成之后，我李某人绝不会没有表示的。"他说着，随手将皮包打开，从里面悄悄摸出个厚厚纸包来，塞到王亚樵口袋里："这是一点小意思。八千块，先讨个吉利。事成后……"

"Exactly that, Ninth Master," said Li Guojie, grave as an oath. "No one can save me now but you. And be easy on one point: when the thing is done, Li Guojie will not be found ungrateful." With that he opened the leather case at his side, slid out a thick paper packet, and pressed it into Wang Yaqiao's pocket. "A small token. Eight thousand — call it luck-money. And when it's done…"

> "好吧，既然国杰贤弟在上海受人欺负，我王九光岂能坐视不救？"王亚樵将钞票点了点，又交给了李国杰，说，"可是，贤弟办这么大一桩事，难道只舍得拿这只够喝茶的零钱来吗？"

"Very well. Since my worthy brother is bullied on Shanghai ground, shall Wang Jiuguang sit and watch?" Wang Yaqiao counted the notes through — and handed them back. "But tell me, brother. For a matter of this size, is this all you'll part with — tea money?"

> "不不，九爷，这点小钱确实不成敬意。我是想请您和弟兄们喝茶的。"李国杰忽然把心一狠，"这样吧，九爷也是见过世面的人物，我给钱不知给多少合适。不过，先请你们把事给办了。只要把姓赵的结果了，到时候我会给九爷一个惊喜！"

"No — no, Ninth Master, that little sum was never meant as the respect itself. Tea for you and the brothers, nothing more." And then Li Guojie hardened his heart. "Let us do it this way. The Ninth Master has seen the world; what the proper figure is, I confess I don't know how to name. But — do the thing first. Once the Zhao one is finished, there will be, I promise you, a great glad surprise."

> 王亚樵让牛安如把钱收了，喝口酒说："说说看，究竟是什么惊喜？"

Wang Yaqiao had Niu Anru take the money in, drank a mouthful, and said: "Let's hear it, then. What surprise?"

> "九爷，是这样，办这种大事的价码，我当然不会不知道。可是，如今招商局的钞票，都进了他赵铁桥的手，我哪会一下子拿出那么多钱来孝敬九爷呢？"李国杰也是个商海里的精明人物，他看出王亚樵已对暗杀赵铁桥的事情接受了下来，只是嫌他给的订金太少，所以迟迟不肯拍板。李国杰为促成此事，亦为让王亚樵尽快动手，于是说，"这样吧，为报答九爷的恩情，我李国杰情愿舍出一艘大船来。就是我们招商局的那艘'江安号'货轮，这总该是一个大礼物吧？"

"It's like this, Ninth Master. The price of work at this scale — of course I know there is one. But every banknote in the China Merchants company passes through Zhao Tieqiao's hands now. Where would I find such a sum at one stroke to honor you with?" Li Guojie was a shrewd fish of the commercial sea: he had seen that Wang Yaqiao had accepted the killing and was only holding out against a thin deposit. To close the bargain, and to hurry the hand that would do it, he said: "So — this. In repayment of the Ninth Master's grace, Li Guojie will part with a ship. A great one. Our company's freighter — the Jiang'an. Now surely that stands as a gift?"

> "江安号货轮？"王亚樵初时暗暗一惊，他没想到李国杰为杀个仇人赵铁桥，竟肯舍弃一艘海上行驶的大船来作为给他的回报。王亚樵心里一喜，认为一艘船当然比李国杰多送他几千元钞票更为有利，如果他们斧头帮将来有艘大船，那么从黄浦江上前往合肥，就多了种得心应手的运输工具。只是王亚樵又多个心眼，说，"国杰贤弟，其实你拿招商局的船换赵铁桥的人头也是值的，因你将来可以放手大肆捞钱了。只是，要知道这'江安号'毕竟是公产，你把它送给我，将来会不会惹是生非呀？"

"The freighter Jiang'an?" For a moment Wang Yaqiao was honestly startled: to kill one enemy, the man would sign away an ocean-going ship. Then came the pleasure. A ship was worth more to him than any few thousand more of Li Guojie's dollars — with a great ship of its own, the Hatchet Gang would hold the run from the Huangpu up to Hefei in the palm of its hand. But he kept one fold of caution. "Worthy brother — trading a company ship for Zhao Tieqiao's head is good business for you; with him gone you can rake with both hands. Only remember: the Jiang'an is public property. You give her to me — will that not breed trouble down the years?"

> "不会，我李国杰什么时候言而无信？"

"It will not. When has Li Guojie's word ever failed?"

> "那好，咱一言为定。"王亚樵毕竟是久闯江湖的老手，当即首肯拍板，"官凭文书私凭印。既然你国杰贤弟情愿送这么大的礼，空口无凭总怕不行吧？"

"Then it's settled at one word." Wang Yaqiao was an old hand of the rivers and lakes; he nodded and struck the bargain there. "Officials trust the document, private men trust the seal. Since my worthy brother is pleased to send so great a gift, an empty mouth will hardly serve for proof — will it?"

> 李国杰把胸一拍："九爷放心，我马上派人给您送一张契约，如何？"

Li Guojie smacked his chest. "Rest easy, Ninth Master. I'll send a man with the deed of contract at once. Will that do?"

> 王亚樵当即应允："那好，君子一言，驷马难追。你的契约一到我手，我们马上就让赵铁桥的人头落地就是了！"

"It will," said Wang Yaqiao. "A gentleman's word once out, four horses cannot fetch it back. The day your contract reaches my hand, Zhao Tieqiao's head goes to the ground. No more need be said!"

> 当即，几个斧头帮干将和李国杰在大鸿运酒楼里计议已定，然后纷纷将杯子举起，砰然相碰。至此，诛杀赵铁桥的大计就在推杯换盏中定了下来。

And so it was settled, then and there in the Great Fortune restaurant, between Li Guojie and the stalwarts of the Hatchet Gang; and the cups came up all around and rang together. The execution of Zhao Tieqiao was fixed amid the pushing of cups and the trading of toasts.

> 漆黑的马路上，远远亮起一副车灯。

On the pitch-black road, far off, a pair of headlamps came alight.

> 这是1930年6月30日深夜。夜深人静的法租界，在盛夏子夜里显得格外幽静和安谧。一幢幢亮着灯盏的小洋楼，都静悄悄隐蔽在一片片幽黑的法国梧桐林中。但是，路边仍然不时出现夜行的情侣。一些在夏夜里因燥热睡不着觉的人们也在外边纳凉，谁也不会想到就在这安静的夏夜里，在这条平时不时有法国巡捕游动的路口，竟会响起一阵刺耳的枪声。

It was deep in the night of June 30, 1930. The French Concession lay dead still, uncommonly hushed and mild in the midsummer midnight. The little foreign houses with their lit lamps hid quietly in stand after gloomy stand of plane trees. Still, the roadside was not empty: night-walking lovers passed at intervals, and here and there people driven out of bed by the parching heat sat taking the cool. No one could have dreamed that on this quiet summer night, at a crossing where the Concession police drifted by on their ordinary rounds, a burst of gunfire was waiting.

> 预先埋伏在法租界戈登路口一片梧桐树阴影里的几个杀手，人人手持短枪，头上蒙着黑色面罩，这是王亚樵精心挑选的牛安如、王干庭、夏少恩等三人。以牛安如为首的三个杀手，都参与过南京行刺陈调元的行动，因此都有暗杀行刺的经验。早在一个月前，当王亚樵和李国杰在大鸿运酒店商定了除赵大计后，王亚樵就派出了精悍杀手，分别组成两个行动小组。一个是由吴鸿泰和刘德山等人组成的侦察小组，专门在暗中监视赵铁桥的行踪。另一个则是刺杀小组，策划了几种行刺方案。

The killers planted in the tree-shadow at the Gordon Road crossing each held a short gun, faces hooded in black: Niu Anru, Wang Ganting and Xia Shao'en, three men, chosen by Wang Yaqiao with care. All three, with Niu Anru at their head, had been in the Nanjing attempt on Chen Diaoyuan; the work was not new to them. A month earlier, as soon as the bargain was struck at the Great Fortune restaurant, Wang Yaqiao had formed his picked killers into two action groups: a scouting group under Wu Hongtai and Liu Deshan to shadow Zhao Tieqiao's movements, and an assassination group, with several schemes of attack drafted and ready.

> 王亚樵为尽快把赵铁桥除掉，特给吴鸿泰派了一辆脑拿牌轿车。这样吴鸿泰等就可以不断乘坐小汽车出入在戈登路至上海招商局之间的马路上，对赵铁桥进行秘密监视。对赵什么时候上班，什么时候回家，什么时候去出席酒会和舞会，甚至对赵每天的一行一止都了若指掌。由于吴鸿泰等人的日夜监视，最后发现赵铁桥经常在夜里有应酬活动，各种饭局几乎应接不暇。在掌握了这夜夜外出的规律以后，王亚樵才决定派出第二个行动小组，即王干庭和牛安如等杀手，在六月下旬最炎热的深夜里开始施行暗杀了。

To speed the work, Wang Yaqiao had assigned Wu Hongtai a sedan — a "Naona," whatever marque hid behind the name. With it, Wu Hongtai's men could travel the roads between Gordon Road and the China Merchants offices at all hours, keeping their quiet watch: when Zhao went to the office, when he came home, when he went out to his banquets and his dances — every start and stop of the man's day held like a thing in the palm. Night after night of watching yielded the pattern: Zhao Tieqiao's evenings were an unbroken chain of engagements, feast upon feast past the answering. Once that nightly rhythm was firm, Wang Yaqiao committed the second group — Wang Ganting, Niu Anru and their fellows — to strike in the hottest deep nights of late June.

> 这天傍晚，牛安如得到吴鸿泰报告的消息：赵铁桥又在"大世界"出席一个京剧清唱晚会，便决定在戈登路口深夜伏击赵铁桥的汽车，经请示获得王亚樵的首肯。由于事前吴鸿泰等已经摸清了赵铁桥的夜行规律，又知道他的家就住在距戈登路口只有一里远的地方，所以他们断定赵铁桥在戈登路口即会放缓车速。这时候突然从前方和左右两侧同时包抄上去，开枪击碎他的汽车前窗，司机肯定当场身死或逃命，到那时赵铁桥就成了瓮中之鳖。一阵乱枪射去，肯定会让这叛徒即刻毙命。

At dusk that day, word came from Wu Hongtai: Zhao Tieqiao would be at the Great World for an evening of Peking-opera singing. Niu Anru resolved to ambush his car at the Gordon Road crossing in the deep of the night, and asked and received Wang Yaqiao's assent. The scouts had long since worked out the night route, and knew his house stood barely a li beyond the crossing; the car, they judged, would have to slacken for the turn. At that instant they would close from the front and both flanks at once and shatter the windscreen with fire; the driver would die where he sat or bolt; and Zhao Tieqiao would be a turtle in a jar. One burst of wild fire, and the traitor's life was rendered up on the spot.

> 现在，时钟已经指向12点，果然不出牛安如和王干庭所料，一辆别克牌小轿车已在漆黑的夜色中驶了过来。车灯一闪，车速果然在路口转弯的时候陡然放慢了。就在这时，王干庭向躲藏在梧桐树后阴影里的牛安如和夏少恩一招手，三个人蓦然冲了出来，分别从三个方向向那辆已经减速的小汽车扑过去。冲在最前面的王干庭说时迟那时快，手举枪响，砰然一声，向小汽车的前窗玻璃打去。可是，大大出乎于他意料之外的是，由于出手紧张，他的枪弹射偏了，并没有击碎汽车的前玻璃窗，却震醒了半睡在汽车后座里的赵铁桥。他突然惊醒过来，向车外一看，发现马路边同时冲出几个蒙着面罩的人影，情知遇上了可怕杀手。他尖叫一声："不要停车！"那惊呆了的司机眼见着几个黑影向车子疯狂扑来，便陡然一踩油门，猛地加快了车速。小汽车顿时像发了疯一般直向法租界弄堂深处没命地开去了。

Now the clock stood at twelve; and just as Niu Anru and Wang Ganting had reckoned, a Buick sedan came on through the dark. Its lamps flashed; sure enough, the speed fell away sharply at the turn. Wang Ganting's hand went up; Niu Anru and Xia Shao'en broke from the tree-shadow; and the three of them were on the slowing car from three sides. Wang Ganting, foremost — quicker done than told — raised his hand, and the gun spoke once, hard, at the windscreen. But — far outside all reckoning — the hand was strung too tight: the bullet flew wide. The glass did not break. And the shot jolted awake the Zhao Tieqiao who had been dozing in the rear seat. One look through the window — masked shadows rushing the car from the roadside — and he knew what had found him. He screamed: "Don't stop the car!" The stunned driver saw the black shapes hurl themselves at the car, stamped the pedal to the floor, and flung the machine forward. The car tore off like a mad thing into the deep lanes of the Concession, running for its life.

> "砰砰砰"，几乎与此同时，牛安如和夏少恩也同时举枪向飞驰的汽车射击，枪声立刻响成一片。正是这突发的枪声，惊醒了巡夜的法国巡捕，他们马上吹起警笛，那些在夜里值勤的法国巡捕和中国警察们都从四面八方向出事的戈登路奔来。牛安如、王干庭和夏少恩本想继续追去，此刻听见警笛之声连成一片，不得不采取撤退方案，丢开法租界而向英租界逃去。在那里早已等候着一辆小汽车，那是刘德才在负责接应。三个人跳上了汽车，司机急速发动马达，车飞也似的向一条黑暗小巷里驰去。等法国巡捕追来时，行刺的杀手们早已不见了踪影。

"Crack — crack — crack." In almost the same breath, Niu Anru and Xia Shao'en opened up on the flying car, and the shots ran together into one long rattle. It was exactly that burst that woke the night patrols: whistles went up everywhere, and the French police and Chinese constables on duty came running on Gordon Road from all four sides and eight directions. The three would have pressed the chase; with the whistles knitting into one sound around them, they took the withdrawal plan instead, quit the French Concession, and ran for the British one — where a car already sat waiting, Liu Decai at the receiving end. Three men in; the motor raced; the car flew into a black side-lane. When the French police reached the spot, there was not a trace to pursue.

> "别慌，一次不行再来第二次。总之，这一次非要他姓赵的命不行！"6月30日小试锋芒以后，上海滩出现了种种流言。有人说这是黄金荣对赵铁桥采取的行动，因为这是上海三大亨惯用的手法，凡是刚发迹的新贵们，大多都难免遭此厄运。但是黄金荣竟在媒体上公开否认此事与他有关系，理由是赵铁桥就任招商局长以后，已去他家里拜过码头了。这样一来舆论又称这是杜月笙和张啸林对赵铁桥的联合行动。杜月笙对这一谣传不屑作答，而张啸林则公开对报界记者说："杜老板是赵铁桥的朋友。既然我们都是朋友，岂有这样的荒唐之举？再说，我们如果真想对赵局长有什么行动，也不至于糊涂到去法租界上闹事的地步呀！"

"No panic. What fails once is done twice. One way or another, the Zhao one's life will be had!" After the blade showed its point on June 30, the Shanghai waterfront ran with rumor. Some said this was Huang Jinrong's doing — wasn't it the habitual method of the three great bosses, and don't the newly risen commonly meet exactly this fate? But Huang Jinrong denied it in the press, publicly, on the impeccable ground that Zhao Tieqiao, on taking office, had already called at his house and paid his respects at the wharf. So opinion moved on: a joint action, then, of Du Yuesheng and Zhang Xiaolin. Du Yuesheng did not stoop to answer. Zhang Xiaolin told the reporters to their faces: "Boss Du is Zhao Tieqiao's friend. We are all friends. Where would such an absurdity come from? Besides — if we truly meant business against Director Zhao, do you think we'd be so muddled as to raise it inside the French Concession?"

> 流言四起的同时，法国巡捕在戈登路附近增设了哨卡，夜过八时，所有形迹可疑的中国人一律不得进入租界。赵铁桥经此凶险之后，再也不敢在夜间外出应酬了。他几乎天色不黑就从招商局回家，然后再也不敢出门半步。他身边又增加了一些私人保镖，大门内外，到了夜晚，几乎是十步一岗，五步一哨。别说王亚樵不敢继续在戈登路附近街上派出杀手，即便派来了刺客，也无法进入刺杀的程序。

While the rumors ran, the French police threw new sentry posts around Gordon Road: past eight in the evening, no Chinese of doubtful appearance entered the Concession at all. And Zhao Tieqiao, after his brush with the guns, gave up the night entirely. He was home from the company before the sky darkened and did not step out again by half a pace. New private bodyguards appeared around him; by night, inside his gate and out, it was a post every ten steps and a sentry every five. Never mind sending killers back to Gordon Road — a killer delivered to the spot could not even begin the work.

> 如此一来，刺杀行动暂且中止了。

So the assassination hung suspended.

> 王亚樵不时和宣济民、吴鸿泰等密议行动方案。李国杰由于已支付了定金，几乎每天都用电话催促王亚樵说："九爷，你不是说万无一失吗？怎么刚出手就闹了个鸡飞蛋打？你要知道，我的'江安号'可早就停泊在吴淞码头上了。"

Wang Yaqiao sat in council with Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai night after night. And Li Guojie, his deposit paid, pressed by telephone nearly every day: "Ninth Master — wasn't it to be ten-thousand-fold sure? One move, and it's chickens flown and eggs smashed? You do know my Jiang'an has been lying at the Wusong wharf all this while."

> 王亚樵自然心焦如火。他在上海滩上行事，从没遇上如此棘手的事情。他日夜都想着如何尽快锄掉赵铁桥。王亚樵曾经亲自坐人力洋车，沿法租界附近几个可通赵家的路口到处转悠。当王亚樵发现法租界已是哨卡林立，警员如麻的时候，他知道继续在这搞暗杀活动已经没有任何希望。他又把目光移向上海繁华的闹市区。然而，那些平时赵铁桥经常露面的酒吧、舞厅等娱乐场所，再也见不到他的影子了。在盛夏之夜惊了枪的赵铁桥，一朝被蛇咬，三年怕井绳。若想在那些地段行刺赵铁桥，几乎找不到任何机会。就在王亚樵茫然无策的时候，有一天，他乘汽车经过上海招商局的大门。忽然，他下令小汽车放缓车速，然后远远刹在距此大门不远的树荫下，王亚樵透过车窗外望，静静地观察着大门前的动静。就在这时，他忽然萌生了一个大胆的设想：招商局才是最理想的行刺之地！

Wang Yaqiao's heart scorched like fire. In all his years on the Shanghai waterfront nothing had been this thorny. Day and night he turned over how to hoe the man down. He rode a rickshaw himself, round and round the crossings that led toward the Zhao house — and found the Concession a forest of sentry boxes, police thick as hemp-stalks. No hope remained there; he moved his eye to the roaring downtown. But the bars and dance floors that had known Zhao Tieqiao's face now saw no shadow of him. Once bitten by the snake, three years afraid of the well-rope: the man startled by guns on a midsummer night offered those quarters no chance at all. And then, one day, adrift for a plan, Wang Yaqiao's car happened to pass the great gate of the China Merchants company. On the sudden he ordered the driver to slow, then to stop well back, under the tree-shade short of the gate. Through the window he sat quietly reading the traffic of the entrance. And there it sprouted in him, the one bold thought: the China Merchants company itself was the perfect killing-ground.

> "我之所以要选中招商局的大门内外，就因为那里是赵铁桥防范最薄弱的地方。而且我们的行刺再也不能选在夜间进行了。赵铁桥一入夜就胆战心惊，甚至连门也不敢出，这时候我们在任何地方准备对他下手都是徒劳的。"王亚樵在灯下召开了又一次会议。宣济民和吴鸿泰开始时对王亚樵的白天行刺计划曾表示担忧和反对，但是，当王亚樵说明了在白天行刺的道理以后，大家都心悦诚服。王亚樵说："赵铁桥的所有防范措施都集中在夜间，他还想不到有人会在白天去暗杀他。招商局又是个客人随便出入的地方，甚至连门卫也对进来的客人不太介意。这就给我们提供了一个击毙他的最好机会。这就叫出其不意，攻其不备。所以，我们最好在赵铁桥早晨上班或中午下班的时候，对他发起袭击。"

"Why do I fix on the company gate, inside and out? Because that is where Zhao Tieqiao's guard runs thinnest. And because our strike can no longer be made at night at all. Come dark, the man is all shaken gall and alarmed heart — he won't so much as open his door. Prepare for him anywhere by night and we labor for nothing." Wang Yaqiao had called yet another council under the lamp. Xuan Jimin and Wu Hongtai began by doubting the daylight plan, and said so; but as he laid the reasoning out, they came over heart and mouth together. "Every measure of Zhao Tieqiao's guard is built against the night. That a man might murder him in broad day has not yet entered his thought. And the company is a place where callers come and go as they please — the gatekeeper barely looks at them. There is our best opening to shoot him down. This is what the ancients called: strike where none is looked for, attack where none is guarded. So — we take him in the morning as he comes to office, or at noon as he leaves."

> 新的行刺方案就这样确定下来了。

Thus the new plan was fixed.

> 7月24日，上海又是一个炎热无比的高温天气。

July 24. Shanghai again under burning, matchless heat.

> 大清早，王干庭、牛安如、夏少恩和一个临时增派的小杀手，都装扮成前来招商局谈生意的老客。他们大摇大摆地先后进了大门，守门老头对来客几乎不加盘问。牛安如等人走进楼内，发现走廊冷冷清清，几乎见不到办公人员。这正是几位杀手求之不得的。可是，他们从7点半上班时间走进大门以后，始终没见到赵铁桥的影子。按照吴鸿泰等侦察得知的规律，赵铁桥这时早该来到招商局了，但是不知何故今晨却不见他的影子。赵铁桥会不会已经察觉到什么？还是王亚樵预先安排的这场好戏，又出了什么破绽？万一赵铁桥今早不来上班，那么，今后他们继续执行这白昼行刺的计划，就会大为不利。因为如果他们多次进出招商局，难免会引起人们的注意。

In the early morning, Wang Ganting, Niu Anru, Xia Shao'en and a junior killer added for the day, all dressed as old customers come to talk business, swaggered through the great gate one after another. The old gateman hardly troubled to question callers. Inside, the corridors stood cold and empty — barely a clerk in sight. Nothing could have suited the killers better. But from half past seven, the opening of office hours, when they had entered, Zhao Tieqiao's shadow never once appeared. By every pattern in the scouts' book he should long since have arrived; this morning, for no reason anyone knew, there was nothing. Had Zhao Tieqiao sensed something? Had the play Wang Yaqiao staged sprung a flaw somewhere? If the man failed to come to work this morning, the daylight plan itself began to rot — walk in and out of the China Merchants company often enough, and eyes find you.

> 就在牛安如守在招商局客厅里心绪焦灼的时候，忽然，他发现门外人影一闪，那是负责监视大门的夏少恩，向他发出了赵已出现的信号！

Niu Anru was keeping his post in the reception hall with his heart on a slow fire when a shadow flicked past the doorway — Xia Shao'en, on watch at the gate, flashing him the signal: Zhao has appeared!

> 牛安如霍地跳了起来，飞步冲出一楼大厅。这时，他发现一辆熟悉的别克牌小汽车，已经驶进大门，在楼前平台下稳稳地刹住了！就是这辆车在一个月前曾与他们擦肩而过，今天他再也不能让车里的赵铁桥从他们的眼皮底下逃走了。牛安如刚走出玻璃门，就见汽车里傲然走下一个人来。他高高的身材，上穿灰白短衫，下着黑色西装裤，手里握着支显示身份的文明棍。正是他们守候多时的赵铁桥！牛安如等人顿时紧张起来，他们发现跟随在赵铁桥身后的是一位戴眼镜的秘书，看来保镖白天是不跟在他身边的。秘书替赵铁桥夹着只皮包，两人一前一后走向玻璃门，赵铁桥刚登上楼前的台阶，牛安如就听到"砰"一声枪响，原来夏少恩已经先开枪了。那枪声在招商局大院里显得格外清脆！但是，大院里并没因为这声枪响引起骚动。因为赵铁桥身饮一弹后并没叫喊，也不曾倒下，而是手指着流血的胸口左右环顾。他痛苦地张了张嘴，却叫不出声来。

Niu Anru came up off his seat and was through the ground-floor hall at a run. There it was — the familiar Buick, already through the great gate, braking smooth and steady below the front terrace. The same car that had slid past their fingers a month before; the Zhao Tieqiao inside it would not slip from under their eyelids today. Niu Anru had barely cleared the glass door when a man stepped proudly down from the car: tall, a gray-white shirt above black western trousers, and in his hand the gentleman's cane that announced his station. The man they had waited on all these days — Zhao Tieqiao. The killers went taut. Behind Zhao walked a secretary in spectacles — by day, it seemed, the bodyguards stayed home. The secretary carried the leather case; the two came on toward the glass doors, one before, one behind; and just as Zhao Tieqiao's foot found the steps, Niu Anru heard the crack of a shot — Xia Shao'en had fired first. In the great court of the China Merchants company the report rang uncommonly crisp and clean. And yet no stir followed it. For Zhao Tieqiao, having drunk the bullet, neither cried out nor fell. He stood pointing at his own bleeding chest, looking left and right, mouth working in pain, and no sound would come out of him.

> "砰砰！"牛安如对准赵铁桥一连开了两枪。夏少恩和另一个杀手也同时射击，赵铁桥顿时身中数弹，前胸一片血污。他再也坚持不住，扑通一声扑倒在门前台阶上。

"Crack! Crack!" Niu Anru put two rounds into him, aimed and steady. Xia Shao'en and the junior killer fired in the same moment; Zhao Tieqiao took bullet upon bullet, his shirt-front one sheet of blood, and could stand no longer. With a single heavy fall he went down on the steps before the door.

> "不好了，有人刺杀总办！"夹着皮包的秘书没命地冲进空荡荡的大厅，冲着楼内高声大叫起来。这才惊动了那些坐在办公室里喝茶聊天的职员，等人们神色惊慌地从大楼里拥出来时，才发现赵铁桥已经僵直地躺在血泊里不动了！

"Help — they've shot the Supervisor!" The secretary, case still under his arm, fled headlong into the echoing empty hall, screaming up the stairwell. Only then did the clerks look up from their tea; and by the time the building had emptied its frightened people onto the forecourt, Zhao Tieqiao lay stiff and still in his own blood.

> 上海滩大哗。各报都以通栏醒目黑体字，迅速报道了这位商界新贵惨死在无名杀手枪下的惊世新闻。这时候，王亚樵却正在筹划如何接管那艘泊靠在吴淞口江面上的"江安号"货轮。

The Shanghai waterfront went up in one roar. Every paper, in banner columns of heavy black type, rushed out the world-startling news: the new prince of commerce shot dead by killers without names. And at that very hour Wang Yaqiao was busy with a different question: how to take possession of a certain freighter riding at anchor off Wusong — the Jiang'an.

> "济民、鸿泰，现在该你们两位出场了！"8月上旬的一天，当刺赵事件归于平静之后，王亚樵把宣济民和吴鸿泰召进安徽会馆，"现在我们杀了赵铁桥，可李国杰却不再提'江安号'了。看起来，如果我们自己不去接收，姓李的是不会主动拱手交出来的。你们这就去，把'江安号'给我夺到手里再说！"宣济民和吴鸿泰听了，都叫喊起来："咱们手中有李国杰当初写的契约，还怕他赖账不成？"两人说着，就怒冲冲带着几个身强力壮的汉子，风风火火直奔吴淞码头而去。

"Jimin — Hongtai — now it's your turn on the stage!" It was a day in early August, with the affair settling toward quiet, when Wang Yaqiao called the two of them into the Anhui Guildhall. "We have killed Zhao Tieqiao — and Li Guojie no longer breathes a word of the Jiang'an. Plainly, unless we go take delivery ourselves, the Li one will never hand her over of his own will. Go now — and bring the Jiang'an into my hand first, whatever else!" At that the two of them shouted together: "We hold the contract Li Guojie wrote with his own brush — is his welshing anything to fear?" And off they went in a fury, several big strong fellows at their backs, wind-and-fire straight for the Wusong wharf.

> 王亚樵守在会馆里吸着水烟，躺在烟榻上和夫人亚英说着闲话，只等吴鸿泰和宣济民来报好消息。可是，就在他们陶醉自乐的时候，门外忽然响起一阵急促的脚步声，一个小头目慌慌然跑进门来，大喊道："九爷，不好了！江边上打起来了！"

Wang Yaqiao kept to the guildhall, drawing on the water-pipe, stretched on the smoking couch trading idle talk with his lady Yaying, waiting only for Wu Hongtai and Xuan Jimin to bring the good news. And just there, in the pleasure of the waiting, came a rush of hurried feet outside the door, and a junior chief burst in shouting: "Ninth Master — it's bad! Fighting — fighting has broken out at the river!"

> 王亚樵和王亚英从榻上爬起来，都不知江边究竟发生了什么事。

Wang Yaqiao and Wang Yaying came up off the couch together, and neither knew what on earth had happened at the riverside.
