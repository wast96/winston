### White Russian Drifters

In the Soviet Union of today the Reds hold sway, and the White elements, not to be borne of course in their own homeland, have been forced to it, driven south by roundabout ways, to drift about Shanghai in great numbers. The men of them, apart from a portion who serve as bodyguards to men of standing and note, mostly run little shops — the Russian-style barbershops, cafés, general stores, and sundries stalls on Avenue Joffre are all set up by this crowd; and those a notch lower wander the streets hawking felt rugs and bolts of cloth, or shine shoes for people in a corner of the road and sell stain-removing paste, of whom there are also many. The women of them serve as nothing but masseuses in the massage parlors, dance-partners in the dance-halls, and "live meat" in the salt-meat houses.

There are besides the truly destitute with nowhere to turn, some of whom have sunk to being clinging-limpet beggars. Let no one make light of them: this crowd of drifting White Russians, back in the days when they held power, were every one imperial kin and titled nobility, or had held high office; and were one to look into it case by case, the number is truly no small one!
