### An Invitation to the Dragon Palace

In years gone by a set of aged bawds took a few shabby rooms in a lonely, desolate spot at the west end of Nanjing Road — the neighbourhood of what is now Bubbling Well Road — and kept there four or five she-devils and hags of the coarsest sort, a place set apart for the trysts of low, dissolute monks; they called it the "Dragon Palace." When a monk went to disport himself there, they had a password for it: "an invitation to visit the Dragon Palace."

How grand and glittering the two words "Dragon Palace" sound — and now they were made the name of a sordid, secret rendezvous. Dragon Palace, Dragon Palace: how many of the world's wickednesses are carried on under a borrowed name! People talk as though the secret dens of vice were a thing of the present day, and never dream that scores of years ago this same shameless traffic was already being plied.
