### The Three-Lights Gang

This "Three-Lights Gang" has nothing to do with the three lights of sun, moon and star; its three lights are eating-clean, spending-clean and pawning-clean. The word "clean" is here a plain synonym for "all gone." Men of this stamp, having once embraced the three-lights creed, none of them follow any honest calling, but bend their whole effort to swindling; the moment they lay hands on money they put the three lights into practice, and when it is all gone they cast about for more, round upon round without end. And a man or woman of weak will who meets one of these three-lights gentry some fine day may well come at last to a ruined and beggared house, to lost virtue and lost life. Look into their doings, and it is simply the peeling-white gang under another name.
