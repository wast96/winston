### Empty Tricks

What is meant by *xutou*—an "empty trick"? How are the two words to be construed? It is this: there is a thing worth, say, only a dollar, but ask the man selling it and he will rattle off, without a blush, two dollars, three dollars—and that is what is called *xutou*. In the shops of the city, apart from the few that keep to one fixed price and mean what they say, all the rest have their *xutou* built in, and the honest man who is off his guard for a moment gets badly taken in. The biggest *xutou* of all belong to a certain sort of sharp-dealing shop and to the fish, shrimp, and vegetable stalls at the little markets: they "ask the sky," and we have no course but to "offer the ground," or we shall be the losers. ("Asking the sky" and "offering the ground" mean simply that they price it high and we must bid it low.)
