### Permanent Waves

Ever since women cut off their hair-buns, two years or so went by and then the perming of hair came into great vogue. The modern girls and fashionable young wives of today mostly wave their hair into the "water-ripple" style or the "snail-coil" style and think it becoming; this is the commonest sort of wave. There is besides a set of dance-girls who part the hair to left and right and wave it up stiff, so that it sits on the nape like a perched butterfly, and reckon it the height of beauty—as though without waving it so a girl could not even be a proper dancing-star.

Some perm their own hair; some have a hairdresser do it. An iron crimping-rod is heated over a spirit-flame and then run through the hair, curling it this way and teasing it that—a very troublesome business. Moreover, the year before last a film-trade worker, a Miss Pu Jinghong, while perming her own hair knocked over the spirit-flask; her clothes caught fire, the flames overcame her, and so she lost one precious life. After it happened, Mr. Yan Duhe, a reporter for the Xinwenbao, gave an interview earnestly urging the modern women to perm no more, and so avoid such accidents. But the censure of public opinion had not the least effect; it was rather a case of "let the dead die, the perms go on as before—what does it matter if one is lost?"—for a permanent wave is a thing the modern woman can by no means do without.

By now it is not only women who must have their hair waved: every man who preens over his own reflection and fancies himself modern must have his done up glossy and curled too, and so once again the barbershop proprietors do a brisk stroke or two of business. On the glass windows of the barbershops there are now pasted up the four large characters "Perms for Men and Women." When your author first saw them he was a little puzzled, thinking: what hair has a bewhiskered man to perm? But on asking about it he learned that the modern young men, too, have this new fashion of waving their hair.

Since the "New Life" began, the highest authorities once issued an order forbidding women to perm their hair, and it is now strictly enforced—another good measure for rescuing society from its frivolous and decadent ways. Let us hope the young men and women will be modern no longer, and come quickly to their senses!
