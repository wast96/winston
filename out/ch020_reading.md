### White Tile Standoff

In the game of mahjong there are four white-tile pieces; suppose Player A holds two and Player B holds two as well, and everyone waits to pair them, and the pairing simply never comes: this is called a "white tile standoff."

The phrase has now been carried over into the houses: when a single girl has two clients at once, both waiting their turn to lose their souls to her, that too is called a "white tile standoff." And the more fashionable a girl, the better she is at leading men on, the more of these white-tile clients she has — and who knows why? One account has it that a girl who is fashionable and a practiced flirt must be the more accomplished in the arts of company; her circle being wide, the men whose heads she turns must be many; and the more smitten clients she has on her string, the oftener is played out the living drama of the "white tile standoff."
