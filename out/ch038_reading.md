### Weng Meiqian Sings in the Streets

To speak the three words "Weng Meiqian" is to name someone whose fame every old hand of the flower-world and every seasoned connoisseur of the opera can call to mind. Her lot in life has been much like that of the late Lin Daiyu: Daiyu passed from courtesan to actress and back again from actress to courtesan, marrying out again and again and being cast off again and again; and Weng Meiqian, turning and returning the same way, has indeed a few points of likeness to old Lin. Only, Lin long ago closed her eyes for good, and the lid being on the coffin the verdict is fixed, whereas Weng is still in the world, sunk to singing for coppers in the streets, living the life of a beggar. To think back on all that once was, all of it, one cannot but feel the whole weight of the world's changes.

Thirty years ago Weng was a courtesan in Ankang Li, and titled her house "The Hall of a Single Plum Tree in Flower" (the fashion of the brothels in those days being to style themselves this or that "immortals' pavilion" and this or that "hall"). On the lantern below she pasted "Public Affairs, Presiding," and wrote too the five characters "Hall of a Single Plum Tree in Flower"; every night she rode out in a flying sedan-chair to her engagements, much remarked by the passers-by — a courtesan of blazing renown. Afterward she married out several times and was cast off several times, none of it to any good end. She had by nature a fine voice, and later sang at the Qunxian all-girl opera house in the Hu family compound on Fourth Avenue, taking the bearded-male roles, and won no little favor from the connoisseurs; only when the Qunxian closed did she give up performing. In the seventh year of the Republic she showed her face once more with an all-girl troupe at the New World, though by then her vogue was far short of what it had been.

Now that she is old, and has taken to opium besides, she can be neither courtesan nor actress; and so, driven to it, she tucks a fiddle under her arm every night and sings her way along the lanes and the roadsides, back and forth, hoping to earn the few small coins that will keep her wretched life going.
