### Gilded Doctorates

A set of foreign-returned students who chase after empty fame and care nothing for real learning go abroad, muddle about for a few years, wheedle out a diploma and a mortarboard, and come home full of airs, fit to swagger and to lord it over their wives; but test their actual learning and there is, one may say, not a jot of it. The men of the day call this class of returned student a "gilded doctor" — spoken with feeling indeed.

Of the students who go each year to study in the countries of the East and the West there is no knowing how many; but those from whom one may hope for a return home, their learning accomplished, to serve the nation and society are simply not often met with, and to look for one who has made himself a truly genuine solid-gold doctor is rarer still than phoenix feathers and unicorn horns — the great majority carry no more than the title of gilded doctor.
