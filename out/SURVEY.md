## What this edition is (read alongside the tables above)

This is **not a translation.** The source (Harold Isaacs, *The Tragedy of the
Chinese Revolution*, Haymarket 2009, a reprint of the Secker & Warburg 1938
first edition) is already in English, and the PDF is born-digital with a clean
text layer &mdash; **no OCR and no translation are needed.** The job is an
**annotated reading edition**: Isaacs's own text, reset and preserved verbatim,
with an added apparatus.

**Source facts (verified against the scan):** 409 PDF pages; front matter runs a
roman sequence (i&ndash;xxii), the body restarts at arabic 1; the PDF&rarr;print
offset is a constant **23** across the whole body (printed = PDF-page − 23),
confirmed at every one of the 20 chapter openers. In-text reference marks are
5.5&nbsp;pt superscript digits, numbered per chapter; Isaacs's endnotes run
printed 340&ndash;373; the index runs 374 to the end. There are **no titled
sections inside chapters** &mdash; each chapter is one continuous unit.

**The apparatus, three layers:**
1. **Isaacs's own reference notes** (his numbered endnotes) &mdash; moved from the
   back of the book to the point in the text they document, as popup footnotes.
2. **A new editorial footnote layer** &mdash; identifying the people, places,
   institutions, offices, and party/Comintern terms a reader with no background
   in modern Chinese history would miss, and flagging where a claim is
   corroborated by later scholarship. Editorial notes are marked *Ed.*; every
   other note is Isaacs's own.
3. **A glossary + Principal Characters page** &mdash; giving Isaacs's Wade-Giles
   spellings, the modern pinyin, and Chinese characters, so a name here can be
   matched to one in a present-day account.

## Batch order (proposed) &mdash; grouped, ~8-10 batches

The auto-table above (one row per chapter) is the *upper bound*, not the plan.
The real driver is the ~65%-of-context cap plus generous footnoting, but the
cast is **front-loaded**: almost every name, institution, and Comintern term
gets its editorial note at first appearance and is never re-noted, so the early
chapters are heavy and the later ones are light. So:

- **B01 = ch01** Seeds of Revolt &mdash; alone. It is the format/annotation gate

## Decisions (settled by the commissioner)

1. **The index is KEPT and made navigable.** The printed index (printed
   374&ndash;end) references print page numbers that do not survive reflow, so
   every page reference becomes a **link to that printed page's anchor**. Each
   batch writes a `data/pagemap/` for its chapters (feeding pagebreak anchors);
   the final batch parses the index and renders it as a linked back-matter page.
2. **Two note layers, told apart by numeral system.** Isaacs's own reference
   notes stay **arabic (1, 2, 3)**; the editorial layer is **roman (i, ii,
   iii)** &mdash; no *Ed.* tag. Numbering restarts each chapter. (Isaacs numbers
   in arabic, so editorial takes roman.)
3. **The 2009 Foreword is kept** as front matter (ch00a).
4. **Copyright.** The 1938 text is very likely **still under copyright** (UK
   life+70; US 95 years), and the 2009 Foreword is separately Arnold Isaacs's.
   The `rights` field states this honestly; this edition is a derivative
   prepared for private study.
