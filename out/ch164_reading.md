### Counterfeit Small Coin

Besides counterfeit notes and counterfeit dollars, the market also has a sort of counterfeit small coin and debased coin (the Shanghai people call it "jiao pieces"). The counterfeit small coin is made wholly of copper or of lead, with a thin layer of silver added over its face, and is then passed off in the dark. Debased coin is of two kinds, the acid and the newly cast (the Shanghai people call the latter "new jiao pieces"): the acid small coin is of the same fineness as the acid dollar, while the new jiao piece is a mix cast of little silver and much copper, of a fineness that, set beside a good small coin, falls far short indeed.

There are always greedy, never-satisfied tobacco-and-paper shops that make a special business of the new jiao pieces, secretly buying them up in quantity at a cheap price (a single dollar, they say, will buy twenty-odd jiao) and passing them out again little by little, so that the profit left over is already well worth having. Such a fine trade, as things now stand, is on its way to becoming an open one; and so long as there is a skimming to be had, whether the man who takes the coins is hurt by it or not is nothing they will trouble their heads over.
