In the back alley of the Chunhe Hospital, A-Chu's car sped away; in its boot lay the body of Li Qinhong.

> 半个小时后，春和医院恢复了正常秩序，原来是火警铃声出了故障，纯属误报。
Half an hour later the Chunhe Hospital was back to normal order: the fire alarm, it turned out, had malfunctioned, a false alarm and nothing more.

> 三天后，查到蛛丝马迹的俞晓江带领国民党宪兵、军警等闯进了四马路的一家酒楼。人去楼空，过道上还有明显焚毁文件资料的黑色痕迹，酒楼里保温瓶的水依旧是滚烫的……
Three days later, Yu Xiaojiang, having traced a thread of evidence, led Nationalist gendarmes and military police in a raid on a restaurant on Fourth Avenue. The place was empty, the people gone; in the passage were the plain black marks of documents burned in haste, and the water in the restaurant's vacuum flasks was still scalding hot...

> 俞晓江表现的万分沮丧，一个多星期以来超负荷的工作量换来得竟是一无所获。以至于杜旅宁不得不出言抚慰，以定军心。
Yu Xiaojiang made a great show of dejection: more than a week of crushing overwork had yielded nothing at all, so much so that Du Luning had to offer a few soothing words to steady the ranks.

> 又过了一个星期，杜旅宁接到了沪中长官公署侦缉处熊自达呈上来得有关李沁红组长离奇失踪的报告。这让杜旅宁陷入一种焦炙状态，他的感觉异常微妙。
Another week passed, and Du Luning received the report submitted by Xiong Zida of the Detective Division of the Central Shanghai Commandant's Office concerning the strange disappearance of Section Chief Li Qinhong. It threw Du Luning into a state of anxious unease; his feelings were exceedingly delicate.

> 李沁红曾经疯狂的爱过杜旅宁，她对杜旅宁狂热的崇拜，让杜旅宁感到“无比厌恶”，这个疯女人，就像是一双他曾经试穿过的鞋，无论他把这双鞋丢弃在哪里，这双鞋里依旧存储着他脚上的气息，甚至是脚上的温度。
Li Qinhong had once loved Du Luning to the point of madness, and her feverish adoration had filled Du Luning with "boundless disgust." This mad woman was like a pair of shoes he had once tried on: no matter where he threw them away, the shoes still held the scent of his feet, even the warmth of them.

> 不过，当李沁红真的失踪以后，当这双鞋将永远不复存在的时候，杜旅宁心中突然有一种莫名的伤感，他甚至开始回忆那双鞋的款式、色彩，还有他当年试穿时的随意和散漫。
And yet, once Li Qinhong had truly vanished, once those shoes were gone forever, an inexplicable sorrow rose in Du Luning; he even began to recall the style and colour of the shoes, and the idle carelessness with which, in those days, he had tried them on.

> “处座。”
"Chief."

> 就在杜旅宁冥思遐想之际，俞晓江敲开了杜旅宁办公室的门。
Just as Du Luning sat lost in his musings, Yu Xiaojiang knocked and opened the door of his office.

> “处座，我们在这一带发现不明电波。”俞晓江指着杜旅宁铺在办公桌上的上海市地图说。
"Chief, we have detected unidentified radio signals in this area." Yu Xiaojiang pointed at the map of Shanghai spread out on Du Luning's desk.

> “以前监听过没有？”杜旅宁关心的是，这部电台是否从前存在过。
"Have they ever been picked up before?" What concerned Du Luning was whether this transmitter had existed in the past.

> “有，不过是在一年前。”
"Yes, but that was a year ago."

> “也就是说，这部秘密电台曾经静默过整整一年。”
"Which is to say, this secret transmitter has fallen silent for a whole year."

> “是，处座。”
"Yes, Chief."

> “熊自达到底是干什么吃的！”杜旅宁气愤地把手中红色铅笔用力掷向地图，一点猩红蘸上颜色灰暗的地图。很刺眼，也很显眼。
"What on earth is Xiong Zida good for!" In his anger Du Luning flung the red pencil in his hand hard at the map; a spot of scarlet stained the drab-coloured paper. It was glaring, and it stood out.

> “你认为，这部秘密电台应该是哪方面的？延安？远东？日本人？”
"Whose do you think this secret station is? Yan'an? The Far East? The Japanese?"

> “日本人。”俞晓江回答的十分干脆。
"The Japanese." Yu Xiaojiang answered without the slightest hesitation.

> “日本人？为什么你这么肯定？”
"The Japanese? Why are you so certain?"

> “是直觉。”
"It is intuition."

> “女人的直觉往往很可靠。”
"A woman's intuition is often reliable."

> “处座，自从九、一八，日本关东军炸毁南满铁路柳条湖段路轨，继而炮轰东北军驻地，攻占沈阳。今年又成立伪满，妄图独霸东北，局部抗战的格局已经拉开序幕。上海是中国最为繁华的城市，也是世界情报汇集之所，日本人在上海安插谍报人员应是蓄谋已久。”
"Chief, ever since the eighteenth of September, when the Japanese Kwantung Army blew up the rails on the Liutiaohu stretch of the South Manchuria Railway, then shelled the Northeast Army's barracks and seized Shenyang. This year they have set up the puppet Manchukuo, in their vain design to swallow the Northeast whole, and the pattern of a partial war of resistance has already been drawn open like a curtain. Shanghai is China's most flourishing city, and a place where the intelligence of the whole world converges; that the Japanese should have planted espionage agents in Shanghai must have been long premeditated."

> “辽宁、吉林、黑龙江，还有，北平？”杜旅宁深深吸了一口气。“大厦将倾。”
"Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang, and Beiping too?" Du Luning drew a deep breath. "The great mansion is about to fall."

> “局座？您很悲观。”
"Chief? You are very pessimistic."

> “我很现实。”杜旅宁说。“查，查下去，看能不能抓住这条毒蛇。”
"I am very realistic," Du Luning said. "Look into it, keep looking, and see whether we can catch this venomous snake."

> “是，处座。可是，共产党？”
"Yes, Chief. But the Communists?"

> “也不能放松，他们的会议虽然结束了，他们的人还得分批出上海，监视所有的港口、火车站，严密监视，不能放过一丝一毫的线索。”
"They are not to be relaxed on either. Their conference may be over, but their people still have to leave Shanghai in batches. Watch every harbour and railway station, watch them closely; not a shred of a lead is to be let slip."

> “是，处座。”
"Yes, Chief."

> 此际，天空灰蒙蒙的，下起了绵绵细雨。
Just then the sky turned a hazy grey, and a fine, unbroken drizzle began to fall.

> 黄浦江上，海轮即将起航。
On the Huangpu River, an ocean liner was about to weigh anchor.

> 丛锋穿着海员的工作服，他和轮船上的工作人员一起拎着随身行李，从内部员工通道走向海轮。
Cong Feng, in a seaman's work clothes, carried his hand luggage along with the ship's crew and went toward the liner by the internal staff passage.

> 随即登上甲板，进入船舱。
Then he mounted the deck and entered the cabin.

> “雪狼”撑着一张黑色的伞，目送他离去的身影，听着海轮起航的声音，“哗！哗！”的汽笛锐叫，在他听来很是悦耳动听。
"Snow Wolf," holding up a black umbrella, watched the departing figure, and listened to the sounds of the liner setting sail; the shrill "hwah! hwah!" of the whistle was, to his ear, most pleasant to hear.

> 他默默地转过头来，上了钟云迪的车，汽车与海轮几乎是同时缓缓驶离了码头。
Silently he turned and got into Zhong Yundi's car; the car and the liner drew slowly away from the wharf at almost the same moment.

> 雨渐渐停歇，雨痕逶迤的走廊上，透着几许清凉，春和医院的住院部里弥漫着香樟树浓郁的香气，这种常绿植物，一经雨水洗涤，显得格外精神。慕次的病房里很清静，慕次睡着了，因为天气好，他的心情也好，所以睡得十分香甜。
The rain gradually eased. Along the corridor, where its traces wound and lingered, there was a breath of coolness; the inpatient wing of the Chunhe Hospital was steeped in the rich fragrance of the camphor trees, an evergreen that, once washed by rain, looks especially fresh and alive. Muci's ward was very quiet. Muci was asleep; the weather was fine, and so his mood was fine, and he slept sweetly and deep.

> 夏跃春和阿初都靠在窗台上看天色，看表。他们彼此心照不宣，因为，丛锋的海员差事，是阿初叫韩正齐从海关朋友那里办妥的。
Xia Yuechun and A-Chu both leaned against the windowsill, watching the sky, watching their watches. Between them was an unspoken understanding, for Cong Feng's berth as a seaman had been arranged by A-Chu, who had had Han Zhengqi see to it through a friend in the Customs.

> “好了，你放心了。”阿初说。
"There. Now you can rest easy," A-Chu said.

> “这话说得蹊跷，他不是你朋友啊？”夏跃春抗议了一句。
"That's an odd way to put it. Isn't he a friend of yours?" Xia Yuechun protested.

> 阿初脸上做出一种奇怪的笑，夏跃春只当看不见。
A strange smile came over A-Chu's face; Xia Yuechun simply pretended not to see it.

> “你听过一个古老的波斯传说吗？”阿初问。
"Have you heard an old Persian legend?" A-Chu asked.

> 夏跃春摇头。
Xia Yuechun shook his head.

> “有一个国王和他的臣子赌棋。既然是赌，就一定会有输赢。那么，输的一方会答应另一方的一个请求。结果，国王在对弈中败北。臣子的请求是，在棋盘的六十四个方格中以指数放上米粒。第一格放一粒米，第二格放两粒，以此类推。当放满六十四个方格时，已达一万亿米粒。”
"A king wagered on a game of chess with one of his ministers. Since it was a wager, there had of course to be a winner and a loser, and the loser would grant the other a single request. As it fell out, the king was defeated in the match. The minister's request was this: that grains of rice be laid on the sixty-four squares of the board by powers of two, one grain on the first square, two on the second, and so on. By the time all sixty-four squares were filled, the tally had reached a trillion grains of rice."

> 夏跃春知道他要表达什么含义了，他在指责自己以“欺瞒”的方式来获取他的帮助，利用他的“亲情”来达到自己的目的。
Xia Yuechun understood now what he was driving at: A-Chu was accusing him of having secured his help by "deceit," of having used his "brotherly feeling" to attain his own ends.

> “等等。”夏跃春说。“我不是你说的那位臣子，你说的那位在床上。”夏跃春朝病床上的慕次努努嘴。
"Wait." Xia Yuechun said. "I am not the minister you speak of; the one you mean is lying in that bed." Xia Yuechun pursed his lips toward Muci on the sickbed.

> “不，你才是那个真正得胜的臣子，站在幕后的人。你用我弟弟的生命做筹码，赌我的性命。我就是那个国王。在不知不觉中迈进你所设下的圈套，沿着你指定的方向前行。说穿了，我为你工作。愿赌服输。”
"No. You are the minister who truly won, the man standing behind the curtain. You used my brother's life as your stake and gambled for mine. I am the king, who, all unknowing, walked into the snare you had laid and went forward in the direction you appointed. To put it plainly: I work for you. A fair wager, and I take my loss."

> “口才很好。”夏跃春说。
"You have a fine tongue," Xia Yuechun said.

> “判断能力强。”阿初很自得。
"And strong judgment," said A-Chu, well pleased with himself.

> “我是一个重友尽责的人。”
"I am a man who values his friends and does his duty."

> “同时也是一个敲响战鼓的人。”阿初说。
"And a man who beats the drums of war as well," A-Chu said.

> “在这个喧嚣、战乱的世界里，我们不应该寻找自己的出路吗？”
"In this clamorous, war-torn world, ought we not to seek our own way out?"

> “那么，你承认？”
"Then you admit it?"

> “承认什么？我没杀过人，从来没有。”夏跃春显然在纠正阿初的某些异想天开的想法。“我信耶稣。”
"Admit what? I have never killed a man, never once." Xia Yuechun was plainly correcting some flight of A-Chu's fancy. "I believe in Jesus."

> “我信自己。”
"I believe in myself."

> “你跟他是一家人。”夏跃春反复强调阿次和阿初的血缘关系。
"You and he are of one family." Xia Yuechun kept pressing the blood tie between A-Ci and A-Chu.

> “我看，正相反。”
"As I see it, quite the opposite."

> “你疑心病太重。”
"You are far too suspicious."

> “你敢说你跟他……”阿初的手指向慕次。“没有任何瓜葛？”
"Do you dare say that you and he..." A-Chu's finger pointed at Muci, "...have no entanglement of any kind?"

> “我跟你有感情。”夏跃春在笑。
"It's you I have a bond with," Xia Yuechun said, smiling.

> “轻描淡写的混不过去。看看他最近对你的态度，他尊重你。”
"You won't slip past me with such light brushstrokes. Look at the way he's treated you lately; he respects you."

> “我是他的主治医生。”
"I am his attending physician."

> “同时他开始敬畏你。”
"And he has begun to hold you in awe."

> “那是因为你的缘故，他对我有了兄长般的敬意。”
"That is on your account: he has come to feel for me the respect one feels for an elder brother."

> “扯淡。”阿初嘴角有了一丝不屑。他伸出手指，比出一个“三”来。“我弟弟对你的敬意，也许就来自这……三枪。”
"Rubbish." A trace of scorn touched the corner of A-Chu's mouth. He held up his fingers, making a "three." "My brother's respect for you may come from just this... these three shots."

> “你数着来得？”
"You counted them?"

> “你在现场。”
"You were on the scene."

> “大家……各自打扫门前雪吧。”
"Let each of us... sweep the snow from his own doorstep."

> “你是叫我……不管他人瓦上霜？”阿初从衣兜里掏出三枚子弹壳。
"Are you telling me... not to mind the frost on another man's roof-tiles?" A-Chu drew three cartridge cases from his pocket.

> “这只是工作中发生的一点瑕疵。”夏跃春说。
"This was merely a small flaw that arose in the course of the work," Xia Yuechun said.

> “我在工作中力求完美。”他把子弹壳硬塞到夏跃春手上。“物归原主。”
"In my work I strive for perfection." He pressed the cartridge cases firmly into Xia Yuechun's hand. "Let the thing return to its owner."

> “你把她埋了？”
"Have you buried her?"

> “我不喜欢干体力活。”阿初偏了偏头。“也许过十天半个月，她会飘上来。”
"I don't care for manual labour." A-Chu tilted his head. "Ten days, a fortnight perhaps, and she may float up to the surface."
