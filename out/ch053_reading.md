### The New World Tunnel

The late Huang Chujiu was the founding father of the amusement hall. He first got up the Tower-Beyond-the-Tower; then, breaking away from it and gathering shareholders afresh, he set up a large-scale amusement hall on the western plot by Nicheng Bridge, hard by the Racecourse, and named it the New World. Three years on, falling out with his fellow shareholders, he broke away from the New World in his turn and founded the Great World. Thereafter the New World was consolidated and run single-handed by Mr. Jing Runsan, who added a North Wing and, going so far as to build a tunnel beneath the roadway, let his patrons pass back and forth between the two at will. The tunnel was faced throughout with ceramic tile and cost upward of a fortune; yet a visitor laid out only two jiao in small silver to enjoy both the North and South Wings, which was value indeed. Mr. Jing's aim at the outset had been to compete with Huang Chujiu, and to that end he grudged no sacrifice of capital; but the running of the two wings proved costly, and since the price of admission could not be raised, within four or five years his losses ran to several hundred thousand dollars, and he was driven at last to close down. The South Wing was long ago made over into the New World Hotel; only the North Wing is now leased out and run as an amusement hall by other hands, and even that opens and shuts by fits and starts, while the tunnel beneath the road has long since been blocked up and can no longer be walked through.
