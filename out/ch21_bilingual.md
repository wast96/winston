## H2 A Thousandweight on a Hair, the Arrow on the String

> 慕次走到花园通往佛堂的铁栅门前，铁栅门被一根铁皮条拴住，他正准备打开铁栅门，突然第六感告诉自己，自己背后有人。他从准备开门的动作中突袭式转身，他的手在要掐住来人下巴的一瞬间，停在半空中，他的黑眸凝住了，他险些失声惊叫，整个人被卡住般懵了。
Muci walked up to the iron-barred gate that led from the garden to the Buddha hall; it was fastened shut with a strip of sheet iron. Just as he was making ready to open it, all at once a sixth sense told him there was someone behind him. Out of the very motion of reaching for the gate he spun around to strike — and his hand, in the instant it was about to clamp down on the intruder's jaw, halted in midair. His dark eyes froze; he all but cried out aloud, and stood there stunned, as though his whole body had jammed fast.

> 他看见了他的母亲。
He had seen his mother.

> 徐玉真穿着睡袍，赤着足，披着发。她眼球充血，眼神空洞，失魂落魄般直愣愣望着前方，她的眼里仿佛并没有阿次的存在，她茫然无助地向前走。
Xu Yuzhen stood there in a nightgown, barefoot, her hair loose about her. Her eyes were bloodshot, her gaze hollow; like one whose soul had fled her, she stared blankly straight ahead, and in her eyes it was as though A-Ci had no existence at all. Lost and helpless, she walked forward.

> 阿次半秒中清醒过来，母亲似乎是梦游。他闪身让路，他看见母亲机械地打开铁栅门，然后身体僵硬地向佛堂走去，阿次紧跟上去。突然，意外发生了，徐玉真晕倒了，她的身体蜷缩起来，嘴唇边泛出白沫……
In half a second A-Ci came to his senses: his mother seemed to be walking in her sleep. He slipped aside to let her pass, and saw her open the iron gate mechanically and then walk, her body stiff, toward the Buddha hall; A-Ci followed close behind. Then, all at once, the unlooked-for happened: Xu Yuzhen fainted away, her body curling in upon itself, a white froth rising at the corners of her lips . . .

> “妈！”慕次跑过去，脱下外套，包裹住母亲的头。“妈妈，妈妈？您可别吓我。”他抱起母亲向主楼跑去。
"Mother!" Muci ran to her, pulled off his coat, and wrapped it about her head. "Mama, Mama? Don't frighten me so." He lifted his mother up in his arms and ran toward the main house.

> 雨还在下，丝毫没有停的意图。
The rain still fell, with not the least intention of stopping.

> “徐玉真”的头包裹在慕次的风衣下，她的脸紧贴着慕次温暖的胸膛，她冰凉的唇在黑暗中绽放出一丝阴森地笑纹……
"Xu Yuzhen's" head was wrapped beneath Muci's trench coat, her face pressed close against his warm breast, and in the darkness her ice-cold lips broke into a thin, sinister thread of a smile . . .

> 风雨潇潇，河桥下，荣华的车子熄了火，关闭了前灯，唯有风挡上的擦拭器还在不懈的努力工作。
Wind and rain came sighing down. Under the river bridge Ronghua's car stood with its engine off and its headlamps out; only the wipers on the windshield still labored on, tireless and unrelenting.

> 中央特科书记方致同披着雨衣从河桥上走下来。
Fang Zhitong, Secretary of the Central Special Branch, came down off the river bridge with a raincoat thrown over his shoulders.

> 荣华打开车门，撑开一把伞，下了车。雨点趁着风势迎面砸了过来，荣华伸手拂开唇边的几缕湿发。
Ronghua opened the car door, put up an umbrella, and got out. The raindrops, driven by the wind, dashed straight into her face, and she reached up to brush aside a few strands of wet hair from her lips.

> “早来了？”方致同说。黑暗里，他嘴里镶的金牙熠熠闪光，他的布鞋却为雨水浸烂了。
"Been here long?" said Fang Zhitong. In the darkness the gold tooth set in his mouth glinted and gleamed, but his cloth shoes had been soaked to ruin by the rain.

> “来了一会。”
"A little while."

> “云南和广东的特委到了没有？”
"Have the special-committee men from Yunnan and Guangdong arrived yet?"

> “云南的特委还没有出发，我已经询问过了，回电是：病笃。广东的特委已经出发了，但是由于山体滑坡，造成火车不通，他们说只要能赶上海轮，就不会耽误会议时间。”荣华说。
"The Yunnan man hasn't set out yet — I've already made inquiries, and the wire back read: 'gravely ill.' The Guangdong man has set out, but a landslide has cut the railway; they say that so long as he can catch the coastal steamer he won't miss the time of the meeting," said Ronghua.

> 方致同很焦虑。“我们不能再等了，再这样盲目的等下去，我怕会横生枝节。这么多的特委聚集在上海开会，这本身就是在冒险。我不明白中央特科为什么会同意这样做。难道仅仅是为了做给第三共产国际的人看吗？看我们有多么的神勇，势力有多强大？”
Fang Zhitong was much troubled. "We can't wait any longer. To go on waiting blindly like this — I'm afraid it will throw up complications. For so many special committeemen to gather in Shanghai for a meeting is in itself a hazard. I don't understand why the Central Special Branch would agree to it. Can it really be only to make a show for the people of the Third Communist International — to let them see how valiant we are, how great our strength?"

> “老向，现在不是发牢骚的时候。”荣华并不是不同意他的观点，不过，她认为，中央特科在明知危险的情况下，决定召开这次特委扩大会议，一定有其特殊意义所在。“会议的地址订了吗？”
"Old Xiang, now is not the time for grumbling." It was not that Ronghua disagreed with his view; but she believed that if the Central Special Branch had resolved to convene this enlarged meeting of the Special Committee in the full knowledge of the danger, there must be some special significance in it. "Has the place of the meeting been settled?"

> “暂订在大光明旅社。”风太冷，方致同在雨地里打了一个喷嚏，然后掏出手绢来，翘起他的断指在鼻下唇上擦拭。“我打算，明天晚上八点钟在中央秘书处开一个特委会议的预备会，大家商量商量一下会议的保障措施，你通知'飘风'密切关注敌人的动向。预备会嘛，'飘风'就不用参加了，这也是对他的保护。”
"For the time being, the Grand Light Hostel." The wind was too cold; Fang Zhitong sneezed out in the rain, then took out a handkerchief and, crooking his severed finger, wiped it beneath his nose and over his lip. "I mean to hold a preparatory meeting for the Special Committee at the Central Secretariat tomorrow at eight in the evening, so we can all talk over the safeguards for the meeting. You are to notify 'Drifting Wind' to keep close watch on the enemy's movements. As for the preparatory meeting — 'Drifting Wind' needn't attend; that too is for his protection."

> “好的。”荣华说。
"Very well," said Ronghua.

> “今天晚上太晚了，我就在河船上宿了。”所谓：河船，就是私娼开的乌篷船，可以留客人借宿，流动性和隐蔽性较强。虽是如此，荣华依旧问了他一句：“安全吗？”
"It's too late tonight — I'll put up on the river boat." By a "river boat" was meant one of the black-awninged boats run by unlicensed prostitutes, which could take in a guest for the night and had the merits of being mobile and easily hidden. For all that, Ronghua still asked him: "Is it safe?"

> “绝对安全。”
"Absolutely safe."

> 荣华和方致同在河桥下分手。荣华发动汽车，向大路驶去。方致同走进残枝掩覆的羊肠小道，很快消失在黑黝黝的夜底。
Ronghua and Fang Zhitong parted beneath the river bridge. Ronghua started the car and drove off toward the main road. Fang Zhitong turned into a narrow, winding footpath overhung with broken branches, and was soon swallowed up in the pitch-black depths of the night.

> 死寂的夜，杨家主楼的灯全部点亮了。
In the dead-still night, every lamp in the main house of the Yang family had been lit.

> 佣人们一趟趟地穿梭在走廊两侧，一会是送热毛巾、一会是递热茶、一会是端水盆、一会是拿保温瓶，忙得不亦乐乎。德国大夫一脸严肃地站在门口跟杨羽桦谈有关徐玉真的病情。房间里，杨慕次焦虑地握着母亲冰凉的手，一刻不离地守在她的病榻前。
The servants shuttled back and forth along both sides of the corridor, now bringing a hot towel, now handing in hot tea, now carrying a basin of water, now fetching a thermos flask, all in a merry bustle. A German doctor, his face grave, stood at the door discussing Xu Yuzhen's condition with Yang Yuhua. Within the room Yang Muci held his mother's ice-cold hand in his anxiety, and kept watch at her sickbed without stirring for a moment.

> “徐玉真”很冷静，很惬意地享受着棉被底、方寸中的温暖，这里不仅仅是她息眠止疲的地带，这里同样也是她攻城拔寨的战场，是她表演的舞台。
"Xu Yuzhen" was very calm, savoring with great contentment the warmth beneath the quilt, in that little square of space. This was not only the place where she rested and put off her weariness; it was also her battlefield for storming cities and taking strongholds, the stage on which she performed.

> 她没有输过，她告诉自己，自己是永远的赢家。
She had never yet lost; she told herself she was the eternal winner.

> 至少在此地此时此刻，她是。
At least here, at this place and in this moment, she was.

> 慕次此刻相当清醒，他的精神世界刚刚遭遇了一次“雪崩”，他在握住母亲枯瘦的手的同时，暗暗告诫自己，不可感情用事。
Muci at this moment was quite clear-headed; his inner world had just suffered an "avalanche," and even as he held his mother's withered hand, he warned himself in secret: he must not let feeling govern him.

> 门开了，杨羽桦走了进来。
The door opened, and Yang Yuhua came in.

> “大夫走了？”慕次问。
"Has the doctor gone?" Muci asked.

> “是的。”
"Yes."

> “什么时候的事？”
"When did it happen?"

> “很久以前。”杨羽桦点燃一根雪茄烟。
"A long time ago." Yang Yuhua lit a cigar.

> “为什么您一直瞒着我？”慕次的音调拔高了。“为什么？”
"Why have you kept it from me all this while?" Muci's voice rose. "Why?"

> “我不想把你的母亲送进精神病院。”
"I did not want to send your mother into a madhouse."

> “您宁可毁了她！”慕次的眼睛发出锐利的寒光。
"You would sooner destroy her!" A sharp, cold light flashed from Muci's eyes.

> “她早就毁了。二十年前，她就已经这样了。”
"She was destroyed long ago. Twenty years ago she was already like this."

> “什么意思？”
"What do you mean?"

> “记得你有个孪生哥哥吗？”
"Do you remember you had a twin brother?"

> “记得。他两岁的时候去世了。”
"I remember. He died when he was two years old."

> “知道他是怎么死的吗？”
"Do you know how he died?"

> “病死的。”
"He died of an illness."

> “他不是病死的。他是被人害死的！”
"He did not die of an illness. He was murdered — done to death by human hands!"

> 慕次的眼睛睁大了！
Muci's eyes went wide!

> “您说什么？”
"What are you saying?"

> “二十年前的往事了。”杨羽桦坐了下来。“二十年前，你的母亲还很年轻，喜欢浪漫，喜欢做白日梦。我呢，生意太忙，应酬过多。当然，我也不否认，我曾经也在外面拈花惹草、逢场作戏。你的母亲是一个妒忌心很强烈的女人，她不允许自己的丈夫越雷池一步。于是，我们开始了无休无止的家庭战争。我很累，很不愿意回家。你的母亲和我们家里一个姓韩的司机……你应该听得懂我话中的意思，他们做了对不起杨家的事！”杨羽桦情绪异常激动。“我不能容忍，无法容忍。”他的双肩在颤抖，喉骨撕裂般的疼。
"It is a thing of twenty years ago." Yang Yuhua sat down. "Twenty years ago your mother was still very young, fond of romance, fond of daydreaming. As for me, my business kept me too busy, with too many social engagements. And of course I won't deny it — I too, in those days, dallied with flowers and toyed at love away from home. Your mother was a woman of the fiercest jealousy, who would not let her husband step one pace beyond the line. And so began an endless war within the household. I grew weary, and was most unwilling to come home. Your mother, and a driver of ours by the name of Han . . . you can surely take my meaning: they did a thing that wronged the house of Yang!" Yang Yuhua's feeling ran extraordinarily high. "I could not endure it — could not endure it." His shoulders trembled, and the bones of his throat ached as if being torn apart.

> 慕次走近父亲，他温驯地屈膝蹲下，伸出双手来攀住父亲的双膝。他温婉的目光，很好地控制住了杨羽桦激烈的动作，杨羽桦平静下来。
Muci drew near to his father; meekly he bent his knees and crouched down, and reached out both hands to hold fast to his father's knees. His gentle gaze checked Yang Yuhua's violent movements well, and Yang Yuhua grew calm.

> “本来，在情爱的世界里，谁也无法描绘出爱情的准确颜色，五彩缤纷，绚烂璀璨。无分对错，只有爱，或者不爱。你的母亲她是爱我的，她出轨的目的仅仅是想报复我的人，挽留我的心，分享我的爱。可是，情被欲所湮没了。二十年前的一个没有月色的夜晚，她和她的情夫点起了蜡烛，在老宅里幽会，他们饮酒作乐，大醉酩酊。然后，他们去了花园的佛堂，去寻求爱的刺激。就在他们走后不到半小时，老宅出事了。落地的烛火引燃了整个楼房，熊熊烈火吞噬了你的哥哥，还有你们的乳娘岳嬷嬷。儿子，你那天因为发高烧被医生留住在儿童医院，幸免于难！当我第二天找到你母亲的时候，她还在情人的怀抱里高枕酣眠。她醒来后，知道所发生的一切，她非常痛苦，很痛苦，但是，无法挽回。初儿下葬以后，你的母亲完全沉浸在悲哀里，她每日每夜都处于愁苦凄惨之状。从此，她患上了间歇性精神疾病。她发病的时候，会梦游，会撕咬，会疯狂。她曾经夜半三更半裸的走去佛堂，在梦里去企求菩萨的原谅，她的踝骨上全是草刮的血痕，她的人生彻底完了。这就是，我为什么让你从小就离开家庭的真正原因！我不想让你的容貌来刺激她的病。她的情人因此而抛弃了她，我没有足够的勇气和同情心去面对她，我恨她！”杨羽桦的泪水突然滴溅到慕次的手背上。“你是个懂事明理的孩子，所以我不想窜改你母亲的病因，这是一个永远无法回避的事实。你了解我的苦心吗？”
"In the world of love, when all is said, no one can paint the exact color of it — a riot of hues, dazzling and resplendent. There is no dividing it into right and wrong; there is only loving, or not loving. Your mother did love me; the sole aim of her straying was to take revenge upon my person, to hold back my heart, to have a share of my love. But feeling was drowned out by desire. On a moonless night twenty years ago, she and her lover lit candles and kept their tryst in the old mansion; they drank and made merry until they were dead drunk. Then they went to the Buddha hall in the garden, to seek the thrill of love. Not half an hour after they had gone, disaster struck the old mansion. A fallen candle-flame set the whole building alight, and the raging fire devoured your brother, and your wet-nurse Amah A-Yue as well. My son — that day, because you were running a high fever, the doctor had kept you at the children's hospital, and so you were spared! When I found your mother the next day, she was still sleeping soundly, at ease, in her lover's arms. When she woke and learned all that had happened, she was in agony, in bitter agony — but it could not be undone. After Chu'er was laid in the ground, your mother sank wholly into grief; day and night she was in a state of sorrow and misery. From that time she fell ill with an intermittent disorder of the mind. When the fits came on her, she would walk in her sleep, she would bite and tear, she would rave. Once, in the dead of night, she walked half-naked to the Buddha hall, to beg the Bodhisattva's forgiveness in her dream; her ankle-bones were all over bloody scratches from the grass. Her life was utterly finished. This is the true reason I sent you away from the family when you were small! I did not want your face to inflame her illness. Her lover abandoned her over it; I had not courage and pity enough to face her — I hated her!" Yang Yuhua's tears fell suddenly, spattering onto the back of Muci's hand. "You are a sensible, understanding child, and so I would not tamper with the cause of your mother's illness; it is a fact that can never be evaded. Do you understand the pains I have taken?"

> “爸爸。”慕次的心情很复杂。
"Papa." Muci's feelings were tangled and complex.

> “二十年前，一夜之间，可怜我，儿子死了，妻子疯了，老宅烧了。我当时真不晓得人生还有什么值得我留恋的？我心中的伤痕至今无法熨平。你的母亲也是如此，她的记忆里始终徘徊在佛堂这个晦暗的空间，她不肯原谅自己，二十年了，她深居简出，以泪洗面，活生生枯死在罪恶的阴霾里。”
"Twenty years ago, in a single night — pity me — my son died, my wife went mad, the old mansion burned. In that hour I truly did not know what was left in life worth clinging to. The scars within me are not smoothed flat to this day. And it is the same with your mother: her memory forever paces that dim, dark space of the Buddha hall; she will not forgive herself. For twenty years she has lived shut away and gone out but little, washing her face with tears, withering alive to death in the black mists of her sin."

> “这也是您一直不肯原谅她的原因，是吗？爸爸。”慕次明白了，为什么父母长期以来分居，却不离婚的道理，原来，是因为曾经死去的爱子，彼此都无法面对对方，所以，造成了父亲冷酷地对待自己的妻子，母亲忧郁成疾的局面。
"And this, too, is why you have never been willing to forgive her — is that it, Papa?" Now Muci understood the reason his parents had lived apart so long and yet never divorced: it was because of the beloved son once dead, whom neither could face in the other; and so had come about this state of things — the father treating his wife with cold cruelty, the mother grieving herself into sickness.

> “如果，爸爸，我说是如果，现在有人告诉你，我哥哥没有死，他还活着……”
"If, Papa — I say if — someone were now to tell you that my brother did not die, that he is still alive . . ."

> “谎言！”杨羽桦粗暴地吼叫，一张脸涨得紫红，像新切出来得猪肝。“谎言，无耻的谎言！荒谬！”
"Lies!" Yang Yuhua roared savagely, his face flushing a purplish red, like a freshly cut pig's liver. "Lies, shameless lies! Absurd!"

> “爸爸。”
"Papa."

> “我知道你想告诉我什么，荣家的私生子嘛，那位卑鄙无耻的初先生！”
"I know what you want to tell me — the Rong family's bastard, that base and shameless Mr. Chu!"

> “爸爸，您很早就知道这个人吗？”
"Papa — have you known of this man for a long time?"

> “是的，他是一个表面斯文，本性贪婪的家伙。他曾经冒充荣家大小姐的男友来参加你妹妹的生日宴会。由于此人的行为粗鄙，在舞会中与汤家兄妹发生了争执，还大打出手，没有修养，不，应该说缺乏教养。他还是一个极不守规矩的人，他居然擅自闯入我们家的佛堂，偶然地发现了你哥哥童年遗照，你那不谙世事的母亲，因为他酷似你的容貌，而向他讲述了你哥哥不幸夭亡的悲惨故事。于是……”
"Yes. He is a fellow gentle on the surface and greedy at the core. He once passed himself off as the beau of the Rong family's eldest young lady to attend your sister's birthday party. Because the man's conduct was coarse, he fell into a quarrel with the Tang brother and sister at the ball, and even came to blows — no breeding; no, I should say, no upbringing. He is a man, too, who keeps no rules at all: he actually broke uninvited into our family's Buddha hall, and there chanced upon a childhood photograph of your dead brother; and your mother, who knows nothing of the world, because he so closely resembles your face, told him the whole sad story of your brother's untimely death. And then . . ."

> “于是怎么样？”
"And then what?"

> “听说他现在，在社会上纠集了一帮亡命之徒，妄想利用他的容貌来大做文章。两天前，他试图绑架你的母亲……”
"I hear that now he has gathered a band of desperadoes about him in the world outside, and vainly dreams of making great capital out of his face. Two days ago he tried to abduct your mother—"

> “他想要干什么？”
"What is it he wants to do?"

> “他想取而代之。”
"He wants to take another's place."

> “谁？取代谁？”
"Whose? Take whose place?"

> “还有谁？我和你！”杨羽桦说。“他是一个奸邪的小人，他别指望从我这里得到他想要的任何东西……”“徐玉真”认为杨羽桦说的话太多了，在慕次这种人面前，话说得愈少愈妙，她挣扎起来，脸色惨白。“啊！初儿！初！”她突然坐起来。“初儿！”
"Whose else? Mine, and yours!" said Yang Yuhua. "He is a wicked, treacherous little man, and he need not hope to get anything he wants out of me—" "Xu Yuzhen" thought Yang Yuhua was saying far too much; before a man of Muci's sort, the fewer words the better. She began to struggle up, her face deathly pale. "Ah! Chu'er! Chu!" She suddenly sat upright. "Chu'er!"

> “妈妈！”慕次闻声坐到床畔，安慰她。
"Mama!" At the sound Muci sat down at the bedside and comforted her.

> “你哥哥回来了。”

Yang Muci leaned in with every show of interest. "When did this happen?"

> 高磊故作神秘地说：“今天早上，不，应该确切地说是昨天夜里，抓到的。”
Gao Lei said, with an air of mystery: "This morning — no, to be exact, it was last night that we took him."

> “有没有抓错？记得，上次，上次在法租界，你们把警备司令部的探子当共产党抓来了，害得处座到处去给人赔礼道歉。这一次？”
"Are you sure you've not taken the wrong man? Remember last time — last time in the French Concession, you seized one of the Garrison Command's own agents for a Communist, and made the chief go round apologizing to everyone. And this time?"

> “这一次绝错不了，这个人有特点。”
"This time there can be no mistake — the man has his marks."

> “什么特点？”
"What marks?"

> “金牙、断指。他是中共中央特科的高级长官。”
"A gold tooth, and a severed finger. He is a senior officer of the Chinese Communist Party's Central Special Branch."

> 杨慕次脸上露出极为惊讶的表情，肚子里却灯火通明，这个“金牙、断指”就是中共中央特科书记方致同。很显然，出大事了。
Yang Muci put on a look of the utmost astonishment, but within him all was lit up bright as day: this man of the "gold tooth and severed finger" was none other than Fang Zhitong, Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party's Central Special Branch. Plainly, something grave had happened.

> “你知道，这一票是谁的杰作吗？”
"Do you know whose masterstroke this was?"

> 慕次摇头。
Muci shook his head.

> “军统之花：李沁红小姐。我们二处的王牌！”
"The Flower of Juntong — Miss Li Qinhong. The trump card of our Second Section!"

> “是谁允许你们在这里随意谈论机密的？”侦缉处处长熊自达不知何时已经站在了他们的背后。
"Who gave you leave to be discussing secrets so freely here?" Xiong Zida, chief of the Detective Division, had come to stand behind them, neither knew when.

> 两个人立即站的笔直。
The two of them at once stood bolt upright.

> “岂有此理！”熊自达满脸阴沉地从他们身边走过去。
"It's outrageous!" His face all overcast, Xiong Zida walked past them.

> 杨慕次和高磊打了个手势，随即，紧跟上去。
Yang Muci made a sign to Gao Lei, and then followed close behind.

> “马上通知侦缉处所有的参谋、组长、队长到我办公室来开会。”熊自达一走进办公室就开始发号施令。
"Notify every staff officer, section head, and captain of the Detective Division at once to come to my office for a meeting." No sooner had Xiong Zida walked into his office than he began issuing orders.

> 杨慕次毕恭毕敬地接过熊自达脱下的外套和军帽，说：“是。处座。”
With the utmost deference Yang Muci took the coat and the military cap Xiong Zida had removed, and said: "Yes, chief."

> 杨慕次将熊自达的外套和军帽挂在衣架上，转过身打电话，通知下面的人上来开会。
Yang Muci hung Xiong Zida's coat and cap on the rack, then turned and telephoned to summon the men below to come up for the meeting.

> “我刚才喝了一杯红酒，我觉得味道好极了。”熊自达欣欣自得地说：“你闻到什么气味了吗？杨副官？”
"I've just had a glass of red wine, and I found the taste excellent." Xiong Zida said, well pleased with himself: "Do you smell anything, Adjutant Yang?"

> “我什么也没有闻到，处座。”其实，杨慕次已经闻到了，从今天下午一上班就闻到了一股强烈的血腥味。
"I smell nothing at all, chief." In truth Yang Muci had smelled it — from the moment he came on duty that afternoon he had caught a strong reek of blood.

> “死亡。”熊自达笑得很阴险。“是死人的味道。”他大声笑起来。“方致同是个胆小鬼。他居然跟我谈条件，你知道吗？一个死人还想开口谈条件。你说，我应不应该答应他？”
"Death." Xiong Zida's smile was full of malice. "It's the smell of a dead man." He laughed aloud. "Fang Zhitong is a coward. He actually tried to bargain terms with me, do you know that? A dead man, and still he wants to open his mouth and bargain terms. Tell me — ought I to grant them?"

> 方致同叛变了！
Fang Zhitong had turned traitor!

> 这对杨慕次而言，无疑是一个晴空霹雳。
To Yang Muci this was, beyond doubt, a bolt from a clear sky.

> 昨天夜里被捕，今天下午就叛变。中共中央特科的书记叛变，意味着中共中央办事处、中共中央特委的住所、中共中央秘书处已经全部落进了熊自达的口袋，包括他自己。
Taken last night, and turned traitor by this afternoon. That the Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party's Central Special Branch had defected meant that the Party's Central Office, the lodgings of the Party's Central Special Committee, and the Party's Central Secretariat had all fallen into Xiong Zida's pocket — himself included.
