### Chatting Them Up

What is meant by "picking up a shell"? It is a byword for chasing after the opposite sex; in common speech it is called "tailing," and, to drop into a riddle-saying, it goes "A, B, C..."

These three words, "picking up a shell," are now all the rage in the hoodlums' mouths; when they meet they are sure to ask, "What shell did you pick up today?" If the other has tailed a pretty modern woman, he answers, "A Beauty-brand"; if he has tailed some homely creature, he sighs and says, "A Pirate-brand," to show his disappointment and disgust. There are some, too, who take a room at a hotel and say to the boy, "Any pretty shells about? Call one up for a bit of sport." This is nothing less than to call a woman a "shell."
