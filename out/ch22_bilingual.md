## H2 Chapter 22. To Dam All the Currents Takes a Vast Resolve

> “你还没有回答我，杨副官。”熊自达说。
“You still haven’t answered me, Adjutant Yang,” said Xiong Zida.

> “报告处座，既然他即将是一个死人，我想，应该满足他的要求。”慕次机警地回答。“不过……”
“Reporting, sir. Since he will soon be a dead man, I think we ought to grant his request.” Muci answered smartly. “Only—”

> “不过什么？”
“Only what?”

> “像他这样的胆小鬼，即要千方百计的保住自己的性命，同时又想保住自己的名节；既已变节，又想通过跟我们谈条件来捞取更多的政治资本；他已经是阶下之囚，有什么资格来妄谈合作？”
“A coward like that wants to save his own skin by a thousand schemes, and to keep his good name into the bargain; having already turned his coat, he now wants to bargain with us and scrape up more political capital. He is a prisoner in the dock. What standing has he to prate of cooperation?”

> “不，不。”熊自达说。“你知道方致同在中共中央的地位吗？你不知道。你相信吗？他能够在一夜之间使中共中央的主要机关全部瘫痪、倾覆。他可以提供在上海所有中共高官的名单，懂吗？'他'对我们意味着什么？意味着'红色情报网'破网在即。准确点说，由于他的落网投诚，我们剿灭共党在沪的所有机关将有突破性进展。至于他所提出的条件嘛……”熊自达突然控制不住自己的兴奋而嘲笑起了对手的愚蠢。“他现在就算是提出天价，我也答应他。”
“No, no.” said Xiong Zida. “Do you know Fang Zhitong’s standing in the Central Committee of the Communist Party? You do not. Would you believe it? In a single night he can leave every principal organ of the Party’s Central Committee crippled and overturned. He can hand us a list of every senior Communist in Shanghai, understand? What does ‘he’ mean to us? It means the ‘Red intelligence net’ is on the verge of being torn open. To put it exactly: because he has fallen into our hands and come over, our extermination of every Communist organ in Shanghai will make a breakthrough. As for the conditions he has laid down—” All at once, unable to master his excitement, Xiong Zida fell to sneering at the stupidity of his adversary. “Even if he named a sky-high price now, I would grant it.”

> 杨慕次的脑海里千流万溪的在湍动，还有机会，还有一线生机。
In Yang Muci’s mind a thousand streams and ten thousand brooks went churning: there was still a chance, still one thread of life.

> 只要自己在方致同开口之前见到他，至多不过半秒钟，就可以解决他。慕次甚至想到了三个步骤，第一，找到方致同，开枪击毙；第二，杀死熊自达；第三，饮弹自决。
If only, before Fang Zhitong opened his mouth, he could get to him—half a second at the most—he could settle him. Muci even worked out three steps: first, find Fang Zhitong and shoot him dead; second, kill Xiong Zida; third, swallow a bullet himself.

> 很干净，很迅捷，很有效地化解掉这场“灭顶之灾”。
Clean, swift, effective, and this “drowning catastrophe” would be dissolved.

> 但是，方致同现在何处呢？
But where was Fang Zhitong now?

> “处座，您对一个共匪一味的让步，您不怕……”
“Sir, you give way to a Communist bandit at every turn. Are you not afraid—”

> “怕什么？”
“Afraid of what?”

> “他在虚与尾蛇，拖延时间，故意夸大自己在共党的力量，虚张声势，以换取他自己的利益。”慕次用了很强调的口吻。“我认为，处座应该对他施加压力，利用强有力的有效手段，迫使他认清形势，竹筒倒豆子。而不是客客气气的等待，等待他一点一滴的挤牙膏。”
“He is playing the snake for time, deliberately magnifying his strength inside the Party, making an empty show of force to trade for his own advantage.” Muci laid heavy stress on the words. “In my view, sir, you should bring pressure to bear on him, use forceful and effective means, make him see how things stand and tip out the beans like an upended bamboo tube—not wait on him courteously while he squeezes it out drop by drop like toothpaste.”

> “他的如意算盘，我心知肚明。”熊自达注视着慕次的眼睛说。“可是，你知道吗？方致同只所以还没有开口，是因为他知道侦缉处里有内鬼！”
“His fond little calculation—I know it inside out.” said Xiong Zida, gazing into Muci’s eyes. “But do you know? The reason Fang Zhitong has not yet opened his mouth is that he knows there is a mole inside the Detective Division!”

> “是吗？那就应该先把内鬼给捉出来。”慕次抱定了必死的决心。
“Indeed? Then the mole ought to be caught first.” Muci had steeled himself to die.

> “说得对！”熊自达举起酒杯，一口干了。
“Well said!” Xiong Zida raised his wine cup and drained it at one gulp.

> 大约五分钟后，侦缉处二处行动大队的队长高磊、破译组组长焦同顺、情报组组长徐诚和侦缉处少校刘云副官全部到场，唯有二处特情组组长李沁红缺席。
About five minutes later Gao Lei, captain of the action brigade of the Division’s Second Section, Jiao Tongshun, head of the decryption group, Xu Cheng, head of the intelligence group, and Major Liu Yun, an adjutant of the Detective Division, had all arrived; only Li Qinhong, head of the Second Section’s special-intelligence group, was absent.

> 杨慕次到侦缉处工作已经三、四个月了，他只见过李沁红一面，印像很深。因为第一面是在侦缉处的刑讯室见的，慕次亲眼看见她亲手掐死了一个有亲共嫌疑的疑犯。这是一个心狠手辣的女人，不，不应该叫女人，确切地说是：女魔。
Yang Muci had worked at the Detective Division three or four months now, and had seen Li Qinhong only once, but the impression went deep—for that first sight had been in the Division’s interrogation room, where with his own eyes Muci had watched her strangle to death, with her own hands, a suspect held to be pro-Communist. Here was a woman of cruel and vicious heart—no, she ought not to be called a woman; to be exact, a she-devil.

> “大家都知道，我们二处的军统之花李沁红小姐，在昨天夜里设局，于今天早上成功捕获了共匪方致同。向匪成发在中共特科担任极其重要的角色，他的落网，意味着我们已经撕开了共党在上海红色谍报站的神秘面纱，打开这一关键缺口，诸位，我们会将共党在沪的残匪一网打尽！”
“You all know that Miss Li Qinhong, the Flower of Juntong of our Second Section, laid a trap last night and this morning succeeded in seizing the Communist bandit Fang Zhitong. The bandit Xiang Chengfa holds an extremely important role in the Party’s Special Branch; his capture means we have torn the mysterious veil from the Communists’ Red intelligence station in Shanghai. Force open this crucial breach, gentlemen, and we shall net in one sweep every last remnant of the Communist bandits in Shanghai!”

> 熊自达的话音未落，掌声已经响起来。高磊一边鼓掌，一边讨好地四处颔首。
Before Xiong Zida’s voice had died away the applause had risen. Gao Lei clapped and nodded ingratiatingly on every side.

> “向匪成发已经决定向国民政府投诚。”
“The bandit Xiang Chengfa has already decided to come over to the National Government.”

> 又是一片掌声。
Another wave of applause.

> “不过嘛。”熊自达话锋一转，阴森森地笑着说：“方致同也向我们提出了投诚的条件，第一，绝对保证他的人身安全；第二，安全地把他的女人陆阿贞送到这里，跟他会合；第三，等我们破获了共党的红色天网之后，他们夫妻要去美国定居。”
“However.” Xiong Zida’s tone took a turn, and he smiled with a grim, sinister air. “Fang Zhitong has also put certain conditions to us for coming over. First, an absolute guarantee of his personal safety; second, that his woman, Lu A-Zhen, be brought here safely to join him; third, that once we have cracked the Communists’ Red heaven-net, husband and wife are to go and settle in America.”

> 在熊自达说出第三点的时候，满座发出嘘声。
When Xiong Zida spoke the third point, the whole room hissed.

> “还有最重要的一点，他透露给我们最重要的情报，今天晚上八点，共党特委将会召开一个预备会议，至于地点嘛，我想等我们满足他第二个条件后，他会给我们一个满意的答复。”
“And the most important point of all: he has let slip to us a most important piece of intelligence. Tonight at eight o’clock the Party’s Special Committee will hold a preparatory meeting; as for the place, I think that once we have met his second condition, he will give us a satisfactory answer.”

> 慕次知道李沁红缺席的原因了，她去接方致同的女人了。这个女人只要一跨进侦缉处的门槛，自己的真实身份就会暴露，阿次想，自己才应该是方致同向侦缉处交出的第一张满意答卷。
Now Muci understood the reason for Li Qinhong’s absence: she had gone to fetch Fang Zhitong’s woman. The moment that woman set foot over the threshold of the Detective Division, his own true identity would be laid bare. A-Ci thought: it is I who ought to be the first satisfactory answer-sheet Fang Zhitong hands the Division.

> “全体起立！”
“On your feet, all of you!”

> 随着熊自达的一声口令，大家整齐地站起来。等待新的命令，奇怪的是熊自达并没有再发出新的指令，他只是阴沉沉地来回看着他们的脸，在他们身边踱步。
At Xiong Zida’s word of command everyone stood up in good order, waiting for a fresh command; but strangely Xiong Zida issued no new order at all. He only paced among them, looking gloomily back and forth at their faces.

> “刚才忘了告诉诸位了。方致同告诉我，他说，我的身边有共产党！”熊自达恶毒地扫视着在场的每一位同仁。长时间地扫视，长时间地凝视，长时间地冷眼。他不阴不阳的态度令在场的每一个人都不安，大家几乎同一时间处于窒息状态。紧张、出汗、不知所措。
“I forgot to tell you just now. Fang Zhitong told me—he said there are Communists at my very side!” Venomously Xiong Zida swept his eyes over every colleague present. A long sweeping, a long staring, a long cold gaze. His manner, neither warm nor cold, left every man in the room uneasy; almost in the same instant they were all suffocating—tense, sweating, at a loss.

> 熊自达在杨慕次跟前驻步。
Xiong Zida halted before Yang Muci.

> “杨副官。你为什么不紧张？”熊自达问。
“Adjutant Yang. Why are you not nervous?” Xiong Zida asked.

> “我为什么要紧张？”慕次很从容。
“Why should I be nervous?” Muci was perfectly composed.

> “侦缉处里混进了共产党，你不应该紧张吗？”
“A Communist has slipped into the Detective Division—should you not be nervous?”

> “报告处座。紧张的应该是那个即将暴露的共匪，我不是共产党，所以我不紧张。”
“Reporting, sir. The one who should be nervous is that Communist bandit about to be exposed. I am not a Communist, so I am not nervous.”

> “事不关己。”熊自达又走到刘副官的面前，刘副官被他阴冷地目光射得浑身不自在。“关己则乱。”
“A matter that does not touch you.” Xiong Zida moved on before Adjutant Liu, who, pierced by that cold, shooting gaze, was thoroughly ill at ease. “Touch a man, though, and he loses his head.”

> 刘副官小腿微微发颤，吞咽着突然从口腔里多出来得唾液。
Adjutant Liu’s calves trembled faintly, and he swallowed the saliva that had suddenly gathered in his mouth.

> “刘副官，你为什么紧张？”熊自达问他。
“Adjutant Liu, why are you nervous?” Xiong Zida asked him.

> “因为，因为侦缉处混进了共产党。”刘副官回答。
“Because—because a Communist has slipped into the Detective Division.” Adjutant Liu answered.

> “你又不是共产党，你紧张什么？”
“You’re not a Communist either. What are you nervous about?”

> “报告处座，我是你身边的人，我怕受人诬陷，所以紧张。我觉得这是正常反应，不紧张，只能证明他定力强。”
“Reporting, sir, I am one of the men at your side, and I fear being framed, so I am nervous. I think it a normal reaction. A man who is not nervous only proves his nerve is strong.”

> “说得好！”熊自达走回桌子中间。“惊慌失措，未必就是嫌疑犯；定力强，也许是会伪装。我不相信你们的表情，因为你们都是训练场上脱颖而出的人。任何表情与姿态都可能是迷惑人的假像。我只相信证据。从现在开始，直到方致同开口，这间屋子所有的人，不准回家，不准打电话，不准交头接耳，不准离开侦缉处大楼。否则，以通共嫌疑，格杀勿论！听清楚了吗？”
“Well said!” Xiong Zida walked back to the middle of the table. “To be thrown into a panic does not necessarily mark the suspect; strong nerve may only be good disguise. I do not trust your expressions, for you are all men who came to the top on the training ground. Any look, any posture, may be a mask to fool the eye. I trust only evidence. From this moment, until Fang Zhitong opens his mouth, no one in this room may go home, may make a telephone call, may put heads together and whisper, may leave the Detective Division building. Otherwise—on suspicion of dealing with the Communists, shot on sight! Is that clear?”

> “是！”全体异口同声。
“Yes, sir!” from every throat at once.

> “杨副官，刘副官。”
“Adjutant Yang. Adjutant Liu.”

> “处座。”慕次和刘副官立正。
“Sir.” Muci and Adjutant Liu came to attention.

> “你们两个，从现在开始，交出佩枪，不准离开我的视线半步，互相监督，随叫随到，否则……”
“You two—from this moment you surrender your sidearms, you do not stir half a step out of my sight, you watch each other, you come the instant you are called; otherwise—”

> “以通共嫌疑，格杀勿论！”慕次重复命令。
“On suspicion of dealing with the Communists, shot on sight!” Muci repeated the order.

> “好。执行命令。”熊自达说完，转身进了办公室的里间。
“Good. Carry out the order.” With that Xiong Zida turned and went into the inner room of the office.

> 杨慕次掏出佩枪，放到高磊所站的桌面。高磊收了慕次的枪，说：“不好意思。”慕次知道他话里的涵义，于是转身靠墙，双手斜举抵住墙，这是请他搜身，以示清白。
Yang Muci drew out his sidearm and laid it on the desk where Gao Lei stood. Gao Lei took Muci’s gun and said, “Sorry about this.” Muci knew what the words meant, so he turned and leaned against the wall, both hands raised at a slant to brace against it—inviting a search, to show himself clean.

> 高磊说声：“得罪。”走形式一样搜了慕次的身。
Gao Lei said, “Excuse me,” and searched Muci as one goes through a formality.

> 刘副官骂骂咧咧地掏出佩枪，扔给高磊，转身靠墙，让高磊搜身。他对慕次小声说：“等事情做完了，我第一个灭了方致同。”
Cursing under his breath, Adjutant Liu pulled out his sidearm, tossed it to Gao Lei, turned and leaned against the wall, and let Gao Lei search him. In a low voice he said to Muci: “When this is over, I’ll be the first to finish off Fang Zhitong.”

> “别胡说，找死啊。”慕次用眼神制止他的不满情绪。
“Don’t talk rot—you’ll get yourself killed.” Muci checked his discontent with a look.

> “等着瞧。”
“You wait and see.”

> “对不起二位，改天兄弟请你们喝茶。”高磊笑着拍了拍二人的肩膀。
“Sorry, you two. Another day this brother will stand you tea.” Gao Lei smiled and patted them both on the shoulder.

> “少来这套。”刘副官不买账。
“Spare us the act.” Adjutant Liu wasn’t buying it.

> “走，门口抽烟去。”慕次拉着刘副官到门口吸烟去了。
“Come on, let’s go smoke at the door.” Muci drew Adjutant Liu off to the doorway for a cigarette.

> “他以为他是谁？”刘副官气不顺。
“Who does he think he is?” Adjutant Liu was out of temper.

> 慕次替他点烟。“不就是缴枪嘛，一会还不给咱们送回来。”
Muci lit the cigarette for him. “It’s only handing over a gun. They’ll give them back to us before long.”

> “送回来。”刘副官“哼”了一声。“咱们这位主，见过什么大世面？一个方致同，就把他给乐嗬成这样。”
“Give them back.” Adjutant Liu gave a snort. “This master of ours—what great affairs of the world has he ever seen? One Fang Zhitong, and he’s beside himself with joy.”

> “方致同可是中共特科的书记。”
“Fang Zhitong is secretary of the Communists’ Special Branch, after all.”

> “顾顺章呢？顾顺章是中共中央的委员，特科红枪队的头目，又怎么样了？他叛变的时候，南京以为共党在上海的机构全部玩完，结果怎么样？一封密电，共党一夜之间消逝了，出了这么大一个叛徒，中共在上海的秘密组织毫发未伤。方致同算个什么东西？就凭他？可以消灭红色情报网？鬼都不信。”
“And Gu Shunzhang? Gu Shunzhang was a member of the Party’s Central Committee, chief of the Special Branch’s Red Squad—and what came of it? When he turned traitor, Nanjing thought every Communist organ in Shanghai was finished. And the result? One coded telegram, and in a single night the Communists vanished; for all that so great a traitor came out, the Party’s secret organization in Shanghai was not touched by so much as a hair. What is Fang Zhitong worth? On the strength of him, wipe out the Red intelligence net? Not even the devil would believe it.”

> “少说两句，隔墙有耳。”
“Say less—walls have ears.”

> “怕什么，人正不怕影子歪。”
“What’s to fear? A straight man doesn’t fear a crooked shadow.”

> “枪都被人缴了，死鸭子还嘴硬。”慕次笑。
“Your gun’s been taken and still the dead duck’s beak is hard.” Muci laughed.

> “枪缴了就缴了呗。咱不用那玩意，多血腥啊。”刘副官做了一个快速扭断人颈骨的模拟动作。“一秒钟，连声音都没有。干净。”
“So it’s been taken, so what. I’ve no use for that thing—too bloody.” Adjutant Liu made the motion of snapping a man’s neck with a quick twist. “One second, not even a sound. Clean.”

> “那就不血腥了？残忍。”慕次一边聊天，一边观察楼道里出出进进的人。他知道，自己除了荣华一个上线外，还有一个下线，只是这个下线，他不到万不得已，绝不能启用。他启用下线的那一刻，也就是下线完成任务后，撤离的一刻。
“And that’s not bloody? Cruel.” As he chatted, Muci watched the people coming and going in the corridor. He knew that, besides Ronghua, his one contact above him, he had one contact below—only this one below he must never bring into play except in the last extremity. The moment he brought his line below into play would be the moment that line, its task done, withdrew.

> 今天，是自己的死期。他必须启用下线，他要告诉下线，自己即将暴露身份，面临死亡。他希望下线能把消息传出去。
Today was the day of his own death. He must bring his line below into play; he must tell that contact that his own identity was about to be exposed, that he faced death. He hoped the contact could get the news out.

> 至于今夜的特委预备会，荣华说过，下午五点钟以前，她联络不到方致同，会议将自动取消。所以，特委应该没有危险，至少在今夜。
As for tonight’s Special Committee preparatory meeting: Ronghua had said that if she could not reach Fang Zhitong before five o’clock in the afternoon, the meeting would be cancelled of itself. So the Special Committee should be in no danger—at least not tonight.

> 今夜之前，必须解决方致同。只要能让自己见到他，就像刘副官比的那样，他会扭断叛徒的脖子，只需要一秒钟。
Before this night was out, Fang Zhitong had to be dealt with. If only he could get to see him—just as Adjutant Liu had mimed—he would snap the traitor’s neck; one second was all it took.

> 慕次背转身去，用力扯断了军装上第二颗纽扣，这是他的“死”扣，他把扣子“遗失”在门前的走廊上。然后他回转身去，依旧和刘副官说笑。
Yang Muci turned his back and wrenched off the second button of his uniform tunic. This was his “death” button; he “lost” the button in the corridor before the door. Then he turned back and went on laughing and talking with Adjutant Liu.

> 悦耳的钟声响起来，荣华书店的挂钟指向下午四点半。
A pleasant chime sounded: the hanging clock in the Ronghua Bookstore pointed to half past four in the afternoon.

> 荣华心神不定地看着天色，她已经做好了“取消”预备会的决定。就在她要拨电话的瞬间，电话铃声响了。
Ronghua watched the sky, unsettled in mind. She had already made up her mind to “cancel” the preparatory meeting. At the very instant she was about to lift the telephone, the bell rang.

> “喂。”荣华接听电话。
“Hello.” Ronghua answered.

> “是我。”方致同的声音干涩。
“It’s me.” Fang Zhitong’s voice was dry and rasping.

> “您在哪？”
“Where are you?”

> “我今天上午身体有些不适，没能来。”这是指今天上午有特务跟踪。
“I was a little unwell this morning; I couldn’t come.” This meant that agents had been tailing him that morning.

> “您的病好一点了吗？晚上家长会还开吗？”
“Are you feeling any better? Is the parents’ meeting still on tonight?”

> “病好了。家长会准时开。”方致同挂了电话。
“I’m better. The parents’ meeting is on, right on time.” Fang Zhitong hung up.

> 荣华挂了电话后，拨通了另一个号码。
After Ronghua put down the receiver she dialled another number.

> “是我。我先生刚才打电话回来了，他说今天晚上，家长会准时开。”
“It’s me. My husband just telephoned home; he says the parents’ meeting tonight is on, right on time.”

> “知道了。家长会准时开。”
“Understood. The parents’ meeting is on, right on time.”

> 两个人同时挂了电话。
The two of them hung up at the same moment.

> 荣华拎起早已准备好的手提包，准备出发，她下意识地看了看手表，又看了看电话，感觉不是很好。她想，慕次为什么到现在还不给自己打电话？
Ronghua picked up the handbag she had made ready long before and prepared to set out. Half unconsciously she glanced at her watch, then at the telephone; the feeling was not good. Why, she wondered, has Muci still not telephoned me?

> 她决定，无论如何，自己要等阿次的电话，必须等。
She resolved that, come what may, she would wait for A-Ci’s call. She had to wait.

> 与此同时。
Meanwhile.

> 参谋部派人来见熊自达。熊自达引来人如内，关上里间的门。杨慕次进来给二人泡茶。他倒了两杯龙井，端进里间去。
The General Staff sent a man to see Xiong Zida. Xiong Zida drew the visitor inside and shut the inner door. Yang Muci came in to make tea for the two of them. He poured two cups of Longjing and carried them into the inner room.

> 四点三刻左右。
About four forty-five.

> 杨慕次从里间出来，他发现自己“遗留”的“死扣”居然放在了自己办公室的窗台上。刘副官懒洋洋地靠在沙发上，睡着。
Yang Muci came out of the inner room and found that the “death button” he had “left behind” was, of all places, sitting on the windowsill of his own office. Adjutant Liu lolled asleep on the sofa.

> 勤务兵小吴刚刚打扫完办公室。
The orderly, Little Wu, had just finished cleaning the office.

> 杨慕次看着小吴走出自己的视线，回头踢了一脚睡在沙发上的刘副官，让他别睡了。
Yang Muci watched Little Wu pass out of his sight, then turned and gave the sleeping Adjutant Liu a kick to wake him.

> 五点一刻。
A quarter past five.

> 桌上的电话铃声骤响。
The telephone on the desk rang out sharply.

> 慕次和刘副官彼此看了对方一眼，都没接。
Muci and Adjutant Liu glanced at each other; neither answered it.

> 熊自达送走了参谋部的访客，回头来，电话铃声依然在响。
Xiong Zida saw off his visitor from the General Staff and came back; the telephone was still ringing.

> “为什么不接电话？”他质问二人。
“Why don’t you answer the telephone?” he demanded of the two of them.

> “没您的命令。”刘副官低声嘀咕。
“No order from you.” Adjutant Liu muttered under his breath.

> 慕次抢上一步，接了电话。“哪里？”
Muci stepped smartly forward and took the call. “Where from?”

> “储……储藏室。”电话里一个男人的声音在喘。
“The—the storeroom.” A man’s voice panted down the line.

> “找谁？”
“Who do you want?”

> “处座。”
“The chief.”

> “处座。”慕次恭敬地把电话递到熊自达的手上。“您的电话。”
“The chief.” Respectfully Muci handed the receiver to Xiong Zida. “For you.”

> “哪里？”
“Where from?”

> “好像是说，储藏室。”
“The storeroom, it seemed to say.”

> 熊自达的脸色一下变了，对着话筒喊：“怎么了？”
Xiong Zida’s face changed in an instant. He shouted into the mouthpiece: “What’s happened?”

> 听到对方答案后，熊自达手上的听筒落地。
When he heard the answer, the receiver fell from his hand to the floor.

> “怎么了？”慕次问。
“What is it?” Muci asked.

> “方致同死了！”熊自达不敢相信地喃喃自语。
“Fang Zhitong is dead!” Xiong Zida muttered to himself, unable to believe it.

> 方致同死了！
Fang Zhitong was dead!

> 杨慕次一时也不敢相信。
For a moment even Yang Muci could not believe it.

> 刘副官第一个反应过来，他大声喊着：“警卫排集合！高队！高队……”他冲到熊自达身边。“处座，赶紧的啊。”
Adjutant Liu was the first to come to his senses. He shouted at the top of his voice: “Guard platoon, fall in! Captain Gao! Captain Gao—” and rushed to Xiong Zida’s side. “Sir, quickly, come on.”

> “去现场。”熊自达黑着脸，疾步如飞地走出去。
“To the scene.” Black in the face, Xiong Zida strode out as fast as flying.

> 刘副官、杨慕次紧跟着他的步伐，高磊截住他们，简单说了两句话，然后跑步下楼。只不过，杨慕次的脚步异常轻快，他甚至能感觉到内心的解放。
Adjutant Liu and Yang Muci followed hard on his steps. Gao Lei headed them off, said two brief words, then ran downstairs. Only Yang Muci’s own steps were uncommonly light; he could even feel the liberation within his heart.

> 方致同真的死了。
Fang Zhitong really was dead.

> 死得很难看。
He had died a hideous death.

> 他的面部痉挛，手脚呈抽搐状，七窍流血，模样古怪地瘫倒在椅子上，已经断了气。很显然他是中毒死亡。桌子上散放着零星的纸片，也许是毒性发作时留下的杰作，剩下的半杯牛奶白森森透着冷刃般的蔑笑和寒光，让人不寒而栗。
His face was convulsed, his hands and feet drawn up in spasm, blood running from every orifice, sprawled grotesquely in the chair, already without breath. Plainly he had died of poison. Scraps of paper lay scattered on the table—perhaps the handiwork left as the poison took hold—and the half cup of milk that remained gleamed with a cold, blade-white sneer and a chill light that made the flesh creep.

> 有人闪电般的采取了行动。
Someone had acted like a bolt of lightning.

> 直接的“谋杀”过程很简单，一个人在恰当的时机给另一个人送了一杯奶。轻而易举地杀进重围，在敌人的眼皮底下把该死的叛徒送上了黄泉路。
The direct process of the “murder” was very simple: at exactly the right moment one person brought another a cup of milk. Easily, effortlessly, he had cut his way into the heavy cordon and, under the enemy’s very eyes, sent the traitor who deserved to die down the road to the Yellow Springs.

> 熊自达的脸色变得恶毒起来，一点也不逊色那剩下的半杯奶。
Xiong Zida’s face turned venomous, no less so than that remaining half cup of milk.

> 杨慕次从心底长长地出了一口气，他知道，自己幸运的和死神擦肩而过了。不，不仅仅是自己，还有许许多多战斗在敌人心脏里的同志们得以死里逃生。
From the bottom of his heart Yang Muci let out a long breath. He knew that he had luckily brushed shoulders with the god of death and passed by. No—not only himself, but a great many comrades fighting in the heart of the enemy had escaped from the jaws of death.

> “谁？谁送的牛奶？”熊自达问。
“Who? Who brought the milk?” Xiong Zida demanded.

> 看守们浑身都在抖。
The warders were trembling all over.

> “说话呀。”高队咬牙切齿地吼。
“Speak!” Captain Gao roared through gritted teeth.

> 一个看守战战兢兢地说：“他，他自己要喝牛奶，他自己要的。”
One warder, shaking with fear, said: “He—he wanted milk himself, he asked for it himself.”

> “谁送来得？”熊自达再问。
“Who brought it?” Xiong Zida asked again.

> “勤务兵，您的勤务兵小吴。”
“The orderly—your orderly, Little Wu.”

> “人呢？”
“Where is he?”

> “不，不见了。”
“He’s—he’s gone.”

> 熊自达抬手一枪，毙了一个看守，吓得另外两个看守双腿发软，跪下去了。
Xiong Zida raised his hand and shot one warder dead, so that the other two, their legs turning to water in terror, sank to their knees.

> “侦缉处里有鬼！”熊自达喃喃地说，“很快就会水落石出。”他发布命令。“掘地三尺，也要把人给我找出来。快！”
“There’s a ghost in the Detective Division!” Xiong Zida muttered. “It will soon come to light.” He issued his order: “Dig down three feet if you must, but find the man for me. Quick!”

> 高磊带人开始行动了。
Gao Lei took his men and set to work.

> 储藏室就在侦缉处大楼的地下室里，久已不用了，李沁红的特情组把这里改建成了二处秘密关押室，专门关押级别高的政治犯，因为杨慕次是新人，所以，他是第一次接触到这个特殊的关押地点。
The storeroom was in the basement of the Detective Division building, long disused; Li Qinhong’s special-intelligence group had rebuilt it into the Second Section’s secret detention room, kept especially for high-ranking political prisoners. Because Yang Muci was new, this was his first contact with this special place of confinement.

> 高磊和刘副官他们在逐层搜查每一个房间。杨慕次紧跟在熊自达身后，熊自达怒发冲冠地朝他吼叫。“你老跟着我干什么？！”
Gao Lei, Adjutant Liu and the rest were searching every room, floor by floor. Yang Muci stuck close behind Xiong Zida, who, hair bristling with rage, roared at him: “What are you always following me for?!”

> “处座。是您叫我寸步不离的。”
“Sir. It was you who told me not to stir a step from you.”

> “我现在不想看见你！明白吗？还杵在这里干什么？滚啊。”
“I don’t want to see you now! Understand? What are you still standing here for? Get out.”

> “是。处座。”杨慕次用最快的速度，迅速消失在楼梯口。
“Yes, sir.” At his utmost speed Yang Muci vanished at the stairhead.

> 五点三刻。
Five forty-five.

> 杨慕次偷偷回到办公室，迅速走入里间，他蹲在办公桌下，把电话拖到地上，冒险拨通了荣华的电话。
Yang Muci slipped back to the office, went swiftly into the inner room, crouched under the desk, dragged the telephone down to the floor, and at great risk dialled Ronghua’s number.

> “喂。”
“Hello.”

> 他清晰地听到了荣华的声音。
He heard Ronghua’s voice clearly.

> “是表叔吗？我一直在家等你的电话，表婶的病好了吗？”
“Is that Uncle? I’ve been waiting at home all this while for your call. Is Auntie’s illness better?”

> “表婶心脏病复发，虽然她答应和'医生'配合，但还是回天乏术。家长会去不成了。”慕次挂断了电话。他听见走廊上的脚步声，迅即把电话放好，推开窗子，手借砖缝之力，身子飘逸地挂了出去。
“Auntie’s heart trouble has flared up again. Though she agreed to work with the ‘doctor,’ there was no saving her. The parents’ meeting is off.” Muci hung up. He heard footsteps in the corridor, at once set the telephone back in its place, pushed open the window, and, his hands taking purchase in the seams of the brickwork, swung his body lightly out and hung there.

> 一分不差，熊自达走进房间打电话。“立即封锁沪中长官公署的大门，没有我的手令，任何人不能通行。”
Not a minute off, Xiong Zida walked into the room to telephone. “Seal off the gate of the Central Shanghai Commandant’s Office at once. Without my written order, no one is to pass.”

> 慕次的身体下移，脚尖踩在砖缝上，身体触到另一层楼的窗棂，他的手准确无误地抓到窗棂，身子一跃，飞了进去。
Muci’s body moved downward; his toes trod the seams of the brick, his body touched the window-lattice of another floor, his hand seized the lattice unerringly, and with a spring he flew in.

> “有没有发现？”
“Anything found?”

> 慕次听见刘副官在过道上叫喊。
Muci heard Adjutant Liu calling out in the passage.

> 他很自然地推开门出去，向刘副官耸了耸肩。
Naturally he pushed open the door and went out, shrugging his shoulders at Adjutant Liu.

> “去停车场。”
“To the car park.”

> 他们听见高磊在楼下喊，于是，他们对视一眼，二话不说，也向楼下奔去。对刘副官来说，抓到共党嫌犯，就可以洗清自己身上的嫌疑；对慕次来讲，找到小吴，或许能帮他死里求生。
They heard Gao Lei shouting downstairs, so, with a glance at each other, they said no more and ran downstairs too. For Adjutant Liu, to catch the Communist suspect was to clear the suspicion off himself; for Muci, to find Little Wu might help him snatch life out of death.

> 两个人怀着不同的目的，朝着一个共同的目标搜索……
The two of them, with different aims, searched toward one common goal—

> 荣华在接到慕次电话的那一刻，就开始火速行动了。
From the moment Ronghua took Muci’s call, she began to act with all speed.

> 她先给中共中央特科设在威海路的老家打电话，告诉他们，向同学的家长心脏病复发，向家长积极和“医生”配合（暗示方致同叛变），抢救无效，已经死亡。请求立即取消晚上的“家长会”。
First she telephoned the old base of the Party Central’s Special Branch on Weihai Road and told them: the parent of the student Xiang has had a heart attack; the parent worked actively with the “doctor” (a hint that Fang Zhitong had defected); the rescue failed; he is dead. She asked that tonight’s “parents’ meeting” be cancelled at once.

> 老家告诉她，现在正式取消会议，但是，有许多学生家长外出，收不到消息了。请荣华竭尽全力，想方设法，通知到学生家长。
The old base told her: the meeting was now officially cancelled, but many of the students’ parents had gone out and could no longer be reached. They asked Ronghua to do everything in her power, by every means, to get word to the students’ parents.

> 威海路的特科总部在接到荣华消息的刹那，立即行动。通知设在云南路的中共中央特委、设在广东路清河坊的中共中央军委迅速转移。
The instant the Special Branch headquarters on Weihai Road received Ronghua’s message, it acted. It notified the Party Central’s Special Committee, on Yunnan Road, and the Party Central’s Military Commission, at Qinghefang on Guangdong Road, to move at once.

> 但是，由于戈登路正值检修电讯线路，总部通知不到中央政治局会议室和机要处的工作人员，戈登路的恒吉里，正处在极度危险中。
But because the telegraph lines on Gordon Road were just then under repair, headquarters could not reach the staff of the Central Political Bureau’s meeting room and the confidential office; Hengji Li on Gordon Road was in the gravest danger.

> 荣华开着车，飞驰到“大光明旅社”，通知所有特委转移。但是，有些特委出于安全考虑，已经单独搬离，不知去向。
Ronghua drove at a tearing pace to the “Grand Light Hostel” to warn every Special Committee member to move. But some of them, out of concern for safety, had already moved out on their own, no one knew where.

> 荣华急三火四地回到梅花巷，丛锋已经提前走了，给她留了一个便条：久违上海风光，先走一步，饱览海市，恒吉里预备会上见。锋。
In frantic haste Ronghua came back to Plum Blossom Lane. Cong Feng had already left ahead of her, leaving her a note: Long a stranger to the sights of Shanghai, I go on ahead to feast my eyes on the mirage of the city. See you at the preparatory meeting at Hengji Li. Feng.

> 六点三十分。
Six thirty.

> 杨慕次和刘副官得到了一个准确消息，小吴找到了，他一直潜藏在一辆军用运输车中，企图通过军车的掩护，顺利走出关卡。
Yang Muci and Adjutant Liu got a piece of exact news: Little Wu had been found. He had been hiding all along in a military transport truck, meaning to pass the checkpoint under cover of the army vehicle.

> 不幸的是，他被发现了。
Unluckily, he had been discovered.

> 束手就擒。
Taken without a struggle.

> 与此同时。
Meanwhile.

> 荣华开车来到了戈登路的恒吉里1141号。这里是中央政治局会议室和机要秘书处所在地。
Ronghua drove to No. 1141 Hengji Li on Gordon Road. This was where the Central Political Bureau’s meeting room and confidential secretariat were.

> 家里没人，只有一个老保姆。
No one was home—only an old amah.

> “您找谁？”保姆警惕地扫视着荣华。
“Who are you looking for?” The amah scanned Ronghua warily.

> “我是林谭先生的女朋友。”这是寻人的口令。
“I’m Mr. Lin Tan’s girlfriend.” This was the password for finding someone.

> “您有什么事？”
“What is it you want?”

> “向先生出了车祸，不能来了。”
“Mr. Xiang has had a road accident; he can’t come.”

> “可是，现在家里没主人。”这是告诉荣华，主要负责人不在。
“But there’s no master at home just now.” This was to tell Ronghua that the chief responsible person was not there.

> “能通知到吗？”
“Can they be reached?”

> “恐怕不能。”
“I’m afraid not.”

> “为什么？”
“Why?”

> “他们事先约好了家长会上见。”
“They arranged beforehand to meet at the parents’ meeting.”

> “家长会已经取消了。”
“The parents’ meeting has been cancelled.”

> “我们没有接到班主任的通知。”
“We’ve had no word from the class teacher.”

> “我就是班主任。”
“I am the class teacher.”

> 老保姆真的紧张起来。“不好。”
The old amah grew truly alarmed. “This is bad.”

> “怎么了？”
“What is it?”

> “伍先生，今天晚上会来。”
“Mr. Wu is coming tonight.”

> 伍先生，指的是伍豪。中共中央特科最高负责人。
Mr. Wu meant Wu Hao—the highest man in charge of the Party Central’s Special Branch.

> “不能让伍先生来，绝对不能。”荣华说。
“Mr. Wu must not come—absolutely must not.” said Ronghua.

> “你放心，我来想办法。”老保姆说。
“Set your mind at rest; I’ll think of a way.” said the old amah.

> 荣华离开恒吉里1141号的瞬间，恒吉里1141号的小阁楼上的晾衣架上晾出了红色床单。预示着“禁止通行”。
The instant Ronghua left No. 1141 Hengji Li, a red bedsheet was hung out on the drying-rack of the little attic of No. 1141 Hengji Li, signalling “no passage.”

> 六点三刻。
Six forty-five.

> 侦缉处的过道上吹着阴冷的风，仿佛刚下过一场雷暴雨，杀气覆盖着整栋大楼。
A cold, dank wind blew through the passages of the Detective Division, as though a thunderstorm had just passed; a killing air lay over the whole building.

> 杨慕次、刘副官、高磊等人都站在二处的处长办公室里，大气不敢出。小吴被修理得很惨，身上、脸上都是血，衣服被揉成烂菜叶。他年龄只有十八岁，他很年轻，很稚嫩。
Yang Muci, Adjutant Liu, Gao Lei and the rest all stood in the Second Section chief’s office, not daring to breathe loud. Little Wu had been badly worked over; blood on his body, blood on his face, his clothes crushed like rotten cabbage-leaves. He was only eighteen, very young, very tender.

> “我有108套刑具可以让你开口说话，就算梁山泊的硬汉全到了，也撑不住。”熊自达很急躁，很凶狠。
“I have 108 instruments of torture that can make you talk; even if all the stout fellows of Liangshan Marsh came, they couldn’t hold out.” Xiong Zida was fretful and savage.

> 汗珠从小吴的额头上滴下来。
Beads of sweat dripped from Little Wu’s forehead.

> “我需要你的帮助！”熊自达在笑，他从小吴惶恐的眼神中看到了希望。“如果你拒绝帮助我，那么，我会帮助你。你知道我会怎样地去帮助一个人，在痛苦中帮助他改变一些愚蠢的想法，我需要你的答案。”
“I need your help!” Xiong Zida was smiling; in Little Wu’s frightened eyes he had seen hope. “If you refuse to help me, then I will help you. You know how I go about helping a man—helping him, in his pain, to change some of his foolish notions. I want your answer.”

> “我想喝杯水。”小吴机械地舔了一下干裂的嘴唇。
“I’d like a cup of water.” Little Wu licked his cracked lips mechanically.

> 熊自达喘了口气，半坐在办公桌上，喊：“杨副官。给我们的小朋友倒杯水。”
Xiong Zida drew a breath, half sat on the desk, and called: “Adjutant Yang. Pour our little friend a cup of water.”

> 阿次的步伐有些机械，当他把盛满水的杯子递到小吴手上时，他近距离感觉到小吴杂乱无序的呼吸。
A-Ci’s steps were somewhat mechanical; as he put the cup, filled with water, into Little Wu’s hands, at close quarters he felt Little Wu’s ragged, disordered breathing.

> 他担心了。
He was worried.

> 担心他撑不住。
Worried the boy could not hold out.

> “我最后问你一句，要不要跟我合作？”
“I ask you one last time—will you cooperate with me or not?”

> “要。”小吴开口了。
“I will.” Little Wu had opened his mouth.

> 这一个字，回答得异常干脆，异常轻巧。却很直接地刺穿了杨慕次的耳膜及心脏。慕次的视线愈来愈模糊，头脑一片空白。
That single word was answered with an extraordinary crispness, an extraordinary lightness. Yet it pierced straight through Yang Muci’s eardrum and his heart. Muci’s vision grew more and more blurred, his mind a blank.

> 他想干什么？
What did the boy mean to do?

> 他下一步要做什么？
What would he do next?

An irreversible situation, a hair-trigger threat, lay hidden all soundless around A-Ci. But of one thing A-Ci was certain: this man who might well turn traitor had no standing to know where the Party’s Special Branch was; the only person he could sell was himself.

“Do you want to know how Fang Zhitong died?” said Little Wu.

“No. I have never been interested in dead men. What interests me most now is—how did an orderly come to know that Fang Zhitong had been arrested?”

“I heard people talking of it.”

“Who? Who was talking?”

“Captain Gao’s men.”

Gao Lei’s face was overspread in an instant with ashen gloom.

“What did they say?”

“They said they’d hooked a big fish of the Party’s.”

Why?” Xiong Zida’s near-distorted face pressed almost to Little Wu’s brow, questioning him from above, forcing the words from him: “I was carrying out an order.”

“Excellent. Whose order?” Xiong Zida turned and swept his eyes over the face of every man in the room. Then all at once he bellowed: “Whose order?!”

> 没有声音。
No sound.

> 确切地说没有回答。
More exactly, no answer.

Every man in the room was as if racked over a fire, for their lives all hung on the mouth of this ordinarily unremarkable little orderly.

“Whose order?” Xiong Zida’s tone softened suddenly.

“A superior’s order.”

> “他的名字？”
“His name?”

“Fang Zhitong.”

“You mean Fang Zhitong took his own life?” Xiong Zida’s face was all smiles.

“There’s nothing to laugh at. Fang Zhitong was my superior; once he was arrested, he became a deadly threat to me.”

“And if he had not betrayed your organization? Would you still have killed him?”

“You yourself say ‘if.’ And if he meant to sell me? I was truly terrified at the time.”

> “怕什么？”
“Afraid of what?”

> “怕他第一个把我供出来。”
“Afraid he’d give me up first.”

> “你和他认识多久了？”
“How long had you known him?”

> “三年。”
“Three years.”

“Liar!” Xiong Zida’s face grew hideous with quickened breath. “Fang Zhitong was only transferred from Jiangxi to Shanghai half a year ago to take up his post as a member of the Special Branch. I have read your file; you have worked here nearly four years. How could you have known him three years? It seems we must go somewhere else for a good talk.” Xiong Zida shouted aloud: “Guards! Drag him out for me. I don’t want to waste any more time.”

In fact there was no need to call: everyone in the room wanted to be rid of Little Wu, afraid he would bite wildly and drag them into needless ruin. Captain Gao and Adjutant Liu, two men to one, hauled Little Wu toward the door; struggling, Little Wu cried out: “Fang Zhitong truly was my superior—he knew I worked in the Detective Division, but he did not know I was only an orderly. He thought—”

“Thought what?”

“Thought I was one of the men at your side.”

“And aren’t you?”

“You tell me, Chief Xiong—do I count as one of the men at your side?”

“Whether you are at my side or not doesn’t matter—”

“What matters is that I know where the Special Branch secretariat works.”

“Wait.” At Xiong Zida’s command Little Wu was flung down hard on the threshold of the Detective Division chief’s office. And Yang Muci’s heart was flung down hard along with him. Xiong Zida walked straight over, bent down, and said in a low voice: “I’ll give you one chance now, the one chance to live: tell me where the Communists’ Special Branch secretariat is, and I’ll give you your freedom.” Xiong Zida hauled Little Wu up by the front of him.

> 杨慕次心里第一个念头，就是立即拔枪干掉他，他的手在靠近枪套时，他看见小吴的眼睛正注视着他，那是一双清澈明亮的眼睛。
Yang Muci’s first thought was to draw his gun at once and finish the boy; as his hand neared the holster, he saw that Little Wu’s eyes were fixed on him—a pair of clear, bright eyes.

It made no sense. Why did the boy not give him up at once?

> 他的供词在保护自己。
His confession was protecting him.

> 再等等。
Wait a little longer.

> 一念之差。
A single wrong thought.

He heard Little Wu say clearly: “Gordon Road.”

> 戈登路恒吉里是中央秘书处机关所在地。
Hengji Li on Gordon Road was where the Central Secretariat organ was.

> “如果你们想要知道确切位置，我亲自带你们去。说到做到。”
“If you want to know the exact spot, I’ll take you there myself. My word is my bond.”

> 慕次懂了。小吴一直都在保护自己，保护中央特科的安全，他还不知道，情报已经送出去了。小吴想带着侦缉处的特务们去“逛花园”，以期达到“预警”的作用，通过特殊的方式，把方致同叛变的消息送出去。
Now Muci understood. All along Little Wu had been protecting him, protecting the safety of the Party Central’s Special Branch—he did not yet know the intelligence had already gone out. Little Wu meant to lead the Division’s agents on a “stroll round the garden,” so as to serve as a “warning,” and by this special means to send out the news of Fang Zhitong’s defection.

> 枪声响了！
A gunshot rang out!

> 子弹是从小吴背后射入的，他的头被打穿了。
The bullet entered from behind Little Wu; his head was shot through.

> 他的血一汩汩地冒出来，慕次眼前一片漆黑。小吴牺牲了。他才十八岁。
His blood came welling out in gushes; before Muci’s eyes all went black. Little Wu had given his life. He was only eighteen.

> “是谁？！”熊自达暴跳如雷地怒吼。
“Who?!” Xiong Zida roared, stamping and raging.

> “是我。处座。”全副武装的李沁红出现在门口，她手上枪冒着淡淡的烟，她那一头蓬蓬松松的短发，轻曼地撒开。仿佛在藐视自己的顶头上司，又或是嘲笑。
“It was I. Sir.” Li Qinhong, armed to the teeth, appeared in the doorway, faint smoke rising from the gun in her hand, her thick, loosely tumbling short hair spread lightly loose—as though in scorn of her own superior, or perhaps in mockery.

> “你？你在干什么？”熊自达的声调不自觉地调小了一个音阶。
“You? What are you doing?” Xiong Zida’s pitch dropped, despite himself, by a semitone.

> “我在执行公务，处座。”她毫不示弱地走到熊自达面前，猛地把小吴的尸体拎起来，甩出去。然后冷冷地一笑。“处座，您要是听了这个小共匪的话，带着一帮人马去逛花园，别说抓捕行动会一无所获，还会打草惊蛇，到时候，您连共产党机关的毛发都碰不到。由于您工作上的疏忽，我们已经失去了方致同，我不想最后连一口残羹都吃不到。”李沁红朝后一招手。“带进来。”
“I am carrying out official duty, sir.” Giving no ground, she walked up to Xiong Zida, all at once hoisted Little Wu’s corpse and flung it aside, then gave a cold little laugh. “Sir, if you had listened to this little Communist bandit and led a whole troop off to stroll round the garden, then, far from your arrest coming to nothing, you would have startled the snake in the grass, and by then you’d not so much as touch a hair of the Communist organ. Through your oversight in this work we have already lost Fang Zhitong; I have no wish to end up without even a scrap of the leavings.” Li Qinhong beckoned behind her. “Bring her in.”

> 特情组的特务们押了一个女人进来。女人畏畏缩缩的，神情慌乱，一双腿不停的发颤，眼睛里全是惊恐。
The special-intelligence agents marched a woman in. She shrank and cowered, her look panic-stricken, her legs trembling without cease, her eyes full of terror.

> 慕次知道，她应该是方致同的女人--陆阿贞。她不是共产党，她不会知道党的机密。但是，她可能会知道方致同常去的地方，她的供词一样会对党的机关住址造成致命的威胁。
Muci knew she must be Fang Zhitong’s woman—Lu A-Zhen. She was not a Communist; she could not know the Party’s secrets. But she might know the places Fang Zhitong often went; her confession could just as well pose a deadly threat to the address of the Party’s organ.

“Gordon Road, running north and south, from Bubbling Well Road in the south to Suzhou Creek in the north. Length, 2594 metres.” Li Qinhong, usurping the host’s place, pulled open a topographical map of the city of Shanghai and stood proudly in the chief’s position. “That little Communist bandit meant to lead you pack of good-for-nothings on a stroll along Suzhou Creek.” She paused a moment, then went on: “No. 66 Gordon Road is the Majestic Theatre; No. 511 Gordon Road is the British police station. A theatre is the easiest place for a fish to hide, and the British police station is the very place we need to keep clear of. Now—” Li Qinhong seized Lu A-Zhen by the hair and dragged her before the map of Shanghai. “You tell me the places your husband often went, the places you’ve mentioned before—point them out. Perhaps you may yet save your life!”

> 陆阿贞的手哆哆嗦嗦地指向了恒吉里。
Lu A-Zhen’s trembling hand pointed to Hengji Li.

> 李沁红用红笔把恒吉里圈了起来。
With a red pen Li Qinhong ringed Hengji Li.

> “为什么这么肯定？”
“Why so sure?”

> “他，他前二天告诉我，他这几天都不回来住，他要在恒吉里开会。”
“He—he told me two days ago that he wouldn’t be home to sleep these few days, that he had a meeting to hold at Hengji Li.”

> “他经常去吗？”
“Does he often go there?”

> “是。”
“Yes.”

> “恒吉里多少号？”
“What number Hengji Li?”

> “我不知道。”
“I don’t know.”

> 李沁红用枪顶住了她的前额。“恒吉里多少号？”
Li Qinhong pressed the gun to her forehead. “What number Hengji Li?”

> “我真的不知道！”女人完全崩溃了。
“I really don’t know!” The woman had utterly collapsed.

> “最后一次，恒吉里多少号？”李沁红的手指扣紧扳机，突然女人的裤子湿了，她是真的不知道，李沁红毫不犹豫地开枪了。
“One last time—what number Hengji Li?” Li Qinhong’s finger tightened on the trigger; suddenly the woman wet her trousers—she truly did not know—and Li Qinhong fired without the least hesitation.

> 女人的头裂开，身子仆下去，倒在李沁红的皮鞋尖上，血腥漫开……
The woman’s head split open, her body pitched forward and fell across the toes of Li Qinhong’s leather shoes, the blood spreading wide—

> 屋子里所有的人为之一震。
Every soul in the room started at it.

> “现在是七点一刻。据可靠情报，共党特委今天晚上八点钟将在戈登路恒吉里召开特委预备会。我们就在这里！”她的枪指向地图上红色的圈。“拉网似突袭。力求捕获今天晚上在恒吉里出现的所有共匪。”
“It is now a quarter past seven. According to reliable intelligence, the Communist Special Committee will hold its preparatory meeting tonight at eight o’clock at Hengji Li on Gordon Road. Here is where we go!” Her gun pointed to the red ring on the map. “A dragnet raid. We aim to seize every Communist bandit who shows himself at Hengji Li tonight.”

> 李沁红走到熊自达的身边，和风细雨地说：“处座，下命令吧。”
Li Qinhong walked to Xiong Zida’s side and said, mild as a gentle rain: “Sir, give the order.”

> 接下来，慕次满耳都是熊自达发布命令的声音。
After that, Muci’s ears were full of the sound of Xiong Zida issuing orders.

> “侦缉处全体集合。”
“All the Detective Division, fall in.”

> “请求参谋部部队配合行动。”
“Request the General Staff’s troops to join the operation.”

> “目标：戈登路恒吉里。”
“Objective: Hengji Li on Gordon Road.”

> “要快。快。”
“Be quick. Quick.”

> 眼前的一切发生地太快，楼道上到处是奔跑的脚步声，楼下是汽车发动声。杨慕次完全被动地加入在步履匆匆的人群中。
Everything before his eyes was happening too fast; running feet everywhere in the corridors, engines starting up below. Wholly passive, Yang Muci was swept into the hurrying crowd.

> 侦缉处办公室的挂钟指向：晚上七点二十分。
The hanging clock in the Detective Division office pointed to twenty past seven in the evening.

> 刘副官把吉普车开过来，熊自达坐了上去，杨慕次迅速靠过去，熊自达突然想起了什么。“我的包。”这个公文包里有参谋总部送来得密件，他必须随身带。
Adjutant Liu drove the jeep up; Xiong Zida got in; Yang Muci moved swiftly to join them. Suddenly Xiong Zida remembered something. “My bag.” In that document case were the confidential papers sent over by the General Staff Headquarters; he had to keep them on him.

> “我去拿，处座。”杨慕次说完，迅速地向侦缉处大楼跑去。
“I’ll fetch it, sir.” With that Yang Muci ran swiftly toward the Detective Division building.

> 阿次拿到公文包后，正准备走，办公室电话响了。
Once A-Ci had the document case and was about to leave, the office telephone rang.

> 他愣了一下，习惯性左右看看，接了电话。
He started, glanced left and right out of habit, and took the call.

> 慕次不说话，电话那端先说了。
Muci said nothing; the other end spoke first.

> “我找李沁红组长。”
“I want Section Head Li Qinhong.”

> “她不在。”
“She’s not here.”

> “熊处长呢？”
“And Chief Xiong?”

> “出去了。”
“Gone out.”

> “请您务必转告他们一句话。戈登路恒吉里1141号。”电话挂断了。慕次的脊梁骨透着寒冰，中央特科里也有“内鬼”。
“Be sure to pass one sentence to them. No. 1141 Hengji Li, Gordon Road.” The line went dead. A chill like ice ran down Muci’s spine: inside the Party Central’s Special Branch, too, there was a “mole.”

> 他听见底下车子的喇叭声骤响，他知道，熊自达在催自己。他不再迟疑，飞奔而下。
He heard the sudden blare of the car horn below; he knew Xiong Zida was hurrying him. He hesitated no longer, and flew down.

> 慕次几乎是冲刺的速度跑到熊自达的车前。
Muci reached Xiong Zida’s car almost at a sprint.

> “处座，您的包。”
“Sir, your bag.”

> “阿次，你坐前面的军车吧。”刘副官说。他用眼神暗示，吉普车里已经预留了李沁红的位置。
“A-Ci, you ride in the army truck up front.” said Adjutant Liu. With his eyes he hinted that a place in the jeep had already been kept for Li Qinhong.

> “好的。”慕次说完，朝前面跑去，所有准备出发的军车全部蒙上了黑色的幕布，伪装成民用长途运输卡车。慕次跑到第一辆军车前，坐了上去。把原来得司机赶进了卡车。
“Right.” With that Muci ran forward. All the army trucks made ready to set out had been draped in black cloth, disguised as civilian long-haul transport lorries. Muci ran to the first truck and climbed up, driving the original driver back into the truck bed.

> 发车之前，李沁红照例巡视。她发现了慕次坐在第一辆军车的驾驶室里，她停住了步。
Before the convoy set off, Li Qinhong made her round as usual. She spotted Muci sitting in the cab of the first truck, and stopped in her tracks.

> “高队！”李沁红高声喊着。
“Captain Gao!” Li Qinhong called out loudly.

> “到！”高磊跑过来，脸上堆着笑。
“Here!” Gao Lei ran over, his face wreathed in smiles.

> “你来开第一辆车。”
“You drive the first truck.”

> 慕次很不满，说：“怎么？李组长信不过我开车的技术？”
Muci, much displeased, said: “What? Doesn’t Section Head Li trust my driving?”

> “你说呢？”李沁红在笑。
“You tell me?” Li Qinhong was smiling.

> “我连飞机都能开。”
“I can even fly a plane.”

> “我信。”李沁红点头。她的笑容很快凝成水。“执行命令。”
“I believe you.” Li Qinhong nodded. Her smile quickly froze into water. “Carry out the order.”

> 慕次坐到副驾上，高磊握住了方向盘。
Muci took the passenger seat; Gao Lei gripped the wheel.

> “小心驾驶。”李沁红说。
“Drive carefully.” said Li Qinhong.

> “您放一百个心。”
“Set a hundred hearts at rest.”

> “砰”的一声，李沁红亲手替他们关紧了车门。她左手轻挥：“出发！”车队浩浩荡荡地向戈登路进发。
With a bang Li Qinhong shut their door tight with her own hand. She waved lightly with her left hand: “Move out!” The convoy set off in mighty array toward Gordon Road.

> 晚上，七点四十五分。
Evening, seven forty-five.

> 荣华的车从戈登路66号美琪大戏院开出来，她已经顺利地送走了几名特委。因为不放心的缘故，她始终没有离开戈登路的地段。
Ronghua’s car came out from the Majestic Theatre at No. 66 Gordon Road; she had already got several Special Committee members safely away. Because she could not set her mind at ease, she had never once left the Gordon Road stretch.

> 她想再回恒吉里1141号去看看。
She wanted to go back to No. 1141 Hengji Li and take another look.

> 恒吉里1141号是一幢二楼二底的石库门房子，前后有门，四通八达，只要危险信号一发出，很远的距离都能看见，来开会的特委，亦包括丛锋，只要发觉异常，就会很快离开。
No. 1141 Hengji Li was a shikumen house of two storeys front and back, with doors at front and rear, open in every direction; the moment a danger signal was given it could be seen from a long way off, and the Special Committee members coming to the meeting—Cong Feng among them—would leave quickly the instant they noticed anything amiss.

> 可是，出错了。
But something had gone wrong.

> 荣华看见，二楼晾衣架上晾出的红色床单不见了，取而代之的是蓝色窗帘，这是安全信号，代表一切照常。
Ronghua saw that the red bedsheet hung out on the second-floor drying-rack was gone, and in its place a blue curtain—this was the safe signal, meaning all was as usual.

> 天啊！荣华的血液霎时凝固了！
Heavens! In that instant Ronghua’s blood froze!

> 恒吉里1141号的阁楼上，老保姆平静地躺在床上，她的胸前被人刺了两刀。红色的床单被扔在地上，像血。
In the attic of No. 1141 Hengji Li the old amah lay peacefully on the bed, two knife-thrusts in her breast. The red bedsheet had been flung to the floor, like blood.

> 蓝色窗帘在微风中召唤着聚会的人们，像幽灵。
The blue curtain beckoned the gathering people in the light breeze, like a ghost.

> 慕次坐在军车上，想着荣华一定会安全把情报送出去的，现在，离自己送出情报的时间已经过了两个小时。
Muci sat in the army truck, thinking that Ronghua would surely get the intelligence out safely; by now two hours had passed since the time he sent word out.

> 按常规推断，荣华自己都应该全身而退了。
By ordinary reckoning, Ronghua herself should already have got clean away.

> 可是，半秒钟不到，结论被推翻了，慕次清晰地看见了荣华的汽车。
But in less than half a second the conclusion was overturned: Muci saw Ronghua’s car, clear and plain.

> 荣华看到了慕次的脸，她知道，伪装的军车到了，侦缉处的特务们到了，毁灭性的袭击到了。同样，没有接到临时通知的特委们也到了。
Ronghua saw Muci’s face. She knew: the disguised army trucks had arrived, the Detective Division’s agents had arrived, the annihilating strike had arrived. And so too had the Special Committee members who had not received the last-minute word.

> 七点五十二分。
Seven fifty-two.

> 没有时间了，没有任何回旋余地了。
There was no time left, no room to turn at all.

> 荣华看见了丛锋，他正欣欣然夹了张报纸往前走……
Ronghua saw Cong Feng; he was walking cheerfully forward, a newspaper tucked under his arm—

> 东西南北每一个方向、每一个角落几乎都有来自全国各地的中共特委夹杂在车水马龙的人流中前行……
From every direction—east, south, west, north—and from nearly every corner, Party Special Committee members from all over the country came forward, threading through the moving stream of traffic and people—

> 他们的目的地：戈登路恒吉里1141号。
Their destination: No. 1141 Hengji Li, Gordon Road.

> 荣华没有退路了。
Ronghua had no way back.

> 大约两秒钟时间，荣华做出了她人生最后的抉择。
In about two seconds Ronghua made the last choice of her life.

> 她神情坚毅，稳坐如山，猛踩油门，加速、加速、再加速，她的车狂飙飞驰，横冲直撞地朝即将进入恒吉里路口的隐蔽军车扑来。
Her face was resolute, she sat as steady as a mountain, she stamped down hard on the accelerator—faster, faster, faster still—her car tore forward in a wild rush, careering headlong at the hidden army truck just as it was about to enter the mouth of Hengji Li.

> 阿次懂了。
A-Ci understood.

> 自己的战友在用“生命”向通往“陷阱”的同志们预警。
His own comrade-in-arms was using her “life” to warn the comrades on the road to the “trap.”

> 荣华在危急关头选择了“死亡”。
At the crisis Ronghua had chosen “death.”

> 只有在通往恒吉里的路上，制造一场严重的车祸，引起交通极度混乱，让隐藏在卡车上的特务连全部暴露。让租界的军警、巡警全都搅和进来，堵住恒吉里的交通要道，才有可能截断前往恒吉里1141号开会的同志们，使他们趁乱逃生。
Only by causing a serious traffic accident on the road to Hengji Li, throwing the traffic into utter confusion, exposing the whole company of agents hidden on the trucks; only by dragging in all the Settlement’s soldiers and police and patrolmen, blocking the vital artery to Hengji Li—only so was there any chance of cutting off the comrades on their way to the meeting at No. 1141 Hengji Li, and letting them escape with their lives in the chaos.

> 荣华的车速疾若飞鹰，迅如闪电。
Ronghua’s car sped swift as a soaring hawk, fast as a lightning-flash.

> 高磊措手不及，一边大甩方向盘，一边恐慌地高声咒骂。“神经病！”“疯子！”“疯了！”他在闪避的同时，自然而然的把最危险的撞击甩给了副驾的位子。
Caught wholly off guard, Gao Lei hauled the wheel hard over, cursing aloud in panic. “Madwoman!” “Lunatic!” “She’s insane!” As he swerved to dodge, he naturally flung the most dangerous impact onto the passenger seat.

> 慕次在第一时间内接受了荣华的选择。同生共死的瞬间，卡车和小汽车相撞的刹那，他们从玻璃车窗里都清晰地看到了彼此的脸上的表情，荣华神态从容、镇定、眼睛里透着永生不灭的大无畏精神、裹挟着义无反顾的豪迈、撼动人心的刚毅抉择……呼啸而来！
Muci, in that first instant, accepted Ronghua’s choice. In the moment of living and dying together, in the instant the truck and the little car collided, through the glass of the windows they each saw clearly the look on the other’s face: Ronghua’s bearing calm, composed, her eyes shining with an undying, dauntless spirit, wrapped in a heroism that would not turn back, a heart-shaking, iron resolve—roaring down upon him!

> 他们都没有眨一下眼，彼此含着一丝笑容，迎接那悲壮的瞬间。
Neither of them blinked an eye; each with the trace of a smile, they met that solemn, valiant instant.

> 血火迸溅！
Blood and fire burst and scattered!
