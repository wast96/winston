### The Cold Stalls

In the City God Temple there are several places where old books are laid out for sale on stalls called "cold stalls." The books on them are set out any which way, all in a jumble and a muddle; among the kinds there are old books, new books, magazines, novels, and rubbings from stone inscriptions, some tattered and incomplete, some in full sets with nothing missing, and most of them come from having been bought up. The price of a book runs from a few copper coins up to a few jiao or a few dollars, all by haggling. Now and then, if one is lucky, a rare unique copy, a precious edition, or a woodblock print from some family's collection lies buried in the heap, waiting for a knowing buyer to come, and the price is not so very dear; whereas over such a thing as a complete set of new books they will haggle down to the last bit and hold out for a good price—all because these book-hawkers have but a limited knowledge of their trade.
