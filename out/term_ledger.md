# Term ledger

Every proper name, institution, place and term of art in this edition, with
the Chinese as printed, a pinyin reading, the English rendering used, and how
far that rendering can be checked. **This table is designed to be audited by
someone who reads no Chinese**: the status column tells you which renderings
rest on outside authority and which are mine.

One rendering per referent, across the whole edition. Where the book prints a
name inconsistently, the note says so and the ledger gives the form used.

## How to read the status column

- **attested** — Form used in English-language scholarship or standard reference works; checkable.
- **provisional** — My romanization. NOT found in outside sources — the referent may be unverifiable, or may exist under a form I could not locate.
- **decided** — A project style decision, not an external attestation. Chosen for consistency and explained in the note.
