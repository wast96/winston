### Selling Sutras

Up and down the lanes one often meets a man walking along with a little bundle in one hand, calling out: "Will you have a Gaowang Sutra? Will you have a Diamond Sutra? Will you have a Great Compassion Mantra? Will you have a Heart Sutra?" This line of business is the selling of sutras. They all give themselves out for disciples of the Buddha, keeping a lifelong fast and never knowing the taste of meat; and their little bundles are stuffed full of a sheaf of yellow papers, dabbed all over with little circles in vermilion ink. Ten red circles, say, are reckoned as ten scrolls of sutra-penance. But whether they have truly chanted the scrolls through, one by one, or have merely dabbed the marks on at random—of that there is no way of checking; only the Buddha's disciples themselves can know.
