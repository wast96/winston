## H2 Chapter 10. By Error the Flower of One Heart Is Cut

> 绯红的晨霞在晴朗的天空底绽放，雨后的庭院里是一片翠润的草地，同济医院宽阔的走廊上，站着一些等待医生的病患者，他们短暂的呻吟和叹息，混合着早晨的阳光，组成一组组反差极大的画面。
The scarlet glow of dawn spread across a clear sky; after the rain the courtyard was a stretch of fresh green turf. Along the broad corridor of the Tongji Hospital stood a number of patients waiting for the doctor, and their brief moans and sighs, mingled with the morning sunlight, made up scene upon scene of the starkest contrast.

> 健康与疾病，生命与阳光。
Health and disease, life and sunlight.

> 和雅淑就是处在一种及其混乱的情绪中，来到医院复诊的。
It was in a state of the utmost inner confusion that He Yashu came to the hospital for her follow-up examination.

> 她平躺在检查室的床上，不停地调整自己的呼吸。阿初轻轻移动听诊器，温和地说：“您放松，没事的。……您的身体恢复得很好，恭喜你和小姐，我想，再过一阵子，您可以打篮球了。”
She lay flat on the bed in the examination room, adjusting her breathing again and again. A-Chu moved the stethoscope gently and said in a mild voice, “Relax; it’s nothing. …You’ve recovered very well. Congratulations, Miss He; I think that in a little while you’ll be able to play basketball.”

> 和雅淑坐起来得瞬间，她看见阿初谦逊地微笑。
The instant He Yashu sat up, she saw A-Chu smiling modestly.

> “我全好了吗？初医生？”
“Am I completely well now, Dr. Chu?”

> “没大碍了。不过，现在的天气正是'乍暖还寒，最难将息'的时候，您体质弱，要注意养生。您住的房间要保持室内通风，中午可以多晒晒太阳，夜间适度保温。”
“Nothing serious remains. But the weather just now is exactly the season that is ‘warm one moment, cold the next, and hardest of all to nurse oneself through’; your constitution is weak, and you must mind your health. Keep the room you live in well aired; get plenty of sun at midday, and keep moderately warm at night.”

> 雅淑问：“还开药吗？”
Yashu asked, “Will you still prescribe medicine?”

> “我替您开了些温补的药，您在这等我一下。我去替您把药拿了。”阿初把处方整整齐齐地撕下来。
“I’ve written you some warming, restorative medicine. Wait here for me a moment; I’ll go and fetch it for you.” A-Chu tore off the prescription slip, neat and straight.

> “那怎么好意思，每次都麻烦您。”雅淑低着头说。
“How can I presume—troubling you every time.” Yashu said it with her head bowed.

> “您跟我客气什么？”阿初笑着走了。
“Why stand on courtesy with me?” A-Chu smiled and went off.

> 阿初对雅淑特别尊重和客气，那是因为他知道荣升救了雅淑，并且，荣升最近行踪神秘，也许，就跟眼前这位和小姐有关，她到同济医院来看病，一定是荣升极力推荐的。说不准，那天这位落难“公主”摇身一变，成为荣家新大少奶奶。
A-Chu was especially respectful and courteous to Yashu, and that was because he knew Rong Sheng had saved her; moreover, Rong Sheng’s movements had lately been mysterious, and perhaps it had to do with this very Miss He before him. That she came to the Tongji Hospital for treatment must have been at Rong Sheng’s strong urging. It might well be that one day this fallen “princess” would, with a turn, transform into the Rong family’s new eldest young mistress.

> 和雅淑可不这么想，她认为初医生心里一定爱慕自己，不然，为什么她每次来看病，他都格外用心呢？
He Yashu, however, did not think so. She believed that in his heart Dr. Chu must surely admire her; else why should he be so especially attentive each time she came for treatment?

> “爱情”的种子在苦难的泥潭里浸泡的太久了，很难冲破沼泽，再次萌芽。就算是外力所助，让爱复活，强行挣扎突破冻土的嫩芽，也带着畸形的媚态，蕴涵着无奈的苦涩，在微风中展露出一线生机。
The seed of “love” had soaked too long in the mire of suffering; hard it was for it to break through the marsh and sprout again. Even if some outside force revived that love, the tender shoot that forced and struggled its way through the frozen soil bore a deformed coquetry, held within it a helpless bitterness, and unfurled a single thread of life in the light breeze.

> 和雅淑日渐麻木的心灵，早已感觉不到爱的甜蜜和痛楚了。她在学堂里原有个要好的男朋友，交往了两年后，那个负心人居然跟自己同寝室最要好女生结婚了。临走，也没忘了拿走她积攒很久的私房钱。她的姐姐在一个月黑风高的夜晚，跟一个男人私奔了。后来，给她寄来一封信，说：不久就回家来，接她一起走。她永远都铭记信的末尾写着：未来得日子里，我们可以自由而幸福的生活在一起，希望雅淑你和我一样，企盼光明得到来。
He Yashu’s heart, growing number by the day, had long since ceased to feel love’s sweetness or its pain. At school she had once had a dear sweetheart; after they had been together two years, that faithless man had actually married the girl she was closest to in her own dormitory. On leaving, he had not forgotten to take away the private savings she had scraped together over so long. Her elder sister, on a night of black moon and high wind, had eloped with a man. Later she sent Yashu a letter, saying she would come home before long and take her away with her. Yashu remembered forever what was written at the letter’s end: In the days to come, we can live together, free and happy; I hope that you, Yashu, like me, will look forward to the coming of the light.

> 可是，她没有等到一丝一毫的光明，她认为，最亲的姐姐选择抛弃了她。
But she never waited out one jot or tittle of light; she believed her dearest sister had chosen to abandon her.

> 和雅淑其实是一个爱走极端的人。在她的世界里，所有的人只分成两类，一类是“爱”她的人，一类是“害”她的人。
He Yashu was in fact a person given to extremes. In her world all people fell into but two kinds: those who “loved” her, and those who “harmed” her.

> 她对自己婚姻的前景始终有着朦胧的担忧。“情投意合”的人无情地欺骗了她纯真的感情；“父母做主，媒妁之言”的汤大少，是个烟鬼加流氓；“邂逅相遇”的荣升，虽然关怀体贴，诸事周到，但终究也是一个曾经吸食鸦片的神经质，心理和生理也许都不健康。将来如果有缘结成夫妻，不知道婚姻幸福到底能维持多久？何况，荣升心里始终都有前妻的影子存在，这种挥之不去的阴霾，本身就是婚姻幸福的“定时炸弹”，对自己的情感也是及其不公平的。和雅淑实在不想得到一个循环往复“悲剧婚姻”的结果。
She had always felt a dim dread about the prospects of her own marriage. The man she had been of “one mind and one heart” with had heartlessly deceived her innocent affections; the eldest Tang, offered by “parents’ decision and matchmaker’s word,” was an opium fiend and a rogue besides; and Rong Sheng, met by “chance encounter,” though caring and considerate, thorough in all things, was after all a neurotic who had once smoked opium, perhaps unsound in mind and body alike. If in time they were fated to become man and wife, who knew how long the happiness of the marriage could last? What was more, in Rong Sheng’s heart the shadow of his former wife lingered always, and that unshakable gloom was in itself a “time bomb” set beneath marital happiness, and most unfair to her own feelings besides. He Yashu truly had no wish to end up with a “tragic marriage” that came round and round again.

> 她认识阿初医生以后，她感觉自己在感情上有了新的收获。
After she came to know Dr. A-Chu, she felt she had made a new gain in matters of the heart.

> 阿初是个留学生，医学博士。他和蔼可亲，正直，有同情心。最关键的是：他健康。而且，阿初对自己格外关心照顾，每次看病开方，他都替自己排队、拿药，他殷勤体贴的笑容远远超出了医生对病人的关爱。这是为什么？或许他悄悄爱上了自己？和雅淑反复的想这个萦绕在脑海里很久的问题。
A-Chu was a returned student, a doctor of medicine. He was gentle and amiable, upright, full of sympathy. And the crucial thing was: he was healthy. Moreover, A-Chu was especially caring and attentive to her; each time she came for a consultation and prescription, he would queue and fetch the medicine for her, and his warm, solicitous smile went far beyond a doctor’s care for a patient. Why was this? Could it be he had quietly fallen in love with her? He Yashu turned over and over this question that had haunted her mind so long.

> 于是，她也刻意多去医院走动，常常“无意”的在医院的走廊上遇见他。
And so she took deliberate care to come often to the hospital, frequently meeting him “by chance” in its corridors.

> 她开始欣赏他纯净的脸庞和圣洁的笑容，属于她的，独特的温馨问候。她为此陶醉，难以自拔。
She began to delight in his pure face and holy smile, and in the warm, singular greeting that was hers alone. She was intoxicated by it, unable to pull herself free.

> 可是，她现在又不愿意冒冒失失的跟荣升摊牌，结束这段“奇遇”。如果，她理想中的阿初不能走进她的现实生活，所有“爱”的感觉，都来自幻想，那么，她是不会放弃荣升这棵参天大树的。
Yet she was unwilling now to rashly lay her cards on the table with Rong Sheng and end this “strange romance.” If the A-Chu of her ideal could not step into her real life, if all her feelings of “love” sprang from fancy, then she would not give up Rong Sheng, that sky-high tree.

> “婚姻”比“爱情”更重要。一个女人，无论她的智慧有多高，无论她的容貌有多美，一旦在婚姻的选择上“脚踏两只船”，她就会变得疑神疑鬼、患得患失、难以取舍、甚至寝食不安。
“Marriage” mattered more than “love.” A woman, however high her wisdom, however great her beauty, once she set “a foot in two boats” in the choosing of a marriage, would grow suspicious and fretful, anxious over loss and gain, unable to choose between them, restless even in her sleep and food.

> 和雅淑清楚的知道，如果自己踩踏的两船平行平速，那么，她可以从容选择收哪一只脚；可是，如果两只船在风急浪险的时候突然分道扬镳，那么，自己很可能失足落水，跌入万丈深潭。
He Yashu knew clearly that if the two boats she straddled ran parallel and at the same speed, she could choose at her leisure which foot to draw in; but if, in a moment of fierce wind and perilous waves, the two boats should suddenly part ways, then she might well lose her footing and fall into the water, plunging into a bottomless abyss.

> 自己现在所得到的、所拥有的全部被“牺牲”掉，而且，永远失去复活的“机会”，那就太不划算了。
To have all she had now gained, all she now possessed, “sacrificed,” and to lose forever the “chance” of its revival—that would be too poor a bargain.

> 就在她胡思乱想的时候，阿初拿了药进来了。他不厌其烦地讲述煎药的方法，处处替雅淑着想。而雅淑此刻根本听不见他在讲什么，她的眼睛里闪烁着另一种暧昧的光芒。
Just as she was lost in these wild fancies, A-Chu came in with the medicine. He explained, with untiring patience, how to decoct it, thinking of Yashu’s good at every turn. But at that moment Yashu heard not a word of what he said; in her eyes there glimmered another, ambiguous light.

> “您自己叫车来得吗？”阿初问。
“Did you call the rickshaw here yourself?” A-Chu asked.

> “是的。”
“Yes.”

> “你家住在？”
“And where do you live?”

> “祥和里。”
“Xianghe Lane.”

> “那您回府的时候，叫黄包车不要穿小弄堂。昨天晚上下雨，路上积了不少水，怕车轮打滑。您叫他走洋灰马路，保险。”阿初的形像光一般耀眼，水一样清澈。和雅淑的心为此狂跳不止，她真真切切的感受到什么是美的感官享受。
“Then when you go home, tell the rickshaw man not to cut through the small lanes. It rained last night, and a good deal of water has pooled on the roads; I’m afraid the wheels might skid. Have him take the cement road—that’s the safe way.” A-Chu’s image was dazzling as light, clear as water. He Yashu’s heart pounded wildly at it, and she felt, truly and keenly, what a sensual delight beauty could be.

> 而阿初对此一无所知。
And of all this A-Chu knew nothing at all.

> 和雅淑感觉自己一会儿在火里、一会儿在水里。欲念越来越清晰，心里就越来越焦灼，离开诊室的脚步也因此缓慢而犹疑起来。
He Yashu felt herself now in fire, now in water. The clearer her longing grew, the more her heart burned, and her steps in leaving the consulting room turned slow and hesitant with it.

> “您还有什么吩咐吗？”
“Is there anything else you wish?”

> “你能替我叫辆车吗？”她怯生生试探了一句。话一出口，她就后悔了，不该这样讲。万一，他拒绝呢？他一定拒绝的，这个要求确实过分了。
“Could you call a rickshaw for me?” she ventured timidly. The words no sooner out than she regretted them—she should not have spoken so. What if he refused? He was sure to refuse; the request really was too much.

> 她没想到，阿初只是很短的愣了一下，随即脱下白大褂，挂在衣服架子上，说：“没问题，您稍待。”
To her surprise, A-Chu only paused a very short moment, then took off his white coat, hung it on the clothes rack, and said, “No trouble at all; wait just a moment.”

> 阿初出去叫黄包车了。
A-Chu went out to call a rickshaw.

> 和雅淑自己都弄不清楚自己要做什么，她神使鬼差的将自己的玉镯抹下来，留在了阿初白大褂的衣兜里。
He Yashu could not herself make out what she was about; as if led by some spirit or devil, she slipped off her own jade bracelet and left it in the pocket of A-Chu’s white coat.

> 她猜测阿初看见自己留下的玉镯，一定会欣喜若狂。
She guessed that when A-Chu saw the jade bracelet she had left, he would surely be wild with joy.

> 在医院门口，阿初送走了和小姐。他走回诊室过道的时候，有护士小姐冲他做鬼脸。
At the hospital gate A-Chu saw Miss He off. As he walked back along the corridor to his consulting room, a young nurse made a face at him.

> “我关心病人，有错吗？”阿初说。
“Is it wrong of me to care for a patient?” said A-Chu.

> “那你怎么不关心关心我呢？我昨天就重感冒了。”护士小姐端着医用瓷盘从他身边走过去。
“Then why don’t you care a little for me? I’ve had a bad cold since yesterday.” The young nurse walked past him carrying a medical porcelain tray.

> 阿初走进自己的诊室，穿上医生的白大褂，无意中摸到一只玉镯。
A-Chu went into his consulting room, put on his doctor’s white coat, and, unawares, felt a jade bracelet.

> 他记得，这是和雅淑手上常戴的装饰物件。
He remembered it: the ornament He Yashu often wore on her wrist.

> 她想干什么？
What was she after?

> 阿初微微叹息了一声，心想：人虽然纤尘不染，然而这只碧绿纯色的镯子却轻佻的代表了人心的挑逗意味。
A-Chu gave a faint sigh, thinking: the person may be spotless as unstained dust, yet this bracelet of pure emerald green frivolously bespoke the teasing intent of a human heart.

> 很快，荣升在“墨菊斋”里发现了这只镯子。
Soon Rong Sheng discovered the bracelet in the Moju Studio.

> 镯子放在书桌上最显眼的红绒布里，绿得华丽而优美，像它的主人。
The bracelet lay in the most conspicuous spot on the writing desk, on a piece of red velvet, green with a rich and graceful beauty—like its owner.

> 可是这只镯子，怎么会在这里出现呢？荣升想不明白。一时间，纸墨昏淡，脑海里呈现出“袅娜多情春尽”的无聊句子。
But how had this bracelet come to appear here? Rong Sheng could not make it out. For a moment the paper and ink before him dimmed, and into his mind rose the idle line, “slender and full of feeling, and the spring all spent.”

> 他看看时钟，今天正好约了和雅淑到“法国公园”去喝下午茶，该走了。他把玉镯揣进兜里，从“墨菊斋”出来，沿着回廊到“梨云阁”去。
He glanced at the clock: today he had arranged to take He Yashu to the “French Park” for afternoon tea, and it was time to go. He tucked the jade bracelet into his pocket, came out of the Moju Studio, and went along the covered walk toward the Liyun Pavilion.

> 白云漾空，绿荫如幄。荣升还没走到“梨云阁”的院门，就听见里面一片欢声笑语。玻璃窗户上倩影频闪，绛红娇紫，暗香浮动。小丫鬟云儿身靠着院门，眼睛瞅着院子里掩着嘴笑。荣升走过来问：“里面做什么？大太太出门了，你们就造反啊？”
White clouds rippled across the sky, and the green shade hung like a canopy. Before Rong Sheng had even reached the courtyard gate of the Liyun Pavilion, he heard within a burst of merry talk and laughter. Fair figures flickered against the glass windows, crimson-red and tender purple, with a hidden fragrance adrift. A little maid, Yun’er, leaning against the courtyard gate, was watching the courtyard and smiling behind her hand. Rong Sheng came up and asked, “What’s going on inside? First Madam’s gone out, and so you all rise in revolt?”

> 云儿笑着说：“今天丽水表小姐约了男朋友见面，她给未来得表姑爷买了几条领带，叫阿初少爷帮他选呢。”
Yun’er said with a smile, “Today Cousin Lishui has arranged to meet her sweetheart; she’s bought a few neckties for the future cousin-in-law, and has asked young Master A-Chu to help her choose them.”

> “选领带罢了，哪值得你们这么开心？”
“Just choosing neckties—what’s in that to make you all so merry?”

> “不是啊。表小姐不会打领带，叫阿初少爷教她，结果，院子里的姐姐们都来凑热闹，跟着学。”
“That’s not it. Cousin Lishui doesn’t know how to tie a necktie, so she asked young Master A-Chu to teach her, and then all the elder sisters in the courtyard came to join the fun and learn along with her.”

> 荣升抬眼望去，丫鬟们众星捧月似的围着阿初，听他妙语高论，看他捷手灵活地在丽水脖子上系领带。不时由于阿初的幽默解释，而引起莺欢燕笑，场面异常香浓花艳。
Rong Sheng raised his eyes and looked: the maids were gathered around A-Chu like a host of stars cupping the moon, listening to his witty talk and watching his quick, nimble hands tie a necktie about Lishui’s neck. Now and again A-Chu’s humorous explanations set off a warbling of oriole-glee and swallow-laughter, and the whole scene was rich with fragrance and gay with bloom.

> “选领带呢，最好是真丝的。真丝的色彩光泽，色调柔和，手感细腻。仿真丝的就差点。色彩发亮，色调刺眼，手感挺刮的。”阿初一边说，一边拿起一条银灰色领带。“这条就很好。表面光洁，花色清晰。拼接处的花纹也很一致。”他把丽水的高领子竖起来，亲手给丽水示范打领带。“如果表姑爷穿黑色西服，你就给他配这种银灰色，或者蓝色，显得庄重大方，优雅内敛。”
“For choosing a necktie, real silk is best. Real silk has a lustrous color, a soft tone, a fine feel to the hand. Imitation silk is a shade poorer—the color is glaring, the tone harsh to the eye, the feel stiff.” As A-Chu spoke, he picked up a silver-gray necktie. “This one is very good. The surface is smooth and clean, the pattern distinct, and the design matches well even at the seams.” He turned up Lishui’s high collar and, with his own hands, showed her how to tie a necktie. “If the cousin-in-law wears a black Western suit, then pair it with this silver-gray, or with blue—it looks dignified and generous, elegant and restrained.”

> “如果表姑爷穿白色西服呢？”杏儿问。
“And if the cousin-in-law wears a white Western suit?” Xing’er asked.

> “那就配虹色或褐色的领带，彬彬有礼，光彩夺目。”
“Then pair it with an iridescent or a brown necktie—courteous and refined, and dazzling to behold.”

> “米色西服呢？”红儿问。
“And a beige Western suit?” Hong’er asked.

> “配海蓝色。”阿初打了一个漂亮的“温莎结”。“怎么样？”
“Pair it with sea-blue.” A-Chu tied a handsome “Windsor knot.” “How’s that?”

> “好看。”丫鬟们捧场。
“Lovely.” The maids gave him their applause.

> “这个结和刚才打的那个结不一样。”丽水说。
“This knot isn’t the same as the one you tied just now,” said Lishui.

> “当然不一样，刚才打的是'浪漫结'，现在打的是'温莎结'。”
“Of course it’s different. Just now I tied a ‘romantic knot’; now I’ve tied a ‘Windsor knot.’”

> “什么是'温莎结'？”有人问。
“What is a ‘Windsor knot’?” someone asked.

> “这种结形比较宽，最适合这种浪漫柔雅的真丝领带搭配。”阿初耐心地解答。“你们知道温莎公爵的爱情故事吗？”
“This kind of knot is fairly wide, and best suits a romantic, soft, and elegant silk tie like this.” A-Chu answered patiently. “Do you know the love story of the Duke of Windsor?”

> “不知道。”丫鬟们异口同声地答。
“No,” the maids answered with one voice.

> “长话短说。在国外，有一位王子，他爱上了一个平民。懂吗？”
“To make a long story short. Abroad, there was a prince who fell in love with a commoner. Understand?”

> “就是少爷爱上了丫鬟呗。”杏儿说。
“That is, a young master falling for a maidservant.” Xing’er said.

> “对。但是，如果王子娶了'灰姑娘'以后，就必须放弃王位继承权。他为了自己的爱情，放弃了江山。”
“Just so. But once the prince married his ‘Cinderella,’ he had to give up his right to succeed to the throne. For the sake of his love, he gave up the realm.”

> “他真伟大。”杏儿的口吻充满了艳羡。
“How great of him.” Xing’er’s tone was full of envy and longing.

> “所以啊，你们要把眼界放开。不要把钦羡的眼光停留在少爷、小姐的身上。就拿丽水表小姐来说，她出身清寒，刻苦自励，勇敢的选择自己所爱……”
“So you see, you must broaden your horizons. Don’t let your admiring eyes rest only on young masters and young ladies. Take Cousin Lishui here: born into poverty, she strove hard and disciplined herself, and bravely chose the one she loves…”

> “你夸我还是损我？”丽水不依了。
“Are you praising me or making fun of me?” Lishui would not have it.

> “我当然是夸赞你了。”阿初说。
“I’m praising you, of course,” said A-Chu.

> 丽水的眼睛瞄见了荣升，她故意问：“我和表哥的那位和小姐比，哪一个更好？”
Lishui’s eyes caught sight of Rong Sheng, and she asked on purpose, “Compared with that Miss He of my cousin’s, which of us is the better?”

> 阿初想也不想地说：“你比她可爱多了。”
Without a moment’s thought A-Chu said, “You’re far more lovable than she is.”

> “真的？”
“Really?”

> “真的。”
“Really.”

> “那为什么，表哥选她不选我？”丽水的问题越来越刁钻。
“Then why did my cousin choose her and not me?” Lishui’s questions grew ever more pointed.

> “那是因为，他失落了许多美好的东西，在漫长的人生旅途中，他选择逃避，逃避跟过去有关的一切美好回忆。他想做一个寂寞的智者，却不防被扭曲的情感误剪了同心，做了个看热闹的庸人。”阿初说。“所以，我们不要随意去涂抹自己的心灵，因为最初每个人的心灵都是美丽的。在这个世界里，地位虽然有悬殊，但是，每个人的情感思想是绝对平等的。以后呢，你们不必叫我阿初少爷，叫我阿初就行了。”
“That’s because he has lost many beautiful things; on the long journey of life he has chosen to flee—to flee from every fair memory bound up with the past. He wished to be a solitary sage, but, off his guard, let a warped affection cut the flower of one heart by mistake, and so became a common man who merely looks on at the spectacle.” A-Chu said. “And so we must not daub over our own hearts as we please, for at the very first every heart is beautiful. In this world, though rank may differ vastly, yet the feelings and thoughts of every person are absolutely equal. From now on, you needn’t call me young Master A-Chu; A-Chu will do.”

> “我们可不敢。”丫鬟们互相推搡着笑。
“We wouldn’t dare.” The maids jostled one another and laughed.

> 丽水怂恿阿初说：“你不是天天把平等、自由挂在嘴边上嘛。为什么不从自己做起呢？你从今天起，直接称呼大少爷的名字，我保证，这些丫鬟们明天就改口叫你阿初。”
Lishui egged A-Chu on: “Don’t you have equality and freedom on your lips every day? Why not begin with yourself? From today you address the eldest Young Master by his given name, and I promise you these maids will change their tune and call you A-Chu tomorrow.”

> “你厉害。”阿初笑起来。“你知道打蛇打七寸。”
“You’re formidable.” A-Chu laughed. “You know to strike the snake at its seven inches.”

> 丫鬟们和丽水都哄笑着让开一条路，阿初看见了荣升。阿初有些不好意思，随意发挥的激情自然而然的烟消云散。
The maids and Lishui all laughed and made way, and A-Chu saw Rong Sheng. A-Chu felt a little abashed, and the freely flowing high spirits of his performance melted of themselves away like mist and smoke.

> “忙着呢？”荣升问。
“Busy, are you?” Rong Sheng asked.

> “闲着呢。”阿初回着少爷的话。
“Idle, rather.” A-Chu answered the young master.

> “今天不用上班吗？”
“No work today?”

> “我轮休。”
“It’s my day off in the rotation.”

> “正好，我要出去，阿福陪太太出去进货了，你来开车。”荣升吩咐完了，回头不经意地扫了一眼丽水，笑起来。“今天表姐很漂亮。”
“Good timing—I’m going out. A-Fu has gone with Madam to lay in goods, so you drive.” His orders given, Rong Sheng glanced back casually at Lishui and smiled. “Cousin looks very pretty today.”

> “是吗？”丽水开心地笑了。“表弟，你不是信口恭维我吧？”
“Is that so?” Lishui laughed happily. “Cousin, you’re not just flattering me off the top of your head?”

> “有点自信心嘛！”荣升说。
“Have a little self-confidence!” said Rong Sheng.

> 荣升一踏出门，阿初就指了指丽水和丫鬟们，说：“回头找你们算账。”大家笑成一团。
The moment Rong Sheng stepped out the door, A-Chu pointed at Lishui and the maids and said, “I’ll settle accounts with you later.” They all dissolved into laughter.

> “回来。”丽水忍着笑，把阿初拉回来，“你没系领带，我送你一条。”一边说，一边亲自动手给阿初打领带，她替阿初打了一个“浪漫结”，为他整理好衣领。
“Come back.” Holding in her laughter, Lishui pulled A-Chu back. “You’ve no necktie on—let me give you one.” As she spoke, she set to with her own hands to tie A-Chu’s necktie, tying him a “romantic knot” and setting his collar straight for him.

> 阿初借着丽水靠近自己时，悄悄地说：“我情愿少爷娶你不娶她。”说完，他就走了。
Taking the chance while Lishui was close to him, A-Chu said softly, “I would rather the young master married you than her.” That said, he went off.

> 一句话说得丽水一天也高兴不起来。
That one remark left Lishui unable to be happy the whole day.

> 法国公园门口，游人熙熙攘攘，因为天气格外好的缘故，所以游客的心情也很好。
At the gate of the French Park the sightseers came and went in bustling crowds; because the weather was especially fine, the visitors’ spirits were fine as well.

> 阿初把车停下，透过车边镜看见和雅淑打着遮阳伞，站在公园门口。阿初明白过来。“怪不得急着催我走，原来佳人有约。”
A-Chu stopped the car and, through the side mirror, saw He Yashu standing at the park gate beneath a parasol. A-Chu understood. “No wonder he was in such a hurry to be off—so it’s a tryst with a fair lady.”

> 荣升笑骂道：“这么多话，滚远点。”
Rong Sheng scolded with a laugh, “What a lot of talk—off with you.”

> 阿初替荣升打开车门，并友好地与和雅淑打招呼。阿初的出现，令和雅淑措手不及，甚至有些狼狈。
A-Chu opened the car door for Rong Sheng and greeted He Yashu warmly. A-Chu’s appearance caught He Yashu off guard, and even threw her into some confusion.

> 阿初问：“什么时候来接你们？”
A-Chu asked, “When shall I come to fetch you?”

> 荣升说：“晚上吧。”
Rong Sheng said, “This evening.”

> “几点？”
“What time?”

> “九点吧。”
“Nine o’clock, say.”

> 阿初开动车子，对和雅淑说：“和小姐，改天我请你喝茶。”
A-Chu started the car and said to He Yashu, “Miss He, another day I’ll treat you to tea.”

> 荣升与雅淑在公园里请专业摄影师拍了两张照片。姿态是由摄影师帮忙设计的，两个人在花丛中笑得很甜美，像新婚不久的夫妇。然后，他们亲亲热热坐在露天花园的茶座里品茶。小餐桌上摆放着细长脖子的玻璃花瓶，花瓶上斜插了一枝红色的玫瑰。
In the park Rong Sheng and Yashu had a professional photographer take two photographs. The poses were arranged with the photographer’s help; the two of them smiled sweetly amid the flowers, like a couple not long wed. Then, close and affectionate, they sat in an open-air garden tea booth and sipped tea. On the little table stood a slender-necked glass vase, in which a single red rose was set aslant.

> 荣升以为自己“恋爱”了。他很久没有这种感觉了，有些不习惯。
Rong Sheng supposed himself “in love.” He had not felt this way in a long time, and was a little unused to it.

> 他第一眼看到雅淑的时候，有些朦胧的冲动，他救了雅淑后，自己的精神世界也仿佛“复活”了，有生气了。他甚至想过跟雅淑闪电结婚，然后另租房子搬出去住，像所有讨生活得夫妻一样，自己早上每天去上班，太太隔着窗子目送自己下楼。住的房子也不大，五、六十平方，要有凉台，上面放一些自己种的花草。有一个属于自己的孩子，孩子最好是个女儿，整天缠着自己，让自己爱她、宠她。每逢周末，一家三口出门旅行，迎着阳光，踏着朝露，和和睦睦的过着属于自己的幸福生活。
When he first set eyes on Yashu, he had felt a dim stirring; after he saved her, his spiritual world too seemed to “come back to life,” to take on vigor. He had even thought of marrying Yashu in a lightning match, then renting another house and moving out to live, like all the couples who make their own way—going off to work each morning, his wife seeing him down the stairs from behind the window. The house need not be large, fifty or sixty square meters, with a balcony where he could set out some flowers and plants of his own growing. A child of his own—best of all a daughter—who would cling to him all day long and let him love and dote on her. Every weekend the three of them would go off traveling, walking to meet the sunlight, treading the morning dew, living in peace and harmony the happy life that was their own.

> 是雅淑救了自己，而不是自己救了她。荣升想。阿初说的对，自己失落了许多美好的东西，他一直都在逃避跟过去有关的一切美好回忆。他跟雅淑在一起，没有任何压力，雅淑不了解他的过去，她在他眼里是一个单纯的女孩。每次他告诉她一些海外奇闻，她都会做出惊奇的表情，并提出一些迷惑不解的问题让自己解答，满足了他作为一个男人的虚荣心。就算是他讲出来最平凡、最无趣的故事，她也会专心聆听，从来没有不耐烦和不愿意。分手的时候，她总是恋恋不舍，主动地上前留给他一个情意缠绵的吻。荣升在她带有暗示性的举动中，看到了美好的未来。既然自己不能做一个寂寞的智者，那么，做一个平凡的庸人也不错。
It was Yashu who had saved him, not he who had saved her. Rong Sheng thought. A-Chu was right: he had lost many beautiful things, and had all along been fleeing every fair memory bound up with the past. With Yashu he felt no pressure of any kind; Yashu knew nothing of his past, and in his eyes she was a simple girl. Each time he told her some marvel from overseas, she would put on a look of astonishment and pose some puzzled question for him to answer, gratifying his vanity as a man. Even when he told the most ordinary, most tedious of stories, she would listen with full attention, never impatient, never unwilling. When they parted she was always loath to let him go, coming forward of her own accord to leave him a lingering, tender kiss. In her suggestive gestures Rong Sheng saw a beautiful future. Since he could not be a solitary sage, then to be an ordinary common man was no bad thing either.

> “你认识初先生？”和雅淑的问话打破了彼此的沉寂。
“You know Mr. Chu?” He Yashu’s question broke the silence between them.

> “不止认识。”
“More than know him.”

> “你跟他很熟？”
“You’re well acquainted with him?”

> “很熟。”
“Very well.”

> “你们很早就认识了？”
“You’ve known each other a long time?”

> “怎么？你不看报吗？”
“Why? Don’t you read the papers?”

> “看报？”雅淑诧异。“他经常上报吗？我从来没有留意过。”
“The papers?” Yashu was surprised. “Is he often in the papers? I’ve never paid it any mind.”

> “有人说，他是我们荣家的'私生子'。”
“Some say he’s the Rong family’s ‘love child.’”

> 雅淑的茶泼了些出来。“不好意思。”她拿出手绢来擦袖口。
Some of Yashu’s tea spilled over. “Excuse me.” She took out a handkerchief to wipe her cuff.

> “阿初是我们家四姨娘的干儿子，二十年前从大街上拣回来得一个孤儿。他从小就跟着我，我父亲爱屋及乌，很喜欢他，让他跟我一样上学堂，他功课好，人品不错。我在英国这几年多亏他事事照顾，我才没有客死他乡。我说真的。”荣升娓娓道来，雅淑的脸色一阵青、一阵红。
“A-Chu is our Fourth Aunt’s foster son, an orphan she picked up off the street twenty years ago. He has been with me since we were small; my father, loving the house for the crow upon its roof, was very fond of him, and had him attend school just as I did. He did well at his lessons, and was of good character. In these years of mine in England, it was thanks to his care in all things that I did not die a stranger in a strange land. I mean it truly.” Rong Sheng recounted it smoothly and warmly, while Yashu’s face turned now pale, now red.

> “新闻杂志，总是捕风捉影，津津乐道别人的隐私。”荣升说。
“The news magazines are forever chasing shadows and catching wind, dwelling with relish on other people’s private affairs,” said Rong Sheng.

> “这样说来，他只是荣家的一个下人？”雅淑问。
“So then, he’s only a servant of the Rong family?” Yashu asked.

> “现在不是了。”
“Not now.”

> “曾经是？”
“He once was?”

> “重要吗？”荣升反问了一句。“时代不同了。他有学识，有能力，他有一定的经济基础，有社会地位。谁会去追究他的身世？英雄莫问出处嘛。你跟我的地位也在变啊，以前女人是没有社会地位的，现在不一样出来做事？女人可以融进男人的社会，男人同样可以成为女人的陪衬。”
“Does it matter?” Rong Sheng countered. “The times are different now. He has learning, he has ability, he has a certain economic footing and a social standing. Who would go prying into his origins? ‘Ask not a hero whence he came.’ Your standing and mine are changing too. In the old days women had no social standing at all; now don’t they go out and work all the same? A woman can merge into the world of men, and a man may just as well become a woman’s foil.”

> “时代没什么不一样。”雅淑的心里有一股酸酸的怪味，阿初的形像就像是黑夜底突然腾空的烟花，绽放以后，就只剩下灰了。
“The times are no different at all.” A sour, strange taste rose in Yashu’s heart; A-Chu’s image was like a firework shot suddenly into the black night, which, once it had bloomed, left nothing behind but ash.

> 和雅淑恨自己的愚蠢，为什么会留下玉镯给一个荣家的下人呢？如果，自己有朝一日做了荣家大少奶奶，这只镯子就是自己给自己种下的心病。
He Yashu hated her own folly. Why had she left the jade bracelet with a servant of the Rong family? If one day she should become the Rong family’s eldest young mistress, this bracelet would be a heartsickness she had planted for herself.

> 怎么办？
What was she to do?

> 她心乱如麻。
Her heart was tangled as raveled hemp.

> 这时，荣升忽然想起了什么，他从怀里取出了一只玉镯，放到雅淑面前。雅淑的脸色变得异常难看，内心的疼痛再度袭来。
Just then Rong Sheng suddenly remembered something; he drew from his breast a jade bracelet and set it before Yashu. Yashu’s face turned extraordinarily ugly, and the pain within her came surging back.

> 是阿初“举发”了自己吗？
Had A-Chu “informed” on her?
