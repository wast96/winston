### The Milk President

In the spring of the ninth year of the Republic — the gengshen year, 1920 — the Qimei Milk-Candy Company, wishing to push the sale of its sweets, got up a flower-election, styled the "Qimei Grand Election of the Fragrant Kingdom." It was held in a corner of the Tianyun Tower, the roof-garden of the Wing On Company; Zhang Tonghua (styled Yiming) was engaged as director of the poll, and Wu Shuzhen's Dianguang Ribao — the Electric Light Daily — was borrowed as the returning office, printing each day the courtesans' photographs and the news of the vote. When the ballots were counted on the sixteenth of March, Qinyu Laoliu was elected President, Leqing and Chendi Vice-Presidents, and Qinlou Premier. After the result was published, the candy company presented Qinyu with a new-style great brass bed and a whole room of teak Western furniture; Leqing and Chendi with a teak Western wardrobe apiece, a dressing-table, and a sofa-couch; and Qinlou too with a dressing-table, a sofa, and the like.

Qinyu had the honor of being chosen President of the flower world, but everyone afterward set her down as the "Milk President" — a rare joke, seeing that this exalted station was the offspring of a milk-candy company. Now, many years on, the resplendent President and Premier have vanished no one knows where, the Dianguang Ribao has long since ceased to print, and Wu Shuzhen and Zhang Tonghua have died one after the other; and to call it to mind by chance is to feel, in spite of oneself, the pang of how the world has changed.
