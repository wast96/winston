### A Corner of the Street of Mystery

North Sichuan Road, that one street, everyone sets down as the Street of Mystery; for the diversions along it are truly many — play, drink, whoring, gambling, everything in its kind, and all with something secret about them — so that "Street of Mystery" it may be called, and none too grandly.

The stretch of Range Road running north is nothing less than a colony of the ×× people, with several Eastern-Ocean salt-meat houses (and Russian ones too). Their premises are cramped and their fittings plain: for the most part they rent a single upper floor, set out two or three wooden beds and a few sticks of furniture, and hire one old serving-woman. The one floor is then divided into two, with no wooden partition between, only a length of colored cloth hung across for a screen.

For their custom they keep a set of go-betweens taken on as need arises to draw the men in — the "harbor-pilot friends," that is — and some use rickshaw pullers for guides. Every afternoon these men go pacing to and fro on the pavement with their furtive, sneaking looks, and when they see a well-dressed man go by they murmur behind him, "Sir... shall I take you to an Eastern-Ocean house, a Russian house, for a bit of fun?" Once it is done they take their commission for reward. As for the price of a turn, there is no fixed rule; it is set by the man, and runs from two dollars up to four or five, while a night in the room is above four and under six.

There is besides a kind of rickshaw puller who turns pimp-broker on the spot: he pulls his rickshaw slowly along the roadside, and if you so much as glance at him, he murmurs low, "Sir... shall I pull you to an Eastern-Ocean house, a Russian house, for a bit of fun? Whatever you please, sir!" Nominally he pulls a rickshaw, but a proper fare is the last thing on his mind; his whole aim is to bring the two sexes together, and once it is done he too takes his commission — the sum most often reckoned on the two-eight, that is, the house takes eight parts and the go-between two.

To go for your fun at a Russian house costs a shade more than at an Eastern-Ocean one — because, I suppose, hanging out the signboard of foreign girls, they cannot but raise the price of their "flesh."
