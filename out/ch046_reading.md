### A Flower Vase

The women clerks who serve in the various offices and companies are looked down on by all as "flower vases." A flower vase, after all, is but a piece of parlor ornament, good to look at and of no practical use; and to liken the woman clerk to a flower vase is only to say that she is there for people's amusement, no more.

Speaking with a level mind, the women clerks who put all their pains into their dress and none into bettering their work — daubing on rouge, painting their brows, splashing on scent, decking themselves in outlandish clothes, and mincing seductively in and out — are indeed not few, and to dub these "flower vases" is no more than they deserve. But where there is a plain, unadorned woman clerk who does her work faithfully, to call her too a "flower vase" — is that not an insult to womankind? Yet great as Shanghai is, and many as its women clerks are, to look among them for one with none of the flower-vase about her is, frankly, very rare indeed!
