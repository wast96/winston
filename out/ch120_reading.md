### Pinching Feet

We who live in the damp-aired region of Shanghai, men and women alike, mostly suffer from the "damp." The commonest form of it is an all-year itching in the clefts between the toes of both feet; the mere itch is no great matter, but a step further and the flesh festers and swells, and then—worse luck—walking grows hard and one cannot get about. So a man with itching toes goes to the bathhouse to bathe, and when he has washed, the waiter sets about kneading his feet for him; and if the damp is truly heavy, one must even have the foot-man scrape the place once over with a little knife before it feels right.

Most bathhouse waiters can knead feet. Just how is this kneading done? The bather lies on the couch and the man sits below on a low stool, and with a dry towel he pinches crosswise and rubs lengthwise in the clefts of the toes, teasing it out as one draws silk from a cocoon—and this is foot-kneading. For a man with a heavy case of the damp, he finishes by pressing on a towel steeped in scalding water, and then it feels fine. Minor art though this is, the skill of it varies greatly, for in the working of it the light and the heavy, the quick and the slow, are suited to the man—graded by how heavy his damp is—and can give you a most delightful sensation. But if an unskilled waiter, not knowing the method of light and heavy, quick and slow, just pinches and rubs at random and calls the job done, then far from getting any pleasure of it you will feel that the itch cannot even be stilled.

People who live in the dry, high country of the northern provinces scarcely ever suffer the damp; but let one of them live a few months on end in Shanghai and his honourable pair of feet will start to itch of their own accord, and by then, when he goes to bathe, unless he has the waiter give them a kneading he feels a hundred discomforts. It is a matter of the local air, and try as one might there is no avoiding it! The massage-houses about town, which used to advertise themselves on "pressing and rubbing," have now, out of the demand for foot-kneading, mostly added foot-kneading as a line as well.
