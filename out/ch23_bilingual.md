## H2 Chapter 23. A Foul Miasma Fills the Sky; Blood and Fire Consume

> 一股浓烟撕裂铁皮般蹿升，小汽车像出鞘的利刃扎进了卡车那坚硬的铁壳。小汽车保险杠支离破碎，车体肢解。
A column of thick smoke shot up as though tearing through sheet iron; the little car drove like a bared blade into the hard iron shell of the truck. The small car's bumper was smashed to fragments, its body dismembered.

> 生命如火似的燃烧。
Life burned like fire.

> 生命的音符随着烈焰升腾后戛然而止。
The notes of a life leapt up with the flames and then broke off short.

> 巨大的冲击力撞击着阿次的身体，阿次的头撞上了挡风玻璃，他的头部、脖子、胸部遭到正面袭击，双腿的膝关节处仿佛断裂般疼痛。血从他的额头漫出……
A vast force of impact struck A-Ci's body; his head slammed against the windscreen, his head, his neck, his chest took the blow full on, and the knee joints of both legs ached as though snapped. Blood ran down over his forehead—

> 他有知觉，但是，动不了。
He had feeling, but he could not move.

> 高磊只受了轻伤，他跳下来大声喊叫。
Gao Lei had only light injuries; he jumped down and shouted at the top of his voice.

> 车上隐蔽的特务全都被撞得七荤八素，纷纷下车。车祸现场一片狼藉。
The agents concealed on the truck had all been jolted senseless, and one after another they climbed down. The scene of the crash was a shambles.

> 丛锋在拥堵的人群里，眼睁睁看到了荣华撞车的一幕，他悲愤地转过身，挤进人群，很快穿进小巷，消失在夜幕中。
Cong Feng, in the packed and jostling crowd, watched with his own eyes the moment of Ronghua's crash. In grief and fury he turned about, pushed into the throng, and soon slipped down a side lane and vanished into the night.

> 无数个特殊身份的人，都默默转过身去，从街角拐进小巷，绵长的石板路上，留下他们斜长的身影和无声的泪水。
Countless people of hidden identity all silently turned and went, cutting from the street corner into the lanes; along the long flagstoned road they left their slanting shadows and their soundless tears.

> 风摇曳着大街上的法国梧桐。
The wind swayed the plane trees along the great street.

> 火燃烧着。
The fire burned.

> 人疏散了。
The people scattered.

> 警笛声，风声，火势，人喧，乱做一团。所有声浪骤然轰响，草木皆腥。
Police whistles, the wind, the blaze, the clamour of the crowd—all one chaos. Every wave of sound roared out at once; the very grass and trees seemed to stink of blood.

> 李沁红大声地斥责高队长，高磊也在发脾气，慕次的身体被卡住了，高磊想尽办法才把他给弄出来，阿次昏迷了。所有侦缉处的车无一例外地被挡在了恒吉里路以外。
Li Qinhong loudly berated Captain Gao, and Gao Lei too was losing his temper. Muci's body was pinned fast, and only by every device did Gao Lei work him free; A-Ci was unconscious. Without exception every Detective Division vehicle had been blocked outside the Hengji Li road.

> 拉网袭击，彻底破产了。
The dragnet raid had utterly collapsed.

> 丛锋穿过小巷后，沿着大街全速奔跑，他的目的地是荣华的“华美书店”，他必须抢在特务前面去焚毁隐藏在那里的电台或是机要文件。
Once through the side lane, Cong Feng ran at full speed along the main street. His destination was Ronghua's "Huamei Bookstore"; he had to reach it ahead of the agents and burn the wireless set or the confidential documents hidden there.

> 恒吉里路口上，李沁红正和闻风赶来得租界巡警交涉，警察局副局长韩正齐也带着人于第一时间赶到事发现场。
At the mouth of the Hengji Li road Li Qinhong was parleying with the Settlement patrolmen who had hurried up at the news, and Han Zhengqi, deputy chief of the police bureau, also arrived at the scene with his men in the first moment.

> 熊自达气哼哼地从吉普车上下来。韩正齐是曾经在一次市政府会议中认识熊自达的，当时他只是配合侦缉处处理一些复杂管区的人口调查，包括租界地段的人口户籍普查。他对熊自达的了解不多，但是，他知道，侦缉处历来有“秘密逮捕”、“秘密枪决”的特权，他们今天如此大规模的卡在恒吉里路上，一定有特殊的原因。所以，韩正齐主动走过来，礼貌地和熊自达握手。
Xiong Zida climbed down from the jeep in a huff. Han Zhengqi had once come to know Xiong Zida at a municipal-government meeting; at the time he had merely been assisting the Detective Division with a population survey of certain complicated districts, including a census of household registration in the Settlement stretches. He knew little of Xiong Zida, but he knew the Detective Division had always held the privileges of "secret arrest" and "secret execution," and that for them to block the Hengji Li road today on so great a scale, there must be some special reason. So Han Zhengqi came over of his own accord and shook Xiong Zida's hand politely.

> “熊处长，你们是不是有秘密任务？”
"Chief Xiong, do you have a secret mission on?"

> “已经不是秘密了。”熊自达一边取手套，一边用手驱赶烟尘。
"It's no longer a secret." Xiong Zida drew off his gloves and waved a hand to drive off the smoke and dust.

> “您需要我们做什么？”韩正齐公事公办地问询他的意见。
"What do you need us to do?" Han Zhengqi asked his opinion in a strictly businesslike way.

> 熊自达没好气地说：“你什么都不做，就帮忙了。”
Sourly Xiong Zida said: "Do nothing at all, and you'll have helped."

> “不，处座。韩副局长来得正是时候。”李沁红跑了过来。“韩副局长，我们需要你立即替我们查出这辆肇事小汽车的来历。在上海拥有汽车的家庭并不多，请您马上配合我们的工作，谢谢。”
"No, sir. Deputy Chief Han has come at just the right moment." Li Qinhong ran over. "Deputy Chief Han, we need you to trace at once the origins of the car that caused this crash. Families that own an automobile in Shanghai are not many; please cooperate with our work right away. Thank you."

> “好的。没问题。”韩正齐说完，立即去办事了。他叫人抄小汽车的牌号，自己亲自打开了乌黑的车门，里面的人已经不复容颜了……
"Very well. No problem." With that Han Zhengqi went off at once to see to it. He had a man copy down the car's plate number, and himself opened the blackened door; the person inside no longer had any face to speak of—

> “是个女人。”他说。“应该很年轻。”
"It's a woman." he said. "She should be quite young."

> 同时，在另一处卡车旁，高磊和刘副官两个人满身大汗地就地抢救人事不醒的慕次。刘副官用力按压慕次腿上的出血创口，他的手帕已经被血浸湿透了，高磊撕了上衣替代绷带，替慕次包扎伤口。
Meanwhile, over by another truck, Gao Lei and Adjutant Liu, both of them bathed in sweat, were giving first aid on the spot to the senseless Muci. Adjutant Liu pressed down hard on the bleeding wound in Muci's leg, his handkerchief already soaked through with blood; Gao Lei tore off his own shirt to serve as a bandage and bound up Muci's wound.

> 李沁红再一次来催促高磊带人去沿街搜捕可疑人员，这一次，高磊不买账了。“你眼瞎了，阿次伤到动脉了。会死的！你到底是不是人啊！”他无所顾忌地高声咒骂。
Once again Li Qinhong came to press Gao Lei to take men and search along the street for suspicious persons; this time Gao Lei would have none of it. "Are you blind? A-Ci's hit an artery. He'll die! Are you even human?" He cursed aloud without a care.

> 李沁红伏下身子来看。果然，杨慕次脸如白纸，呼吸困难，他左腿的伤口处血势凶猛，鲜艳的红色呈喷射状涌出。
Li Qinhong bent down to look. True enough, Yang Muci's face was white as paper, his breathing laboured; the wound in his left leg bled fiercely, bright red spurting out in jets.

> 高磊费力地按压住慕次的股动脉，以免失血过多。“该死！我根本无法松手！”
With an effort Gao Lei kept pressure on Muci's femoral artery, to guard against too much blood lost. "Damn it! I simply cannot let go!"

> “你叫手下来做。”
"Have one of your men do it."

> “你以为谁都会做吗？杨慕次是杜先生的爱徒，他要不明不白的死在我手上，你认为杜先生会放过我吗？”
"You think just anyone can? Yang Muci is Mr. Du's favourite pupil. If he dies unaccountably in my hands, do you think Mr. Du will let me off?"

> “你怎么知道他是杜旅宁的徒弟？阿次告诉你的？”
"How do you know he's Du Luning's pupil? A-Ci told you?"

> “是杜先生打电话告诉我的。要我看着他！”高磊在吼。
"Mr. Du telephoned to tell me. Told me to keep an eye on him!" Gao Lei was shouting.

> “叫担架！”李沁红终于妥协了。“最近的医院在哪里？”
"A stretcher!" Li Qinhong gave way at last. "Where's the nearest hospital?"

> “春和医院，就在附近。”一个小特务说。
"The Chunhe Hospital, right nearby." said one of the little agents.

> “快，送医院。”
"Quick, to the hospital."

> 一辆军车很快掉转头，开过来。高磊、刘副官等人亲自把杨慕次送上车。李沁红见高磊包扎得太狠，怕缺血引起股头坏死，亲自上车给慕次松止血带，两、三分钟松一次，她和高磊换着来。他们两个跟着车走了，刘副官留下来，带了一队人，挨家挨户地盘查可疑人员。
An army truck swiftly turned about and drove up. Gao Lei, Adjutant Liu and the others put Yang Muci aboard themselves. Seeing that Gao Lei had bound the wound too tight, and fearing that the lack of blood would bring on necrosis of the femoral head, Li Qinhong got aboard herself to loosen the tourniquet for Muci, releasing it once every two or three minutes, she and Gao Lei taking turns. The two of them went off with the truck; Adjutant Liu stayed behind, and with a squad of men made a house-to-house check for suspicious persons.

> 韩正齐从另一侧，默默地看着那一边的混乱和焦虑，他看不清伤者的脸。这时，他的一个手下跑来了。
From the other side Han Zhengqi silently watched the confusion and anxiety over there; he could not see the injured man's face clearly. Just then one of his men ran up.

> “报告局长，车子查到了。”
"Reporting, chief—we've traced the car."

> “谁家的？”
"Whose is it?"

> “上海荣家。”
"The Rong family of Shanghai."

> “荣家？”韩正齐愣住了。“车主的姓名？”
"The Rong family?" Han Zhengqi was taken aback. "The owner's name?"

> “荣华。”
"Ronghua."

> “好，我知道了。你去吧。”
"Good, I understand. Off you go."

> “是，局长。”
"Yes, chief."

> “回来。”韩正齐叫住了他。“这件事，不要再告诉第二个人。”
"Come back." Han Zhengqi called him back. "This matter—not a word to anyone else."

> “是。”
"Yes."

> 韩正齐走到自己的汽车前，他来回巡检一遍，悄悄地钻进一个临街的绸缎店，柜台上，有一个显目的红色电话机。
Han Zhengqi walked to his own car, made a round of inspection, then quietly slipped into a silk-goods shop fronting the street; on the counter stood a conspicuous red telephone.

> 晚上八点半左右，正是华灯初上之时，阿初正准备出门去梅花巷。他已经买下那套荒芜的小院，并请人去打扫干净了，野草也除得差不多了。岳嬷嬷提议在院子里挂上红灯笼，喜庆。她说，杨家很久没有什么喜事了，不如借着买新房，放放炮仗。
Around half past eight in the evening, just as the lamps were coming on, A-Chu was preparing to go out to Plum Blossom Lane. He had already bought that ruined little courtyard house and had it cleaned; the weeds, too, were nearly all cleared away. Amah A-Yue had proposed hanging red lanterns in the courtyard for the festive look. She said the Yang family had had no happy event in a long while; better to seize on the buying of the new house to set off some firecrackers.

> 阿初知道岳嬷嬷误会了，她以为自己有了女人了。阿初不解释，他喜欢保持沉默。
A-Chu knew Amah A-Yue had it wrong: she thought he had a woman now. A-Chu did not explain; he liked to keep silent.

> 阿初的贴身保镖刘阿四进来，告诉阿初的车子已经准备好了。就在阿初出门之刻，客厅里电话铃声大作。
A-Chu's personal bodyguard, Liu A-Si, came in to tell him the car was ready. Just as A-Chu was going out, the telephone in the sitting room rang shrilly.

> “喂。”阿初接电话。
"Hello." A-Chu answered.

> 电话那边是夏跃春的喘息声：“阿初，你弟弟出事了。”
On the other end came Xia Yuechun's panting breath: "A-Chu, your brother's had an accident."

> 阿初心里猛地“咯噔”一下。稳住神，问：“什么事？”
A-Chu's heart gave a sudden lurch. Steadying himself, he asked: "What's happened?"

> “车祸。”
"A road accident."

> 阿初的手不自觉地抖了一下。凝住气，再问：“人还在吗？”
A-Chu's hand shook involuntarily. Holding his breath, he asked again: "Is he still with us?"

> “在。”
"Yes."

> 阿初心口顺了一口气。“在哪里？”
A-Chu let a breath out of his chest. "Where?"

> “我的医院里。”
"In my hospital."

> “清醒吗？”
"Conscious?"

> “昏迷。”
"Unconscious."

> “有没有生命危险？”
"Is his life in danger?"

> “有。”跃春又补充了一句。“很危险。”
"Yes." Yuechun added a further word. "Very much."

> “出血吗？”
"Bleeding?"

> “大出血。”
"Heavy bleeding."

> “静脉还是动脉。”
"Vein or artery?"

> “动脉。”
"Artery."

> “血压？”
"Blood pressure?"

> “测不到。”
"Can't get a reading."

> “他需要血浆，你知道，他和你一样是Rh阴性A型血。我已经把你预存的血浆全部取出来了，先给他用。估计不够，你必须来。”
"He needs plasma. You know he's Rh-negative type A, like you. I've already drawn out all the plasma you had in store and started him on it. It won't be enough, by my estimate—you must come."

> Rh阴性A型血，是稀有血型，在国内的比例是：千分之三。阿初是学医的，他知道自己的血液属于稀有类，所以，为了预防万一，他在夏跃春的血库里，预存了自己的血浆，没想到救了慕次。
Rh-negative type A is a rare blood type; the proportion of it in the country is three in a thousand. A-Chu had studied medicine, and knew his own blood belonged to a rare class, so as a precaution he had kept a store of his own plasma in Xia Yuechun's blood bank—never dreaming it would save Muci.

> “我马上来。”阿初刚挂电话，电话铃声再次响起。阿初拿起电话，说：“我马上到。”
"I'll come at once." A-Chu had barely hung up when the telephone rang again. He picked it up and said: "I'm on my way."

> “先生！是我。”电话里传来韩正齐的声音。
"Sir! It's me." Han Zhengqi's voice came over the line.

> “有事吗？”阿初隐隐约约地感觉到要出大事，或者说，已经出了大事了。
"What is it?" A-Chu had a faint, obscure sense that something big was about to happen—or rather, that something big had already happened.

> “荣家二小姐没了。”
"The Rong family's second young lady is gone."

> “没了？怎么死的？”
"Gone? How did she die?"

> “车祸。”
"A road accident."

> 阿初下意识地一跺脚。
A-Chu stamped his foot without thinking.

> “荣二小姐开车撞了警备司令部侦缉处的军车，现在，他们正催着我查这辆车的主人是谁。我正在拖延时间……”
"The second Miss Rong drove her car into a Garrison Command Detective Division army truck; now they're pressing me to find out who owns the car. I'm dragging out the time—"

> “做得好！”阿初说。“半个小时后，你告诉他们答案。”
"Well done!" said A-Chu. "In half an hour, give them the answer."

> “好的，先生。”
"Yes, sir."

> “还有，韩禹在家吗？”
"One more thing—is Han Yu at home?"

> “在。”
"He is."

> “你马上给他打电话，叫他立即到春和医院，我需要他帮忙。”没等回答，阿初就挂了电话。一边疾步如飞，一边吩咐刘阿四。“带上几个弟兄，马上到'华美书店'，二十分钟内，烧掉那家书店。”
"Telephone him at once, tell him to go straight to the Chunhe Hospital; I need his help." Without waiting for an answer A-Chu hung up. Striding along fast as flying, he gave orders to Liu A-Si. "Take a few of the brothers and go to the 'Huamei Bookstore' at once. Within twenty minutes, burn that bookstore down."

> “烧？”刘阿四愕然。
"Burn it?" Liu A-Si was startled.

> “烧！”阿初全速行进，走到车门处，说：“全烧掉，要烧得一干二净。才能保证二先生绝对安全。告诉陆良晨立即找到少爷，送少爷到春和医院，越快越好。”
"Burn it!" A-Chu was moving at full speed. Reaching the car door, he said: "Burn it all, burn it clean to nothing. That's the only way to keep the second young master absolutely safe. Tell Lu Liangchen to find the young master at once and bring him to the Chunhe Hospital, the faster the better."

> 阿初坐上车，急驰而去。
A-Chu got into the car and sped away.

> 春和医院。手术室的走廊上，散坐着高磊和几个侦缉处的小特务，他们窃窃私语，大约在讨论撞车事件的蹊跷。李沁红抽着烟，想着前前后后发生的事。
The Chunhe Hospital. In the corridor outside the operating theatre sat Gao Lei and several of the Division's little agents, whispering among themselves, probably discussing the strangeness of the crash. Li Qinhong smoked, turning over in her mind all that had happened, first and last.

> 阿初到了。他行色匆匆、满脸乌云。
A-Chu arrived. He came in a rush, his whole face like a black cloud.

> 高磊吓了一跳，他不自觉地转过身子，看手术室的灯，灯光依然明亮，说明手术还在进行。
Gao Lei jumped in fright and, without meaning to, turned round to look at the light over the operating theatre; the lamp still shone bright, which meant the operation was still going on.

> 阿初一看他们，就知道他们是慕次的同事。
One look at them and A-Chu knew they were Muci's colleagues.

> “谁开的车？！”阿初厉声问。他的眼底闪着寒光，锋芒足以杀死在场的每一个人。
"Who was driving?!" A-Chu demanded sharply. A cold light glinted in his eyes, keen enough to kill every man there.

> 其实，不等他开口问，高磊已经反应过来，杨家兴师问罪的来了。他主动站出来，以示歉意。
In fact, before he had even asked, Gao Lei had grasped it: the Yang family had come to call them to account. He stepped forward of his own accord, to show contrition.

> 阿初走到他面前，两眼冒着火星，逼得高磊不自觉地退缩到墙角。
A-Chu walked up to him, sparks flying from his eyes, so that despite himself Gao Lei shrank back into the corner.

> “大……哥，有话好说。”
"Big—brother, we can talk this over."

> “谁开的车？！”阿初居高临下地逼着高磊问。
"Who was driving?!" From above, A-Chu bore down on Gao Lei.

> “我……对不起。”
"I—I'm sorry."

> 话还没讲完，高磊已经被迎头痛击，阿初动手打人了。侦缉处的人一下子围拢过来，连李沁红都觉得非常意外，虽然阿初一进门，她就知道此人会衅事，但是，敢动手打侦缉处的人，她还没见过。
Before the words were even out, Gao Lei took a blow full in the face—A-Chu had struck him. The Division's men closed in at once; even Li Qinhong found it wholly unexpected—though from the moment A-Chu came through the door she had known the man meant trouble, she had never yet seen anyone dare to lay hands on a Detective Division man.

> “干什么？”
"What are you doing?"

> “想打架啊？”
"Looking for a fight?"

> “有话好说啊。”
"There's no need for this—we can talk!"

> “不想活了！”
"Tired of living?"

> 虽然有一帮兄弟撑腰，但是，高磊始终觉得自己在阿初面前腰硬不起来，因为，同一辆车坐着，一个重伤，一个连头发丝都没断，说不过去。
Although he had a whole gang of brothers to back him, Gao Lei felt all along that he could not stiffen his spine before A-Chu; for the two of them had ridden in the same car, and one was gravely hurt while the other had not so much as a hair broken—it did not look good.

> “大哥，冷静点，冷静。有话好说。”高磊的鼻子虽然出了血，但是“认错”的态度很端正，没有逃避责任的意思。
"Big brother, calm down, calm down. We can talk it over." Though blood ran from his nose, Gao Lei's attitude of "owning his fault" was perfectly correct; there was no wish to duck the responsibility.

"He's a doctor." The nurse's answer was flat and to the point.

A doctor?

However Gao Lei looked at him, he did not seem like a doctor.

Li Qinhong came over and asked him. "Who was that just now?"

"What do you mean?" Gao Lei was very ill at ease.

"I'm joking with you." With that Li Qinhong strode into the operating theatre.

Li Qinhong looked through the white curtain. She saw A-Chu scrubbing and sterilizing his hands, and heard the doctors talking with A-Chu.

"How is it?" A-Chu asked.

"Systolic?"

"10.8 kilopascals."

"Pulse?"

"120 a minute."

"15 a minute."

"Yes!" Sure enough, Han Yu had come. Li Qinhong shrank into the corner.

"I need you as my assistant." said A-Chu, his eyes still fixed on the white curtain.

"I haven't touched a scalpel in two years." Han Yu was changing clothes and drawing on surgical gloves.

> “你比她们强。”阿初泛指这里的护士们。
"You're better than they are." A-Chu meant the nurses here in general.

Li Qinhong parted the seam of the curtain with her gun and saw a spread of deep red on the white cloth.

"Wire." Xia Yuechun's voice. He and A-Chu worked together, winding wire round the breastbone and twisting it tight to close the break.

> “针。止血钳。”阿初开始缝合皮下组织和皮肤。
"Needle. Haemostat." A-Chu began to suture the subcutaneous tissue and the skin.

> “血浆快用完了。”韩禹提醒阿初。
"The plasma's nearly gone." Han Yu reminded A-Chu.

> “我在这里，把输血针管直接接过来。”
"I'm right here—run the transfusion line straight over from me."

> 李沁红感到恶心，她没想到自己杀人都不眨眼，为什么看见做手术，她会有晕眩感，她不理解。径直跑出了手术室，脸色煞白。
Li Qinhong felt nauseous. She had never expected that she, who killed without a blink, should feel dizzy at the sight of surgery; she did not understand it. She hurried straight out of the operating theatre, her face white as chalk.

"What is it?" Gao Lei asked. "All right?" What he feared was that Yang Muci would die and he alone be left carrying the blame.

> “没事。”李沁红大口地呼吸了一下新鲜空气。“阿次的哥哥，绝对是个医生。”
"It's nothing." Li Qinhong drew a great breath of fresh air. "A-Ci's brother is a doctor, no question about it."

> “高队！”一个小特务跑过来。“高队，警察局来电话了，肇事汽车的主人是华美书店的老板荣华。”
"Captain Gao!" A little agent ran up. "Captain Gao, the police bureau telephoned. The owner of the car that caused the crash is Ronghua, proprietor of the Huamei Bookstore."

> “华美书店？”李沁红来劲了。“立即去华美书店，快，要快！”
"The Huamei Bookstore?" Li Qinhong came alive. "To the Huamei Bookstore at once—quick, quick!"

> 高磊也看到了抓捕共党的新线索和新希望，顾不得还躺在手术台上的阿次了，说声：走。跟着李沁红的脚步，带着人就往外冲。
Gao Lei too saw fresh clues and fresh hope of catching Communists, and, with no more thought for the A-Ci still lying on the operating table, said "Let's go," and followed on Li Qinhong's heels, leading his men out at a rush.

They all but brushed shoulders with Yang Sitong, who came racing up.

> “哥！大哥！”杨思桐冲进手术室。
"Brother! Big brother!" Yang Sitong rushed into the operating theatre.

> “你不能进去，小姐。”小护士在阻挡。
"You can't go in, miss." the little nurse was blocking her.

> “我大哥在里面。”杨思桐的声音里夹杂着哭腔。
"My brother is inside." Yang Sitong's voice was choked with weeping.

> 阿初不说话，简单地给夏跃春做了一个截断的动作，然后继续工作，夏跃春把手中的医用剪和镊子移交到韩禹手上，韩禹接着继续缝合，夏跃春转身出去。
A-Chu said nothing; he made a brief cutting gesture to Xia Yuechun, then went on working. Xia Yuechun passed the surgical scissors and forceps in his hand over to Han Yu, who took them and carried on suturing; then Xia Yuechun turned and went out.

"Brother—" Yang Sitong had taken the Division men's telephone call at home, telling her that her brother had had a road accident and that her family should come at once to the "Chunhe Hospital"; frantic with worry, without even time to notify her parents, she had run there in one breath.

> “杨小姐。”夏跃春神情严肃地截住了杨思桐地去路。
"Miss Yang." Grave-faced, Xia Yuechun barred Yang Sitong's way.

> “我大哥呢？大哥怎么样？”
"Where's my brother? How is he?"

"He's in surgery; there must be absolute quiet here."

"I want to see him."

"Not now."

"How—how is he now?"

"He's lost too much blood; his vital signs are very grave."

"You can't help him." said Xia Yuechun.

> “您什么意思？”
"What do you mean?"

"Rh-negative type A? Are you people quite sure? I'm his own sister."

"You must trust the science."

"I am."

"Doctor Xia, Mr. Han is asking you to come in." the little nurse called from inside.

By chance his gaze met Yang Sitong's, and Yang Sitong, of all things, returned him a faint smile.

The "Huamei" Bookstore, fierce flames roaring, its neon lights shattered, the savage blaze spreading to the innocent shops along the street.

"Who are you people?" Cong Feng was utterly at a loss over their conduct.

> “我们是杨先生的人。”刘阿四说。“杨先生不希望这家书店留下任何有关二先生的痕迹，懂吗？”
"We're Mr. Yang's men." said Liu A-Si. "Mr. Yang doesn't want this bookstore to keep a single trace of the second young master, understand?"

"Who is the second young master?"

> “您说呢？”刘阿四不回答。
"You tell me?" Liu A-Si would not answer.

Cong Feng roughly grasped A-Chu's intention. A man of medical training, after all, A-Chu meant to destroy every fingerprint, footprint, even every hair the so-called second young master had ever left in the "Huamei" Bookstore.

Never mind if the pond and its fish went with it.

Twenty minutes later Li Qinhong and Gao Lei reached the scene; the fire ground was all confusion, the fire brigade of the fire bureau fighting the blaze and giving aid.

Li Qinhong slammed a furious fist down on the bonnet of a car.

The bonnet gave a dull sound and dented slightly, like something gone limp, all its strength let out.

Muci's operation went very smoothly, a great success; Xia Yuechun, A-Chu and Han Yu worked well together—though the three of them were an "iron triangle" thrown together on the spur of the moment, their practised skill, cool judgement and exact coordination came of five years of medical-college study and clinical experience.

When A-Chu saw his own blood and Rong Chu's blood mingling together with A-Ci's blood, his feeling for A-Ci underwent a subtle change. The secret of their origin was showing its first sign through the seeping of blood; the blood-kin flesh lost and found again first drew, deeply, A-Chu's pitying, cherishing heart.

> 阿初第一次对慕次有了心疼的感觉。以前从没有过，他甚至只是顾及到兄长的责任而已，没有真心要疼他，或是顾怜他，帮助他，不过，现在，阿初觉得自己很在乎阿次的生死存亡，也许，这就是血浓于水的浅显道理。
For the first time A-Chu felt a pang of tenderness for Muci. Never before had he felt it; before, he had merely reckoned with an elder brother's duty, without any true wish to cherish him, or to pity him, or to help him. But now A-Chu found that he cared very much whether A-Ci lived or died—perhaps this was the plain truth that blood is thicker than water.

> “阿初，你还真行。”夏跃春摘了口罩。
"A-Chu, you really are something." Xia Yuechun took off his mask.

> “怎么？”
"How so?"

> “人都说，医生不敢给自己的亲人动手术。几乎成了金科玉律了，你是例外。”
"They all say a doctor daren't operate on his own kin. It's become all but an iron rule—and you're the exception."

> “不。”阿初说。“从今夜起，我也在例中了。”
"No." said A-Chu. "From tonight on, I'm inside the rule too."

> “是吗？”夏跃春不信。
"Oh?" Xia Yuechun did not believe it.

> “是的。”阿初神色诚恳。“我现在真的对他有感觉了。他是我弟弟。”
"Yes." A-Chu's look was sincere. "Now I truly do have feeling for him. He's my brother."

> 荣初也出来了，他脸色惨白，脚步有些飘。
Rong Chu, too, came out; his face was ghastly pale, his steps a little unsteady.

> “冷吗？”阿初关心地问。
"Cold?" A-Chu asked with concern.

> “很冷。”荣初回答。
"Very cold." Rong Chu answered.

> 陆良晨站在门口，手里拿着荣初的外套。
Lu Liangchen stood at the door, Rong Chu's coat in his hands.

> “照顾好少爷。”阿初把荣初交到了陆良晨手上。
"Take good care of the young master." A-Chu handed Rong Chu over to Lu Liangchen.

> “我哥哥怎么样？”杨思桐从走廊的长椅上站起来，神色紧张地注视着三个医生。
"How is my brother?" Yang Sitong rose from the long bench in the corridor, and, tense-faced, watched the three doctors.

> “过了今天晚上，就没事了。”韩禹说。
"Once tonight is past, he'll be out of danger." said Han Yu.

> “真的吗？”
"Truly?"

> “您不用担心。”夏跃春宽慰地说。
"You needn't worry." said Xia Yuechun, comfortingly.

> 阿初暗中掐了荣初一下，荣初明白过来，一下栽倒在地。众人“呼啦”一下围过去。
A-Chu secretly gave Rong Chu a pinch; Rong Chu understood, and crumpled to the ground. In a rush the others crowded round.

> “他怎么了？”杨思桐问。
"What's wrong with him?" Yang Sitong asked.

> “他失血过多，他没事，过一会儿就会恢复的。”夏跃春说。
"He's lost too much blood; he's all right, he'll come round in a while." said Xia Yuechun.

> “我来照顾他吧。”杨思桐脱口而出。几个人都有些意外，思桐连忙解释了一句。“他是为救我哥哥才这样的。”
"Let me look after him." The words were out of Yang Sitong's mouth before she knew it. Several people looked a little surprised, and Sitong hastened to explain. "It was to save my brother that he came to this."

> “好吧。”夏跃春看着阿初默许的眼神，答应了杨思桐。
"Very well." Seeing the tacit consent in A-Chu's eyes, Xia Yuechun granted Yang Sitong's wish.

> 一夜之间。
In the course of a single night.

> 杨慕次的下线和上线牺牲了。他的最高领导人方致同叛变革命，被处死了。阿次一夜之间，成了无主的孤魂。
Yang Muci's contact below and his contact above had given their lives. His highest superior, Fang Zhitong, had betrayed the revolution and been put to death. In the course of a single night A-Ci had become a lone, masterless soul.

> 没有人知道他的存在了。
No one knew any longer that he existed.

> “飘风”难道真的要随风飘逝吗？
Was "Drifting Wind" truly to drift away and vanish upon the wind?

> 问题是，他活过来了。
The thing was, he had come back to life.

> 他幸运地睁开朦胧的双眼，东升的旭日从窗子的缝隙中微笑着挤进来。
By good fortune he opened his hazy eyes; the sun climbing in the east squeezed smiling through the crack of the window.

> 慕次完全清醒了。
Muci had come fully to himself.

> 又一次在晨曦中礼叩光明。
Once more, in the light of dawn, he bowed in worship to the light.
