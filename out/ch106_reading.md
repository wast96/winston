### Advertising Doctors

There is a kind of doctor whose learning is very middling and whose repute is very thin, so that his customers are few and he might net sparrows at his door. Whereupon he hits on a clever scheme and puts all his effort into advertising. When such men run an advertisement, they will often take a whole page or a half-page, and the wording of it is spun out to make the very flowers fall from heaven, all self-trumpeting and self-praise; and in this way business will pick up of itself, and the doorway be busy as a marketplace.

Not only is the wording of the advertisement made vivid as life and mighty fine to look upon; at the same time they enlist a few score of society's notables to lend their names in recommendation. Once such an advertisement is out, need they fear the patients will not come streaming in? For Shanghai is a wide place with a great many people, and those who fall ill and cannot find a good doctor are not a few; and now, seeing this big advertisement so lively in its wording, how should their hearts not go pit-a-pat as they hasten to seek his aid?

People nowadays dub this sort of doctor the "advertising doctor," and nothing could be more apt, for they put no effort into the study of medicine but only into their advertisements.
