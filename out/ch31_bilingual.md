## H2 Chapter 31. The Roaming Fish Sees the Bait, Not the Hook

> 杨慕次一声“思桐”，搅得小山缨子方寸大乱，手眼不一，枪口一晃，慕次飞脚重踢，正中小山缨子的手，她像一匹受了伤的野兽号叫一声，手上的枪遗落到枯树底，慕次爬上树干，才发现树干和树叶几乎都是用木条伪装的，小山缨子迅速撤离险境。
Yang Muci's single cry of "Sitong!" threw Koyama Eiko into utter disarray; hand and eye no longer answered each other, the muzzle wavered, and Muci's flying kick came down hard, catching Koyama Eiko full on the hand. She gave a cry like a wounded beast, and the pistol slipped from her grip to the foot of the withered tree. Muci climbed the trunk, and only then found that trunk and leaves alike were nearly all counterfeited out of wooden slats; Koyama Eiko was already slipping swiftly clear of danger.

> 慕次眼睁睁看着她在绳索的帮助下，穿梭而去。
Muci watched, powerless, as she went threading away with the help of a rope.

> 慕次想着阿初的安危，不敢去追。他复又返回，把阿初拽了上来，然后，两人凭借小山缨子离去时所抛弃的绳索，依次下到地面。
Thinking of A-Chu's safety, he dared not give chase. He turned back instead and hauled A-Chu up, and then the two of them, using the very rope Koyama Eiko had thrown aside as she fled, descended one after the other to the ground.

> 慕次看了看表，时间是早上六点二十分。
Muci looked at his watch. The time was six twenty in the morning.

> 紧接着，他们听见了杂乱的脚步声和山门外的汽车声。慕次掏出手枪，准备防御。
Hard upon that came a clatter of disordered footsteps and the sound of a motorcar beyond the mountain gate. Muci drew his pistol, ready to defend them.

> “舅舅，舅舅……”
"Uncle, Uncle..."

> 阿初听见了荣初的声音。
A-Chu heard Rong Chu's voice.

> “我的人。”阿初用手制止慕次。
"My people." A-Chu raised a hand to stop Muci.

> 朦胧的雾霭中，阿初看见荣初领着刘阿四和陆良晨等一干人匆匆赶来，趁着曙色，他们很快看到了对方，并快速跑了过来。
In the dim mist A-Chu saw Rong Chu hurrying up at the head of a party of men, Liu A-Si and Lu Liangchen among them; in the growing light of dawn they soon caught sight of one another and came running over at speed.

> “没事吧，舅舅？”荣初第一个跑到阿初的面前，关心他的安危。
"Are you all right, Uncle?" Rong Chu was the first to reach A-Chu, anxious for his safety.

> “没事。”阿初一边回答，一边走向自己人的包围圈，一群人上来问长问短，慕次被无形的冷淡抛在孤独的风中。
"I'm fine." Even as he answered, A-Chu walked into the circle of his own people; the crowd pressed round with a hundred questions, while Muci was cast aside by an unspoken coldness, alone in the wind.

> “先生，侦缉处的人已经到山门了。”刘阿四说。
"Sir, the Detective Division's men have already reached the mountain gate," said Liu A-Si.

> “我们从后山走。”阿初说。
"We'll go out by the back of the hill," said A-Chu.

> “杨先生！”慕次不知怎的，突然叫住阿初。阿初停下来，以为他要说什么要紧话，可是，慕次并没有下文。
"Mr. Yang!" Somehow Muci suddenly called out to stop him. A-Chu halted, thinking he had something urgent to say; but no more came from Muci.

> “还能再见吗？”慕次问。
"Will we meet again?" Muci asked.

> “那要看你的表现。”阿初的话很硬、很冷，没有一丝和缓。慕次突然意识到阿初对他的依赖和信任霎时冷却，他变得从容有度了，他身上那种无声的威慑很自然地放射出来，让慕次感到自己突然之间被他有意的疏远了。
"That depends on how you conduct yourself." A-Chu's words were hard and cold, without a trace of softening. Muci suddenly realized that A-Chu's reliance on him and his trust in him had cooled in an instant; A-Chu had grown composed and self-possessed, and that soundless power to overawe radiated from him quite naturally, so that Muci felt himself all at once deliberately held at a distance.

> “你很势利，杨先生。”慕次说。“你现在不需要我了，是吧？”
"You're a snob, Mr. Yang," said Muci. "You've no more use for me now, is that it?"

> “你为我做过什么？”阿初的口吻陡然厉害起来。“你身上有枪，刚才为什么放她走？母子情深？还是，刻意让她回去报信，好救你心目中的慈父？对了，还有你那位跋扈成性的好妹妹？”
"What have you ever done for me?" A-Chu's tone turned suddenly savage. "You had a gun on you; why did you let her go just now? Mother-and-son feeling running deep? Or did you let her get back on purpose to carry word, so as to save the kindly father of your dreams? Oh, and that overbearing, tyrannical dear sister of yours as well?"

> 慕次哑口无言。
Muci was struck dumb.

> 显然，自己的某种莫名的举动，刺激到了阿初敏感的神经。
Plainly some inexplicable act of his own had grazed a raw nerve in A-Chu.

> “你没有切肤之痛！”阿初话里有话。
"You've never known a pain that cut to the bone!" There was an edge of meaning under A-Chu's words.

> 阿初从慕次身边走过，仿佛眼前这个人并不存在，一群人紧随他的步伐。
A-Chu walked past Muci as though the man before his eyes did not exist, the whole party following close on his steps.

> “先生，你的车还停在山门。”刘阿四说。
"Sir, your car is still parked at the mountain gate," said Liu A-Si.

> “是吗？”阿初停下来，有意无意地侧过身，说：“那车开不了了。”然后大跨步向后山走去。
"Is it?" A-Chu stopped, and half turned, whether by design or not, and said, "That car won't be going anywhere." Then he strode off toward the back of the hill.

> 慕次看着他们的身形逐渐在眼前消失，想着阿初临去的一句话。那车开不了了？慕次突然反应过来，危险！！他快速向山门冲去。
Muci watched their forms fade slowly from sight, turning over A-Chu's parting words. That car won't be going anywhere? All at once it came to him: danger! He went racing toward the mountain gate.

> 慈云寺山门前，杜旅宁带着两、三个手下正在踏勘地形，杜旅宁仔细观察了一下停放在山门前一辆黑色汽车，他走过去，沿车的外围踱了一圈步，没发现什么特别的，于是，他的手准备试着去开车门。
Before the mountain gate of the Ciyun Temple, Du Luning and two or three of his men were surveying the ground. Du Luning studied for a moment a black motorcar parked before the gate, walked over, paced once round its outer edge, found nothing out of the ordinary, and so reached out his hand to try the car door.

> 车门一线之间，杜旅宁听到了“滴答”声。敏锐的嗅觉警告他，是炸弹？！
In the very instant before the door, Du Luning heard a "tick-tick." His keen instincts warned him: a bomb?!

> “危险！”慕次扑过来，杜旅宁就势一滚，好在两个人都是训练有素，迅疾地翻滚，协作般的保护，抵挡住“死神”的脚步。他们身后“轰”的一声巨响，汽车炸开了花。慕次听到有人的惨叫声，一个小特务被炸伤了，挂了彩。
"Danger!" Muci threw himself over; Du Luning rolled with the movement. It was well that both men were seasoned and trained, rolling and tumbling swiftly, guarding each other as if in concert, holding off the footfall of the Angel of Death. Behind them came a great roar; the motorcar burst into bloom. Muci heard someone's ghastly scream; a little agent had been caught by the blast and wounded.

> 杜旅宁站起来，脸色铁青。
Du Luning rose to his feet, his face iron-grey.

> “谁要致你于死地？”杜旅宁问。
"Who wants you dead?" Du Luning asked.

> “我想，应该是我家里人。”慕次没有掩饰。
"I imagine it would be my own family," Muci said, hiding nothing.

> 杜旅宁好像并不感到特别意外，他说：“我要一份详细的报告。”
Du Luning seemed not particularly surprised. He said, "I want a detailed report."

> “是。”
"Yes, sir."

> “你不要回家了。”
"Don't go home."

> 慕次没有答话。
Muci made no reply.

> “你回家会很危险。”杜旅宁指了指身后焚毁的汽车，危险的后果已经初见端倪了。“立即搜查愚园路上的秘密电台。”
"It would be very dangerous for you to go home." Du Luning pointed behind him at the burnt-out car; the dangerous consequences had already begun to show themselves. "Search the secret radio station on Yuyuan Road at once."

> “是。”慕次答。
"Yes, sir," Muci answered.

> “就你一个人？”
"You alone?"

> “……除了我，夜半三更谁有胆子敢到这荒郊野外？”
"...Besides me, who would have the nerve to come out to this desolate wilderness in the dead of night?"

> “这车是谁的？”
"Whose car is this?"

> “……我的，私家车。”
"...Mine. My own private car."

> “可惜了。”杜旅宁惋惜地说。
"A pity," said Du Luning with regret.

> “您喜欢？”慕次说。“我送您一辆。”
"You like it?" said Muci. "I'll give you one."

> “算了吧，你有钱吗？”
"Forget it. Have you got the money?"

> “家里有啊。”慕次笑。
"There's money at home," Muci smiled.

> 杜旅宁眯缝着眼睛看了看慕次。说：“最近没看报纸？”
Du Luning narrowed his eyes at Muci. He said, "Haven't you read the papers lately?"

> “什么？”
"What?"

> “杨氏银行快倒闭了。”
"The Yang Bank is about to go under."

> “啊？！”慕次惊讶。
"What?!" Muci was astonished.

> “买份报纸，自己看。”杜旅宁说。
"Buy a paper and read it yourself," said Du Luning.

> “啊哟！”慕次的脚踝扭了。
"Aiya!" Muci's ankle had turned.

> “怎么了？”
"What is it?"

> “脚扭了，还有……好像旧伤复发了。”慕次说。
"Twisted my ankle, and... it seems an old wound has opened again," Muci said.

> “严不严重？”杜旅宁俯下身去看，慕次裤管里渗出血来，他是在地窖里受的伤。“怎么搞的？”
"Is it serious?" Du Luning bent down to look; blood was seeping through the leg of Muci's trousers, the wound he had taken in the cellar. "How did this happen?"

> “我想，我得去医院。”
"I think I'll have to go to the hospital."

> “把我的车开过来，送杨副官去医院。”杜旅宁吩咐小特务。
"Bring my car round and take Adjutant Yang to the hospital," Du Luning ordered the little agent.

> “处座，阿九伤得很重。”小特务在喊。
"Chief, A-Jiu is badly hurt," the little agent called.

> “一起吧，一起去。”慕次朝小特务努嘴示意。
"Together, then; all of you go together." Muci jerked his chin toward the little agent.

> “随你。”杜旅宁说。“别弄脏我的车。”
"As you please," said Du Luning. "Just don't dirty my car."

> 慕次表面点头微笑，心中万马狂奔。颇难想像，一夜之间，精心构建了数十年的杨氏大厦将要倾覆了？可能吗？
On the surface Muci nodded and smiled, but within him ten thousand horses were galloping wild. It was hard to imagine: in a single night, the great house of Yang, built up with such care over decades, was about to topple? Could it be?

> 杨慕初到底干了些什么？
Just what had Yang Muchu done?

> 素来在商场上纵横无敌的“父亲”，是怎样阴沟底翻船的了？难道真是天网恢恢，疏而不漏。
His "father," who had always ridden unbeaten through the marketplace, how had he come to capsize in a gutter? Could it truly be that Heaven's net is vast, and though its mesh is wide it lets nothing slip?

> 上海证券交易所外雪风扑面、空气清新，交易所内却是乌烟瘴气、人声鼎沸，股市在战争的阴影下，股票震荡性狂泻，经济停滞，造成通货膨胀，国家还在试图开辟新税源，增加了股票、期货外汇交易税，股市的杠杆被变本加厉地倾斜，股市里随处可见一夜暴富的新贵和一夜之间破产的资本家，来来往往寻求运气的人中，有一个走路跌跌撞撞的人，踉踉跄跄走进了证券交易所贵宾室的大门。
Outside the Shanghai Stock Exchange the snow-wind beat at one's face and the air was fresh, but inside the Exchange all was a foul, seething haze and a din of human voices. Under the shadow of war the market was in convulsions, stocks plunging in a mad slide, the economy at a standstill, inflation setting in; the state, still trying to open new sources of revenue, had added a transaction tax on stocks, futures, and foreign exchange, so that the market's leverage was tipped further and further out of true. Everywhere in the market were new men made rich overnight and capitalists ruined overnight; and among the comers and goers seeking their fortune was one man who walked as though he could not keep his feet, staggering and stumbling in through the great door of the Exchange's guest room.

> “情况很糟啊，杨老板。”明堂说。
"Things look very bad, Mr. Yang," said Ming Tang.

> 明堂，三十八岁，是明氏企业的掌门人，家族生意是经营矿产，同时也是上海证券交易所的负责人之一。
Ming Tang, thirty-eight, was the head of the Ming enterprises; the family business was in mining, and he was also one of those in charge of the Shanghai Stock Exchange.

> 此刻，他正对着满脸愤慨之色的杨氏银行的总裁杨羽桦讲话。
At this moment he was speaking to Yang Yuhua, president of the Yang Bank, whose whole face was full of indignation.

> “比想像的还要糟。”杨羽桦坐了下来。“东洋公司的高价棉纱严重积压，没有了市场，进口棉纱的行业完全崩溃，完全崩溃。”当然，令杨羽桦感到惊慌和恐惧的，却远远不止这些。“有人设计了一个高水平、高水准的骗局。欺骗了我，我把一大笔东洋公司存放在我银行里储蓄的钱，投入到棉纱期货市场，全完了……”
"It's worse than I'd imagined." Yang Yuhua sat down. "The Dongyang Company's high-priced cotton yarn is badly overstocked, there's no market for it, the imported-yarn trade has collapsed utterly, collapsed utterly." Of course, what filled Yang Yuhua with alarm and dread went far beyond this. "Someone laid a high, polished swindle, and it deceived me. A great sum the Dongyang Company had deposited for safekeeping in my bank, I poured into the cotton-yarn futures market; all gone..."

> “是啊，现在全民抵制日货，囤聚日本棉纱，无疑纵火焚身。”明堂一边说话，一边敲了敲秘书小姐的门。“两杯红茶。谢谢。”
"Just so; now the whole nation is boycotting Japanese goods, and hoarding Japanese cotton yarn is nothing less than setting fire to your own body." As he spoke, Ming Tang knocked at the door of the secretary's room. "Two cups of black tea. Thank you."

> 少顷，和雅淑端了两杯红茶进来，她轻轻地把茶杯放到书桌上，步履轻捷地离开。
Presently He Yashu carried in two cups of black tea; she set them gently on the desk and left with quick, light steps.

> “我现在是腹背受敌。”杨羽桦说。
"I am beset front and rear now," said Yang Yuhua.

> “你可以和东洋公司摊开来谈谈。”明堂说。“你也帮他们不少了，他们不会见死不救吧？”
"You could lay it all open and talk with the Dongyang Company," said Ming Tang. "You've done a great deal for them; surely they won't stand by and watch you die?"

> 杨羽桦听懂了明堂的暗示，自己一直替东洋公司洗黑钱，明堂多多少少知道一些，何况他以前通过明堂用投资的手段也洗过黑钱，但是，这些通过各种合法渠道洗干净的钱，并不是归杨氏企业独有，而是源源不断地流进了日本人的口袋。
Yang Yuhua caught Ming Tang's insinuation. He had long been laundering dirty money for the Dongyang Company, and Ming Tang knew something of it; besides, Yang had in the past laundered dirty money too through Ming Tang's hand, by way of investment. But this money, washed clean through every kind of legal channel, was not the Yang enterprises' alone: it flowed in an unbroken stream into Japanese pockets.

> “你得帮我。”杨羽桦说。
"You have to help me," said Yang Yuhua.

> “怎么帮？你现在资不抵债。”明堂的口气很冷峻。“杨老板，我们都是打开门做生意的，生意场上无父子，杨氏企业破产已经迫在眉睫了，依我之见，你不如把手上所有不动产变成现金……”
"Help you how? You're insolvent now." Ming Tang's tone was cold and hard. "Mr. Yang, we're all in business with our doors open to the street; in the marketplace there's no father and son. The bankruptcy of the Yang enterprises is upon you. As I see it, you'd do best to turn every piece of real property you hold into cash..."

> “你逼我卖房、卖厂？”杨羽桦只觉得一股血腥气直冒脑门，他脸上的青筋暴出来，眼珠子迸出火花。
"You'd force me to sell my houses, sell my mills?" Yang Yuhua felt a gust of bloody air rush straight to his crown; the veins stood out on his face and sparks seemed to fly from his eyeballs.

> “你没有时间考虑了，杨老板。”明堂没有丝毫怯意。“你不听我劝告，一味和日本人合作，导致投资决策一错再错，还有，你开虚假汇票欺骗政府银行……”
"You've no time left to deliberate, Mr. Yang." Ming Tang showed not the least timidity. "You wouldn't heed my advice, you kept on collaborating with the Japanese, so that your investment decisions went wrong again and again; and there is more: you drew false drafts to cheat the government bank..."

> “你说什么？”杨羽桦一头雾水。
"What are you saying?" Yang Yuhua was utterly at sea.

> “我们之间就不必绕弯子了。”明堂显然对杨羽桦的表情极不满意，难道是自己在诬陷、栽埋他吗？
"There's no need for us to talk in circles." Ming Tang was plainly most displeased with the look on Yang Yuhua's face; was he, then, framing the man, burying him under false charges?

> “你把话讲清楚。”杨羽桦很激动。
"Say it plainly," Yang Yuhua said, much agitated.

> “回去问问你儿子。据说，他用你开的假汇票，到处兑现大笔现钞。”看见杨羽桦呆若木鸡状，明堂索性敲他一敲，说：“挥霍钱财、浪费资金是小事，盗取国有资产、非法牟利、洗黑钱，可是要坐牢的。说老实话，我也不希望看到你苦心经营的大厦化做一片废墟，老来受罪。我的杨老板。”
"Go back and ask your son. They say he's been using the false drafts you drew to cash out large sums everywhere." Seeing Yang Yuhua struck dumb as a wooden chicken, Ming Tang decided to give him a further rap: "Squandering money, wasting capital, that's a small matter; but stealing state-owned assets, illicit profiteering, laundering dirty money, that can land a man in prison. To be honest, I've no wish either to see the mansion you built with such painstaking care turned to a heap of rubble, and you suffering in your old age. My dear Mr. Yang."

> 杨羽桦此刻的心中五味杂陈，从明堂的“恶劣”态度上来看，他已经被“某人”收买了，也许自己也走到绝境了。
At this moment Yang Yuhua's heart was a jumble of five flavors. From Ming Tang's "vile" manner, it was clear he had been bought over by "someone"; perhaps he himself had reached a dead end as well.

> 明堂有一句说得很对，自己没有多余的时间了。如果银行一旦宣布倒闭，自己的所有不动产都将在一瞬间化为乌有。
There was one thing Ming Tang had said quite rightly: he had no time to spare. If once the bank declared its failure, all his real property would vanish into nothing in a single instant.

> 从东洋公司对自己不问不顾的态势上看，自己对他们已经没有用处了，是什么促使东洋公司对自己的安危如此漠视呢？原因只能有一个，自己的真实身份暴露了，缨子这个恶毒的贱人在背后做了手脚？
Judging by the Dongyang Company's posture of complete indifference to him, he was of no further use to them. What had driven the Dongyang Company to be so cold to his very safety? There could be only one reason: his true identity had been exposed. Had that venomous slut Eiko played some trick behind his back?

> 也不对啊，自己暴露了身份，缨子不也就完了吗？
No, that was not right either. If his identity was exposed, would not Eiko be finished too?

> 那么，是阿次在对付自己，他利用自己开的汇票做诱饵？不会，阿次为人重情意，纵有存疑，以他的性格，他会直接来找自己寻求答案。
Then was it A-Ci moving against him, using the drafts he had drawn as bait? No; A-Ci set store by feeling, and even if he had doubts, given his character he would come straight to him for an answer.

> 杨慕初呢？就功底来讲，他不是学经济出身，而且，他也没有强大的经济实力来吞食整个棉纱市场。
And Yang Muchu? As for grounding, the man had not studied economics; and besides, he had not the vast financial power to swallow the whole cotton-yarn market.

> 杨羽桦实在想不通。
Yang Yuhua truly could not puzzle it out.

> “杨老板？”明堂在催。
"Mr. Yang?" Ming Tang was pressing him.

> “你刚才说的，的确是救急之计，不过，我还需要想一个久远之图。我不能死得不明不白。”
"What you said just now is indeed a stopgap for the emergency; but I still need to think of a longer plan. I can't die without knowing how or why."

> “你的意思呢？”
"And your meaning?"

> “你找一个买家，他必须吃掉我手上所有的棉纱，我把所有不动产卖给他，绝不食言。”杨羽桦知道，如今要解决目前的危机，只能依赖眼前这个无耻的投机商。
"You find a buyer; he must eat up all the cotton yarn I hold, and I'll sell him all my real property, without going back on my word." Yang Yuhua knew that, to resolve the present crisis, he could rely only on this shameless speculator before his eyes.

> “可以。”明堂回答得很干脆。
"That can be done," Ming Tang answered without hesitation.

> “我要现金。”
"I want cash."

> “多少？”
"How much?"

> “杨氏企业的招牌值多少？”杨羽桦反问。
"What is the Yang enterprises' name worth?" Yang Yuhua returned.

> “至少三千万。”
"Thirty million at the least."

> “我要七千万。”杨羽桦说。
"I want seventy million," said Yang Yuhua.

> “你干脆要一个亿。”明堂冷风透骨地说。“你自己开价自己买吧。”
"You might as well ask for a hundred million." Ming Tang's words came cold enough to pierce the bone. "Set your own price and buy it yourself."

> “五千万。”杨羽桦说。“这是底线，东山再起的底线。”
"Fifty million," said Yang Yuhua. "That's the bottom line, the bottom line for making a fresh start."

> “好，成交。”明堂说。
"Good. Done," said Ming Tang.

> “等一下。我要和买家见面。”
"Wait. I want to meet the buyer."

> “可以。”明堂回答得异常爽快。“我来安排。”
"That can be arranged." Ming Tang answered with unusual readiness. "I'll see to it."

> “要快！”杨羽桦心中已经酝酿好了另一个“金蝉脱壳”的计划，如果自己能够借此“死亡”的危机，摆脱掉自己的身份，甚至是日本人，他将获得永远的“新生”。这是他梦寐以求的，他不想“死”于杨羽柏之名。
"And be quick!" In his heart Yang Yuhua had already brewed another "golden-cicada-sheds-its-shell" plan: if he could use this crisis of "death" to shed his own identity, and even the Japanese, he would win a new life forever. This was what he had longed for in his dreams; he had no wish to "die" under the name of Yang Yubo.

> “你放心吧，我做事效率第一。”明堂说。
"Set your mind at rest. In my work, efficiency comes first," said Ming Tang.

> 杨羽桦眼帘下垂，仿佛这一生所有的事业付诸于东流。他不知道自己是如何离开交易所“贵宾室”大门的，他像一只流浪狗一样，惶惶不安。他走到证券交易所的门口时，听见里面“炒金”的浪潮，一浪比一浪高，他惶惑，仿佛回到二十年前的某一天，一不留神，撞翻了交易所门口的绿色盆栽。
Yang Yuhua's eyelids drooped, as though the whole enterprise of his life had been poured out into an eastward-flowing stream. He did not know how he left the door of the Exchange's "guest room"; like a stray dog he went, uneasy and afraid. When he reached the door of the Exchange, he heard within the tide of "gold speculation" rising, wave upon wave, each higher than the last; bewildered, as though returned to some day twenty years before, in one careless instant he knocked over a green potted plant at the Exchange door.

> “杨先生，留神走路。”一个音容笑貌异常熟悉的男子，映入杨羽桦的眼帘。这一惊非同小可，他看见了衣冠笔挺、风度翩翩的杨慕初。那个做“鬼”都不肯饶放自己的冤家对头。
"Mind how you go, Mr. Yang." A man whose voice and face were strangely familiar came into Yang Yuhua's view. This was no small shock: he saw the trim-clad, gracefully mannered Yang Muchu, that mortal enemy who would not spare him even as a "ghost."

> “是你？”他咽了咽口水，令自己恢复威严。
"It's you?" He swallowed, recovering his dignity.

> “是我。”阿初满面春风地说。他身后停放着一辆豪华小汽车，汽车的漆水十分耀眼，甚至明亮到过分招摇。“怎么样？杨老板听说你最近走背运哦？你积压的棉纱会不会全扔进黄浦江？想到解决危机的办法了吗？需不需要我帮帮你啊？”
"It's me." A-Chu spoke with a face full of spring. Behind him was parked a luxurious little motorcar, its paintwork dazzling, bright almost to the point of flaunting. "Well, Mr. Yang? I hear your luck's been running against you lately. Is all that overstocked cotton yarn of yours going to end up thrown into the Huangpu? Have you thought of a way out of the crisis? Do you need me to help you along?"

> “你懂经济吗？”杨羽桦反唇相讥。
"Do you understand economics?" Yang Yuhua retorted.

> “是啊，我是不太懂经济。不过，我这个人有一个很好地优点，就是绝不'不懂装懂'，我请了两个经济顾问和一个法律顾问替我打理生意，还好，他们没有您'聪明'，做事也不敢蛮干，所以，生意上还算是井井有条。”
"That's true; I don't understand economics very well. But I have one fine virtue: I never 'pretend to understand what I don't.' I've engaged two economic advisers and one legal adviser to manage my business; luckily they're not as 'clever' as you, and they don't dare to act recklessly, so the business is in fairly good order."

> “你教训我？”
"You're lecturing me?"

> “怎么会？”阿初笑得阳光灿烂。不过，杨羽桦很快从他笑意里读出了仇视和永远无法消弭的敌意。
"How could I?" A-Chu's smile was radiant as sunlight. Yet Yang Yuhua soon read within that smile a hatred, an enmity that could never be dissolved.

> “祥和纱厂是你开的？”
"The Xianghe Cotton Mill is yours?"

> “是。”
"It is."

> “你一直和我作对。”
"You've been setting yourself against me all along."

> “生意场上无父子，这个道理，不用我来教你了吧？”
"In the marketplace there's no father and son; I hardly need to teach you that truth, do I?"

> “你无耻的把手伸进别人的口袋里，拿了别人的金钱，还以胜利者的姿态出现，你不觉得自己的行为很恶心吗？”
"You shamelessly thrust your hand into another man's pocket, took another man's money, and then appear in the posture of a victor; don't you find your own conduct disgusting?"

> “这一句骂得真是很精彩，'你无耻的把手伸进别人的口袋里'，仅此一项，我就望尘莫及。我只想拿回原本属于自己的东西，决无与你一争长短之心。”阿初说。
"That's a truly splendid piece of abuse. 'You shamelessly thrust your hand into another man's pocket'; on that one count alone I could never catch up with you. I only want to take back what was mine to begin with; I've no least wish to contend with you for the upper hand," said A-Chu.

> “什么是你的？我的财富是靠我的奋斗得来得，我为此付出了人生最惨痛的代价。”
"What is yours? My wealth was won by my own striving; I paid for it the most cruel price of my whole life."

> “你口中所谓的代价，就是牺牲亲情，杀埋骨肉，彻底剥却人皮，丧尽天良！有时候，我觉得跟你讲话都是我的一种耻辱。我的叔叔。”阿初居然还在笑。
"The price you speak of was to sacrifice kinship, to slaughter and bury your own flesh and blood, to strip off the human skin entirely, to lose the last shred of conscience! There are times I feel it a disgrace even to speak with you. My dear uncle." A-Chu was, incredibly, still smiling.

> “是你？你和卖方市场勾结，合谋出卖杨氏企业。你这个下三烂的败家子。”杨羽桦咬牙切齿地骂。
"It's you? You've colluded with the sellers' market, plotted together to sell off the Yang enterprises. You lowborn wastrel of a prodigal son." Yang Yuhua ground his teeth as he cursed.

> “出卖杨氏家族的人是你，你长期和日本人合作，打压国内棉纱市场，高价抛售日本棉纱，垄断经营，发国难财。你在卖国啊，叔叔。”阿初藐视地说。
"The one who sold out the Yang family is you. You collaborated with the Japanese for years, beat down the home cotton-yarn market, sold Japanese yarn at high prices, monopolized the trade, made your fortune off the nation's calamity. You're a traitor to your country, Uncle." A-Chu spoke with contempt.

> 杨羽桦的额头渗出汗珠，他听见了他这一生中最忌讳听到的话。
Beads of sweat oozed from Yang Yuhua's brow; he had heard the words he most dreaded to hear in all his life.

> “你到底想怎样？”
"What is it you want, in the end?"

> “我要你缴一份人世上最惨痛、最惨烈的账单。”阿初微笑如常。
"I want you to render the most cruel, the most searing account in all the human world." A-Chu smiled as calmly as ever.

> “我已经缴纳了。”杨羽桦喃喃自语。
"I have already rendered it," Yang Yuhua murmured to himself.

> “No.”阿初否认。
"No," A-Chu denied.

> “我失去了一切。一夜之间，财富、金钱……就像二十年前，我失去了我最心爱的女人……我的嫂子……”
"I lost everything. In a single night, wealth, money... just as, twenty years ago, I lost the woman I loved most... my sister-in-law..."

> “你住口。”
"Hold your tongue."

> “你母亲。”杨羽桦显得很冲动。“她会原谅我的，我是你的亲叔叔。”
"Your mother." Yang Yuhua seemed greatly agitated. "She would forgive me; I am your own uncle."

> “你罪有应得！”
"You have brought this on yourself!"

> “你的意思呢？”
"And your meaning?"

> “斩草除根！”
"Cut the grass and take out the roots!"

> 杨羽桦的脸第一次剧烈抖动起来。“我的女儿，是你堂妹。”
Yang Yuhua's face convulsed violently for the first time. "My daughter is your cousin."

> “她是一个逆种。你知道，中国封建大家族是怎么对待逆种的吗？她会被人浸进猪笼，活活呛死，淹死！”
"She is a degenerate seed. Do you know how a great feudal Chinese clan deals with a degenerate seed? She would be steeped in a pig-cage, choked and drowned alive!"

> “她是无辜的！”杨羽桦疯狂地大叫。
"She is innocent!" Yang Yuhua shrieked like a madman.

> “她才是你最心疼的人，她的命才是你所缴纳的最后一份账单。这是你毁家灭门、杀人偿命的最痛快的一份账单。”
"She is the one you most dote on; her life is the very last account you have to render. This is the most satisfying account of all: for the wrecking of a house and the extinction of a line, a life paid for a life."

> 杨羽桦的眼神阴森得可怕。“你特意到这里来，向我挑战？”
Yang Yuhua's eyes went dreadful and grim. "You came here on purpose, to challenge me?"

> “你太高估自己了，你也配？”阿初嘴角又挂起一丝讽刺的笑纹，他抬头看见了什么，反手打开车门，伸手拿出一束鲜花来，朝上走去。
"You think too much of yourself; are you even worthy?" A wisp of a mocking smile hung again at the corner of A-Chu's mouth. He raised his head, saw something, reached back and opened the car door, drew out a bunch of fresh flowers, and walked up toward it.

> 杨羽桦回头看去，他看见一个粉妆旗袍女郎娴雅地笑容。也就是他在明堂办公室看见的秘书小姐。
Yang Yuhua turned to look. He saw a girl in a powder-toned qipao with a graceful smile: the very secretary he had seen in Ming Tang's office.

> 和雅淑没想到会在下班的时候，看见阿初。而且，阿初还亲热地献花、示爱，这些举动很不合阿初的性格，不过，雅淑还是举止得体地接受了他的“爱”，他们并肩走下台阶，十分亲密无间。
He Yashu had not expected to see A-Chu as she came off work. And A-Chu was even affectionately presenting flowers, declaring his love; such gestures were quite out of keeping with A-Chu's character, yet Yashu still accepted his "love" with perfect composure, and they walked down the steps side by side, close as could be.

> 杨羽桦走了，他记住了阿初所“爱”。这朵平素里美得很有档次，养眼怡神的花，只要运用的好，花也会变成钩人肺腑的利器，这就是生活得另一面。
Yang Yuhua left, having marked well the one A-Chu "loved." This flower, ordinarily a thing of high-class beauty, a delight to the eye and spirit, could, if only it were well used, become a sharp weapon to hook a man by the heart. This was the other face of life.

> 杨羽桦还没有绝望，他还有生路可觅。
Yang Yuhua had not yet despaired; there was still a way to live to be found.

> 阿初成功地放下香饵，接下来需要把鱼钩磨得更锋利。阿初俯身打开车门，殷勤地让雅淑坐进去。
A-Chu had successfully laid down the fragrant bait; next he needed to grind the hook sharper still. A-Chu bent and opened the car door, and with every courtesy handed Yashu in.

> “今天想吃什么菜？”阿初亲昵地问。“川菜好不好？够辣。”
"What would you like to eat today?" A-Chu asked with tenderness. "How about Sichuan food? Good and spicy."

> “你转向了杨先生？”杨慕次不知什么时候从车尾站出来。“你想吃辣的，一个人吃好了，何必硬拉人下水呢？”
"So you've turned to Mr. Yang?" Yang Muci had stepped out, no one knew when, from the rear of the car. "If you want spicy food, eat it alone; why must you drag someone else down into the water with you?"

> 阿初直起腰，皱了皱眉头。
A-Chu straightened up and knit his brows.

> “你跟踪我？”
"You're following me?"

> “我关心你。”慕次说。
"I'm concerned for you," Muci said.

> “你想说什么？”
"What do you want to say?"

> “我不想跟你说。”
"I don't want to talk to you."

> “哪你想跟谁说？”
"Then who do you want to talk to?"

> “嫂子！”慕次俯身到车门另一端。“嫂子，我想单独跟你说两句话。”
"Sister-in-law!" Muci bent to the other side of the car door. "Sister-in-law, I'd like a private word with you."

> 阿初敲了敲车顶，说：“你也知道叫嫂子了，小叔子别跟嫂子走得太近，保持距离。”
A-Chu rapped on the roof of the car and said, "So you do know to call her sister-in-law. A younger brother-in-law shouldn't get too close to his sister-in-law; keep your distance."

> “多少？”
"How much?"

> “五米。”
"Five metres."

> 慕次夸张地退后一步。“嫂子，你是姓和吧？”
Muci took an exaggerated step back. "Sister-in-law, your surname is He, isn't it?"

> “是。”和雅淑机械地回答。
"Yes." He Yashu answered mechanically.

> “你很面善。”慕次说。
"You've a very kind face," said Muci.

> “很多男人第一次见她，都会讲这句话。”阿初补充。
"A great many men say that to her the first time they meet her," A-Chu added.

> “和雅姗是你什么人？”慕次突然点题。
"What is He Yashan to you?" Muci suddenly came to the point.

> “我姐姐，你认识我姐姐，她在哪里啊？”雅淑的情绪波动起来。
"My elder sister. You know my sister; where is she?" Yashu's feelings began to stir.

> “我是你姐姐的朋友，我现在以你姐姐朋友的身份告诫你，千万不要相信这位杨慕初先生，他的所作所为，都是极端自私的，他在利用你、欺骗你……他从来就没有爱过你，放弃他，保护好自己。”
"I'm a friend of your sister's, and now, in the capacity of your sister's friend, I warn you: on no account trust this Mr. Yang Muchu. Everything he does is utterly selfish; he's using you, deceiving you... he has never once loved you. Give him up, and protect yourself."

> “你说够了没有？”阿初用力关紧车门，大跨步走到慕次面前。“我看你是羡妒交加，跑来胡言乱语。”
"Have you said enough?" A-Chu slammed the car door hard and strode over to stand before Muci. "It looks to me as if you're eaten up with envy and jealousy, come running here to talk nonsense."

> “我只是在履行一个好朋友的委托。”慕次解释。“她姐姐授权给我的，叫我好好照顾她妹妹。”
"I'm only carrying out a good friend's charge," Muci explained. "Her sister authorized me, told me to take good care of her little sister."

> “授权书？”
"A letter of authorization?"

> “口头嘱咐。”
"A spoken charge."

> “口说无凭。”
"Words spoken carry no proof."

> “我跟她姐姐真的是同学。”
"Her sister and I really were schoolmates."

> “同学照？”阿初伸手要证据。“立照为据。”
"A class photograph?" A-Chu put out his hand for evidence. "A photograph would settle it."

> “没有。”
"I don't have one."

> “那就是无凭无据了？”
"Then you've no proof at all?"

> “和小姐，你要相信我。”慕次侧身喊了一句。阿初下意识推了慕次一把。慕次马上抗议：“你干吗？过分了。”
"Miss He, you must believe me." Muci turned aside and called out. A-Chu, on reflex, gave Muci a shove. Muci protested at once: "What are you doing? You're going too far."

> “谁过分？”
"Who's going too far?"

> “你无药可救。”慕次说。“你要对付杨羽桦，你自己真刀真枪地去干啊，你干吗利用女人？”
"You're past all cure," said Muci. "If you mean to deal with Yang Yuhua, go and do it yourself, with real blade and real spear; why must you use a woman?"

> “你说什么？”
"What did you say?"

> “你故意的，你故意为之。欲将取之，必先予之。你专程跑来看杨羽桦的惨状，然后激怒他，再叫他看见你所'爱'的女人。只要杨羽桦动这个女人，绑架也好、谋杀也罢，你就可以用法律制裁他，公开的、合法的杀死他。你不觉得这种行为很卑鄙吗？很下流吗？”
"You did it on purpose, you did it deliberately. To take, you must first give. You came here on purpose to see Yang Yuhua's wretched state, then to enrage him, and then to let him see the woman you 'love.' The moment Yang Yuhua touches this woman, be it abduction or murder, you can bring him down by law, kill him openly, legally. Don't you find such conduct base? Don't you find it vile?"

> 阿初猛地抬起手来，慕次的头下意识地偏向另一侧。但是，阿初没有动手，他硬生生把手撤了回来。
A-Chu abruptly raised his hand; Muci's head flinched by reflex to the other side. But A-Chu did not strike; he forced his hand back down.

> 阿初使劲地搓揉着拳头，拳头落在车顶上。他吞咽了一口气，慕次却没松口。
A-Chu kneaded his fist hard, and the fist came down on the roof of the car. He swallowed a breath, but Muci did not let up.

> “你是七尺男儿，一个有血性的男人。你怎么能用我妹妹的命去激怒你的仇人？用自己女人做诱饵，你有没有顾及到、考虑到无辜性命的安危？”慕次的眼睛瞪着阿初。
"You're a man seven feet tall, a man with blood in him. How can you use my sister's life to enrage your enemy? Using your own woman as a decoy, have you given a thought, taken any account, of the safety of innocent lives?" Muci's eyes glared at A-Chu.

> “我告诉你，我就是要公开的、理性的、冷血的、合法的杀死他！至于用什么手段，跟你没关系。”阿初低头看见地下的血滴。“还有，该看医生尽早去，我身上没有多余的血再给你。”
"Let me tell you: I mean to kill him openly, rationally, cold-bloodedly, legally! As for what means I use, that is no business of yours." A-Chu lowered his head and saw the drops of blood on the ground. "And another thing: go see a doctor, and the sooner the better. I've no spare blood on me to give you."

> 慕次不答话。
Muci did not answer.

> “要不要我送你去医院啊？”阿初冷冷地说。“哦，我忘了，大家冰炭不同炉。”
"Shall I drive you to the hospital?" A-Chu said coldly. "Oh, I forgot; ice and coals don't share a stove."

> 慕次眼睁睁地看见阿初开车，载着雅淑远去，他恨恨地跺了一脚，疼极了，才反应过来，自己的腿脚伤的不轻。
Muci watched, powerless, as A-Chu drove off, carrying Yashu away into the distance. He stamped his foot in hatred, and the pain shot through him; only then did he realize his leg and foot were no light hurt.

> 阿初的心情十分恶劣，慕次的话像刀片一样割着他的心，自己到底在干什么？自己到底要做什么？自己到底是哪种人？到底还是不是男人？
A-Chu's mood was foul in the extreme. Muci's words cut at his heart like razor blades. What on earth was he doing? What on earth did he mean to do? What kind of man was he, in the end? Was he still a man at all?

> 他不知道。
He did not know.

> 他拒绝去想、去思考、去辩白、去证明。
He refused to think, to reflect, to argue, to prove.

> 阿初的车像风一样飞驰在马路上，雅淑不说话，静静地坐在阿初身边，静如止水。这种平静的对抗远比大哭大闹更具有杀伤力。
A-Chu's car flew along the road like the wind. Yashu did not speak; she sat quietly at A-Chu's side, still as unmoving water. This calm resistance was far more wounding than any loud weeping and railing.

> 阿初感到窒息，他难以控制好自己的情绪，但是，他清楚地知道雅淑的感受，她需要释放，不然，雅淑的心也会在窒息中死亡。这是阿初所不愿意看到的结局。
A-Chu felt he could not breathe; he could scarcely master his own feelings. Yet he knew Yashu's feelings clearly: she needed release, or else her heart too would die of suffocation. And that was an ending A-Chu did not wish to see.

> “说话啊。”阿初冷冰冰地说。
"Say something," A-Chu said coldly.

> 雅淑低下头，她手上紧紧地捧着阿初送的玫瑰花，温室里培植的花朵散发着诱人肺腑的缕缕清香。
Yashu bowed her head; in her hands she clasped tightly the roses A-Chu had given her, the hothouse blooms giving off wisp on wisp of a fragrance that pierced to the heart.

> 阿初回头看看她，猛地一脚踩住刹车。雅淑没防备，险些扭了腰，她忍着心痛、忍着眼泪，不抬头，不说话，不看他。
A-Chu glanced back at her, and suddenly stamped down on the brake. Caught unprepared, Yashu nearly wrenched her back; she bore the ache in her heart, bore back her tears, would not lift her head, would not speak, would not look at him.

> 阿初长叹一声，说：“对不起。”
A-Chu heaved a long sigh and said, "I'm sorry."

> 雅淑的眼泪像珍珠一样滴洒在湿润的花瓣上，阿初的心突然有了“痛”的感觉。
Yashu's tears dripped like pearls onto the moist petals; A-Chu's heart suddenly knew the feeling of "pain."

> “下车。”阿初的脸色寒透了。
"Get out," A-Chu said, his face frozen through.

> 雅淑一动也不动，鲜花和美人像一幅静止的画面。
Yashu did not stir; flowers and fair woman were like a still, arrested picture.

> “下车吧，我们分手……”阿初的心弦为之撩动，声音渐有愧意。
"Get out; we're finished..." The strings of A-Chu's heart were plucked by it; his voice took on, little by little, a note of guilt.

> 雅淑突袭式地香唇紧紧地贴在了阿初的唇上，她疯狂地吻着她的心上人。阿初不自觉地配合着她狂野般的爱抚。
Yashu, in a sudden ambush, pressed her fragrant lips hard against A-Chu's, kissing the man of her heart wildly. Without meaning to, A-Chu answered her savage caresses.

> 直到雅淑肯放手。
Until at last Yashu was willing to let go.

> “我爱你，我知道你不爱我，可是，从现在起，你心里有我，不然，你干吗要我离开？我不介意做棋子，我不介意做诱饵。如果，如果上天把你赐给我，我愿意为你做任何事情，包括去死！”
"I love you. I know you don't love me. But from now on you have me in your heart, or why would you want me to leave? I don't mind being a chess-piece; I don't mind being a decoy. If Heaven has given you to me, I'm willing to do anything for you, even to die!"

> “你不觉得自己很傻吗？”阿初替她揩泪。
"Don't you think yourself very foolish?" A-Chu wiped away her tears for her.

> “跌进爱河的人，没有一个不是傻子。”雅淑含着盈盈泪光，越发楚楚动人。“我承认自己是一个愚蠢的女人，我以前不懂得什么是爱，什么是珍惜眼前人，我试图用容貌和家世去获取一份属于贵族女性的生活方式，我输了，输得很惨，输了你对我的所有信任，输入了你对我所持有的偏见。可是你错了，你和我都错了，你的音容笑貌反复出现在我梦境里，每一次都是你离开了我，尽管你的心并不属于我，包括在梦里。可是我已经全身心投入去爱你、尊敬你、疼你、恨你、怨你，我相信这个世界上没有任何一个女人像我这样疯狂地爱着你，不管你接不接受，无论你当初接近我、亲近我是何种目的，你在我心中，就是我的男人，唯一的男人。我不想一个人在孤独、乏味中度过一生，我不能再次失去我所爱的人，尽管我无法驾御你的感情，但是，我绝不会让你再离开我，除非我死！”
"No one who's fallen into the river of love is anything but a fool." Yashu, brimming with glistening tears, was more piteously lovely than ever. "I admit I'm a foolish woman. Before, I didn't understand what love was, what it was to cherish the one before your eyes. I tried to use my looks and my family to win the way of life that belongs to a woman of rank; I lost, lost wretchedly, lost all your trust in me, lost you into the prejudice you hold against me. But you were wrong, you and I were both wrong. Your voice and face appear again and again in my dreams, and every time it is you who have left me, though your heart does not belong to me, not even in dreams. Yet I have already thrown myself, body and soul, into loving you, revering you, aching for you, hating you, resenting you; I believe there is not one other woman in this world who loves you as madly as I do. Whether or not you accept it, whatever your purpose was in first drawing near me, coming close to me, you are, in my heart, my man, my only man. I don't want to pass a whole life alone in loneliness and tedium; I cannot lose the one I love a second time. Though I cannot govern your feelings, I will never let you leave me again, unless I die!"

> 阿初没有料到自己已经完全占据了雅淑的精神世界，他显得束手无策，应对无方。
A-Chu had not reckoned that he had already wholly taken possession of Yashu's inner world; he seemed at a loss, without a way to respond.

> “雅淑，我不会让你死的！你信我！”阿初说。
"Yashu, I won't let you die! Believe me!" A-Chu said.

> “我信！”
"I believe you!"

> “这件事情办妥了。我娶你！”阿初口气很坚决。
"Once this business is settled, I'll marry you!" A-Chu's tone was full of resolve.

> “初，你知道吗？咬住香饵的鱼就是你，我才是鱼钩，美丽的鱼钩，永远不会放弃的鱼钩。”雅淑眼底的泪花终于释放成了点点心花，心花开了，爱情还会远吗？
"Chu, do you know?" The tears in the depths of Yashu's eyes were released at last into little flowers of the heart. "The fish that has bitten the fragrant bait is you; I am the hook, the beautiful hook, the hook that will never let go." The flowers of her heart had opened; and when the heart's flowers open, can love be far behind?
