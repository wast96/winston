### Leftover Concubines

There is a certain "Parasite Mama" who, succeeding Xue the Big Fellow, took the lead of the pimping world; the lovesick men and pining women all worship her as a god, and her wide connections and great resource are truly the match of Xue the Big Fellow in his day — her power is no small thing. Whenever she meets a regular in the street she is sure to say, "I've a certain 'leftover concubine' — let me introduce you; will you have her?" and then she gives her tongue free rein, telling how bewitching this "leftover" is, how full of coquetry. Or the regular demurs: "I'd take her if I could, but worse luck I'm a poor scholar who can hardly keep himself from day to day; where should I find the means to keep up such an affair? My apologies — I must respectfully own myself unequal to it." And only then does she go off in a huff.

There was once a beggar who every evening at the doors of the great mansions would strain his cracked throat and bawl, "Master, mistress — any leftover bird's-nest or ginseng? Do a kind deed, spare a bit for a poor man to eat...!" — and we always thought it a fine joke. Now here is a "leftover concubine" as well; one may fairly say the two are a matched pair. Ha, ha!
