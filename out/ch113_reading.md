### Firing the Big Gun

In the provinces of Jin (Shanxi) and Sui (Suiyuan), the poison called morphine—otherwise known as "white flour"—has an enormous market. The people there need it not only if they are addicts themselves; even those with no smoking habit will, on meeting a friend, more often than not treat him to a little morphine. Their way of taking it is simple enough: they take a single paper cigarette, pack it tight, empty out the tip, drop a pinch of morphine into the hollow, and set light to it and smoke. But once a man does this, one who had no smoking habit will keep at it without a stop and grow addicted too; and this way of taking it they call "firing the big gun." Of late this game of firing the big gun has turned up in Shanghai as well; there are even men who make a business of mixing morphine into cigarettes and selling them in secret to the red-and-white-pill addicts to feed their craving, and so turn a profit. Formerly there were only "shooting marbles" (that is, taking the red-and-white pills) and jabbing the drug-needle—two methods; now there is one more, the firing of the big gun.
