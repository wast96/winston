### Builder change (source-note apparatus)

Chapter One is the first to reach the source's own notes ([1] at "Light, Heat,
Power!"; [2] at "Grafton"). The builder had no channel for these and its notes
page hard-coded "the source book carries none of its own." Added a SEPARATE
source-note stream, kept visibly distinct from the translator's footnotes and
NOT folded into them:

- New source_notes.json (shape {unit_id:[{anchor,n,note}]}); the author's OWN
  numbering [1]..[9] is preserved (not the builder's running count).
- Markers render as a bracketed number in a distinct colour (teal), linking to a
  "Notes in the Original Edition" section, itself distinct from the translator's
  "Notes." qa ids sref/snote do not collide with the translator ref/note regex.
- Notes-page intro rewritten to explain the two streams; the misleading "carries
  none of its own" line is gone.
- Builder refuses to build on an unmatched source-note anchor, same as translator
  notes.

Also fixed a real bug in check_numbers.py: the builtin noise `[十几幾]多` was
splitting `二十多` ("twenty-odd") into an orphan `二`, producing a phantom `2`.
Guarded `十多` with a negative lookbehind so it only strips when 十 is not the
tail of a larger number. Benefits every future batch (二十多/三十多 recur).

### check_numbers.py fixes (safe, mirror B01's 二十多 precedent)

1. `千萬/千万` intensifier strip now carries a negative lookbehind, so
   一千万/五千万 (ten-million / fifty-million) are read as QUANTITIES, not the
   "by all means" intensifier. Without this, 一千万 was mangled to a stray 万
   (=10000) or a bare 2 from 两千万.
2. Vague magnitudes 几十万/几百万/几千万 are stripped WHOLE (and BEFORE the
   2-char measure-word strip, longest-first) so no bare 万 is orphaned as 10000.
3. Target side: million/billion multipliers now also accept tens/teens/ten words
   ("ten million", "twenty million", "fifty million"). WORD_NUM gained the
   ordinals "eleventh":11 and "sixteenth":16 (the Fourth/Eleventh Armies, the
   sixteenth year = 1927).
   data/noise_zh.txt gained a Chapter-Two block: 百宝, 几两几钱, 五云, 两样,
   其二, 三角脸, 六角, 零星, 三分, 秋千, 四乡, 夹七夹八, 十万八万 (each a name,
   idiom, shape-word or set phrase, never a dropped quantity).
