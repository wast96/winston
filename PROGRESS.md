Chapter One is the first to reach the source's own notes ([1] at "Light, Heat,

  none of its own" line is gone.

splitting `二十多` ("twenty-odd") into an orphan `二`, producing a phantom `2`.
Guarded `十多` with a negative lookbehind so it only strips when 十 is not the
tail of a larger number. Benefits every future batch (二十多/三十多 recur).
