- Fact-check against scholarship (check 7), all corroborated except as noted:
  * Birth name: the memoir's own text names her 黄彰定 (Huang Zhangding), style
    Shuyi; she took 慕兰 (Mulan) in 1926 and 定慧 (Dinghui) later, in 1932. So
    book.json's author_note ("birth name Huang Dinghui") is loose; the SOURCE is
    right and the translation follows it. Footnoted in ch01 and set straight in
    the glossary (three name rows). book.json author_note left as-is for now
    (metadata only); worth tidying in a later pass.
  * Birth year 1907 (per the book and Chinese sources) vs 1906 in some English
    sources (虚岁 reckoning): footnoted, follows the book.
  * Autumn Harvest Uprising: the memoir places it "at Wenjiashi"; more precisely
    Wenjiashi (Liuyang) was the 19 Sept 1927 rally/turning point of an uprising
    that broke out 9 Sept. Footnoted with the qualification.
  * Corroborated: Tan Sitong / Tang Caichang / Ouyang Zhonghu; Zhan Tianyou
    (1861-1919); Zhounan founded by Zhu Jianfan (1905), alumnae Xiang Jingyu /
    Cai Chang; Qiu Jin (executed 1907); Northern Expedition (began July 1926,
    Wuchang taken 10 Oct 1926); Du Yunzhang = Zhang Yun; China Relief Society
    (20 Sept 1925); Wan Xiyan (1903-1928, her first husband); Sun Yat-sen d. 1925.
