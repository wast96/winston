## B01 = ch01 "Seeds of Revolt" (DONE)

PDF 24-41, printed folios 1-18. 51 body paragraphs, no block quotations, no
white-space scene breaks in this chapter.

### Extraction (faithful reset, no OCR)
- `scripts/extract_isaacs.py ch01` writes `out/ch01_reading.md` (verbatim
  Isaacs, one paragraph per line, "## 1. Seeds of Revolt" h1) and
  `data/pagemap/ch01.json` (18 folios -> body-paragraph index).
- Mechanical layer: drop-cap folded; running heads (7pt), folios (9pt), chapter
  number/title stripped; superscript reference digits removed (they become
  anchors) with a space restored where the mark had separated two words; inline
  asterisk footnote markers stripped; de-hyphenation by Isaacs's own dominant
  usage + the system word lists (147 line-break hyphens; only "forty-two" kept
  as a real hyphen; all others correctly fused).
- **Fidelity verified byte-for-byte**: the extracted body, reduced to a
  whitespace/hyphen/quote-stripped character stream, is IDENTICAL to the same
  reduction of the PDF's own body spans (45,509 chars). Nothing dropped, added,
  or reordered. (This replaces a tail-only rule-4 check with a whole-chapter
  one.)

### Notes (two streams, numeral system distinguishes them)
- 32 AUTHOR notes (arabic 1-32): Isaacs's 30 numbered endnotes converted to
  popup footnotes (bodies verbatim from printed 340-342, cleaned of line-break
  hyphenation and italic-run splits), PLUS his 2 asterisked page-foot footnotes
  (picul; Pu Yi), folded into the same arabic stream by position. Generated by
  `scripts/build_ch01_notes.py` (bodies) and `scripts/dump_endnotes.py`.
- 53 EDITORIAL notes (roman i-liii): the reader-facing layer, generous density
  model. `scripts/build_ch01_editorial.py` (anchors resolved against
  reading.md, checked unique and non-colliding with author anchors). Fact-checks
  verified against Britannica / Wikipedia / standard scholarship (Opium War
  dates, Taiping span & toll, Boxer indemnity 450M taels ~ US$333M vs Isaacs's
  $350M, Shimonoseki, Puyi/Manchukuo, KMT 1912, Xinhai 1911). Verdict tags are
  used ONLY where an Isaacs claim is actually weighed.
- 85 notes total; both streams restart per chapter; ids n-ch01-N / en-ch01-r.

### Source discrepancies (kept visible, not repaired)
- **Orphan endnote 31.** Isaacs's back matter numbers ch01's notes 1-31, but
  only 30 reference marks appear in the text (plus his 2 asterisks). His note 31
  ("Fong, China's Industrialization, Tables 1 a and 1 b") duplicates note 29 and
  has NO in-text anchor. Its citation is already carried by author note 31 in
  this edition (endnote 29), so nothing is lost; per the voice-gate ruling this
  bibliographic quirk is logged here, not made a reader footnote.
- **Idiosyncratic romanization.** Isaacs spells the Taiping leader "Hung
  Tsui-chuen" (standard WG: Hung Hsiu-ch'uan; pinyin Hong Xiuquan). Kept in the
  body as printed; flagged in the glossary and his first-appearance note.

### Deliberate note-density decisions
- The trivial place-name tier (Kaiping, Woosung, Lanchow, and the one-off
  provincial capitals Changsha/Chengtu) is covered in the GLOSSARY but NOT
  footnoted, to avoid list-glossing padding. Load-bearing places are footnoted
  at first appearance (Canton, Kwangsi, Manchuria, Annam, Tientsin, Hankow,
  Kiangsu, the Hunan/Hupeh/Szechwan cluster).

### Context / batch-size calibration
- This batch consumed roughly 420K tokens of session budget. A large part was
  ONE-TIME infrastructure that later batches skip (the extractor + its
  de-hyphenation tuning, the two-stream and block-quote builder features,
  qa_epub's rewrite, the annotation critic prompt, the regression test), plus
  three isolated blind-critique subagent runs (~250K combined, in their own

  fact-check the editorial layer, one critique round) is far lower.

  first-appearance editorial notes. Later body chapters inherit most of the cast
  and run lighter. Grouping recommendation carried into HANDOFF.
