NOTE-ADD p088
ANCHOR: A wild goose’s claw leaves its mark in the snow
NOTE: Yang Muchu turns a famous image of Su Shi (&#33487;&#36711;, 1037&#8211;1101), the great Song poet: 人生到处知何似，应似飞鸿踏雪泥&#8212;&#8216;to what may a life be likened, wherever it goes? Surely to a wild goose treading the snow-mud, that leaves the chance print of its claws before it flies on, careless of east or west&#8217; (<i>On the Snow at Mianchi, in Answer to Ziyou</i>, 1061). The image, 雪泥鸿爪, became the stock phrase for the faint, accidental traces the past leaves behind. A-Chu means that Young Tang, for all his professed heartlessness, must somewhere carry the mark of an old love. Corroborated.
WHY: the line is a specific Su Shi allusion whose weight&#8212;the fleeting trace left by a vanished past&#8212;a Western reader cannot recover from the bare image.

NOTE-ADD p169
ANCHOR: seven or eight hundred million fabi
NOTE: <i>Fabi</i> (&#27861;&#24065;, &#8216;legal tender&#8217;) was the paper currency the Nationalist government made China&#8217;s sole legal money in the currency reform of November 1935, taking the country off the silver standard and calling in privately held silver. The term is used a little ahead of its time in this 1932 scene, loosely for the national paper currency; the point is simply that the Dongyang Company&#8217;s sales run to hundreds of millions. Corroborated.
WHY: a Western reader has no idea what &#8216;fabi&#8217; is; it is the era&#8217;s national paper money, and REGISTER_PASS lists the currency as note-worthy.

NOTE-ADD p175
ANCHOR: comprador capitalists
NOTE: A <i>comprador</i> (&#20080;&#21150;, <i>maiban</i>; the word is Portuguese for &#8216;buyer&#8217;) was the Chinese manager who ran a foreign firm&#8217;s business in the treaty ports&#8212;its go-between with local merchants, banks, and officials. Grown rich as the indispensable middlemen of foreign trade, the compradors became a byword in nationalist and Marxist rhetoric for a native bourgeoisie that served imperial capital against China&#8217;s own interest, which is exactly the contempt A-Chu loads into the phrase. Corroborated.
WHY: &#8216;comprador&#8217; is an unfamiliar term of art for the foreign firms&#8217; Chinese agents, and it carries the political charge of A-Chu&#8217;s accusation.
