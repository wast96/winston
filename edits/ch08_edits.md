ANCHOR: then turned left into Park Road
NOTE: <i>Park Road</i> (派克路, today Huanghe Road), a short street along the eastern edge of the racecourse, ran past the Carlton Theatre where the Turandot of this chapter plays; it lay in the bright amusement quarter north of the race club. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: a Guangyi Book Company edition
NOTE: The <i>Guangyi Book Company</i> (广益书局) was a Shanghai commercial publisher (from 1900) known for cheap popular editions of vernacular fiction and reference works such as this joke-book; a specific Guangyi printing of the <i>Xiaolin Guangji</i> is likely but not separately attested. (The publisher corroborated; this edition uncorroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: A charitable donation of one national dollar is deducted
NOTE: The ticket's line reflects the Race Club's practice of skimming a fixed charity levy from every Grand Champagne stake; a <i>national dollar</i> (国币) was one silver yuan, the standard unit of the Nationalist currency before the paper <i>fabi</i> of 1935. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: newly installed neon lights
NOTE: <i>Neon</i> advertising signs (霓虹灯) were a new marvel of Shanghai's nightscape&#8212;the first had appeared on Nanjing Road in 1926&#8212;and by 1933 traced the skyline of the modern city. (Corroborated.)
