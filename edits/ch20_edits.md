# ---- RULE R1-1: wrong-meaning idiom ("all but") ----

### p018 [T2] RECAST
OLD: and we who took part in the actual work were all but the executors of that policy
NEW: and we who took part in the actual work were, every one of us, executors of that policy
WHY: 参与实际工作的我等，都是政策的执行者 = "we were ALL executors of that policy". English "all but the executors" idiomatically means "almost/everything except the executors" — a wrong-meaning calque (RULE R1-1). 都是 = "all were" -> "every one of us ... were executors".
