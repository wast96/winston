ANCHOR: A vase of flowers was the safe signal
NOTE: A prearranged <i>safe signal</i>&#8212;here a vase of flowers in the window (elsewhere a lit lamp, a drawn curtain)&#8212;told an approaching comrade that the house was not being watched; its absence meant danger. Such all-clear signs were standard underground tradecraft. (Texture.)

ANCHOR: hand her a cigarette or a wax pellet
NOTE: A <i>wax pellet</i> (蜡丸) is a written message rolled tight and sealed in a ball of wax&#8212;waterproof, easily hidden or swallowed, and destroyed by dropping it in water; the method of carrying secret dispatches is an old one, attested since the Song dynasty. (Corroborated.)

ANCHOR: We mustn't play the exploiting class
NOTE: <i>The exploiting class</i> (剥削阶级) is the Marxist category of those who live off others' labor&#8212;here the landlords; Ling Wen's refusal to raise the rent is a point of Communist principle kept up even inside her cover as a sub-letting landlady. (Texture.)
