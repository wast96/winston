NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: The fifteenth of the last month of the year
NOTE: <i>The fifteenth of the last month of the year</i> renders 腊月十五, a date in 腊月 (<i>layue</i>), the twelfth and last month of the Chinese lunar calendar, whose close brings the New Year. The novel opens in the days before the Lunar New Year, which in 1933 fell on January&#160;26; almost the whole action of the book plays out across that turn of the year. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: The detective squad at Baiyunguan
NOTE: <i>Baiyunguan</i> (白云观), the 'White Cloud' Daoist temple in the old Chinese city, gave its name to a nearby base and lock-up of the Songhu Garrison Command's detective squad; real prisoners were held there, among them the leader Deng Yanda (邓演达) in 1931. It is from this Nanshi post, not from Longhua, that the squad musters. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the Nanshi police station
NOTE: <i>Nanshi</i> (南市, 'the southern market') was the old walled Chinese city and its district, south of the foreign concessions and under Chinese administration; its police were a Chinese force ('one of our own, placed there') separate from the Settlement's Municipal Police and the French Concession's own&#8212;one of the three jurisdictions whose seams the underground works. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: down Suzhou Creek
NOTE: <i>Suzhou Creek</i> (苏州河), the tidal lower course of the Wusong River, winds through the north of the city to meet the Huangpu; it was old Shanghai's working waterway, and the market's fresh produce comes down it by boat each morning. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the Gezhi Public School
NOTE: The <i>Gezhi Public School</i> (格致公学) was a real English-style boys' school in the central International Settlement, run by the Municipal Council (工部局) from 1917&#8212;the former Chinese Polytechnic (格致书院) taken under foreign management. It survives today as the Gezhi Middle School. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: a whole orchestra
NOTE: The orchestra Qin Chuan'an comes to hear rehearse is the Shanghai Municipal Orchestra (工部局乐队), the ensemble kept up by the Municipal Council of the International Settlement; under its Italian conductor Mario Paci (梅百器) it was reckoned the finest orchestra in the Far East, and gave its concerts at the Town Hall and the Lyceum Theatre. That it should rehearse here in the World Hotel is the novel's own placement. (The orchestra corroborated; the venue the novel's.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: kept a gramophone in his own clinic
NOTE: A <i>gramophone</i> and a shelf of records were, in 1933, the mark of a Westernized man of means; Qin Chuan'an's, recurring through the book, is of a piece with his German-hospital training and his taste for the orchestra. (Texture.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: an Indanthrene-blue cloth gown
NOTE: <i>Indanthrene</i> blue (阴丹士林) was a German synthetic vat dye, discovered by the BASF chemist René Bohn in 1901 and far more colorfast than indigo; the cloth dyed with it, heavily advertised in China from the late 1920s, became the signature deep blue of students' and scholars' long gowns and of the <i>qipao</i>. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the Central Police Station
NOTE: The <i>Central Police Station</i> (中央捕房), on Foochow Road, was the headquarters of the Shanghai Municipal Police, the force of the foreign-run International Settlement; its many Chinese detectives, of whom Detective Yao is one, did the ground-level work and dealt directly with the Nationalist services. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the station's Political Branch
NOTE: The <i>Political Branch</i> is the Shanghai Municipal Police's Special Branch (established 1925), the Settlement force's political-intelligence arm, which kept extensive files on the Communists and the Comintern. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: extraditing prisoners
NOTE: The passage describes a real accommodation. Through the later 1920s and early 1930s the Shanghai Municipal Police worked with the Nationalist authorities to share intelligence and to hand over&#8212;<i>extradite</i>&#8212;arrested Communists from the Settlement to Chinese jurisdiction. Because the Longhua garrison could not lawfully make arrests inside the foreign Settlement, this cooperation is what makes the whole operation possible. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the Judge Advocate's office of the Longhua Garrison Command
NOTE: The <i>Judge Advocate's office</i> (军法处) was the military-justice department of the Songhu (Shanghai&#8211;Woosung) Garrison Command, set up in 1928; it tried 'political' and Communist cases outside the ordinary courts and ran the detentions and executions at Longhua. You Tianxiao's detective squad operates under it. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: once they had joined the action group
NOTE: An <i>action group</i> (行动小组) was a task cell assembled for one operation from comrades of different lines, who were required to break with their former work and know only one another; the compartmentalization is a security measure, and the reason the arrested strangers can betray so little. (Texture.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the Anhui Hatchet Gang
NOTE: The <i>Hatchet Gang</i> (斧头帮) was the assassination network of the Anhui-born Wang Yaqiao (王亚樵); its gunmen really did shoot Zhao Tieqiao (赵铁桥), general manager of the China Merchants Steam Navigation Company, at the company's door on July&#160;24, 1930. That Li Guojie set the killing on, amid the struggle for control of the company, is the received account rather than a documented fact. (The gang and the killing corroborated; Li's part uncorroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: laid a hand on the tiger's backside
NOTE: To have <i>laid a hand on the tiger's backside</i> renders 摸老虎屁股, to provoke someone too powerful and dangerous to be crossed with impunity. (Idiom.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: fish and dragons mingled
NOTE: <i>Fish and dragons mingled</i> renders 鱼龙混杂, an idiom for a place where the worthy and the worthless, honest men and rogues, are all thrown in together. (Idiom.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: pai gow tiles
NOTE: <i>Pai gow</i> (牌九, 'make nine') is a gambling game played with a set of thirty-two Chinese dominoes (骨牌); the comrades' cover is that each has brought a few dominoes and they are assembling a set between them. That the dominoes serve as the meeting's recognition tokens, and the dice as the sign of the man 'sent from above,' is the mechanism the whole plot turns on. (Corroborated.)
