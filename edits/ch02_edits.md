## --- dialogue / scene naturalness (the priority) ---

### p201 [T1] RECAST
OLD: "This younger brother has been out in the world, and muddled for a good while."
NEW: "I've been out in the world a good while, and I got muddled."
WHY: the humble self-reference 兄弟 rendered as third-person "This younger brother" is an opaque calque; naturalize to a contracted first person so the exchange lands (Zhou's retort "You may be muddled. I'm not." then echoes "muddled"). Keeps: out in the world, muddled, a good while. Content identical.

### p15 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: Mao Zedong charged Huang Lin:
NEW: Mao Zedong ordered Huang Lin:
WHY: "charged" is archaic for an urgent crisis order (命令); "ordered" is the plain word and reads as real speech. Content identical.

## --- wrong idioms / wrong-sense collocations ---

### p449 [T3] TOUCH

NEW: half coaxing, half hauling him, they forced him into the small car
