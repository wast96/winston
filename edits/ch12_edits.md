# The content-frozen register subset: a tone error that inverted the author's
# admiration, a real number-reading ambiguity, a category slip, register/idiom

### p019 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: China's public-security Hundred-and-Eight were full of themselves, every one.
NEW: China's public-security Hundred-and-Eight were riding high, every one.
WHY: "full of themselves" means conceited and inverts the author's admiring stance (意气风发 = high-spirited); the next sentence aggrandizes them, not mocks. "riding high" keeps the spirited sense.

### p021 [T5] TOUCH
OLD: they took up their weapons and dug in, digging trenches around Xibaipo
NEW: they took up their weapons and dug trenches around Xibaipo
WHY: "dug in, digging" repeats the root; the single verb carries it. Same action.

### p025 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: The Social Affairs Department mustered its intelligence chieftains from every quarter
NEW: The Social Affairs Department mustered its intelligence chiefs from every quarter
WHY: "chieftains" carries a bandit/tribal register wrong for the cadres named in the formal roster that follows.

### p027 [T7] TOUCH
OLD: and by June 3,533 agents had registered in all.
NEW: and by June, 3,533 agents had registered in all.
WHY: "by June 3,533" misreads as the date June 3; a comma disambiguates. Both June and the figure 3,533 are unchanged.

### p033 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: the sedan of the Beiping mayor, Nie Rongzhen, was fired on
NEW: the car of the Beiping mayor, Nie Rongzhen, was fired on
WHY: in a 1949 setting "sedan" risks reading as a sedan chair; 轿车 is a car. Clarity only.

### p057 [T6] TOUCH
OLD: Nanjing's police chief changed hands fast indeed:
NEW: The post of Nanjing police chief changed hands fast indeed:
WHY: a person does not "change hands"; it is the office that turns over (the succession follows). Sense unchanged.

### p081 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: began reporting in to the newly hung-out shingle of the public-security bureau
NEW: began reporting in to the newly opened public-security bureau
WHY: "hung out a shingle" is an American idiom for a lawyer/doctor opening a practice and jars for a security bureau; 新挂牌 means newly established. Sense unchanged.

### p089 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: The public-security cadres out of the yellow earth were of high enough quality
NEW: The public-security cadres out of the yellow earth proved more than equal to the task
WHY: "of high enough quality" is flat HR-speak (素质够高); the plain sense is that the peasant-origin cadres were competent enough. Content unchanged.

### p117 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: or growing into a tail too big to wag
NEW: or growing too unwieldy to control
WHY: 尾大不掉 has no parsable image in English ("a tail too big to wag" reads as a garble); naturalize to the sense (an agency grown uncontrollable). Content unchanged.

### p151 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: the Two Chens case drew in no few Canton public-security cadres as well
NEW: the Two Chens case drew in quite a few Canton public-security cadres as well
WHY: "no few" is a litotes calque of 不少; "quite a few" is the plain equivalent. Same quantity sense.

### p157 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: called for a superb art of work
NEW: called for consummate skill
WHY: "a superb art of work" calques 高超的工作艺术; "consummate skill" is the idiomatic equivalent. Content unchanged.

### p181 [T5] TOUCH
OLD: The main matter of this book is the intelligence and security work before the founding of the state
NEW: The main subject of this book is the intelligence and security work before the founding of the state
WHY: "The main matter ... is ... a matter of" repeats "matter" in one sentence; "subject" clears the echo. Content unchanged.

### p185 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: Is the intelligence work of that war abroad not a new book in itself?
NEW: The intelligence work of that war abroad is a new book in itself.
WHY: convert the self-answering 岂不 docent question to the declarative it already implies (part of the coda's list of histories still to be written). Assertion unchanged.

### p201 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: The building of the rule of law is a thing still in progress.
NEW: The building of the rule of law is still a work in progress.
WHY: "a thing still in progress" is flat translationese; "a work in progress" is the idiom. Content unchanged.
