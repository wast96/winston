NOTE: The <i>Settlement Volunteers</i> are the Shanghai Volunteer Corps (万国商团), the International Settlement&#8217;s citizen militia, founded in 1853 and by the early 1930s a multinational body of some twenty companies &#8212; British, American, Chinese, Portuguese, a White Russian regiment, and a Japanese company (formed 1907) among them. The Corps kept a rifle range at the northern edge of the Settlement beside Hongkou Park, where its members drilled with live ammunition. (Real; corroborated.)
