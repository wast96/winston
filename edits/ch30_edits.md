# ==== T1 antique function words (kill list; §3.2 T1) ====

### p001 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: this half-bright, half-clouded weather was besides the freshest, most agreeable day
NEW: this half-bright, half-clouded weather was, what was more, the freshest, most agreeable day
WHY: 况且 = "moreover / what was more," not the sentence-adverb "besides."

### p008 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: And bethinking me that I had been trapped by Zhou Xiyuan
NEW: And thinking that I had been trapped by Zhou Xiyuan
WHY: 又想到 = "thinking"; archaic "bethinking me" fails the read-aloud test.

### p012 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: Of a sudden I bethought me of the papers in my pocket
NEW: Suddenly I remembered the papers in my pocket
WHY: 忽然想起 = "suddenly remembered"; kills "of a sudden" + archaic "bethought me."

### p012 [T1] RECAST
OLD: There was no help for it, so throw it away myself I did.
NEW: There was nothing to do but throw it away myself.
WHY: 没法子…只好自己去扔 = "nothing to do but"; kills "no help for it" + the fronted-object inversion "throw it away myself I did" (T2).

### p014 [T2] RECAST
