ANCHOR: Sancheng Fang, Tongfu Li
NOTE: Shanghai lane addresses stack their units: a named compound of terraced houses (a <i>fang</i> 坊 or <i>li</i> 里&#8212;here 'Sancheng Fang, Tongfu Li'), a numbered lane (<i>long</i> 弄) within it, and a house number. The layered address is itself a small map of the lane-house city. (Texture.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the archway house at the lane mouth
NOTE: The <i>archway house</i> (过街楼) is a room built out over the mouth of a lane, spanning the entrance passage; the clinic occupies one such upper room. (Texture.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: Western-Medicine Clinic
NOTE: A <i>Western-Medicine Clinic</i> (西医诊所) advertised modern, foreign-trained scientific medicine, as against a practitioner of Chinese medicine; the distinction, and the doctor's German-hospital training, place Qin Chuan'an among the Westernized professional class. (Texture.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: he could still be carrying his seal on him
NOTE: A <i>seal</i> (图章/私章), a small stamp cut with one's name, served as a personal signature on Chinese documents and letters; Tian Fei's point is that the letter is validated with such a seal, which an ordinary bank clerk would hardly carry through arrest and prison. (Texture.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: like kites with cut strings
NOTE: <i>Kites with cut strings</i> (断线的风筝) is the idiom for people or things adrift and past all control&#8212;here the comrades cut off from the organization. (Idiom.)
