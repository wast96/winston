ANCHOR: warming, restorative medicine
NOTE: In Chinese medicine health is a balance of heat and cold, and remedies are classed to match: a &#8216;warming, restorative&#8217; (温补) prescription of tonic herbs is meant to build up a depleted, &#8216;cold&#8217; constitution. NOTE-ADD p039
ANCHOR: slipped off her own jade bracelet
NOTE: Jade (玉) was the most treasured of stones in China, an emblem of purity, virtue, and constancy; a jade bangle, worn snug on the wrist and not easily drawn off, was a woman&#8217;s intimate, lifelong ornament, often a betrothal or love token. For He Yashu to leave hers in A-Chu&#8217;s pocket is thus a charged, deliberately compromising gift&#8212;which is why its reappearance can be turned into a threat to her name. Corroborated.
WHY: a Western reader misses that a jade bangle is an intimate, prized love-token, which is what makes the gesture and its exposure so fraught.
