NOTE-ADD p011

NOTE: After the Nationalists and Communists broke apart in 1927 in a bloody purge&#8212;the &#8216;White Terror,&#8217; whose slaughter of Communists in Shanghai that April all but destroyed the Party in the cities&#8212;the CCP survived there only as a deep clandestine network.
