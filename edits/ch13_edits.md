# ch13 register-pass edits — R04 (the Afterword)
#
# The Afterword is the author speaking in his own voice, and it is the single
# most translationese-dense unit in the book (13 nominalizations in 1882 words,
# plus the "the honor" / "relatively" / "of their own" tics the blind critique
# and the batteries both flag). The modern-essayist register target applies
# most directly here, so the edit count is high by design; this is the one unit
# where near-every paragraph earns a touch. Every NEW still preserves its OLD's
# propositional content exactly: no fact, date, title, claim, or the author's
# modest/reflective STANCE is changed. His scare quotes, the "buried treasure"
# metaphor, the four-period/four-volume scheme, and the contact block are kept.
# There are no note or figure anchors in this unit.

### p003 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: is enough to thrill a man.
NEW: is a thrilling thing.
WHY: "thrill a man" uses a wooden generic "a man" for the author's own feeling; "a thrilling thing" is plain. Sense unchanged.

### p005a [T3] TOUCH
OLD: every edition has added important new matter
NEW: every edition has added important new material
WHY: "matter" is a bald calque of 内容/材料; "material" is the natural word. Content unchanged.

### p005b [T3] TOUCH
OLD: but because the subject is too good
NEW: but because the subject is so good
WHY: "too good" reads as "too good for something"; the antithesis (not my writing, but the subject) wants "so good." Sense unchanged.

### p007 [T3] RECAST
OLD: The author has had the honor of interviewing, face to face, many who lived the hidden front themselves.
NEW: I have had the good fortune to interview, face to face, many who served on the hidden front themselves.
WHY: the essay elsewhere says "I"; the third-person "The author has had the honor" plus "lived the hidden front" is stiff translationese. "Good fortune" keeps the modest 有幸; "served on" fixes the collocation. Same claim.

### p009 [T3] RECAST
OLD: The book has the honor, one may say, of being the first relatively complete monograph on China's hidden front.
NEW: The book is, one may say, the first reasonably complete monograph on China's hidden front.
WHY: "the honor ... of being" is the third repeat of the modesty formula (vary it; the hedging "one may say" carries the modesty); "relatively complete" is the 比较 hedge tic. Same claim.

### p011 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: not to be dug out in one go, not to be written out in ten years.
NEW: not to be dug out all at once, nor exhausted in ten years of writing.
WHY: "in one go" clashes colloquially and "written out" is the wrong idiom; the sense is that ten years of writing could not exhaust it. Content unchanged.

### p013a [T3] TOUCH
OLD: It reflects the fruit of my interviewing since the first edition
NEW: It draws on the interviews I have done since the first edition
WHY: "the fruit of my interviewing" is an abstract calque (采访成果); "the interviews I have done" is concrete. Content unchanged.

### p013b [T3] TOUCH
OLD: where many an "old intelligence hand" appears in person.
NEW: where many an old intelligence hand appears in his own words.
WHY: "appears in person" is odd for figures appearing in the notes; "in his own words" is the sense. Content unchanged.

### p015a [T4] TOUCH
OLD: Not only was matter added; the level was raised.
NEW: It was not only that material was added; the treatment went deeper.
WHY: "matter" and "the level was raised" are bald calques (内容/水平提高); the plain sense is that material was added and the treatment deepened. Content unchanged.

### p015b [T3] TOUCH
OLD: it caught a lasting wave of spy-thriller enthusiasm
NEW: it coincided with a lasting wave of spy-thriller enthusiasm
WHY: a book does not "catch" a wave it then rides; 赶上 here means the edition came out just as the wave was rising. Sense unchanged.

### p015c [T4] TOUCH
OLD: The ordering of the material was a process of ordering my own thoughts
NEW: Arranging the material meant arranging my own thoughts
WHY: de-nominalize and clear the "ordering ... ordering" echo. Content unchanged.

### p015d [T3] TOUCH
OLD: among all the trades of modern China, was the earliest to enter the front rank of the world
NEW: of all the fields of modern China, was the earliest to reach world-class standing
WHY: "trades" is wrong register for 行业; "enter the front rank of the world" is a calque of 进入世界前列. Same claim (earliest field to reach world class).

### p017 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: a batch of works titled "The Secret War of So-and-so" has appeared
NEW: a number of works titled "The Secret War of So-and-so" have appeared
WHY: "a batch of works" calques 一批; "a number of ... have" also fixes the agreement. Content unchanged.

### p021 [T3] RECAST
OLD: the united front draws intelligence along, diplomacy screens intelligence, and you can hardly hold it within bounds
NEW: the united front carries intelligence with it, diplomacy gives it cover, and it can hardly be held within bounds
WHY: "draws intelligence along" and "screens intelligence" are opaque calqued verbs (带/掩护), and the second-person "you" clashes with the register. Same sense.

### p021b [T3] TOUCH
OLD: a name relatively vague and yet quite alluring
NEW: a name at once vague and yet compelling
WHY: the 比较 hedge "relatively" and "alluring" (wrong register for a term) both flatten; "at once vague and yet compelling" keeps the paradox. Content unchanged.

WHY: "is exactly the scope of" is an ungrammatical equation; the author equates his subject with the domain of national security. Same claim.

### p025a [T3] TOUCH

WHY: "live ... apart from" is a calque of 一天也离不开; "survive ... without" is the idiom. Sense unchanged.

### p025c [T6] TOUCH

NEW: as its two vice-chairmen — a measure of the very high standing security work now holds.
WHY: "enough to show" dangles off the list; recast as an appositive. Same point.

### p027 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: is, in turn, full of Chinese character
NEW: has, in turn, a distinctly Chinese character
WHY: "full of Chinese character" calques 充满中国特色; "a distinctly Chinese character" is idiomatic. Content unchanged.

### p033 [T5] RECAST
OLD: forming a character of their own, a revolutionary road of their own, a guiding thought of their own, and a system of intelligence and security work of their own.
NEW: forming a character, a revolutionary road, a guiding ideology, and an intelligence-and-security system all their own.
WHY: the fourfold "of their own" (自己的) is a wearing anaphora; one "all their own" carries the possessive for the whole series. Content unchanged.

WHY: "History makes books" reads cryptic; "gives rise to" is the sense (a history this large calls for books). Content unchanged.

### p059a [T3] TOUCH

### p059b [T3] TOUCH
