# ch25 Jiaoli — R3 densification

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: flip like a salted fish come back to life
NOTE: <i>Flip like a salted fish come back to life</i> renders 咸鱼翻身 (&#8216;the salted fish turns over&#8217;): a salted, dried fish is as good as dead, so for one to flip and revive is for a ruined, hopeless man to rise in the world again against all odds. Cui Wentai, a wanted traitor clutching five gold bars he believes are his own, is casting himself as just such a comeback. (Idiom; the image is the point.)
