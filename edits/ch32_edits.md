ANCHOR: mandarin ducks and butterflies
NOTE: &#8216;Mandarin Ducks and Butterflies&#8217; (鸳鸯蝴蝶派) was a school of popular fiction that flourished in Shanghai from the 1910s into the 1930s, turning out sentimental love stories &#8212; often of thwarted or tragic romance &#8212; alongside knight-errant, detective, and scandal tales for a mass readership. The name mocks its stock lovers, forever paired like mandarin ducks and butterflies. The New Culture intellectuals of the day despised the genre as frivolous escapism, which is the disdain behind A-Chu&#8217;s calling such books literary &#8216;trash.&#8217; Corroborated.
WHY: without the note a Western reader takes &#8216;mandarin-duck-and-butterfly dream&#8217; books for nonsense and misses that this was a real, much-derided commercial fiction school of 1910s&#8211;30s Shanghai.
