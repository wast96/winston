# ==== T1 antique function words (kill list; §3.2 T1) ====

### p002 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: and it proves besides that the Communist Party was really nothing so very formidable.
NEW: and it proves, too, that the Communist Party was really nothing so very formidable.
WHY: 同时也证明了 = additive 也 "too"; kills the sentence-adverb "besides."

### p004 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: And so the gravest error in all history was in this wise cast
NEW: And so the gravest error in all history was thus cast
WHY: 就此铸成 = "thus cast"; kills the archaic "in this wise."

### p005 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: and brought pressure to bear on us besides, holding fast to their absurd insistence
NEW: and brought pressure to bear on us as well, holding fast to their absurd insistence
WHY: 且对我施用压力 = "as well"; kills the sentence-adverb "besides."

### p016 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: feeling keenly how the gathering of intelligence grew day by day harder, we bethought us: why not use the way of assault
NEW: feeling keenly how the gathering of intelligence grew day by day harder, it occurred to us: why not use the way of assault
WHY: 才想到 = "it occurred to us"; kills the archaic "bethought us."

### p026 [T1] TOUCH
OLD: and every region on a sudden gave word of peril.
NEW: and every region suddenly gave word of peril.
WHY: 各地区亦顿告吃紧 = 顿 "suddenly"; kills "on a sudden."
