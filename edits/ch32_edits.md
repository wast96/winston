ANCHOR: the Great Stage on Second Avenue
NOTE: The <i>Great Stage</i> (大舞台) on Second Avenue (二马路, today Jiujiang Road) was a real Peking-opera house, opened about 1909 as the &#8216;Civilized Great Stage&#8217; (文明大舞台) and bought in 1919 by the Green Gang boss Huang Jinrong (黄金荣), who renamed it the 荣记大舞台; rebuilt in 1934, it survives as the People&#8217;s Grand Stage (上海人民大舞台). It is a separate house from the Tianchan on Fuzhou Road, with which it is often confused. (Real; corroborated.)
