# ch00 (Preface) register-pass edits — R01

# The preface greps ZERO on every tic battery; it is already modern on syntax.
# Blind critique surfaced ~37 items; most are content-risky (frozen pass),
# the author's deliberate devices (the mining/water/treasure metaphors, the
# "Who knew...?" reveal-questions, the one-line punch fragments), or matters of
# taste where the current rendering is defensible ("stank to high heaven" is the
# DECIDED rendering of 臭不可闻 per STYLE lang-zh; the inclusive "I"/"we" is a
# KEEP-list feature). What follows are the clear, content-preserving register
# fixes only. MOST paragraphs LEAVE.

### p13 [T6] RECAST
OLD: This work, intelligence and security both, the profession of secret struggle, becomes in itself the deepest secret in the world.
NEW: This work, both intelligence and security, is the profession of secret struggle, and it is itself the deepest secret in the world.
WHY: Three stacked appositives (base failure mode #1, transferred syntax); recast to move forward. Preserves note anchor "the deepest secret in the world". Content identical.

### p23 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: When every trade in China lagged behind, its intelligence and security work
NEW: When every other field in China lagged behind, its intelligence and security work
WHY: 百业/各行各业 -> "every trade" reads as commerce; "every field" is the word an English writer reaches for (lang-zh collocation-calque test). Content identical (all walks of life).

### p41 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: Such stories cannot fail to move.
NEW: Stories like these are bound to move anyone.
WHY: 不能不令人感动 carried as objectless litotes ("cannot fail to move" with no object); plain the could-not-but class and supply the object. One-line punch preserved.

### p49 [T6] TOUCH
OLD: and to the specialists whose corrections did still more to sharpen my grasp of the subject.
NEW: and to the specialists whose corrections sharpened my grasp of the subject even further.
WHY: "did still more to sharpen" is stilted periphrasis; the finite verb is cleaner. Content identical.

### p51 [T6] TOUCH
OLD: The newly upgraded illustrated edition I now bring out is, in a sense, an expanded and revised one.
NEW: The new illustrated edition I now bring out is, in a sense, expanded and revised.
WHY: "upgraded" is a tech-register word; "is ... an expanded and revised one" is circular. Keeps the authorial "I now bring out" (inclusive-voice KEEP). Content identical (图文升级版 = the new illustrated edition, expanded and revised).

### p57 [T6] RECAST
OLD: And that cultural inheritance is a task for reader and author to take up together.
NEW: And passing on that cultural heritage is a task for reader and author to take up together.
WHY: "inheritance is a task" mismatches subject and predicate (the thing inherited is not a task); 传承 is the act of passing on. Fixes the book's closing sentence. Content identical.
