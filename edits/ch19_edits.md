ANCHOR: nine-dragon shells, meteor-crackers
NOTE: <i>Nine-dragon shells</i> and <i>meteor-crackers</i> (九龙, 流星) are evocative names from the long Chinese repertoire of fireworks &#8212; &#8216;meteors&#8217; for a rising streak of sparks, &#8216;nine dragons&#8217; echoing the old set-piece 九龙取水 (&#8216;nine dragons drawing water&#8217;) &#8212; descriptive effect-names rather than fixed modern product types. (The festival custom and the &#8216;meteor&#8217; type are corroborated; the names are traditional and literary.)
