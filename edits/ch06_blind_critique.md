1. "A Heavy Charge, Pressing Onward" (title; also "take up his heavy charge," "how did this heavy charge come to be laid on him") — "heavy charge" is a calque of 重任 with a fake-antique ring. Fix: "a heavy responsibility" / "resume his heavy responsibilities."

2. "Since I had passed through enlisted training and graduated and been assigned out" — stacked "and...and" chain; "assigned out" is not English. Fix: "Since finishing enlisted training, graduating, and receiving my first posting."

3. "his bearing awe-inspiring and stern, so that no one dared look him full in the face" — transferred 使人不敢 syntax; only the narrator is present, so "no one" misfires. Fix: "so stern that I hardly dared meet his eye."

4. "asked after such family matters as my native province and county" — "such X as" list-frame calque of 诸如…等 (recurs: "such things as message-carrying," "such items as living expenses," "such posts as"). Fix: "asked about my family — my home province and county, whether my parents were living..."

5. "asking and listening as he went" — 边问边听 calque. Fix: "listening as he asked, and checking the roster against my answers."

6. "The military work of the troops has grown strange to me now" — 生疏 calqued as "grown strange"; wooden dialogue. Fix: "I've grown rusty at troop work; I'd gladly learn anything else."

7. "This audience lasted, from first to last, about three minutes" — 前后 calque, redundant. Fix: "The audience lasted about three minutes in all."

8. "the process being so simple, I felt nothing special at the time" — absolute construction plus 没什么特别感觉 calque. Fix: "It was all so brief that I thought nothing of it at the time."

9. "making a selection through observation; as to his standard, that only the old gentleman himself would know" — nominalization plus "as to" topic-marker calque (the "as to/as for" frame recurs a dozen times). Fix: "sizing us up as he went; what his criteria were, only the old man knew."

10. "marked out to open a class and undergo training" — 开班 calque; "undergo training" bureaucratese. Fix: "picked for a special training class."

11. "This training class was styled 'Special,' and in fact it truly was different from the ordinary run" — "styled" fake-antique; "truly was" filler. Fix: "The class was called 'Special,' and it was."

12. "making do as best we could, we set a few... tables... and so we managed" — "making do" and "managed" say the same thing twice. Fix: cut one.

13. "coming late or leaving early was never interfered with" — passive calque of 不受干涉. Fix: "no one minded if you came late or left early."

14. "we too knew well enough to conduct ourselves with self-respect" — calque of 我们也知道自爱. Fix: "and precisely because of that, we policed ourselves."

15. "apart from specially engaged lectures" — 特聘 calque. Fix: "apart from the occasional guest lecture."

16. "write up what we had gained into notes" — 心得 calqued as "what we had gained." Fix: "write up our reflections as notes."

17. "not even a third of us fourteen could truly read them 'through,' and I was one of the rest" — "one of the rest" is backwards litotes. Fix: "and I was not among them."

18. "its arrangement was neat, its cleaning spotless" — nominal topic-comment strings transferred from Chinese. Fix: "it was neatly kept and spotlessly clean."

19. "the crowd would answer, 'Begin,' and then all fell to together" — "the crowd" wrong register for cadets; "fell to together" awkward collision. Fix: "we would answer 'Begin,' and everyone fell to."

20. "The eating took about ten minutes" — gerund-subject calque. Fix: "The meal took about ten minutes."

21. "exhortation on matters of the spirit" — 精神讲话 calque. Fix: "his talk on moral matters."

22. "Those who could speak freely and reply without a stumble were not often seen" — 不多见 existential calque. Fix: "Few could answer fluently, without stumbling."

23. "yet there were also those itching to try" — 也有…的 existential calque. Fix: "though a few itched for the chance."

24. "at the urging of the senior elder brothers" — "senior elder brothers" doubles the kinship term (recurs constantly: "seven senior elder brothers," "the two senior elder brothers"). Fix: "senior classmates," gloss the brotherhood convention once.

25. "put our names to a report requesting the Commandant to permit the restoration of our party membership" — nominalization pile-up. Fix: "petitioned the Commandant to restore our party membership and enrollment."

26. "The Commandant wrote his ruling in brush" — "in brush" calque. Fix: "wrote his decision with a brush."

27. "has, over these several decades of running about north and south, east and west, ended up who knows where" — 东奔西跑 four-character calque wedged mid-clause. Fix: "was lost somewhere in decades of moving about the country."

28. "it was most likely destroyed by my own hand" — passive plus fake-antique "by my own hand" (the 亲手/亲自 tic recurs: "by his own hand directed," "came in person" many times). Fix: "I most likely destroyed it myself."

29. "the youngest in years and the lowest in class" — "in years" redundant. Fix: "the youngest, and of the lowest class."

30. "the present writer" / "the writer" (at least eight occurrences) — third-person self-reference calquing 笔者; in English memoir it reads coy. Fix: "myself" / "me."

31. "was not the same, but was granted according to class in descending order" — bureaucratic negative. Fix: "varied by class, in descending order:".

32. "for a single man with no family to carry" — 没有家累 calque. Fix: "with no family to support."

33. "steeped in an especially strong literary temper" — you steep in liquid, not temper; 文艺气息很浓 calque. Fix: "with a strong literary streak."

34. "lay all day over his desk writing new-style verse" — wrong verb. Fix: "sprawled at his desk all day."

35. "two whole months passed, we all graduated and left, and we never saw them again.—Both were Cantonese" — comma-spliced run capped with a dash after a period (Chinese punctuation habit). Fix: break into sentences; drop the dash.

36. "Since it is called 'secret,' though the years are long gone by and things have changed, it is still best to keep it so" — 既然说是秘密 calque; three hedging clauses before the point. Fix: "It was secret then, and secret it had better remain."

37. "the affair was one of grave moment, and it holds a page too in the history of political-party activity" — "of grave moment" fake-antique; "holds a page too" calque of 也占一页. Fix: "it was a serious business, and it has its page in party history."

38. "To speak now of what befell the twelve of us:" — 再说 discourse-marker calque plus "befell" (the "To speak of/To speak again of" signpost recurs several times). Fix: "As for the twelve of us who remained:".

39. "just when I stood at a loss, no way forward and no way back" — 进退两难 doubled onto "at a loss." Fix: "just when I was at a loss."

40. "while I, alone, laid a pallet on the floor" — comma-fenced "alone" is stranded. Fix: "while I slept on a floor pallet, rolling my bedding up each morning."

41. "the three of us would dig in around the table—and how good it tasted" — bolted-on exclamation calquing 好吃极了. Fix: "and it tasted wonderful."

42. "held out the hand of friendship to me as well" — 友谊之手 cliché calque. Fix: "offered me his hand as well."

43. "Brother Jianqiu hurried to introduce us to each other, saying:" — 赶忙 calque plus biblical "saying:". Fix: "Brother Jianqiu quickly introduced us:".

44. "That the fifth class should be called 'little brother' and the sixth 'elder brother'—there was something odd in this form of introduction" — topic-comment word order transferred whole. Fix: "It was an odd introduction — the fifth class the 'little brother,' the sixth the 'elder.'"

45. "To look at, he was a good deal older than I" — 看上去 calque. Fix: "He looked a good deal older than I."

46. "uncommon in feature and extraordinary in bearing" — paired four-character formula (相貌不凡) calqued as parallel prepositional phrases. Fix: "a striking, imposing man."

47. "the local burrs in our speech were all rather thick" — "burrs... thick" mixed collocation. Fix: "we all spoke with thick regional accents."

48. "if the light had not begun to fail, we would never have noticed the sun already sinking in the west" — failing light and sinking sun say it twice; 夕阳西下 calque ("in the west" is where the sun always sinks). Fix: "we'd never have noticed dusk coming on."

49. "modest in its scale, only a single storey over a ground floor" — "modest in its scale" stilted; the storey description is confusing. Fix: "a modest two-storey place."

50. "everyone thought it not bad at all" — 不错 litotes calque. Fix: "we all thought it a fine meal."

51. "he made a great show of coming to my aid like a knight drawing his sword" — 拔刀相助 calqued and explained in-line, killing the joke. Fix: "he leapt gallantly to my rescue."

52. "I pressed on, puzzled: if he served at the residence, why, when the Commandant had just now stepped down and returned to Fenghua, had he not gone along?" — question buried under an interrupting clause plus inversion. Fix: "why hadn't he gone along when the Commandant stepped down and went home to Fenghua?"

53. "As we came to know one another better, and our acquaintance deepened not a little" — the second clause repeats the first; "not a little" litotes calque. Fix: cut the second clause.

54. "slept the one over the other in a double bunk" — stilted. Fix: "shared a bunk bed."

55. "He is a graduate of Meiji University in Japan, and I got no further than middle school, yet the two of us actually became fast friends" — dialogue reads like an essay; "actually" mis-renders 居然. Fix: "He'd been to Meiji University in Japan and I never got past middle school, but somehow we became fast friends."

56. "we parted, and from then till now I have never seen him again" — 至今 calque doubling "never...again." Fix: "and I've not seen him since."

57. "And then I asked back:" — 反问 calque. Fix: "Then I asked him in turn:".

58. "That Dai Yunong should ask this quite bewildered me" — that-clause subject inversion. Fix: "The question bewildered me."

59. "engaged in divisive activity against the government" — 分裂活动 bureaucratic calque. Fix: "working to split the government."

60. "had in it something of a sounding-out, or a test" — "had in it something of" stilted (the "in it" tic recurs: "there must surely have been some reason in it," "there was an episode in it," "one tale in it"). Fix: "was something of a probe — a test."

61. "and the times Dai Yunong met with us grew fewer, and each visit shorter" — "the times... grew fewer" calque. Fix: "Dai Yunong came less often and stayed more briefly."

62. "Zhang Binghua showed himself uncommonly busy" — 显得 calque. Fix: "seemed uncommonly busy."

63. "even when he did come back to rest, his rest was cut short" — rest/rest repetition. Fix: "even when he came home, he barely rested before he was off again."

64. "our army fought back with valor" — propaganda-register calque (奋勇抵抗). Fix: "our army put up fierce resistance."

65. "Of late, whenever Dai Yunong met with us, he would remark" — "Of late" is present-anchored inside past narrative. Fix: "Around this time."

66. "'you can hand it to me to pass up—it is the handier way'" — wooden dialogue; "pass up" and "handier way" are calques. Fix: "'give it to me and I'll pass it along — it's quicker.'"

67. "'I have no request to make, nor any memorial to offer'" — "memorial" fake-antique court language in casual speech. Fix: "'I've nothing to ask and nothing to report.'"

68. "Saying so, I felt myself very much in the right" — dangling participle plus calque. Fix: "And as I said it, I felt quite pleased with myself."

69. "it is a pity that at the time I could not take his meaning" — 可惜 formula (recurs: "only it is a pity," "It is a pity that many of the courses..."). Fix: "a pity I missed his meaning."

70. "a heavy, bulging large envelope" — adjective pile with "large" stranded. Fix: "a large, bulging envelope."

71. "He said he was 'entrusting' me to make a special trip" — needless scare quotes (the scare-quote tic pervades: "called me up," "taken in," "commission," "annotated," "startle all with its first cry"); "make a special trip" calques 专程. Fix: "He asked me to go specially to Xuzhou at once"; strip most scare quotes throughout.

72. "Hearing that the Commandant was then quartered at Xuzhou, and that Dai Yunong wanted me to go there and charged me to deliver it in person to the aide Dai—its importance was plain to see" — anacoluthon: the participial opening never gets a subject. Fix: "Since the Commandant was at Xuzhou and I was to hand it personally to Aide Dai, its importance was plain."

73. "ask anyone, and you will find this aide Dai" — demonstrative calque of 这位. Fix: "you'll find Aide Dai easily enough."

74. "he turned it over and over, took out a name card and wrote a few words on it by way of a receipt, gave me no spoken instructions, and simply sent me back" — four verbs chained flat; "no spoken instructions" calque. Fix: split: "...scribbled a receipt on a name card. He said nothing — just sent me back."

75. "a not-very-tidy one-storey house" — hyphenated negative compound. Fix: "a rather untidy single-storey house."

76. "you turned this way and that through several crooked bends" — doubled turning. Fix: "you wound through several turns."

77. "the floor would be spread with carpet; his was spread with straw matting" — "spread with" twice; wrong verb for carpet. Fix: "would have been carpeted; his had straw matting."

78. "I thought a moment and hit on Lian Mou" — 某 ("a certain") transliterated as if a given name; an English reader takes "Mou" for his name. Fix: "hit on a certain Lian — Lian Liangshun, as he was also known."

79. "I introduced them, and they met." — flat doubled statement calquing 一经介绍,两人见了面. Fix: "So I introduced them."

80. "nor had brother Liangshun bidden me farewell" — fake-antique. Fix: "nor did brother Liangshun say goodbye."

81. "Judging from these two matters, where lay Dai Yunong's mission? One might guess well over half of it" — inverted rhetorical question plus 猜出大半 calque. Fix: "From these two errands one could guess most of what Dai Yunong's mission was."

82. "brought his good lady over" — "good lady" mock-archaic for 夫人. Fix: "brought his wife over."

83. "would rather stand in a helping place; and so indeed it proved" — "stand in a helping place" calque. Fix: "preferred to help from the sidelines; and so it proved."

84. "With this for a hub, in the small inns of the surrounding quarter lodged a good many academy classmates" — locative inversion transferred from Chinese. Fix: "The inns round about were full of academy classmates."

85. "any activity out of the ordinary run, beyond the bounds" — the two phrases duplicate each other ("the ordinary run" itself recurs three times). Fix: "anything out of the ordinary."

86. "Seen simply, it was a small matter he had entrusted to me; put another way, it was as good as assigning me a mission" — paired discourse frames calqued (说起来…换句话说). Fix: "On its face a small errand; in effect, an assignment."

87. "I did not quite grasp it—this was what is called 'intelligence work.'" — the dash breaks the logic; tense clash in "what is called." Fix: "I did not quite grasp that this was what is called intelligence work."

88. "By the general record, at the end of February 1931 Dai Yunong had already been marked out as head" — "By the general record" is not an English frame; "marked out as head" reuses "marked out" oddly. Fix: "According to the standard account... had already been designated head." (Also check the date — 1931 here, 1932 everywhere else.)

89. "Ever after, the March 17th Assembly was held year after year" — "Ever after" plus "year after year" doubles. Fix: "The assembly was held every year thereafter."

90. "It is indeed a day worth commemorating." — flat editorial calque of 确实值得纪念. Fix: cut, or "a day well worth remembering."

91. "in that season of our first acquaintance Dai Yunong already bore a mission of 'liaison'" — "in that season" antique; "bore a mission" calques 负有任务 (recurs). Fix: "when we first met, Dai Yunong was already charged with liaison work."

92. "this too is not wholly so" — 不尽然 calque. Fix: "this is not quite accurate."

93. "This is one proof of how very hard it is to write history." — "one proof of how very" stilted. Fix: "Which shows how hard it is to write history."

94. "for his class-standing and position... were both below those of others, so how did this heavy charge come to be laid on him?" — double connective "for...so" transfers 因为…那么. Fix: drop "for": "His class and standing were junior to the other senior cadres' — so how did the job fall to him?"

95. "a certain comrade—now gone abroad, whose name I had better not speak—came to pull me down. He said, 'As for the personnel and funds..., the Commandant has told him to take charge of them.'" — pronoun fog: "him" inside direct quotation should be "me." Fix: make it indirect speech or correct the pronoun.

96. "his taking up the Special Services Department was owed entirely to the support and favor" — "was owed entirely to" passive calque. Fix: "he owed the appointment entirely to the Commandant's support."

97. "And that was because he already had behind him a record of distinguished work." — clause scaffolding. Fix: "He already had a distinguished record."

98. "I heard from an old man more than ten years my senior, who would not let me give his name, much about Mr. Dai Yunong's conduct" — object ("much") stranded far from its verb. Fix: "An old man more than ten years my senior... told me a great deal about Mr. Dai."

99. "which on the one hand made contact with and drew in patriots of high resolve, and on the other could serve as a feeler for intelligence" — mechanical 一方面…一方面; "patriots of high resolve" calques 有志之士; "feeler for intelligence" calques 情报触角. Fix: "which recruited patriotic men of purpose and doubled as an intelligence feeler."

100. "the black-hearted Feng Yuxiang" — 黑心 calqued; name-calling register clash in English. Fix: "the treacherous Feng Yuxiang."

101. "slipped them into Nanjing meaning to commit an assassination; Dai Yunong got word of it beforehand and so cracked the case" — "commit an assassination" stilted; you cannot "crack" a case that hasn't happened. Fix: "smuggled them into Nanjing to carry out an assassination; Dai Yunong got wind of it and broke up the plot."

102. "put terms to the authorities and talked loudly of 'the cut,' threatening this and that if they were refused" — "the cut" is an unglossed calque, opaque; "threatening this and that" vague calque. Fix: name what was demanded (protection money/a share) and the threat.

103. "many a famous 'iron man' of the day, once under his hand, would turn soft enough to wind round a finger" — 绕指柔 calqued raw; "under his hand" also calque. Fix: "once he took them in hand, turned soft as wax."

104. "came to talk with me—what an abrupt thing that was" — tacked-on exclamation calquing 真是唐突. Fix: fold in: "abruptly came to talk with me, unannounced."

105. "from my family circumstances to my ideological schooling" — 思想 + 学历 fused into a non-phrase. Fix: "from my family to my education and ideas."

106. "Turn it over as I might, I truly could not make out what he was driving at" — inverted concessive plus "truly" filler (the "truly/indeed" intensifier tic runs throughout). Fix: "However I turned it over, I couldn't make out what he was driving at."

107. "the more I thought the worse it sat with me" — idiom blend ("sat ill with me" / "didn't sit right"). Fix: "the more I thought about it, the less I liked it."

108. "Was it that I had already been cowed by that irresistible force of his coming?" — "Was it that" calques 是不是; "irresistible force of his coming" garbled. Fix: "Had I simply been overborne by his assurance?"

109. "one of our own, come by order?" — stilted participle. Fix: "one of our own, sent on orders?"

110. "He beat about the bush no longer, but said outright" — "no longer" implies he had been beating it; inversion. Fix: "He didn't beat about the bush."

111. "'Now a political organization full of new life has been born, one may say a product of a great age: whole-heartedly upholding... practicing vigorously... sacrificing... obeying... opening up a new order together.'" — an unbroken participial slogan-catalogue passed off as speech; no English speaker talks in eight parallel gerunds. Fix: break into short declarative sentences (the canned quality can be kept, the ungrammatical stacking cannot).

112. "In my heart I thought:" — 心想 calque (recurs: "we already understood each other in our hearts," "glad of it in my heart"). Fix: "I thought to myself."

113. "'Have you any further considerations?'" — bureaucratic calque of 还有什么考虑. Fix: "'Is anything holding you back?'"

114. "'easily leaves the other party at a loss... it seems it ought to be improved'" — dialogue in memo-speak ("the other party," impersonal "it ought to be improved"). Fix: "'can throw a man off... we ought to handle it better.'"

115. "he hinted that I had passed this barrier too" — 过关 calque. Fix: "passed this test too."

116. "Whether I filled out many kinds of forms and went through what procedures... I can no longer remember" — interrogative structure transferred; "whether...what" cannot share a frame. Fix: "What forms I filled out, what procedures I went through, I no longer remember."

117. "naturally carried a certain private pride" — "carried a pride" collocation error. Fix: "gave me a certain secret pride."

118. "I had been taken in only at the second tier, still on the 'periphery' of the core" — "periphery of the core" is a contradiction as worded. Fix: "still outside the core, on its periphery."

119. "within perhaps three or five days" — 三五天 calque; English says "three or four" or "a few." Fix: "within a few days."

120. "a point I shall account for later in the course of events" — "in the course of events" filler calque. Fix: "as I shall explain in due course."

121. "gave a strict, secret, short-term training in intelligence business" — "intelligence business" calques 情报业务 (recurs); "a training" article misuse (recurs: "a special training," "a short training"). Fix: "gave strict, secret, short-term training in intelligence work."

122. "a definite relation of subordination, of principal and follower" — legalese doublet calquing 主从关系. Fix: "a clear relationship of subordination."

123. "This is hard to say, for even a hair's difference of standpoint can breed a great divergence" — "This is hard to say" is a non sequitur here; the 失之毫厘 proverb is mangled. Fix: "though a hair's difference in standpoint can grow into a great divergence."

124. "half was rumor and half surmise, and none of that can count" — 不能算数 calque. Fix: "and none of it counts for anything."

125. "The long passage below sets down in detail how I answered the call-up" — self-referential signpost calque; "answered the call-up" (recurs) is stiff. Fix: "What follows is how I was called up and trained."

126. "without first asking my consent, he seemed already certain that I was bound to agree to his request. Neither explaining nor accounting for it" — dangling participles front and back; "explaining"/"accounting for" duplicate. Fix: "He didn't ask my consent — he seemed certain I'd agree — and offered no explanation."

127. "a special training of no very long term" — 为期不长 litotes calque. Fix: "a fairly short special course."

128. "I furnished him a list with brief personal particulars attached, for his use" — bureaucratese with "for his use" as filler. Fix: "get him a list, with brief particulars, within the week."

129. "some were half believing and half in doubt, for they could not fathom that I should have this sort of connection, or rather did not wholly believe there was any such thing in it" — 半信半疑 calque followed by a clause that restates itself. Fix: "some were half convinced, half doubtful — they couldn't credit that I had connections of this sort."

130. "Mr. Dai Yunong took the whole list, without a moment's thought" — reads as careless rather than the intended unhesitating acceptance. Fix: "accepted the whole list without a second glance."

131. "This site was not large in area" — 面积不大 calque (recurs: "The platform was small in area"). Fix: "The compound was small."

132. "In one side wall facing south a small narrow door had newly been cut" — inversion plus passive stack. Fix: "A narrow little door had just been cut in the south-facing wall."

133. "Call it a door—it was really more like a hole" — the "call it X" concessive frame is a 说是…其实 calque and recurs four times ("call it black—it was really dark gray," "call her a girl, but she was in fact over twenty," "call it a consequence"). Fix each: "It was less a door than a hole"; "more dark gray than black"; "a young woman, really — over twenty."

134. "Later, the 'Honggongci Training Class' of the Juntong's records had its origin here" — garbled possession. Fix: "This was the origin of the 'Honggongci Training Class' of Juntong records."

135. "Under the class headquarters it was divided into sections" — dangling "it." Fix: "Below the class headquarters, the course was divided into sections."

136. "Yet, high and low, inside and out, the whole class was charged with an air of mystery" — piled four-character doublets (上上下下,里里外外). Fix: "Yet the whole class, top to bottom, was wrapped in mystery."

137. "There was another thing past understanding, and it was this:" — existential scaffold. Fix: "Another thing I could not understand:".

138. "since the Special Service Police Training Class was run by the Lixingshe, yet from the opening to the graduation..." — "since...yet" double connective transfers 既然…却. Fix: drop one connective.

139. "What was this for? It is truly a thing to ponder." — 耐人寻味 calque. Fix: "It gave one pause."

140. "to call them merely 'crude' hardly seems enough; add 'shabby,' and it comes near the mark" — the 光说…再加上 construction calqued whole. Fix: "'crude' hardly covers it; 'crude and shabby' comes nearer."

141. "truly a road of thorns and brambles" — 荆棘之路 cliché calque. Fix: "the going was genuinely hard."

142. "a white curtain that swayed without rest whenever the wind blew" — 不停地 calqued as "without rest." Fix: "that stirred whenever the wind blew."

143. "add the damp of the mildew season, and there was a foul reek fit to sting the nose" — imperative-conditional 加上… calque (recurs: "set out chairs and sit down, and it held only six or seven"); "fit to sting the nose" calques 刺鼻. Fix: "and with the damp of the rainy season, the reek stung your nose."

144. "had strict tidiness of quarters been demanded, it would have been a hard thing to achieve" — inverted conditional plus nominalization. Fix: "barracks-neat quarters would have been impossible to demand."

145. "an oiled, glossy black table, reckoned the most eye-catching new object of all" — "reckoned" hedges a plain fact; "object of all" clunky. Fix: "a glossy black-lacquered table, the newest, most eye-catching thing in the room."

146. "The classroom, hall, and gathering-place—an all-purpose great room—was bare of any adornment" — three-noun subject with singular verb, then an appositive to rescue it. Fix: "This all-purpose hall — classroom, auditorium, meeting-place in one — was bare of adornment."

147. "The wording of the slogans held nothing striking" — "held nothing striking" calque. Fix: "There was nothing striking in the slogans."

148. "hands and feet to the brethren" — "brethren" fake-antique for 兄弟们. Fix: "the comrades' hands and feet."

149. "Of the three daily meals, by rule it was steamed bread and rice gruel in the morning" — "by rule it was" calque. Fix: "The rule was steamed bread and gruel for breakfast, rice at noon and night."

150. "As for whether the food was good or bad—it filled the belly, that was all" — topic frame calque. Fix: "Good or bad, it filled you up."

151. "Washing and bathing were set in an inconspicuous corner" — wrong verb for a place. Fix: "The washing place was in an inconspicuous corner."

152. "The murmur of the drain running off—was that not a sight in itself?" — rhetorical 岂不…一景 calque, and a sound is not a sight. Fix: "The gurgle of the drain was a spectacle in its own right, you might say."

449. "many worthies and talents of Northeastern origin" — 贤达才俊 calqued as a noun pair. Fix: "many prominent and able men of the Northeast."

450. "some got no further than a first meeting or the building of a certain measure of tacit understanding" — nominal pile. Fix: "some got no further than a first meeting or a certain tacit understanding."

451. "passing letters for them and fixing a time of meeting" — "fixing a time of meeting" stilted. Fix: "carrying letters and arranging meetings."

452. "they were mostly a store of talent kept for use" — 储备人才 calqued. Fix: "talent held in reserve."

453. "if the work Mr. Dai led could grow in many directions..., it owed much, of course, to this strong point of his" — conditional-for-factual calque (如果能…). Fix: "that the work grew in so many directions owed much to this gift of his."

454. "he did not rest either, but liked much to gather a few of us" — "liked much to" word order. Fix: "he loved to gather a few of us."

455. "go to some small eating-house each of a distinct flavor" — "each" floats with no plural. Fix: "small eating-houses, each with its own specialty."

456. "to observe a man and understand him, it is in the small and trifling details that you see most truly" — comma-spliced topic clause; "small and trifling" double. Fix: "it is in small details that you see a man most truly."

457. "he too often lingered together with the writer" — "too often" reads as "excessively"; "lingered together with" padded. Fix: "he often stayed behind with me."

458. "we had many points of temper in common" — 脾气相投 calqued. Fix: "we were alike in temper."

459. "Our friendship was very pure, and may be called a natural growth, without a trace even of any thought of 'convenience to the work'" — "may be called a natural growth" calque; "convenience to the work" raw. Fix: "It was a pure friendship, naturally grown, with never a thought of professional advantage."

460. "People call us wine-and-meat friends; I hold that in making friends what harm is there in 'wine and meat'" — 酒肉朋友 calqued unglossed, then argued with. Fix: "People call us fair-weather, wine-and-meat friends; I say, what harm in wine and meat, so long as true feeling lies beneath?"

461. "it will figure many times in the perilous life to come" — "the life to come" reads as the afterlife. Fix: "in the perilous years ahead."

462. "What lay behind it, I had best not guess wildly" — the humility formula again (see item 252) with fronted object. Fix: "What lay behind it, I won't guess."

463. "for a talk at his lodging at Qifenglou—in fact, to listen to him speak" — the correction stumbles. Fix: "for a talk — which meant, in practice, listening to him speak."

464. "Mr. Dai had done a little arranging in the parlor" — 布置了一下 calqued. Fix: "Mr. Dai had the parlor arranged to seat ten or so."

465. "addressed a few especially courteous words to Mr. Zheng Jiemin, who was present, and then began to speak" — "who was present" states the obvious; "words"/"speak" doubles. Fix: "said a few courteous words to Mr. Zheng Jiemin, then began."

466. "He said a great deal, which, summed up, may be put in four points:" — double hedge scaffold. Fix: "It came down to four points:".

467. "strengthen their working disposition—that is, open up intelligence lines on many sides" — 工作部署 calque again. Fix: "strengthen their operations — open intelligence lines on every side."

468. "its support of traitors' work of sabotage" — triple-genitive pile. Fix: "its backing of traitor saboteurs."

469. "he asked that each of us spur and school himself, enrich himself, and strengthen his working ability, that he might bear the heavy burden" — exhortation boilerplate; archaic "that he might." Fix: "he urged each of us to drive himself on, keep learning, and grow equal to the burden."

470. "again very respectfully asked 'Mr. Jiemin' to give some views" — 发表意见 calqued as "give some views." Fix: "asked Mr. Jiemin to say a few words."

471. "drew out a few points along the lines Mr. Dai had spoken" — "along the lines... had spoken" ungrammatical. Fix: "obliged with a few points along the same lines."

472. "brother Tianmu said nothing, and I had all the less to say" — 更没什么可说 calqued. Fix: "brother Tianmu said nothing, and I still less."

473. "The speaking done, all were silent, nor did we exchange any views among ourselves. This too was where we differed from the common run." — absolute opener, "exchange views" again, "the common run" again. Fix: "When the talk ended, no one spoke — no discussion among ourselves either. Another way we differed from ordinary folk."

474. "Then we took a plain meal there at Mr. Dai's, which was as good as a farewell dinner" — "took a meal" stilted; "as good as" calques 就算是. Fix: "We ate a simple meal at Mr. Dai's — our farewell dinner, in effect."

475. "in those days there was no aircraft from Beiping to Nanjing, and the handiest conveyance was the 'Blue Steel express'" — "no aircraft" for "no air service"; "handiest conveyance" stilted. Fix: "there was no air service from Beiping to Nanjing, and the best way down was the 'Blue Steel Express.'"

476. "The 'Blue Steel express,' so called, was simply a train painted blue... and fast, punctual, and smooth in the running—nothing more" — "so called" misplaced; "smooth in the running" stilted. Fix: "The 'Blue Steel Express' was just a blue-painted train — solid, fast, punctual, and smooth, nothing more."

477. "I said a special 'keep well' to brother Mao Wanli" — 道珍重 calqued as a quoted token. Fix: "I made a point of telling brother Mao Wanli to take care of himself."

478. "'I hope you two will cooperate well in the work.'" — bureaucratese in an affectionate farewell. Fix: "'I hope you two will work well together.'"

479. "Mr. Dai suddenly recalled a matter and charged me to see to it" — "recalled a matter" wooden. Fix: "Mr. Dai suddenly remembered something and gave me a job."

480. "I thought it something specially urgent, and listened sharply for his instruction" — "listened sharply" collocation error. Fix: "listened hard."

481. "So that was all it was; hearing it, I felt distinctly flat" — "felt distinctly flat" calque of deflation. Fix: "I felt rather deflated."

482. "still a good eight-tenths new" — 八成新 calqued as a fraction. Fix: "still nearly new."

483. "This was a small matter, not worth a line here; but there just are such coincidences under heaven, and who would have thought that... this car would win first honors in our work" — "not worth a line" contradicts the line it sits in; 天下就有这么巧的事 and 立头功 calqued. Fix: "A trifle, hardly worth a line — except that, by one of those strange coincidences, this car would shortly win first honors in our work."

484. "relayed through the night the urgent sanction order handed down by the highest military and political authorities in Beiping, setting a term of one week to report back" — "sanction order" (制裁令 = execution order) unglossed; "setting a term of one week to report back" bureaucratic and unclear about what is reported. Fix: "relayed overnight an urgent order of sanction — execution — from the highest authorities in Beiping, with one week to report the deed done."

485. "put to death Zhang Jingyao—that former military governor of Hunan, that leftover creature of a spent warlord, blinded by greed and lost to all national feeling, who was willing to be a claw and fang of the Japanese invaders—in the Grand Hôtel des Wagons-Lits" — a five-clause denunciation wedged between verb and location; "leftover creature of a spent warlord" (军阀余孽) and "claw and fang" (爪牙) calqued. Fix: state the killing and place first, characterize Zhang in a following sentence; "a willing tool of the Japanese."

486. "This forceful blow not only quelled the frenzied schemer's design to stir up disorder, but also shattered the plot" — editorial-boilerplate register (一举粉碎); "frenzied schemer" calque. Fix: "The blow ended Zhang's scheming and wrecked the plot Itagaki Seishirō had drawn up."

487. "steadied the frightened hearts of the Beiping and Tianjin people" — 安定人心 calqued. Fix: "calmed the fears of the people of Beiping and Tianjin."

488. "It also played its part in allowing the preparations for a long war of resistance to be laid, unhurried, over the four years that followed" — "played its part in allowing... to be laid, unhurried" tangled passive chain. Fix: "It also bought four unhurried years in which to prepare for the long war of resistance."

489. "using every means of incitement, seduction, bribery, and deception in our highest military and political organs and in our fighting units, he had won over a good many senior officials" — the "in our organs" phrase attaches to the wrong element (the means, not the targets). Fix: "working on our highest military and political organs and our fighting units with incitement, seduction, bribery, and deception, he had won over a good many senior officials."

490. "Had he not been cut off in good time, and a disaster dissolved before it took shape, the consequences would have been fearful to contemplate" — 消弭于无形 calqued as "dissolved before it took shape," doubling "cut off in good time." Fix: "Had he not been stopped in time, the consequences would have been fearful to contemplate."

491. "The working unit at Beiping and Tianjin then, short of hands, crude in equipment, and having to report back within a set term on a matter come up all at once, yet did not fail its mission" — subject buried under three stacked modifiers; "a matter come up all at once" archaic participle; "yet did not" inversion. Fix: "Short-handed, ill-equipped, and handed a sudden job on a deadline, the Beiping and Tianjin units did not fail."

492. "we prided ourselves that it was a flawless and masterly stroke" — "prided ourselves that" wrong construction. Fix: "we prided ourselves on a flawless, masterly stroke."

493. "And the man of the first merit in carrying through this case was Mr. Bai Shiwei" — 首功 calqued as "man of the first merit." Fix: "The chief credit for the operation belongs to Mr. Bai Shiwei, happily still living today."

Recurring tics worth a single global sweep, beyond the instances above: the "as for / as to / in the matter of / on this point" topic frames; scare quotes on ordinary words; "the higher-ups" on every page; "the writer" for "I"; the "truly / indeed / in fact" intensifier padding; "may be called / could be called / might be reckoned" hedges; "in person / by his own hand"; the "no easy matter / not simple / not to be forced" litotes tags; and the "dare not guess wildly / respectfully await guidance" humility formulas — each defensible once as period flavor, but at their current density they are the single loudest source of translationese in the chapter.
