### p001 [T2] RECAST
OLD: and there are cases, besides, that by memory alone I could not keep so many of in mind.
NEW: and there are still other cases that memory alone could not hold, so many were they.
WHY: 更有一些个案，单凭记忆又记不住那么许多了. Kills adverbial "besides" (更有) and the awkward "could not keep so many of in mind"; preserves "more cases than memory can hold."

### p001 [T2] RECAST
OLD: for which, on this point, I ought to render the martyred forerunners who died my deep contrition.
NEW: for which, on this point, I ought to offer my deep contrition to the martyred forerunners.
WHY: 应该对死难的先烈们深致歉疚. Fixes the stilted ditransitive inversion "render [them] my contrition"; 死难 ("died as martyrs") is already carried by "martyred forerunners" (先烈), so "who died" is dropped as redundant, no content change.

### p002 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: has, though it now comes to a pause, on my reading it over from beginning to end left me with no little sense that the words fall short
NEW: has, though it now comes to a pause, on my reading it over from beginning to end left me with a strong sense that the words fall short
WHY: 颇有言未尽当…之感 = "quite a sense / a strong sense"; kills the litotes "no little sense."
