ANCHOR: the Blue Sky and White Sun flag
NOTE: The <i>Blue Sky and White Sun flag</i> is the national flag of the Republic of China under the Nationalists&#8212;a white twelve-rayed sun on a blue canton over a red field (青天白日满地红), adopted in 1928; the blue-sky-white-sun emblem is also the Kuomintang's own party flag. Its flying over the gate marks the ground as Nationalist military territory. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the Longhua Temple
NOTE: <i>Longhua Temple</i> (龙华寺), beside the garrison, is the oldest and largest of Shanghai's Buddhist temples&#8212;its founding traditionally ascribed to Sun Quan of Wu in the third century, its pagoda dating to 977&#8212;and is famous for its spring peach blossoms and temple fair. The killing-ground sits in the shadow of this ancient sanctuary. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: murmured an Amitābha to himself
NOTE: To murmur <i>Amitābha</i> (阿弥陀佛) is to invoke the Buddha of the Western Paradise; the phrase is a reflexive pious ejaculation, said much as an English speaker might sigh 'Lord have mercy.' Here it is a reflex of habit as Mu Chuan glances at the pagoda, with an irony the reader is left to feel. (Texture.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: The Theory and Practice of Secret-Service Work
NOTE: <i>The Theory and Practice of Secret-Service Work</i> lightly renames a real text. After the Communist security chief Gu Shunzhang (顾顺章) defected to the Nationalists in 1931, he wrote up the underground's methods in works such as <i>The Theory and Practice of Special-Service Work</i> (特务工作的理论与实际), which became training manuals for the Nationalist secret services. That You Tianxiao must apply, through channels, for a guarded copy is true to the book's restricted circulation. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the Zhanyuan garden
NOTE: The <i>Zhanyuan garden</i> (瞻园) in Nanjing, into which the Investigation Section moved, is a celebrated Ming classical garden&#8212;the west garden of the mansion of Xu Da (徐达), foremost general of the Ming founding, and later the palace of the Taiping 'Eastern King'; today it houses the Taiping Heavenly Kingdom History Museum. That the Nationalists' secret police kept their 'Special Operations Headquarters' in so storied a garden is historical fact. (Corroborated.)

NOTE-ADD
ANCHOR: the badger-game swindlers
NOTE: <i>Badger-game swindlers</i> renders 仙人跳: a con in which a woman lures a man and a confederate, posing as her wronged husband, bursts in to extort him. The trick is old, attested from the Song under other names and called 仙人跳 by Qing times. (Corroborated.)
