## --- wrong word / wrong connotation ---

### p5 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: the same black hair and sallow faces as everyone else
NEW: the same black hair and yellow skin as everyone else
WHY: 黄皮肤 is the ordinary Chinese complexion; "sallow" wrongly adds a sickly overlay the source does not carry. The point is that the Communists looked like everyone else. Content identical.

### p91 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: government himself stooped to serve as head of the Xi'an office.
NEW: government himself took the lesser post of head of the Xi'an office.
WHY: 屈就 is graciously accepting a post below one's station; English "stooped" adds a demeaning connotation the honorific does not carry. Content identical.

### p419 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: Lin Boqu, stooped to serve as head of the Xi'an office,
NEW: Lin Boqu, took the lesser post of head of the Xi'an office,
WHY: same 屈就 fix, for consistency within the chapter. Content identical.

### p75 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: The Wuhan office held the most bodies of all under one roof:
NEW: The Wuhan office brought the most organizations together under one roof:
WHY: 机构 -> "bodies ... under one roof" invites the corpse reading; the following list names three organizations. Content identical.

### p77 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: The unmarked Yangtze Bureau directed the entire underground Party of southern China.
NEW: The secret Yangtze Bureau directed the entire underground Party of southern China.
WHY: "unmarked" is opaque (it means the bureau kept no public sign); "secret" states its covert nature plainly, against the open Eighth Route office it shared quarters with. Content identical.

## --- calqued idioms (naturalize to sense) ---

### p31 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: he was a fish returned to water
NEW: he was in his element
WHY: 如鱼得水 rendered as the awkward "a fish returned to water"; "in his element" is the natural idiom (Li Kenong thriving back in Shanghai). Content identical.

### p473 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: and has made many an article out of Hu Zongnan.
NEW: and has made much of Hu Zongnan.
WHY: 做文章 is to make an issue of / capitalize on; "made many an article out of" is a literal-plus-fake-antique calque. Content identical.

### p475 [T3] TOUCH
OLD: The Generalissimo's methods really could not bear the light.
NEW: The Generalissimo's methods really could not bear the light of day.
WHY: 见不得光 -> the complete English idiom is "cannot bear the light of day". Content identical.
