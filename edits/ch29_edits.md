NOTE: <i>Laoximen</i> (老西门, &#8216;Old West Gate&#8217;) is a locality of the old Chinese city, named for its western gate &#8212; the Ming-built Yifeng Gate (仪凤门) of 1553, called &#8216;old&#8217; after a smaller west gate was opened nearby in 1908.
