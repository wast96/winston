ANCHOR: deceiving heaven and crossing the sea
NOTE: <i>Deceiving heaven and crossing the sea</i> (瞒天过海) is a set idiom and the first of the classic Thirty-Six Stratagems (三十六计): to mask a great move behind ordinary, open activity, so that watchers grow used to the routine and miss the real intent &#8212; deception carried out in plain sight rather than in hiding. The stock tale has the Tang general Xue Rengui coax the sea-fearing Emperor Taizong across the water by leading him into what seemed a seaside hall but was in truth a ship, already under way before he knew it. (Corroborated.)
