### p002 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: playing the tiger's cat's-paw
NEW: playing cat's-paw to the tiger
WHY: 为虎作伥; "the tiger's cat's-paw" doubles the vehicle and misfits the idiom (a cat's-paw is already the dupe). "play cat's-paw to X" is the idiomatic form and asserts the right image — used by a greater evil to harm others.

### p002 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: but were merely so many plain civilians
NEW: but were merely plain civilians
WHY: 仅是一个纯粹老百姓; "so many plain civilians" reads as a count and is awkward; "merely plain civilians" is the plain sense.

### p002 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: and our own likeness, too, ought to be that of
NEW: and our own image, too, ought to be that of
WHY: 我们的形像 = our image/reputation; "likeness" claims a physical portrait, the wrong assertion (RULE R1-1). "image" is faithful and natural.

### p003 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: The carrying-out of the Wang case may be divided into three phases.
NEW: Carrying out the Wang case fell into three phases.
WHY: 江案的执行，可划分为三个阶段; "the carrying-out of the Wang case" is the gerund-of nominalization → gerund subject + finite verb. Number "three" preserved.

### p003 [T2] TOUCH
OLD: could there be said to be a final decision at last
NEW: was the final decision made at last
WHY: 才算有了最后的决定; "could there be said to be" is a stiff impersonal periphrasis → plain finite verb; keeps 才 ("at last").
