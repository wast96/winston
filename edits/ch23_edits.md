NOTE: <i>The Beginning of Spring</i> (立春, <i>Lichun</i>) is the first of the twenty-four solar terms (二十四节气) of the traditional Chinese calendar, falling about February 4, when the sun reaches celestial longitude 315&#176;. It opens both spring and the farming year, and here fixes the scene within the lunar New Year season. (Corroborated.)
