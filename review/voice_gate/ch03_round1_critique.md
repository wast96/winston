## Systemic patterns
- Name-repetition where English wants a pronoun (Shen Anna, Xiong Xianghui, Luo Qingchang, Xuan Xiafu, Mao Zemin sequences).
- "upper reaches" for society's elite ("Nationalist upper reaches," "upper reaches of society") — in English "upper reaches" is a river; register also lurches to "upper crust"/"top brass".
