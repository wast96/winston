## Systemic patterns
1. Dramatic trailing ellipses ("got away on foot..."; "toward the horizon..."; "never once left the ranks...") read as unfinished in English.
2. "organs" for agencies/offices reads as body parts to an English reader.
3. Rhetorical narrator interjections ("Who could have foreseen it:"; "No one had guessed that"; "One would think... yet"; "Which shows that…"; "Which may show that…").
4. Fake-antique litotes ("learning no little of the craft"; "won over no few turncoats").
5. "within the trade / inside the system / within the circle."
6. Rhetorical questions as calqued emphasis ("how could Yan'an's order not change?"; "Where under heaven were there police as good as these?").
