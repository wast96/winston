# STYLE delta — fiction (genre-fiction)

Genre layer for a work of narrative fiction. Composes onto `_base.md` plus a
language layer. It switches the enrichment license ON (within the base's hard
floor) and refines the mode and apparatus notes for fiction. The voice-target
directive on this file's first line is woven into the base contract by the
composer.

(Obeys CLAUDE.md rule 6: no em dashes in this sheet's prose except inside
quoted examples.)

## The voice
The target is a novel, not a decoding: if a sentence sounds like a careful,
faithful rendering, it fails; if it sounds like a novel, it passes. Where the
book has a strong, nameable authorial voice (a Shiba Ryōtarō, a Jin Yong), the
book's `STYLE.local.md` sharpens the target to that author at the voice gate.
Keep the author's registers distinct: swift cinematic narrative,
character-marked dialogue, and any fourth-wall essayistic aside must each
survive into English as themselves.

## Enrichment doctrine — ON, within bounds (where rendering ends and inventing begins)

In fiction, enriching the RENDERING of what the source already holds is craft.
(The hard floor on invention in `_base.md` still binds absolutely: no new
events, no new facts, no invented precision, ever.)

Legitimate enrichment (make the implicit explicit; choose the exact word):
- ambient conditions the scene has already fixed (name the failing light once
  dusk is established);
- physical properties inherent in a thing already named (the greyness and
  rope-like matting of named river-weed);
- the physical completion of a stated action in its stated setting;
- the sensory quality the source has already asserted.
Test: would a reader of the source already picture this, unprompted, from what
the source built? If yes, enrich. If not, stop.

The dial: a light touch, not a repaint. One exact noun and one grounding phrase
usually suffice to lift a line from decoded to composed. Piling on detail
becomes its own infidelity by overriding the source's economy. And carry no
implication the source withholds: if a herb is offered in general terms, the
listener is NOT told his own stomach is bad.

## Mode application (refines the _base framework)
- **Dialogue** is natural and contracted and differentiated by character (see
  the voice sheets in HANDOFF). Rough men, spies, and peasants do not talk like
  courtiers. Contractions are measured, not stuffed: use them for a living
  voice, but do not pile the same one up, and keep the occasional grave,
  uncontracted line where the weight wants it. A small idiomatic touch that fits
  a speaker is welcome even when it is not literally in the source; that is
  flavour, not licence to invent plot or fact.
- **The author's fourth-wall asides** keep their buttonholing, conversational,
  faintly ironic energy. These are the author talking straight to the reader;
  flattening them into neutral exposition loses the book's signature. This is
  the target register at its most relaxed.

## Apparatus, fiction-specific
- **Do not footnote the invented.** The fiction's own furniture (a made-up
  technique, a codename, a fictional place) gets a glossary line at most.
  Footnotes are for the real world behind the fiction: history, offices,
  customs, money, geography, not for the story's own props.
